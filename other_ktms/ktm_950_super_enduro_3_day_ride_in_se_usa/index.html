Good film sequence, those trails look fun, though I'd rather do them on my WR250 than a 950 Super Enduro - much easier to pick it up when the inevitable happens, and there's less to damage on it! What is it about the magnetic pull that ruts and rain gullies have that draws your wheels in despite your best intentions of avoiding them? I guess the old adage of "you go where you look" applies only too well here. The stony section (after about 7:00) and subsequent "footpath along the side of a hill" bits are reminiscent of some of the gnarlier tracks that we ride in the Spanish Pyrenees, and rocks are much easier to negotiate when they're dry!
