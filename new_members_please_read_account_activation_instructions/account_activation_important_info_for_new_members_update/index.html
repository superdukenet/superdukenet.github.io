Dear all,

We are always happy to welcome new members but unfortunately we moderators are being overwhelmed by bogus accounts - often well over 100 per day - and sifting through them all to find the 'real' members is no longer a practical option.

So what do you need to do to set up your account?

Well, quite simple really. Set up your account as normal then email your details to superduke.net.activation@gmail.com and we'll activate it for you then let you know when you're ready to rock.

We need;

Your username
Your real name
Your home address (number, street, town, country and post/zip code)
Your phone number


The details you give us will not be used for any other purpose than to confirm your identity and will not be passed on to any 3rd parties.

Regards,

Plipton on behalf of the Moderators

Postscript by jmann: If your details don't appear bona fide you won't get activated. Personally, I don't activate accounts where the email address is somebody else's or where the address is a commercial premise as this is exactly what a malicious person would do. I also don't activate accounts emanating from camping grounds, car parks, industrial sites, churches Etc.
