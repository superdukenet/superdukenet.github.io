I frequently see rain tyres for sale on a racing forum which got me thinking...would rain tyres be a decent winter tyre for the SD?

I seem to remember this has been discussed before with Dutch but i cant find the original thread! (it might have been over at the KTM Forum) as he was asking about grip levels in winter on Rain tyres and how long they would last!?

I have used Intermediate wet tyres through winter before, the fronts last real well but the rear only lasted around 500miles!
I think youd be lucky to get 200miles out of the centre band on a rear in winter unless its also very wet!
