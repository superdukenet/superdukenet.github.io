Your dealer talkes nonsense...

There are two versions of the Akra Evo slip-ons available. One specifically for the 2007 model including EC certificate. The other one is the longer existing Evo slip-on for 05, 06 AND 07 models but when used on an 07 model it's without EC certificate. Both versions are technically and visually identical although the 07 version costs about 100 euro's more (don't ask me why).

The 07 version has a delivery time, expected end of March but these estimates can change quickly. The other version is available right now.

07 part #: 61105099200
05/06/07 part #: 61005099200
