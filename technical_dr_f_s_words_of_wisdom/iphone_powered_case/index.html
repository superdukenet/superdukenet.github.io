See . I have this case for a little over a year now. It's not powered, like the one you linked to. I just use the iPhone USB cable and plug it into a USB charger. Works like a charm.

The case itself is waterproof, and I have had no issues with water leakage, not even when riding in heavy rain.

The fixing of the case to the bracket sucks. I would never trust my expensive iPhone to a bracket like this. I have therefore modified it, fixing it with 3 screws. This also gives me the opportunity to make the whole thing a low profile bracket.

Hope this helps a little.
