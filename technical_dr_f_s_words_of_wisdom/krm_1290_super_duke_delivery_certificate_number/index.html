It should have been discussed with you as you received the bike. Nonetheless mine was in the satchel, white A4 paper 2 sheets, 3 pages. It looks like letterhead and has a whole lot of checkboxes that are ticked when the dealer checked things like "shock preload as per owners manual" <---- yes right!!!

It's got a title of Delivery Certificate and all the details of the bike, dealer, customer Etc. Quite small print about 8 point.

When I checked it only had a one liner that said date of purchase Etc. It didn't even have the date of first service of the fact that I have oil leaks.
