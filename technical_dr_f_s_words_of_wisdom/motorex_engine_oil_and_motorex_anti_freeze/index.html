The new rules on manufacturing of oil, almost all of it is the same regardless of who makes it. I always put Castrol in my vehicles. Don't ask why, I simply prefer it. So long as it's a quality motorcycle synthetic oil with the proper weight it doesn't matter what brand it is. Same for coolant. I recommend using a 50/50 blend of coolant during the winter since you'll need more corrosion protection. In the summer you can run less anti-freeze and more water to keep the bike running cooler. I usually do 20% anti-freeze and 80% water.

Anti-freeze itself is not a coolant, the purified water mixed in is. During the winter the extra salt on the roads requires the extra corrosion protection from the anti-freeze. Since that isn't a problem during the summer, I run less anti-freeze and more water mixed with Water Wetter. Worked wonders for my buddy's R6 and my SD has never over-heated even in stop and go traffic during 100F degree days.
