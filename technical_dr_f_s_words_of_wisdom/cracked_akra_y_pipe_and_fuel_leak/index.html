I usually just lurk on the site, but I had to post after my awesome discovery tonight. So I'm trying to find the fuel leak on my wife's SD tonight and happen to notice a long crack in the Y-pipe on here AK slip ons. WTF? It is hard to see in the picture but it goes all the way up the weld.
The fuel leak is coming from the underside of the tank where it rubs against the frame (white spot inside red circle I drew in) Fuel is seeping from what looks like a crack/rub spot.
I love our Super Dukes, but this is getting ridiculous.
The wife is going to call KTM HQ tomorrow and hopefully work the same magic she did with her faulty gauge cluster (Quit working 1 week after warranty expired).
Has anyone else had any problems similar to these?
