Hi all,

I recently bought a used 2008 Superduke 990 that had the stock exhaust on it, and decided to go for the full carbon MIVV Suono slip ons as aftermarket exhaust. I ordered the pipes through who ordered them driectly from MIVV in Italy, which took about three weeks.

Long story short, I finally get the slip ons and take them and my bike to the shop for installation. After playing with it for over an hour, the mech said that there is no way the midpipe could fit for installation of the mivv cans. They said the midpipe (Y-pipe) was far too short. We took some pics and talked to PJs parts, and decided something was definitely wrong. PJ sent the pics to MIVV in order to figure out what was wrong. We assumed mismanufacturing or wrong midpipe.

Now, after several weeks and reminder emails, MIVV has not come back to us with any sort of solution.

I'm pissed at PJs Parts for not resolving the issue, and they are pissed at MIVV for not finding out what is wrong.

Now, apart from sharing this story with you, I wanted to post here because clearly I have to take this into my own hands in order to get it resolved.
So, for all of those with a US model Superduke and MIVV slipons:
Have you experienced or even heard of a similar situation?
Where did you get your MIVV cans from?
Were there any issues during installation?
Here are some pics of the midpipe and bike for illustration. Does your midpipe look like this??




All input on this issue is greatly appreciated!!

Thanks all.
