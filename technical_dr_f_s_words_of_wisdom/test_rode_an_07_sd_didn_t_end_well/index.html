#1
For the bike, that is. When I pulled into the dealer's parking lot there was smoke pouring out from the bike. WTF! Turns out that the oil line from the reservoir had ruptured, coating the right side of the bike and my boot in oil. I was lucky that it happened near the end of my demo and that it didn't put me down.

Up until then it was a great ride. I really liked the motor, handling and ergonomics. But this experience left me with some doubts about KTM engineering. I know sh*t happens, but this is the second time in as many tries that something has gone wrong on a KTM demo. The other time was with an '05 Adventure (private sale) where the clutch slave cylinder failed. I didn't even get out of the driveway that time.

Just don't know if I should risk a 3rd try or ownership???
