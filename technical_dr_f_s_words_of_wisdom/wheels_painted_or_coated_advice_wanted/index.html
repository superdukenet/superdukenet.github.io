Windridge Coatings down by Whitstable are supposed to be good. Long way from you though.

I had the swingarm on my fireblade done black to match the frame. Well actually I bought another swinging arm off e bay for 30 quid and got that powder coated then swapped them = less time off the road. Planned to flog mine once swapped over but havent got around to it yet.
