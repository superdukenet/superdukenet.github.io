#15
The only thing I can think of is to make sure the tabs on the bottom of the door have gone in properly, I usually start at about 45 degrees push them in and then fold it closed.

Definitely best to run an optimate fly lead off it and keep it on charge when you're laying it off for a while, I have no electricity in my garage so just plug mine in via extension lead across the garden a couple of times during the winter to top it back up again.

Had to extend the optimate fly lead so that it went via the moulded channel with the main wire due to the fuse holder being too close to the battery connection, the previous owner had cut a notch out of the battery door so it came over the top but after the wire severed itself twice I extended it to use the proper channel.

The battery box access, and location, is probably the worst designed part of the SD. It's a total fooking nightmare. some bikes I have owned, when the battery was flat or whatever, it was a simple matter of lifting the seat and wapping on a set of jump leads. But this bike takes all records for time and effort to just have a look at the battery, it's total bollox!!

Like Juster said years ago, best mod is to attach a lead so you can at least charge the fooker up without any messing about - every one should have done this by now.

Before I did this I spent so many times lying on the ground, in the rain or cold, taking off the belly pan and all that peripheral stuff just to get access to the terminals. OMG. And then getting it all packed away and back where it belongs.

But... get that additional lead fitted, and you don't really have to bother with that shit ever again.

Do it. Do it NOW!
