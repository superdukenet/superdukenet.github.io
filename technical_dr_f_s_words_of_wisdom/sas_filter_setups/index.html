#2
Get the stage 2 SAS removal kit, stage 3 just adds some bullshit rubber hoses to make throttle body sinc easier if you were planning to do some real fine tuning.


As far as the Air intake kits each stage just adds extra shit and all depends what you plan to do to the bike. Do you plan to run a fuel controller like a Power Commander or are you just planning to get the dealer to flash it and call it good? If your just doing the flash then get the Stage 3 kit as it comes with the shorter velocity stack you will need for the rear cyclinder and the fuel dongles to trick the ECU.

I also put a PCV on my bike when I did the SAS and Rotweiler Intake and my dyno tuner told me that the bike was so close I could easily have gotten away with no fuel controller(PCV).


Only difference a between a stage 3 and stage 4 rotweiler intake system is the stage 4 comes with a PCV.
