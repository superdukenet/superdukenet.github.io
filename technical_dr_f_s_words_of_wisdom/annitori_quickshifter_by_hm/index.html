Well, I finally got arround to installing some of my mods that have been sitting in the shed gathering dust.
Scamb66 came around and helped me put a larger tank on...(They really are a bastard to close! )
And secondly I installed the Annitori QS I got from MrZ's hint.
First impression is... AWESOME!!!
I've been for a couple of rides and played with the settings and it is plenty for on the street.
Track day on the 3rd so we'll see how it goes then.
Instructions are a little vague on the direction of strain but I lifted a pic off google and found that the strain must be away from the wire exiting the gauge.
Also there is no time setting only three sensitvity settings, I found the mid one to be best for the street ( changing around 7-8k ) expect the sensitive will be best for the track, will let you know.

That said, these things will cut power even in neutral... See stalled at lights
Once this was figured, all I had to do was unlearn my throttle dip as I stepped through the gears.
In my mind a value fort money mod, but I have not had a chance to test out other shifters.
