From recent experience of this.

PLEASE ensure you use the right fluid for the clutch, not just stanrdard hydraulic clutch/brake fluid as this will cause your seals to 'pop' and you'll need a new clutch slave cylinder. This should be bought from KTM and is known in UK as 'Magura Blood'

Your symptoms are confusing as they would easily lead me to believe it's a broken seal like i had.. but if you're retaining pressure constantly then it can't be. It's almost as though the system is full of air bubbles.

Is it consistently poor ? or does it gain/lose pressure throughout the ride ?

Have you definately got fluid in the reservoir on the bar/lever ? there's a black rubbery insert which could fool you into thnking there's something in there ?
