I had my full system installed today - stainless steel headers and Evo cans, and the dealer downloaded and installed the Akra full system map into the KTM ECU (no PC or Tuneboy... yet).

Dyno Results:

stock: 106 RWHP
full system, stock map: 108 RWHP
full system, Akra map (db killers removed): 109.5 RWHP

Pretty meager gains I have to say, given the cost of the system.

However: max hp does not tell the whole story. Torque and therefore hp are increased fairly significantly through the midrange.

Other interesting data points:

1. Stock exhaust weight: 31.5 lb
2. Akra system weight: 14.5 lb
3. Exhaust gas analyzer shows a very rich condition with the Akra full system and map from 4k up. Dealer thinks a PC3 with a custom map can yield significant gains. He equated the amount of richness present to 4 main jet sizes on a carbureted bike
4. Stock sound = not bad at all, really
5. Akro sound = very nice, and naughty

I plan to go back to the dealer and have him hook up a PC3 to see what we can come up with. Honestly I'd like (and expect) to see a better max hp for the price of the system. The dealer thinks he can get at least 5hp with a proper air/fuel mix.
