@scotsduke - unless your engine is burning a lot of oil, the 10W/50 should be adequate. Don't use cheap oil, your engine will only burn more of it ultimately costing you more than using a quality oil to start with.

I tried 300V fully synthetic in my Race MX bike,
was getting too much clutch slip off the starts, so I dropped back to the 5100 and problem was solved.
However I changed the oil every 5 engine hrs to keep everything clean and healthy.

I will probably change my SD oil every 5 - 6000 km's using this Motul

If it is extremely cold, like sub freezing, use 10W40. Otherwise, use the the recommended full synthetic 10W50. More important to use a full synthetic than it is switching to 10W40. If there are any issues by using a lighter oil, you'll notice them fairly quickly.
