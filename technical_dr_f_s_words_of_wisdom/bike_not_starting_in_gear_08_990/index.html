#2
You basically answered your own question almost.
Either the bike is in neutral or the clutch needs to be pulled in. There is a little switch under the clutch lever and a pin to engage it, sometimes the switch is a bit too far out and it doesn't work. A bit of plastic sleeve or heat shrink over the pin might help. Bending the little lever of the microswitch is another solution.

Sidestand out kills the bike when it's in gear. If this switch is faulty the bike goes nowhere with the sidestand up as it will kill the engine as soon as you put it in gear.
