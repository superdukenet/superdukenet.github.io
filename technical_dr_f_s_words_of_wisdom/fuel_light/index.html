Dr F Forum member

Post 2 by Dr F, 01 Mar 2008, 17:20

#2
When you've filled up, make sure the trip is in 'F' mode, (when the light comes on, the trip starts giving you the miles you have traveled since the light came on with the letter 'F' in front for 'Fuel') then press and hold the set button. After a few seconds the light will go out. You can only do this with the ignition on and the trip showing 'F'.
