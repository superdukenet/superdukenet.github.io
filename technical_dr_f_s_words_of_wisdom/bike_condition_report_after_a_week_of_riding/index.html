Just got back from my week of vacation, rode the Duchess daily. To remind everyone:

1) Full Akra Evo exhaust
2) KTM Akra Map on the ECU
3) Dynojet 509 map on PCIII with Accel Pump feature enabled
4) SAS removed
5) DNA filter
6) Carbon canister removed, solenoid removed with 1Kohm, 1 watt resistor in place.

The Duchess ran flawlessly, no "snatch", couldn't ask for better, didn't even wish for a G2 cam. I did tighten the throttle cable as much as possible without steering lock travel causing tension on the throttle cable. Able to modulate throttle midcorner no problem. Only after I stretched the chain a little did a notice some abruptness in throttle transitions.

My suspension needs softened up a lot. I dialed out 5 clicks of compression damping and added 3 cliccks of rebound damping on the front fork, and I'm thinking of taking 1/2 to a full round out of the front preload.
Right now, all I can say is that this is the best sportbike I've ever owned, and vacation was great.
