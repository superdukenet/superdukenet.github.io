Hello all,

Was finishing up a delicious 70 mile ride this afternoon - mostly wide open B twisty roads, 60 degree Farenheit weather. When I got back into town, driving the last 3 miles at 35mph, the temperature gauge came on and stayed on. When I got home, I switched it off, said goodbye to my riding buddy, and then pulled out the manual and researched 'cooling' issues. I then started up the bike, and almost immediately the cooling light came on again, but the radiator fan wasn't turning.

Shouldn't the fan be turning at any time that the 'cooling' light is on?
