So I had to take a bit of a break from doing my valve adjustment.

I just got everything buttoned back up today now when I thumb the starter and the bike runs there is a loud clack almost open exhaust sound for half a second then it sounds absolutely normal. I thought maybe the top end was dry so I let the bike idle until it had 5-6 bars on the temp gauge. Turned it off and thumbed the starter again same loud sound for half a second then back to normal, it runs through the rpm range freely just as it did before. I only had to adjust the valves on the front cylinder and made sure to use the TDC locking bolt and marked the cams when I pulled them.
