Don't plug the rear vent tube. The tank could possibly collapse under the vacuum from the pump on a hot day.

I plugged mine once with the tank raised to stop it dripping then forgot to unplug it. Ran about 10 miles then remembered. HUGE hissing noise when I opened the filler cap and the sides popped out about 1/2". Quite a shock to see that happen

I think it's only used by North American market bikes for attaching the charcoal canister. Leave it be, it's fine as it is unless you raise the tank when full.

I think the front one is an overflow from the filler.
