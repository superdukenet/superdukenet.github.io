#2
Yep, 1000km service is about $400-500. Valve adjustment requires removal of the airbox and throttle bodies etc so they can get to the front cylinder. Then it's the same 'major' service at 15,000km and 30,000km. With a $200 service at every other 7,500km interval. (I actually do a minor every 5000km - that's just me - with majors at 15,000)

Forget what the yanks are saying about KTM dealer services and the warranty. It's different down here, well, it has been for me. As long as your guy is a qualified bike mechanic you won't have a drama, and service history has never even come into question for any of the problems I've had which have turned out to be regular things anyway. (Unless your dealership is full of assholes I imagine). I've had sensational support from KTM and I only get the major services done at KTM.
