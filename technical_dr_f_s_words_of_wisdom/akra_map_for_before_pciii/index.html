Post 2 by Colonel_Klinck, 12 Nov 2009, 05:50

#2
Akra map (which is loaded to the bikes ecu) is only necessary for SD's with aftermarket cans WITHOUT a power commander. So you don't need it.

You should have the O2 sensor eliminators fitted also though, is this correct?

Has your power commander been mapped on a dyno?

Best not to compare one twin (your SD) with another (Tuono), the engines have totally different characteristics.

Your thottle bodies may need balancing though. That can cause some vibes.
