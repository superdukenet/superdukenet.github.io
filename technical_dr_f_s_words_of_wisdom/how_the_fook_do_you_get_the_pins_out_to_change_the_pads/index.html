I used a wee 1 or 2 mil allen key as a drift with a mallet.
Pain in the tits bud but it works.
There should be a wee round hole on the outer face of the brake doofer, just find something wee enought to get in there and tap it out.
If you have a decent range of punches, one of them should fit in nicely I suppose, but thats far too organised.
Bodging it is!!
