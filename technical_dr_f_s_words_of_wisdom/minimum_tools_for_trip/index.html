Looked through tool bag and tried to reduce tools. So replaced a lot with multi tools and just made sure I have tools for changing wheels (never know, at least nice to able to tighten if necessary) and filling oil (and yes, I will bring extra oil...and chain lube). Changing plugs (especillay front) is hell anyway so don't think I'll bring equipment or spare plugs.

Always carry an extra oil sight tube after the first one cracked Not even sure what that wire thing is, but was in the tool bag (could actually be from the motard tool bag though...). Have a small roll of gaffa tape as well.

So this is not for crossing Sahara or Himalaya, we are talking about civilized european countries...anything else I should bring
