kevxtx Forum member

Post 4 by kevxtx, 16 Aug 2013, 01:24

#4
ITG JC-30/65 fits by millimeter. Tank deforms some filter foam on the rear side of the filter. So its already compressed and tight. JC-30/80 fits either carcass deformed or not at all on SD. SM tank clearance may be different, that thing I can not comment.

Both stock trumpets and RC8 ones fits perfect on JC-30/65. If you have custom mapping, you can change both trumpets with no changes in F/A ratio, at least in my case there was no difference.
