I was chatting to Stalker the other evening and he came up with a good idea to make a database of dyno runs at this years Crashfest. Parkitt Racing will be there with their dyno. I know they normally charge £20 a pull but I'm hoping if we all jump on board they will give us a group buy option. Possibly at the end of day 1 when we could all just line up and do one pull each. It's not about bragging rights, we don't need to publish who's bike made what and you don't even need to tell anyone else there if you don't want to. All it would be is a list with model, year, what mods and then the output. As all the bikes will be one after the other and conditions the same it will be a great baseline for people to see what mods ect can do. The more bikes and different setups the better. If enough people are willing to do it in theory I'll ping them an email and see what they will do it for. Perhaps we can get them down to £10 each if we all jump on board. If you've never had your bike dyno'd it could come in handy as you may be running dangerously lean and mega rich which isn't great either. Going to try and get a PCIII to take so if anyone wanted an actual setup after seeing their A/F then they could fit it and get a map made there and then. Then just combine it with ECU map in TuneECU and send it ECU and job done.
