You're checking the wrong end. You shouldn't need to mess with the front - it's the arse end that's got you wobbling. If you can drop the ride height -more sag/ less preload in the back, or possibly soften up the damping. My Duke II did the same thing between 80-85, past that it was unrideable. I dropped the rear 1/2" (sag) and it was stable to about 110 (top speed, downhill, with-the-wind). Little tiny steps will get you there.
