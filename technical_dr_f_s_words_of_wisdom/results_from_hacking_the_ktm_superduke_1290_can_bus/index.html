Hi Comrade Dan. I was just on my way to bed and remembered your question about time and date.
As you probably know the 1290 has multiple ECU's sending out canbus messages. The integrated speedo is one such ECU. I imagine that if you just change the time/date settings on the speedo you'll see some different ID's pop up and you'll be able to work out the rest from there. Happy hunting
