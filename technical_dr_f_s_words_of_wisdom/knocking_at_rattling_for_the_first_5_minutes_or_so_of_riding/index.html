I've noticed over the last 2 weeks that when starting my bike and riding for the first 5 minutes or so there is some serious engine knocking and rattling before it goes away. I am not talking about the standard noise/rattle everyone gets at inital startup. This is quite a loud bottom end type knock sound that last several minutes.

Bike has 22,000km on it and serviced regularly. Last service was less than 2000km ago.

Anyone experience this? Cause for concern or can I ignore this?
Andy Forum member

Post 2 by Andy, 03 Oct 2008, 14:19

#2
Hello Topendz, this sounds like reason for concern to me, sounds like a cam chain tensioner, better safe than sorry.

DR J. has had more than his fair share of SD engine malfunctions, I just hate to hear of you having any trouble.

Oil pressure reading would be my first step but I have the gage hanging on my shop wall.

I was talking to my dyno guy this past week, I had mentioned how quiet all of the other bikes engines were compared to the LC8,

he had skillfully pointed out that the LC8 has gear driven cams and this largely contributes to the noise. That eased my mind.
