Was riding today, about 50 miles in, I stopped at gas station. 5 minutes later, I started back up, but the bike wouldn't start. I wasn't paying the closest attention because I wasn't expecting it, but I think it fired up for about a second the first time and died, and then no fire at all since then. the whole ride up until this moment was perfect mechanically. 55 deg F weather. I was only about a mile from home, so I pushed it back... it was really awesome.

What I know so far:
1. Temp was at half bars and cooling fan was not engaged
2. All fuses have been tested good.
3. The engine cranks over fast, showing 9V during cranking and after stopping, and going back to 12.4 after a few seconds.
4. I turn the bike on, and the normal cycle goes through. just N and oil light are on. When cranking, the FI light comes on and stays on after I stop cranking.

Mods:
PC3 tuned
Rottweiller intake with SAS delete
GRB pipes

Any ideas? I don't see any posts so far with this issue really. I'll update as I search more.
