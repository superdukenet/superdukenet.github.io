So over the last few days I notice that my bike is a little softer and getting a little spotty in the throttle response. It began to smell more and more like raw fuel, well today when I went to leave work (luckily) I turned my keys and hit the kill switch and the bike starts showering everything within a 3ft radius with fuel.

Anyways...I take a look at the fitting thinking an o-ring had ripped or deteriorated, I pop the fitting apart and inspect it. Everything seemed fine, I pull the o-ring off and put it back on. After I reconnected it I noticed a lot of play between the two. I take it apart and and that's when the male fitting falls apart in my hand.

As I said luckily I was at work and we had a 2014 KTM 450 SXF, they use the same female fitting and the male made of aluminium. So I popped it on my bike and surprisingly enough it works.

FWIW, I used 2000 Series (http://motorsports.jiffy-tite.com/2000-series):

Qty. 1 JT21505 Socket 5/16" Barbed End (-5 AN)

Qty. 1 JT22505 Plug 5/16" Barbed End (-5 AN)

By no means the only options.

A lot of the online automotive race goody parts houses carry them (including one or two that have Amazon stores - LOL).

Might seem a little spendy, but considering what the KTM OE replacement part costs, and what a burned-down motorbike would cost (no matter how well insured), a pittance. I've used their stuff on other applications, and they've been a good company to work with.
