Hi Top cat2.If you run the PCIII you must have the O2 senses disable other wise you will have the problems you are having.Take the bike to a Dyno Jet tuner(must be heaps in Sydney so do your home work and find the best,not the closest)and get an advanced( separate cylinders) tune with the O2 eliminates fitted.Before you go,remove the secondary TB butterflies and along with your motorbox you will be laughing in your helmet.If the tuner tells you, you can't do an advance tune on a PCIII,he knows shit so go somewhere else.Having said that, a standard dyno tune will be fine if money is a real issue.There are other things you can do to tune you bike.I will be at the eastern creek south circuit on the 19th of Nov so if you can,come and say hello and I will be clad to run over something's with you.Cheers
