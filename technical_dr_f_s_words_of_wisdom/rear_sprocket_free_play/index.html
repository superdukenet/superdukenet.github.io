well I has this happen to mine also, as soon as i found the rear sprocket with loose bolts I took the rear wheel off, removed the sorcket from the rim and re-tightened all the bolts.

One of mine came loose it was grinding up on the swingarm. Not something you want done when you at speed on the freeway, as it could possibly lock up your rear ..!!

I would check on it and fix it up sooner rather than later if I was you..!
