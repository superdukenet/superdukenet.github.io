#2
Sound quality ain't great but given the flickering red oil pressure warning light, noise probably = rear and/or front cam chain rattling: rattle = low oil pressure in hydraulic tensioner = failed tensioner or failed water pump seals diluting oil with coolant - check your coolant, oil and oil filters for contamination re latter - replacing tensioners or water pump seals fairly straightforward.
