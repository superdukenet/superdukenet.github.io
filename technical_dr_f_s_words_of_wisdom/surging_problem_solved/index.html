I guess I am going to be the first to admit I have never had a clue what on earth anyone is talking about when it come to an abrupt throttle response or snatch. I've always considered it to be stella, my beef has only been the hunting / hesitation between 3500 / 4500 rpm which I am hoping is cured with the throttle body sync.

My 2 cents would be the following:

1. Sounds like if you consider the throttle to be too sensitive / abrupt on the SD you want to tame it with the G2.

2. If you have poor fueling / hesitation then TPS is hopefully they way to go.

I find it hard to believe so many people with the same bike are having completely different issues so I would guess those people are not used to such a responsive throttle. I am talking 2007/2008 as I think the early models had another list of issues.
