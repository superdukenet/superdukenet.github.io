Oil level should be checked with engine OFF on level ground. The oil level in the sight tube, on left side of engine, should read between the lines on the sticker on the oil tank. Don't do it on the side stand, the bike needs to be vertical. It's a shit design because if it's not correct you need to run around the other side and top up the oil tank on the right hand side. Then run back and check again. Many people have installed a conventional dip stick on the oil filler cap so you can check the oil level from the left side only. They are available from KTM for the motocross bikes, it makes life a lot easier.

To have to remove the side panels to top up oil is also a crap design. The SD is not perfect!
