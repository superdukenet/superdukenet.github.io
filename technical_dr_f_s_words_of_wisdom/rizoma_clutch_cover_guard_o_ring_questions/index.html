Im not sure if the rizoma was designed to use a different o-ring, but from the reverse enginering I did to make my own covers I wouldnt think so. There realy isnt much room to move the o-ring grove so I think they would have made it fit the stock o-ring. I would order a new stock o-ring to try, I have noticed old o-rings tend to expand ofter being soaked in oil for a long time.

As for the bolt bosses being ground thru you may want to try a liquid thread sealent like pipe dope (not the green kind ) to seal that up, silicone probly wont last.


Hope that helps
