The round akras and y piece will not fit the full system headers. You would have to buy the Y piece seperate, as this normally comes with the cans. Make sure you get the y piece that comes with the evo 'full system' cans, and not the evo 'slip-on' cans as they are different. I believe the evo and round akras have the same inlet size so the rounds should fit to the evo y piece, but please check before spending $$$, I know it's hard to believe, but I have been wrong in the past
