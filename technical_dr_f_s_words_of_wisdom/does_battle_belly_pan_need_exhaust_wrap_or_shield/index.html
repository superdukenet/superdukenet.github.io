Yes - mine has lasted circa 30k plus race miles but is now looking a little tatty/burnt and is about to be replaced- suggest you use a similar material applied to the bellypan, as per the OE unit - you'll need to insulate the bottom and lower right hand side as a minimum but suggest you line the whole unit as, for example, the left hand side adjacent to the side stand can get hot and soft.

Rubber mount and grommet the fixing points (how are you supporting the left hand rear- via side stand fixing points or bracket similar to rear right?) to avoid cracking/hole enlargement) and add some drain holes for road use, sealable with rubber grommets (if needed) for track use.
