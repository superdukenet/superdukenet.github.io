Haven't read the full post - but have you changed the regulator and/or the battery for a known good one ?

Sure sign of problems in this area are the indicators flashing oddly (fast then slow) with engine running but fine with engine off.

I had this and both battery and regulator checked out OK, so couldn't work out which was causing problem so replaced both, runs fine now.

I know this is a different problem to your symptoms but a screwed regulator can have some weird effects with ECU and sensors etc.
