Post 6 by loony888, 25 Jun 2011, 03:25

#6
righto,
the thread on the clutch master and slave is 10x1.00mm std brake stuff so the banjo fittings, crush washers and bolts are all pretty std for european bikes.
the clutch line is 1090mm long, end to end, with a straight banjo at the m/cyl end and a 90 deg bend at the s/cyl end. that should sort you out.
I bought a full set of custom goodridge lines for mine from john stamnas here in QLD, don't use them unless you absolutely have to, they're USELESS!
