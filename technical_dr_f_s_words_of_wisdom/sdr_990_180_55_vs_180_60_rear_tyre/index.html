I know this subject has been covered in the past but as 180/60 rear seems to be the default rear tyre for Supersport racing are 990 owners switching to the more readily available size.

I need some new tyres for Crashfest and Almeria later in the year. Not sure which size the fit
Motogoon Forum member

Post 2 by Motogoon, 10 Aug 2015, 19:34

#2
I always run 180/60, so much better in the corners, 55 just feels weird now!

The 60 Supercorsa is a big tyre though and if your chain isn't long enough to have the axle back a fair way it will not clear the swingarm, the Conti Race Attack 180/60 is a bit smaller and will fit easier. Haven't tried any other brands.
Motogoon Forum member

Post 3 by Motogoon, 11 Aug 2015, 07:02

#3
Continue race attacks are a brilliant tyre, I ran an endurance rear and medium front and was happy at mid fast group pace. Defo go for the 60 profile.
