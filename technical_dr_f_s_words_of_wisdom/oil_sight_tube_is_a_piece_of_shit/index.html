What dipstick?
And then block off the sight tube banjo holes with bolts and sealing washers?
Just keep an eye on your silicon tube and see if it stays ok. The point of having it's so you check it,...more likely than you'd check a dipstick?

Have a look here:

Is it what they call OIL RISING PIPE CPL. ? (33)
And the filler cap is on the other side?
The filler cap is PLUG CPL. M24X3 ...so maybe possible to find a dipstick from another bike that fits...but the dipstick probably wouldn't be long enough.
You could epoxy an aluminium rod into the filler cap. ?

Silicone isn't a good choice for an oil line ... even if under little-to-know pressure. A different material, silicon is 100% gasoline resistant (see remark above about using glass).

I have no idea what RC toy fuel line is made of ... PU, PVC, I dunno ...

After measuring the dimensions of the OEM tube (~6 mm OD, 4 mm ID), I just ordered a chunk of this:

http://www.mcmaster.com/#=f7edbk

Extremely high temperature resistance, and few materials are as oil resistant.

Will see (no pun!) how it goes on the barbed banjo fittings. I figure if it doesn't go on easily enough, I'll get it hot (!), then safety wire it when cooled.
