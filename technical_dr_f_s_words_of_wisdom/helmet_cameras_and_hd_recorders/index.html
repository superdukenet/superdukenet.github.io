Ive run the archos AV500 with their own camera and its good enough quality for me. Battery life is as good if not better than a camcorder, though you can get a bigger one if ye want. I picked the camera unit up in Schipol airport for about 80 quid. Havent seen any in the shops over here.
Their own camera cant be used anywhere else due to the fancy plug, but has its own in line control buttons( with leds so you know what its doing.)
Manual says its aint waterproof (only shower resistant) which was a bit disappointing but i suppose you could condom it for extra proofing.
