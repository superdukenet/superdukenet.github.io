i'm assuming you tried charging it off the bike and it did not hold charge. i think you need a new battery. check the electrolyte level. you can open it and add distilled water if you see some of the cells empty/half empty, but all modern batteries are made "sealed", so that you buy a new one instead (you can still open most of them, it's just more work). if you do decide to open it, be carefull, it has acid inside . If all the cells are full, there might be an internal short, you'll need a new battery.
