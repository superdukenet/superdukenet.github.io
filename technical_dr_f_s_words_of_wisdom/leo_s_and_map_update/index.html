#10
Graphite ring gasket located in the join in your rear header about 10" from the rear cylinder. It'll cure it. You need to repalce it anyway cos it'll be leaking gases onto your swing arm/rear shock and will also make your exhaust sound tinnier. Replace it and your exhaust note deepens, gases flow better and banging stops. All for £7.

I'm on my 4th or 5th gasket now, they blow out fast if you ride hard.
