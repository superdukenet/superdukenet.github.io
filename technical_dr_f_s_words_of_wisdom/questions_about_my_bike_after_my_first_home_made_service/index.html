I think your bike might have the waterpump seal problem. I would change that anyway. The "washed" look of the intake and the state of the plugs points to water being sucked in.
The water mixes with the oil and comes out of the breather pipe in the airfilter and gets sucked in the engine.

The water leak also gives it away.
While you do that replace the shitty hose clamps on the water hoses by good quality stainless ones.
The oil leak could be the cap not good on the oil tank. Also after you done the service wash the bike properly so you can trace where the oil comes from, some talcum powder might help to do that.
