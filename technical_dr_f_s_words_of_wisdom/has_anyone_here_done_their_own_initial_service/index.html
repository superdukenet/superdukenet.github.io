Post 5 by Dr F, 15 Apr 2007, 17:49

#5
Will invalidate waranty. Check out some of the waranty jobs on here, blown gallery oil plug (new engine on waranty!!!), blown piston rings, locked up gearbox, all one-off problems, but to have to repair one of these faults yourself because you scrimped on the service cost is not worth thinking about. I've had a new seat, brake disc and pads and a gear linkage on waranty, these parts alone would have cost more than the first service. Have a ring around other ktm dealers for a price, then go back to your dealer for a haggle, can't hurt.

Post 6 by svvmax, 15 Apr 2007, 18:07

#6
Have the dealer do the initial service. My valves were in check, but I also had them make sure the clutch spring bolts were tight (some sent to the states have come loose). They also happened to find a faulty fan sensor (the fan turned on for me just fine, but when I dropped it off the fan no longer wanted to switch on when it got hot.). They immediately replaced it with a new one (from a bike on the floor!!! Now that is service).

I'll do all my own service from here out, but I'll be sure to keep all receipts for oil/parts and note the mileage and dates when I do the service so the warranty stays intact.
