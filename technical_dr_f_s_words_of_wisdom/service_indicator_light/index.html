There is a mileage Service message and a time based service, so your service message is caused by you changing the clock from 2014 to 2016.
If you press and hold the Up and DOWN buttons until a service Menu appears (I think this can take 5sec or more ) I just did this 4weeks ago after I serviced my bike.

In that service menu you can adjust how many miles or time you want the service message to appear.

I just did this on my '16 1290 R and it was a little different than what I found online in other forums - maybe it's changed for '16? The instructions I found said you have to hold the up and down arrows when turning on the ignition but that doesn't work on my bike. You have to turn it on first, then hold the arrows.


Turn on the ignition and wait for the service reminder to display.

Hold down both up and down arrow buttons and the display will cycle through the various displays until it gets to the settings menu.

At that point, release and again push and hold both the up and down arrows.

A new display will appear that allows you to reset the number of months and/or kilometers until your next service.

Use the return key to end the programming mode.
