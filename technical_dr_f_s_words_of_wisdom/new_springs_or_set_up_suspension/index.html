Up on spring rates, Yes! The rear 160 is way to soft, you may need a 180 to get correct sag numbers at the rear (10mm free sag. 35mm rider). I would change to 9.5 on the front also, to get the proper sag. (20mm free, and 37 rider) than you will have some room to make adjustments. i don't think you can get the right sag numbers with the springs you have now.
HTH

I'm just under 15 stone but with kit on a bit more. HMRacing just set up my rear and said that for my weight the 170 was just a bit soft and the 180 was just a bit hard - both could be set up for the correct sag but at the two outer limits. I have the 180 on now and it's like Freestyler says - it's better on fast, smooth roads but bumpier in town and the 170 is comfier for the road but probably not as good on track.

I'd look out for a used SDR shock so you can experiment.

There was a thread on all this somewhere because it seems that a lot of people need a 180 spring but they are not readily available. I think BUZZ was looking for a good source.
