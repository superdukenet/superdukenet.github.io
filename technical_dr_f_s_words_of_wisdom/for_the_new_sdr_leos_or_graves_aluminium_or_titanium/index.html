As far as titanium vs alumimun, I don't know the weight difference for the Leo SD slip ons. But, if Leo's are like other manufactures, the Ti pipes probably do not weigh significantly less (my guess is under 2lbs). This TwoBrothers link gives some info in the cannister materials section:



That is a pretty low price on the Ti Leo's so make sure, if it matters to you, that they do or don't have a baffle option. The really low price TI Leo's I've seen (like what I bought) did not come with baffles and as far as I know don't allow for baffles to be installed. I was okay with that though. I like the Ti's because of the look and that they don't get very hot, corrosion resistance, easier to clean, etc. I would have bought the aluminium though if I hadn't found a price on Ti's that were not much more.

Can't speak for the sound difference if any or for Leo's look and fit compared to Graves. I noticed too on the Leo site they say no remapping necessary but don't know why Akra's say you do need to.
