I wanted to love the MT10. Looked OK in photos, nice concept with the R1 engine.

Then I went and sat on one at the NEC motorcycle show.

It's comfy enough, but it's a bit disjointed design wise. Sharp front mask, soft edged tank. The forks, risers and bars look cheap. The rear end looks unfinished. The seat looks absolutely shit with the wee extra hump they've glued on half way along it.

This was a pre-production model, apparently, so I'm keeping my hopes up that the ones that make it to dealers will be better spec.

But, for now, I ain't buying one this year. I may reconsider if it turns out that better spec components from the R1 will fit, and if the engine is an absolute stonker. As it stands, the 1290 stays for another year.
