How many volts is your battery holding? I low battery can trigger the EFI light and cause some running issues as the injector duration increases with low battery voltage.

Disconnecting the battery does nothing to the ECU or EFI sensors. The only way the sensors and ECU can be reset is with the KTM special tool.
