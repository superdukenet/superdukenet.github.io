Service and Safety Check

Please enter the 17-digit VIN (vehicle identification number) of your motorcycle along with the running number of your delivery certificate. _______________VBK No.

VBK No.
The VIN (Vehicle Identification Number), which begins with VBK, can be found directly on the right side of the steering head of your motorcycle.

Delivery Certificate No.
The Delivery Certificate Number is printed centric on the top of your Delivery Certificate.
