Post 2 by MrZ32, 19 May 2011, 06:03

#2
Step 1: Download Tuneecu (free)
Step 2: do a search on here and order the cable from one of the US suppliers like this one or one of the members who makes them. http://www.cjdesignsllc.com/TuneECU.html
Step 3: While the cable is being posted to you do a search on this forum for the tuneecu topics and download the suitable map(s) for your bike from the how to/ download section.
Also download the manual from the tuneecu website while you're at it and have a read.
Step 4: After receiving the cable, load the map yourself and save yourself heaps while you can tune also ignition map and diagnose your bike. (like the dealer tool)

Any issues PM any of the members who have experience with tuneecu with your questions.
