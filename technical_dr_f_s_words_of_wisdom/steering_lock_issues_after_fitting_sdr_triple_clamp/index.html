In summary

They're CNCed not cast
anodised Black
Slightly chunkier
There are 4 handlbar positions available by moving the risers forward or backwards on the triple clamp (1&2) and rotating the offset risers (3&4)

I don't believe the offset is any different, you can buy them as a powerpart to fit to your std bike.
