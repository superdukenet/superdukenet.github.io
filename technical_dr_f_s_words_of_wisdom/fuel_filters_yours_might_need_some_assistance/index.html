So lately I have been complaining of my throttle hesitating and some what bogging out when under heavy acceleration.
Having 15K miles on the Dutchess I just thought it's getting old and its just what's going to happen.

I was WRONG!!

Mr. Bubblehead suggested I change my fuel filters... 'yea yea I said' whatever, you live in SoCal an have nice weather stop telling me what to do...

As you can see in the photo.... VERY VERY DIRTY they were. Now that they are
clean and new, the freakin' bike runs like new.
I would have never guessed fuel filters would make such a difference.

Suggestion: if you are experiencing some slow acceleration and hesitation or have 10K or so miles on your SD - change your fuel filters!
