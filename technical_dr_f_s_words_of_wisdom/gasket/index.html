It's a pretty simple job, one you can do in an hour or less. I've attached a how to. I'd make certain the oil is coming from the water pump cover, as it's quite unlikely for oil to come out even if the water pump seal has failed.

Post 9 by Jim H, 11 Dec 2013, 04:53

#9
You have a black frame, there could be a possibility that your cylinder studs are the inferior 10.9 and not 12.9 high tensile studs that were upgraded at some point. Hence the base gasket leak. I can't be bothered to look but this may be something to consider for the future. Check the buyers guide. I would have expected an informed member of the forum to have pointed this out. I used the buyers guide prior to going for an '11 model. The high tensile cylinder stud issue is mentioned, I'm sure. Please check.
