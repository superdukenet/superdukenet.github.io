Hi

I just realised how long it was since last time since I posted.

To make a long story short as much as I can:

I own a 2010 SDR (beautiful amazing bike!).
I got it at late june of 2010.
The bike has only 6500 KMs, just before first service.

Last wendsday I noticed something odd when I entered my workplace underground parking.
The oil indicator started lighting up, flickering really fast.
This started right as I went down the ramp to the parking spot. Was in first gear with the clutch level pressed. Just cruising in.
The bike heat indicator was 2 lines empty, a bit hot.

The moment it started to flicker, I stopped. Didn't turn the bike off yet.
When I pushed the gas a bit, the flicker is faster. The indicator doesn't light constantly like when I light the bike before I push the started.

I stalled the bike, restarted, and the light flickered for a sec and went off. Like nothing never happened.

I called the dealer, he said "come over we will check it".
I drove to him, the indicator never went up. Bike was completely normal.

Dealer checked the bike, said "bad oil" (actually said ripped oil, didn't get his meaning, and I think he didn't get it either...), and replaced the oil and the filter and connected the bike to the computer for a full sensors test.
Everything came out just fine. Payed him, and went on my marry way.

Happily, I drove the bike during the weekend. Nothing special hard riding (except the 200km/h part ) Did about 300 KM on it.
No issues, not lighting up of the oil indicator while driving. No problems. I was happy as a nerdy college boy at his first party with half naked chicks running around.

Today, I entered my work place, walla! oil indicator started to flicker again just as I entered the underground parking.
The entrance is an almost 40 degrees ramp going down.
Again, stalled the bike, restarted, oil indicator went off.
Went back home, no issues. This time, entered the building parking lot (underground). Again, oil indicator started.

Now, I read there some had issues with the oil indicator sensor touching something. I asked the dealer about it. He said its not the problem.
I checked, and there is enough oil. No signs of leaking. The coolant just above the low point in the reservoir when the bike is off.

So, what else should I check?
And, is it fine that I drive the bike?
Except entering the parking lots, the oil indicator is off, no bad noises from the engine, bike feels fine, no power loss. Runs smooth.

I hadn't try to check the sensor myself.
Not sure where its in the bike and even tho I think I can manage to try and find it, I don't really want to add more damage if I can avoid it

Cheers
