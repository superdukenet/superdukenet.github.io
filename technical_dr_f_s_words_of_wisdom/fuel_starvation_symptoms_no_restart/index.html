Post 1 by Lowrance, 02 May 2013, 03:05

#1
Well, I wish i had a better intro and hope to in the future... In the meantime, I had a pretty bummer experience.

Was out in the nice weather today, and had just refilled after the low fuel light came on. About 10 minutes after refueling it hesitated at low throttle and then stalled at a light. Restarted and ran but only briefly... just enough time to get to an open parking lot. Seemed like a a classic running out of fuel scenario, though, I've never run out in the Super Duke I'm quite familiar with the sensation :rolls eyes:.

Diagnosis I was able to do on site off the top of my head:
-No FI light or any other trouble lights on the clocks.
-Turned over would fire then die. If I cracked the throttle it might rev up a few thousand then die.
-Checked all the fuses. All intact.
-Oil level fine.
-Kill switch not engaged.
-Tank was full of gasoline and not diesel. (Had a brief moment when I panicked about a possible mixup, but checked, haha)

I think the fuel pump was operating as normal, as there was no FI light and sounded normal. Are fuel pumps common on these? Wondering if I clogged the fuel filter somehow by running it to the low fuel light? I see the fuel pump runs ~$390. Eesh.

In the end I had it towed to the KTM dealer.

Backstory: Bought the bike 1 week ago and have put ~800 miles on it since. Not a hiccup in that time period. Completely stock except Akra Evolutions.

Any suggestions or comments are appreciated.
