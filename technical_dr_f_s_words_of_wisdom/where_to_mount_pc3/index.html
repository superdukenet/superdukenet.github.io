In the bin is the best place mate

It fits in the headlight mask easily on the early bikes, if i remember rightly, as you stand looking at the front of the bike and drop the mask down, my PC was to the right of the headlight and fitted just nicely down the side!

I routed the cable to the left of the headstock (again facing the bike) down the side of the airbox, grey connectors plugged in the take the ground wire to the earth closest to the grey connector (i think its the started motor earth?)
