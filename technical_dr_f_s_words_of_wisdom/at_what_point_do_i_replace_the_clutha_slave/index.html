If you have a factory LC8 standard silver clutch slave there is no time too soon to change it.

The factory slaves have a single seal on the slave piston and are able to twist and jam in the barrel. Most (all i have seen) aftermarket ones are double sealed and all round sturdier and smother feeling, less prone to leaks too.
