#4
Thicker oil on the forks just masks the problem and makes the damping harsh, correct way is to change the springs if you are on the heavier scale.
You can't rely on oil and a valve to keep the front up, a stiffer spring will do this.
Rear shock needs nitrogen too, won't work without it.
Honestly, best thing to do is to get a suspension place to service it. I like to do all the stuff myself but suspension is a trade by itself, just the tools already makes a difference.
