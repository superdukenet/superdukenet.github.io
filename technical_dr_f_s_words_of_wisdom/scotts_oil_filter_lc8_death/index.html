Yes. Please look at - search - advrider. There's some good discussion of it there. Paper vs ss filters, pros and cons. Interesting that many serious diesel engines, like marine, use both - the ss to keep out the larger particles and the paper filter after that.

Pros and cons...with the water pump seal problem, coolant in the oil, the ss filter does not collapse and block oil flow...but the paper filter shows that the problem's there, it's something for you to notice.

There isn't any absolute conclusion, either way. Except, perhaps, that (all else being equal) paper filters can filter smaller particles.

The one absolute conclusion, to it all, for me, is that the more often the oil gets changed, the better. But then, with the expensive oil we're supposed to use here, that can become a problem.
