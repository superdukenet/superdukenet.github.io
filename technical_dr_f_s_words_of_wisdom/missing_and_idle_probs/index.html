Sounds a likely case of the big grey plug has moisture in it.
Take the right side tank spoiler off, you see a big grey plug in front of the coolant overflow bottle ont ehframe behind and on top of the radiator.
Take appart, (push in the clips with a screw driver, clean properly with contact cleaner and compressed air and then lube/pack with dielectric grease.
let us know if this fixes it.
