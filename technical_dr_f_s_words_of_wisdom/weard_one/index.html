Mine does this occasionally (not necessarily 3000 RPM, but the same idea).

I believe it's the stepper motor compensating for what it thinks is a stall situation (and we all know it ain't perfect). Generally it only happens for the 1st 10 minutes of a ride until the engine is hot, but from time to time it does it on a hot restart.

The 'problem' clears quickly so I'm not that bothered.
