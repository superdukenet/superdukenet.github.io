Of course there isn't.

Amazing how long such stories survive. SAS has nothing to do with engine performance. Getting rid of the hoses and stuff is the only reason to remove it (and a good one BTW). Some bikes show less back firing on a closed throttle with SAS removed.

BUT... SAS does f*ck up air-fuel readings on the dyno. So if your bike has been setup on a dyno with SAS working, it's too rich throughout.
