Be easy enough to modify a lever to be longer. But you will get a lot more travel so your toe will have to lift/push far more. In the old days I used to have Kawa triples and there was quite a complex series of levers and joints - but this was before good rose joints and they used to wear. Sometimes the length of travel on these bikes to change gear was so bad you couldn't do it with a toe and ankle movement. you had to lift the whole foot off the footpeg!

Why don't you get some smaller boots?
