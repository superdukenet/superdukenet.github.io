Hello-

I went on an 800 mile ride last weekend, and parked the bike in the garage 7 days ago (weather turned to crap after I got home). Today I went out to clean/lube my chain. I wanted to warm the chain up before cleaning, so decided to take a quick ride. When I looked down to put my kickstand up, I noticed the base of the stand looked oily and wet. I got off the bike to investigate, and indeed the foot of the kickstand was saturated with oil . Naturally, I looked above it to see where its dripping from, and it appears to be dripping/seeping out of the clutch box. I remember hearing about this warranty issue somewhere before. I snapped some pics-- what do you guys think? This isn't NORMAL, right? I'm curious why it happened all of a sudden after returning from a weekend tour.

I also took a look at the engine case, which has this oily residue as if oil is seeping from it as well. I took a pic of that, and of my knuckle after rubbing it against the engine case. Appears to be oil as well.

Here's the real bitch of the situation-- My bike is out of warranty (U.S. 1 year warranty) by less than a f*cking month. I bought it used in December with 2500 miles. Now has 4000 miles. We'll see how KTM plays this, but I don't think its going to go in my favor. Pics below. Please tell me if I'm wrong about this issue.
