#2
Obviously the bike hasn't done too many miles in 2 years e.g it has been sitting still.
I assume you want to keep it for a while and take care of it so I would:
Change oil and filter so you know what oil is in there and it is been done properly and you get rid of deterioration and contamination that could have build up while sitting there.
Change the brake oil and bleed them properly, this is recommended every year anyway and greatly improves the brakes and reduces risk of jammed calipers.
You could do the clutch also and I would if the clutch oil looks dirty brown. It will improve the lifespan of the slave cylinder.
Always check air filter and even better spend $$$ on a motohooligan airbox.
Valves should be OK till at least 5-6000 miles but if you go the whole hog check them.
Battery on an SD is simple..... if it starts properly the batt is fine!!! (you'll know when it's not when the push on the starter button does hardly anything.)
