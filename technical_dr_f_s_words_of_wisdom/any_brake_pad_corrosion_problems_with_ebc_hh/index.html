Lads,

I'm having the same brake pad corrosion problems as last year. After taking off the LH caliper and checking out the pads (which were a free dealer replacement) i notice that they were Gold Fren HH pads. Now I recall people writing on this forum last year that EBC HH pads do not suffer this corrosion problem.

What is the experience one year on? Is it worth my while to buy £80 worth of EBC's or do they suffer the same symptoms?
In the past I have always used EBC HH pads when replacing pads and the experience has always been good.

Thanks!

Post 2 by TLS_Russ, 09 Jan 2012, 09:10

#2
I've been running EBC HH and the corrosion problem is better with these pads compared to the std pads but it is still there.

The base plate of the EBC pad seems to be better quality material than the Brembo pads.

Having said that I still have to strip the calipers 2 or 3 times over winter.

I think the problem is something to do with the different metals used in the brake system and something is not quite right.

I've had Brembo on other bikes ridden through winter and I've never had this problem before - in-fact I've never had to strip a Brembo caliper other than to change the pads before.
