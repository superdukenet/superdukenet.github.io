Low battery- Put that puppy on a tender.

Loose connections,

Check all plugs, and tighten the battery terminals with the 10mm socket, not the + screwdriver.

I believe the Yuasa batteries are regular Sealed Led Acid batteries (wett cell), not AGM's.

AGM's are actually a bit more resistant to damage from under charging then wett cell batteries.

None the less, if you let your battery sit in a low state of charge for extended periods, you are causing permanent damage to it that it will not recover from.

Tenders are a must!
