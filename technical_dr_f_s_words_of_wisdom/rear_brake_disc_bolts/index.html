it's been said a million times but for gods sake dont replace the front ones with anything other than steel or your speedo WONT work.

I also think the height of the bolt head is crucial to not lunching your speed sensor which is very close to the bolts.

I would just replace with standard SD ones and keep em clean or paint em with clear enamel paint to keep em fresh looking.
