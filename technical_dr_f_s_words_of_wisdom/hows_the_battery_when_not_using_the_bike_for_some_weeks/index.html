#8
ALL modern motorcycle batteries today are subject to rapid discharge and associated damage, a by product of the absorbed glass mat (AGM) technology that makes them so compact and maintenance free. Allowing an AGM battery to sit idle will damage it irreparably. I installed a Battery Tender quick connect on my SD when I got it; when I pull into the garage, as soon as I'm off the bike I plug it in. Before I did this I'd go through a battery a year; now my ST is going on 3 years on its battery and my Duke sat idle all winter but fired up strong the first time I punched the button.

Considering the cost of batteries and the frustration of a dead cell, the investment in a battery tender is a wise.
