I have fitted some second hand Brembo M4 calipers to go with my new cast iron discs but have found out that the bedding in process to expose the disc and removal the Guncote protective paint had ruined my pads. I am getting some weird vibration through the lever and the Braketec importer is saying that I have to chuck away my pads and replace with new ones as the Guncote permanently contaminates the pad material. My question is whether the pads from my standard Brembo SD callipers are the same as the M4s? I have a new set at home which have never been used and it will save me a lot of money if they are interchangable. I can check over the weekend but just wondered if anyone knows the answer now?

The Braketec instructions did advise to use a set of "sacrificial" pads to bed in the discs but who has these lying around eh? I don't make a habit of keeping worn out disc pads - do you?

#6
The pads Fromm the standard calipers are not the same as the m4 pads. The standard pads are a two piece pad. The m4s are a single pad.

Grendel has swapped disks and kept the pads. However carefule prep is necessary. He uses a very flat surface with a high grade sand paper (600 or higher) and carefully sands down the pads until they are perfectly flat. This will make them like new. It is amazing how unevenly they wear as you use them.
