Check out the service manual, it’s at the top of the How To Do It section.

If you are thinking of checking / changing the spark plugs; you will need to take off the throttle bodies and air box. Standard slim spark plug sockets don’t fit, they are 1mm too wide. KTM will sell you a socket for £25 or put a standard slim socket on a lathe and reduce the width by 1mm.
