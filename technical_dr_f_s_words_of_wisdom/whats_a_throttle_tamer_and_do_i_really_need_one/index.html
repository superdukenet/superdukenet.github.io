Just installed the KTM version of the G2 and love the difference it makes. It doesn't rob power but instead just kind of takes the twitch out of the bike at low RPM's . Like the other guys said, it's just a different cam profile so you get a more progressive pull rather than the on/off a lot of people complain about. It's all in preference. If you don't do a lot of in town/ low speed riding you might not benefit much from it but I love it. Definitely worth the price. The KTM one is a bit cheaper than the G2 as well.
