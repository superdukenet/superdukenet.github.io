3mm sounds like a relatively big hole to bung. I had a decent plug put in by the RAC when I got a puncture, but they recommended that I didn't exceed 40mph or 280 miles on it. Probably a standard cover my arse disclaimer. This was one of the tyre on the wheel puncture repairs. Bike shop repairs are guaranteed to be as good as the tyre was before the puncture, but they do involve remving the tyre and patching it from the inside.

I'd see if a shop will repair it and if they will, work out if it is worth the cash. If a bike shop won't fix it I'd trust their judgement and not fix it myself at home, specially not with one of the kits that repairs the puncture with the tyre still on the wheel. They are not as good as the proper shop jobbies and IMO not worth the risk.
