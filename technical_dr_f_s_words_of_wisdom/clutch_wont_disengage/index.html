Can't measure it but 5mm sounds about right and should be enough. I'll have a look at the diagrams of the clutch to see if I have any suggestions.

How did you try to see if it disengages? If the bike has been standing still for a while the cold oil makes the plates stick and it will feels like it doesn't disengage if you try to push it manually.
I know it's dangerous to try other wise but let the engine warm up then switch it off and try again with the oil warm.
