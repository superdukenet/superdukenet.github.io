to add to what KTMguy said, on mine the 3 thick wires are yellow; also, the resistance should be the same, and less then 1ohm if i remember correctly - check in the repair manual (PDF in "How to" section i think)

also, check the other set of wires comming out of the reg/rec, i think they are green... two of them go to the negative battery terminal, and their shape/connection is quite prone to braking...

mine were just broken, same symptoms as yours (did not tow, i just pushed, in full racing gear for 30 min or so... not fun )
but reg/rec is fine!
