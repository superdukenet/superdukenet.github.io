The only can that fits the titanium headers are the Evo cans for full system. The diameter of the header is much bigger than standard where it joins the cans. Also they only connect with springs to the headers. Unless you want to buy the cans to match the titanium headers you need to get the stainless Akra headers that came as standard with the 08-11 SDR.
