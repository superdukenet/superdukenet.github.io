Battery access is a stupid bit of design. But like TJ says, all you have to do is slide off the rubber bit and undo the plastic clip, gently pull away all the wires and the cover will come off allowing access to the battery. Not good to do in the dark, in the pissing rain at the side of the road which is what I've had to do once.

Did I remember wrong?
