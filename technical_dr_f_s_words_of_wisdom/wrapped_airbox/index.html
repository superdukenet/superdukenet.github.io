i think the problem is more that the air being sucked into the airbox is warm, not that the box itself gets warm... it could be that the air picks up some heat while traveling through the ducts, but it wouldn't be much... it's all plastic, not a great heat conductor. you'd have to heat wrap the ducts more then the box itself in that case, i think their total area is actually larger then that of the box.
better solution would be to move the intakes so that they catch colder air, but then if you change the length of the ducts, you'll fook up the resonating frequency...
no easy fix here i'm afraid. Custom airbox, or just leave it be.
