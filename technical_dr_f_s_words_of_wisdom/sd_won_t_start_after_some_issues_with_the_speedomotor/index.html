The condition you are describing does sound like a low battery

Too Bad that ktm does not give us a practical place to have some good thick Auxiliary leads to recharge and test through, my method of load testing

requires the battery to be disconnected from the vehicle, most manufacturers do not want any voltage spikes to make their way to the ECU.

better safe than sorry is my thinking. A dead or defective battery that will not provide the endurance required to hold up to a 10-15 second load test,

needs to be shite canned. I think battery weakness and voltage spikes are the leading cause of ECU failure in the industry.

Practically the cheapest part on the whole bike but the most important one to keep fresh! (tip-top condition!)
