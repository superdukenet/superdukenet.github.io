No advantage to performance.
Weight gain will be improved more if you take a wee before every ride.
Do it if you feel like playing with your bike.

SAS just dumps filtered air from your airbox, into your exhaust runner in the heads just before each header. It helps burn excess fuel and tries to make the bike run cleaner - saving the earth. Nothing more.
