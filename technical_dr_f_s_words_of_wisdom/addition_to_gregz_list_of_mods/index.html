Ah yes... many moons ago... www.autospeed.com did some excellent reviews of electric superchargers. The bottom line is that they are useless, and this can be proven with physics as well as tests.

The gains are prob between a stock air-intake and a hi-performance airfilter and no tract and airbox - and run without heatsoak in the enginebay. LOL

I must say - it does sound like a good idea... but trust me don't bother.

Good suggestion though... I would certainly be interested if something was released that actaully worked. But size and power constraints with the technology we have is the issue.

Cheers!

I have to admit my post was semi tongue in cheek. I figure a 60 watt fan isn't going to make the engine that much more powerful, bearing in mind the many kilowatts a "real" supercharger needs to make it effective.

That said, if a decent one hit the market that was both small and powerful (min 1HP) and was a reasonable price, I might just experiment.

As you say, heat soak is a problem, hence the myriad of intercoolers, charge coolers and water spray systems on the market.

My Scooby had water spray and it needed it (why put the intercooler on top of a hot engine?....D'oh!). The Noble has 2 turbos and a 'kin big intercooler. Again, though, the cooler is atop the rear turbo and above the main part of the exhaust system. Heat soak is horrendous. How much did your heat wrap cost? I think I might get some for the car.
