I got mine fixed, It was the wire. My dealer said that the wire is too close to the motor and needs to have a wrap on it to isolate it from the heat of the motor.

My dealer talked to a KTM rep at the dealers show about this and he said In cases where they replaced the sensor and solved the problem, they also moved the wire and that is what really fixed it.

If you want to do it yourself and save a trip to the dealer just put some wire wrap on the sensor wire on the back of the front cylinder. It's hard to get to but it will stop the light from coming on.
