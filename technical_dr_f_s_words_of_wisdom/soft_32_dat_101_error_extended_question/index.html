The last battery was 4 years old thought I would be fine for at least another 4 years!

Which brings me to yesterday, 90* in denver 60* at 12000 vertical feet, 180 mile ride at a relatively slow pace for the majority of it( dang tourist) same kind of ride 20 miles to and from the mountains and when I get within a mile of home the battery light and then the FI light pop on, I park the bike when i get home and sure enough soft 32 dat 101 error code pops up.

My question is could something be wrong with the stator regulator etc, I daily drive the bike 60 miles round trip to work and have never had an issue besides these two longer rides, I have also taken several other 100+ mile rides with no errors etc.
