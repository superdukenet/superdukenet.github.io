In the past i've struggled with my Abba bead breaker,as i wanted something that wolud hold my wheel while i rotated it and removed my tyre


My two ladds bought me for my birthday (May) a new bead breaker that held the wheel

I was able to rotate the wheel while removing the tyre, perfect untill i realised that in my haste to assemble the thing i'd assembled it wrongly the bolts holding the two parts together should have the heads of the bolt on the inside and not as i had them at first on the outside. As soon as i rotated the wheel and heard the scraping sound i knew instantly that i'd fooked up

Lesson learned the hard way now i'll have two respray the back wheel
