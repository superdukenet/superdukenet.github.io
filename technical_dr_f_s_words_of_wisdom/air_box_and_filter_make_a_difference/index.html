I'll give it a go.

PCIII or Power Commander III is small-ish box of cleverness that plugs in, in between the ECU (bikes brain) and fuel injection/throttle bodies. It can alter the fuel/air mixture entering the engine. You can dl maps from DynoJet (makers of PCIII) to match your bikes configuration, ie Akros or Leo exhausts, or even better have a custom map made that's perfect for your bike by taking it to a Dyno Centre/rolling road. Because you then have perfect A/F mixture you get the most BHP and Torque from your engine but also and in some ways more important a nice smooth power delivery all through the range. The famous snatchy throttle is history.
PCIII http://www.dynojet.co.uk/powercommander/index.htm
People who Dyno'd my bike http://www.fasterbikes.com/rollingroad.html

You bought the really expensive bit in the Akras which let the bike breath out, if you then buy MotoBox, that lets the bike breath in, the next step would be to get the perfect A/F mixture. This does however all add up in price. Plenty of people just use MotoBox without PCIII but i know it made a big difference to mine when the PCIII and custom map were added.

Hope that's clear-ish. Plenty on here that can explain it better than me.
