Hi all,

I recently bought a 2007 Super Duke 990 with the famous erratic idle issue and slow cranking.

I recently replaced the TPS (it had a flat spot in the voltage curve) and the stepper motor. I also replaced the battery for a new OEM Yuasa. The battery solved the slow cranking, but the erratic idle is still there.

This is what is happening:
- Start the bike, idles at 3000 rpm, after a minute drops to a steady 1500 rpm. This seems to be normal.
- Ride the bike and go over let's say 2500 rpm....close the throttle, but the bike will keep pulling
- Pull the clutch and the bike idles very high at 3000-4000 rpm with lots of pops and bangs from the exhaust and rpm's quickly go up and down between 3000-4000 rpm

It seriously drives me crazy and I can't ride the bike. What else can it be (Secondaire butterflies motor? Injector bad?). It is especially weird since the bike idles fine, but when you go over 2500 rpm it goes crazy.

Thanks mates!

ps: It is a 990 Super Duke, with Akra system and Akra map
