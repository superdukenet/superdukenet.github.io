If you want to increase midrange torque I havent seen any other system that can match the full akra. The resonator is the key - take it away and you move the performance curve higher in the rev range. Obviously you will need the akra map and maybe even a pvc and dyno session to tidy up the akra maps AF/rs. Its as simple as that. A modified airbox of your choice makes things even better again. Stick with the longer velocity stack choice for best low/midrange performance.
