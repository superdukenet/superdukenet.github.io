Post 2 by bic_bicknell, 03 Aug 2011, 05:53

#2
It all depends on your weight IMO. I'm 15 stone fully kitted up and the standard white spring on the '07 model was set to nearly full preload to get the sag right. The orange springed 180/185 shock is a bit longer than the standard one, (I put up a picture of the two shocks for comparison somewhere) and the spring is set on the least preload to get the right sag for my weight. The SDR shock I fitted was an ex racers from the Superduke cup series and had been re-shimmed so it was not quite a standard hardparts shock. To be really honest I think for road riding it is too hard and the standard shock was better. But after it was set up by a good suspension guy on track the additional firmness and maybe the revised steering angle, (from the added rear height) makes it really good.
