yep. and no special tools required, just some sockets. Unless GBR have done something twatty with their kit, of course.

The R&Gs just required 2 ratchets and 2 15mm sockets.

(15mm? think so. summat like that anyway)


if you're boshing the lower engine crash bungs in, they come on a rod that threads through the engine. Rather than take the original rod out first, then worry about whether it's moved or not and faff about with trolley jacks, just start it goin, and follow it through with the new rod that's part of the crash bungs.

you'll see what I mean when you get stuck in


either way it's dead simple
