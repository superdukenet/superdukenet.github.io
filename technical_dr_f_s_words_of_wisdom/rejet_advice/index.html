Yes, it's advisable to remap the SD after cans and filter.

Do you have the Akrapovic map for the exhaust? If so it's ok for the air filter too

It's all around the forum with many threads about akrapovic map ( can be download by you with the tune ecu cable and free program or at the stealer for a fee usually ).

With the tuneecu you can remap it with injection, ignition, dual cylinder etc etc

Rejet on the lc8 is for the 950 adventures and supermoto only. All Superduke came with the 990 injection engine
