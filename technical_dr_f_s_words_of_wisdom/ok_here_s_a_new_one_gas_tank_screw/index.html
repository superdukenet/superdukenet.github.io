Had the tank off to do some work on the airbox, reinstalled the gas tank, but the front bolt will not line up with the #$%@^ hole in the steering head. What am I doing wrong? The holes in the aluminium and rubber bushings want to line up too far forward for the bolt to grab the threads. I've spent about an hour loosening/tightening the rear pivot bolts, fooling with the gas tank lift assist, and finally degenerating into a frustrated stream of profanity that would bring a tear of envy to the eye of any sailor.

Didn't toss anything, though.

Ideas?

#4
I had the same problem, even tried the "open" the tank trick. I found the rubber grommet spacer to be the prob. Take it apart, slide the sleeve out and line up the "egg" shape to allow the freeplay needed to put the bolt back home. worked like a charm for me. After 2 hours, one hole in the wall, and 15 beers.

This is a common problem with all the SD's. Even when brand new the tanks never line up perfectly. You can also remove the 2 upper sub frame bolts and drop it down (pulling the tank backwards) until the front screw lines up.
