It comes from the dealer with a tool kit.

The three most common issues seem to be:
-Wobbly rear hub issue
-Front brake pulsing under braking issue
-Loose steering stem nut

Oh, don't forget to have a ready excuse in case you want to take off for a many day ride as soon as you get the bike. Make up a sick relative to see in the hospital or something.
