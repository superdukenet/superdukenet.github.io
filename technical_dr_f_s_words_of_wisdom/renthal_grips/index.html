I can't see any reason why they won't fit, I have Renthal grips fitted to my NC30 (because they come in a charcoal grey which makes the faded switchgear less noticeable!). I'm pretty sure they make different grips for road and dirt applications - dirt bikes have no bar ends so the grips are closed at the end and I don't remember doing any cutting

Fitting is as easy as fitting any grip, I like to use Scott Grip glue but some people use hairspray.
