Post 6 by Swanny, 15 Sep 2015, 06:56

#6
Over the years I went from Rizoma bars like above to even flatter SLS bars. Both need to have about 40mm chopped off each end because they are very wide. (For me anyway)


Then, a few years ago, I tried fitting clip-ons to see what it was like. I bought a set of Woodcraft ones and had to do a fair bit of modifying the headlight panels to get the brake lines etc to route neatly. but everything went together OK. Riding position was much more like a sports bike but if you are used to just yanking and counter-steering with those big, wide high bars you might not like it. Last year I changed back onto the Rizoma ones because I was doing a lot of long distance riding and the clip-ons are not comfortable for this.

All in all it was an interesting experiment.
