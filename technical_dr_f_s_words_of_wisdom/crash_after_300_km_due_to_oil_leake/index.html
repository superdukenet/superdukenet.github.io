Mase, I challenge anyone to pour some fluid (any type) on the ground

and see how slick it is by applying the front tyre with the brake applied

then see if the tyre slides, It is Indeed slicker than snot, does not matter

which fluid, they are all slick and will put you down in a heartbeat.

Mase, in your pics, the 1st one has a full brake resivor and the 2nd and

3rd pic's are empty? I don't know if you get the picture I see? It really

does not matter, but if it was empty after your spill that would have to be

the culprit? If the MC laid on its side for a spell the resivor is vented

at the cap so it would run out.

Shade, I have posted on this subject since the begining of me being here,

I wished I would have made more of an issue of it. The oil level checking

and adding to, is quite controversial, I have to ride mine 5 min's and wait

2 min's then recheck it after an oil change, here is another weird senario,

When I change my oil, I take all of my old oil out, then measure the

volume. Then when I go to pour the exzact same amount back in the

engine it over flows out of the tank, untill I start it and do the procedure

above.
