what works best for me as far as sag at the rear, is 10mm free sag and 35 mm rider sag (30mm for smooth track days). if you can't get 10 mm of free sag with 30 to 35 mm rider sag you will need a different spring. using a zip tie on the fork works pretty good at showing how much travel you are using on any given day. using one on the shock (imho) does not show very well what is happening at the rear. The great thing about the WP shock is you can adjust the high speed compression dampning as well as the low speed compressing. if you are bottomig out on sharp edge hits a little more HS comp. dampning should help. hope this helps ... Tim
