-JP- Forum member Topic author

Post 4 by -JP-, 07 Jun 2011, 02:50

#4
the stick ons seem to have a bit of pre-curve to them and that can get flattened out when you stick them onto really flat surfaces...this gives a looser fit to the male clip and can cause rattling/vibes. Also if you have a bunch of those linked plastic joints making the camera very tall, it can predispose to excess shakes.

maybe try a small wrap of foam adhesive tape round the bars under the camera clamp to dampen some vibes?
