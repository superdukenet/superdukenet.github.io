IF you want access to your fuse boxes You'll need to build a lock mechanism into whatever tail piece you use to lock the seat down, as it is only held on the bike by the lip on the petrol tank and the lock on the SDR tail unit, I'm sure it would be easy enough to use your existing SD seat lock and the parts from the SDR version in the same way as it works with the conversion kit on the new tail piece, you'll just need to drill a hole to fit the lock through and maybe glue some stoppers on the underside for the latch plate to butt against when open and closed.
