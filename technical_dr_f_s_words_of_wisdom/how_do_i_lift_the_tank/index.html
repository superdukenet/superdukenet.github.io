Dude.... no offence but if you don't know should you be touching it?!

The black hose on the left just pulls off, nothing comes out unless you just had a ride in the rain.
I get at least half of the petrol out before I lift it, if you do it full it spills.
If it is a really hot day there is the possibility the tank swells a it and the bolt at the front is hard to get back in, don't leave it too long. Plenty of comments on the forum about the tank bolt.

Hope this helps!
