Martin,

The idea is it adds air into the exhaust to cheat the CO2 emissions rules. (more air means a lower CO2 % when measured by whoever it is that makes up the rules - removal MAY be an issue come MoT time)

As a hugely fun side effect the SAS system makes the zorst pop and bang like a good 'un on the over-run.

This is very infantile behaviour but scaring old ladies and kids in prams is almost as much of a hoot as nailing it through the twisties

Why would anyone want to remove it

I just removed mine the other day. I installed some Akra slip-ons and had the Akra ECU map installed at the same time. I highly doubt removing the SAS did anything for power but it certainly cleaned things up a bit in the engine area and I've always preferred to elminate anything that is useless to performance. As for the popping, mine will still give a nice single pop once and a while if I'm working the throttle hard enough.
