This is a totally noob question but in going through my owners manual on my new '16 1290R on the display functions, there's a setting for "pressure" that the manual describes as tyre pressure displayed in bar or psi. I can't find anywhere that the tyre pressure is displayed. There's also no indication or idiot light in the displays for low tyre pressure so I'm assuming there's no TPMS on the bike either. I'm not complaining but just wondering what I'm missing?

Also in the displays, are a setting for DRL {on/off] If it's talking about the LEDs above the head light, mine are on all the time regardless of this setting. Again, what am I missing?
2014_SDR Forum member

Post 2 by 2014_SDR, 18 Jun 2016, 18:49

#2
The display is shared with the Super Adventure, which does have a pressure monitor. You can ignore it. The DRL is indeed the LED above the headlight. If it's "on", it will illuminate during daylight hours. Check out your bike when it's dark out, your regular headlight will be on instead.
