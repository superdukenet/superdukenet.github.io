This must be done - with the bike on the side stand - at 1000KMs and at least at every service. I have these on my forks and press them occasionally. The pressure build up varies quite strongly. Sometimes these is a clear release after only 2 weeks.



Obviously, pressure build up in the forks stiffens them up unwanted. The result is comparable with increasing the spring preload. I find these release valves very handy.
