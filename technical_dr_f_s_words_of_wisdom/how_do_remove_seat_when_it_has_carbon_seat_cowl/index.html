Just bought seat with carbon seat cowl and it is quite hard to remove the seat when there is no strap. How do to it without damaging the seat cowl?
jambox Forum member

Post 2 by jambox, 26 May 2011, 19:27

#2
As stated in the manual. Lift the rear, whilst putting pressure on the "step" and push/pull rearwards. (after unlocking it of course)

#4
Grendel does not have a seat cowl, however he never uses the strap. Unlock, then give a sharp rap with the heel of your hand where the seat steps up. Do this firmly and back toward the taillight. Should work with a cowl too.
Mr_Trecolareco Forum member

Post 5 by Mr_Trecolareco, 27 May 2011, 10:41

#5
I don't have the strap and I find it as easy to remove as before carbon seat cowl.

Unlock, fingers under seat/seat cowl and another hand on the seat near the pretrol tank. Small wiggles and seat off in secs.

You might want to give the little rubber biscuits on the underside of the seat a shot of silicone spray when you do get it off. It'll make subsequent removals much easier.
