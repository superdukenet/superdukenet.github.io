#2
I used to own a 990 not R and it never weaved under hard acceleration so you must have some other problem.
If you get any head shake when hitting bumps on hard acceleration that is sort of normal but it should settle
Quickly and not continue once past the bumps.
You could have to much rebound on the front forks also try backing it off take the bike for a spin and see what happens.
My old R1 used to love shacking its head but I would just keep the throttle pinned and it would stop.
Have you set your sag?
