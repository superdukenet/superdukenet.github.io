#1
Was out the other day and I ran out of fuel.

Symptoms were the bike "coughed" a couple of times, then stopped, I pulled over shook the bike from side to side to try and free up some fuel but once it stops that's pretty much it.

Refilled from a 5 Litre can and when I got to a fuel pump, about a mile away (1.6km) I put another 10 litres in, so the capacity is pretty much 15L as in the manual.

The bike restarted 1st time no prob's

I always use trip 2 to measure distances between fuel stops, when I fill up I fill the tank with the bike level, still sat on it, and stop as the fuel just goes up inside the fill hole tube, then I let the fuel dispenser drain of fuel and that's it.

This trip meter read 95.8 miles (x 1.609 = 154.14 km)

That's 6.38 miles/Litre or 10.27km/L

About 28 miles/Imp Gallon.

I was riding hard though, and 2 up, across Hampshire and Berkshire's twisty A-roads
