I hope someone else has had this and got a way to do it. (Although I can't imagine what.)
I can't get the velocity stacks to slide into the throttle bodies.
They came out easily and they've been sitting on the shelf for about 6 weeks.

I put silicone spray, didn't help, then a film of grease, didn't help. I can see how the locking tab things align, that's not the problem. They just won't insert into the throttle body inlets. They are just tight. (It seems like they're about half a mm too big.) The air filter was getting mangled and I had to fish out a few shreds of plastic off the butterflies.

The only thing I can think of is heating up the throttle bodies with heat gun, but by the time it's hot enough to make any difference it might be frying the butterfly shaft o-rings or something. I did try that, but not much, I probably just made the butterflys hot.

I can get about 50-60% of the circumference in but then the oother side is cocked at an angle, which is not the way to get something like that to slide in - it should slide in square on.
I tried pulling/pressing, enough to move the bike on the side stand, but not much help. Stronger fingers would probably help, come to think of it. And it is plastic, so I suppose no use to freeze them in fridge for a while.
