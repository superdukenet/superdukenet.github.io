the reset is not myth, it's part of the service tasks that they do. BUT it will not remap any air/fuel mixture following filter mods or airbox or end can stuff. It's not a learning ECU like some others are.

Nah, actually the complete opposite, somebody else found a link and said that it proved the reset did nothing but I took the time to read the whole thing and in the end the guy that started it was proven to not know what he was talking about by some others who did.

Essentially it's like Shade said, it's not a magic uber learning ecu that remaps anything it just take a few measurements from different things like Ash said and gives the settings a little tweak to help it along.

I think the term reset makes people think it completely recalibrates itself but it just tweaks things up a bit

I've never noticed any difference when I've done it but then I've never had any of the problems people have said it has helped, I just figure that it costs nothing, it suggests it in the manual so might as well do it on occassion.

I've mainly done it if I've disconnected the battery for any reason, I also did it when I fitted my DNA just for the hell of it, if I decide to do it which is usually after winter layup, then it's always a good time to check the oil afterwards while your at it.
