After sorting out and a little bit of winter maintenance i finally got to ride the decepticon today!

Got back to the garage and checked the oil level, it was just under max, a little higher than mid way where i was trying to get it on sunday. But saw oil on the side of the engine covers, with a torch it looks like i have oil leaking from between the cylinders and the gearbox, even a little from under one of the SAS covers!? All round seal blowout?

Anybody experienced similar? i'm hoping that tightening the cylinder bolts will nip up the gasket enough, if these have gone what is the likeliness of others having gone pop too? would (could) the cylinder bolts work loose?

I have heard that overfilling can cause the LC8 to spit it everywhere and that alone has made me very paranoid each time i change it.

Any advice appreciated, i can't believe my luck.

#2
I developed a small weep on my rear cylinder towards the end of last summer. Only does it when I thrash her hard. It's right at the bottom and so a pain in the arse/expensive to fix as the engine has to tip forward in the frame to life the rear cylinder head and then the cylinder and piston. Shade had a bad weep on his that came from the rear of the front I believe. His was so bad that it needed doing as it was dripping oil onto the rear tyre. Mine is nothing like that bad.

Both out bikes are fairly heavy mileage wise. His was 45+k miles and mine is approaching 40k miles. Other than that I've not seen a thread on here, that's not to say there hasn't been some though.

Post 9 by ShadeTheChangingMan, 19 Jan 2011, 16:09

#9
Oil pressure switch is a classic leak point.

If it's not these then it's base gasket on the cylinder. I had mine (rear cylinder) done on my old SD. £250 from dealer, but he sealed it up good, real good! Mine had a tiny bit of corrosion on the engine that allowed the leak to worsten with age. Was also worse when redlining it too under higher pressure then one day, bam, it really opened up and pissed out a lot. I was at track that day and ended up lobbing it as the rear slid out on me.....oil on rear tyre? I didnt think so as i was checking it all day....but you never know.
