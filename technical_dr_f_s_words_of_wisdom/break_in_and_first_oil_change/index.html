Personally I would not recommend this.
I did the first service myself and I never have seen so much crap comming out of an engine at the first service as with the SD.
LOTS of fine metal stuck to the magnet and filters and the oil looked pretty grey! Other members here have similar stories.
I even asked the dealer about it and they said all LC4's have this.
After the first service it cleared up however and all is fine.

My advice is, get the first service done as recommended on the SD!
Also don't know why they can't fit you in, it only takes an hour maximum to do the first service on an SD
