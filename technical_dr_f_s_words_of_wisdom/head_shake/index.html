Hi guys, it sounds to me that the wiggles are rider induced. If you are not using your stomach muscles to keep yourself leaning forward you will have a death grip on the handlebars. To keep the bike stable you have to have a light touch otherwise you will cause handling issues. The only instability I get on the SD are from me getting lazy, otherwise it is a very good handler. Keep the front wheel up and the hammer down. Alf
