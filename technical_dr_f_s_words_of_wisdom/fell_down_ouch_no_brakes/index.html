To check disks, put bike on paddock stand and hold a screwdriver on the edge of the disk, sort of 90 degrees away from it.

As you spin the wheel you should feel/see it move.

BUT..

it's got to be REALLY bent to move them that much... and If you didn't already notice this when riding, i'd be shocked...
