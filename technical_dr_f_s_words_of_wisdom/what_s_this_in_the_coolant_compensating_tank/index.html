#2
It is sand, from the casting process I believe,. There's plenty of info about it if you do a search on here.
Clean it out and flush the cooling system - otherwise it can wear the water pump mechanism and cause it to leak. Catch it early enough and you won't have to replace pump seals etc prematurely

#3
I just recently changed a coolant. You can not believe how greasy it will be when you do so Full of hard particles (like sand, yes) and grey mud. I used about 15l of distilled water to flush the system and then poured Motorex 5.0 coolant in. After 200km test drive its pure as just poured in. The link how to change the coolant is here (look for advrider link, however I haven't used any vinegar to be safe, just distilled water): viewtopic.php?f=5&t=19232&start=50#p267063

Your coolant by the way is looking like this :


In theory this contaminated coolant is not good for your water pump seal, so schedule your coolant change and flush the system with no water saved until its clean
