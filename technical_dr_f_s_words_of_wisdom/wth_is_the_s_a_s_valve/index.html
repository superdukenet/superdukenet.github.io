giyf
phrase / abbreviation

Google is Your Friend
"GIYF" is often given as curt response to a question that perhaps should not have been asked, or was poorly researched.

This phrase carries several insinuations, firsty that the poster did not bother to try to
find his/her own solution to a problem, but instead 'dumped it' to the forum/chat group to solve.

It is a shortened, dismissive, response to a question deemed inappropriate. Similar to RTFM, it conveys the intonation that "If you didn't bother to
spend 15 seconds searching for the answer on your own, I'm not going to waste a complete sentence responding to you"

As with RTFM, it carries a cruder double-entendre along the lines of "Get It Yourself F**ker"

Snatch low end is down to incorrect fuelling which in turn implicates the SAS, the ECU, catalytic converters, the OE exhaust and everything else that KTM had to do to get the bike through EU emissions targets.

You can get it back to the way the KTM engineers designed this bike by doing all the mods to correct these things.

As part of this process you need to get either a Power Commander and get custom map made with a Dyno shop or get the cable and make (or download) your own map with a PC.

Si,

I'm advertising my SD on SWB (Graham22). If I have to put it back to standard to trade it in then you're welcome to buy my SAS plates & plug in resistor.

Don't know what pipes you have on your's (appreciate most SDRs have Akras but not always due to the 3 different SDR models sold) but I don't think there's any benefit in running SAS delete on standard pipes - on of it's functions is to bleed air into the exhaust port warming up the cat sooner. If you have aftermarket pipes, the biggest benefit is to stop the popping on the over-run.
