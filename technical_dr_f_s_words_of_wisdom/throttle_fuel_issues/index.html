Have you got a power commander or anything? Either way if you have washed it recently I would have a look at the big grey connector, its a common problem. Basically open it up, dry it out thoroughly, bung some dilectric grease in there and put it all back together again.

If you have tuneecu or power commander try reloading your maps as well.

Like TC said, check the coil (spark pug boots) and make sure they are seated all the way.

I had a similar issue with another bike (R1) and it turned out to be a broken spark plug. Chased my ass for weeks changing jetting and the like. Pissed me off. Replaced the plugs and problem was resolved.
