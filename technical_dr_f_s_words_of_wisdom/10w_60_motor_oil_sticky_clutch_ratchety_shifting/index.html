#6
When the bike is turned off it will be harder to push in gear with the clutch pulled in. There are many clutch plates and when you pull the clutch in they only seperate a few millimeters at best and this creates a lot of friction. When the bike is started the drive plates are spinning which helps the plates release and roll easy. The clutch is also forced lubricated once the bike is started.
