i live in an area that extremely hot . so i have been summerizing (is that a word) my bike. i added an additonal fan on the right side of the radiator and installed a lower actuating temperature switch. it turns the fans on at 6 bars. next i will changing coolant out to engine ice.

so far the additional fan seems to help. i let my bike idle for about 15 minutes in the garage and it never got above 6 bars. and fans only came on for about 30 seconds. normally after 15 minutes temps would have skyrocketed.
