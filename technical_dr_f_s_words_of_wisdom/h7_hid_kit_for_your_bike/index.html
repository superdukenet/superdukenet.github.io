I finally got my H7 HID installed and have the same light out problem. It would come on and then whenever I started the bike, it would go out. I solve it by just switching on the high beam when I start and then flipping back to low when the bike is running. That has worked every time.

I have not run it at night yet, so I have not had a chance to experience the great brightness increase (from both the Euro headlight assembly and the H7 HID). DST is over next week, so I will find out then for sure, unless I work late one night this week.
