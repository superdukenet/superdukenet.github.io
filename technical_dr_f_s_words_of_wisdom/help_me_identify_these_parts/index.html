I dunno what the top pic is of/for....but the bottom one is the mountings for the hangers of the standard exhaust for the SD. They go into the rear subframe of the bike and then the standard cans have a bit each side that slides inot the hole of those parts. The rubber dampens the vibes/noise going into the frame. So basically, you only need em if you're going to fit standard cans back on it.
