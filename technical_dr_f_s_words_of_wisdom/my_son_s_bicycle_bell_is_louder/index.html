Horn update.....model Stebel TM80 Â£12 from nippynormans.com
Arrived and fitted today. Soundwise, a big improvement on the original with a deeper air horn like sound. Slightly muffled by the front spoiler though.
Fitting - a lot of faffage and experimenting to find the best fit. I tried using the original horn bracket but couldn't fit the spoiler back on. Then I removed the original bracket and screwed the new horn directly into one of the bracket holes - this would have been a good position with the spoiler fitting properly but the more I tightened it, the more strangled the sound became I eventually settled on using the 2 metal straps supplied, screwed where the existing bracket was and with a bit of bending and the horn outlet facing downwards, I managed to re fit the spoiler ok. Bit of a tight squeeze in there though. Electrical connections were a straight fit, although I bent the male spade connectors on the horn to angle them away from the exhaust header. Very pleased with the result and a worthwhile mod for the money.
