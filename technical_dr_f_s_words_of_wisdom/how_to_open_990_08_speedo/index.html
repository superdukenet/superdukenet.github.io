Most issues are with the connector on the back, easy to get to.
The other one is water ingress, plenty of units died from that. Try drying it out in a warm and dry environment. (don't turn the oven up too high... )
Otherwise I believe it is sealed and besides cutting the plastic there is no way.

#5
Remove four screws from rear of unit and (gently) pull off cover - to get to bulbs, (very gently) pull off tacho needle, remove two (?) screws from l and rhs and lift out backing plate.

Suggest sealing cover and base unit joint with silicone when reassembling - I've also wrapped joint with black insulating tape to keep water out.
