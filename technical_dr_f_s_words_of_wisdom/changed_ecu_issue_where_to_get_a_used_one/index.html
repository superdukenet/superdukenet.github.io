Shorai batteries suck in any weather below 10C/50F. They are great above that temp. You have to "warm-up" the battery before trying to start the bike. Walk out to the bike without your helmet and gloves on, insert the key and turn it to ON. Of course the headlight will come on. Take your time putting on your helmet and gloves. After about two minutes of warming up the battery it should be OK to start now. This is only for Shorai or similar batteries. If that doesn’t work replace with a lead-acid battery.
