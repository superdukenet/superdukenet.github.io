#5
More that likely it's just clutch lubrication, if your oil has a bit of age on it, it will loose some viscosity so the film between the plates wont be enough to let them slip when you pull the clutch lever in. So when you click it into gear the gear box jolts the plates appart and lets oil back in between.

That's why it only happens the first time you put it in gear.

I usually pull the clutch, blip the throttle to spin the clutch appart, then put it into gear.
