cjc, it is common for the dealer to "not" to loft the front wheel to refill the engine cooling system, (as ktm recommends).

2 dealers did it to me 2 separate times. Over time and heat cycles they will manage to burp out a bubble at a time, then they will take a small gulp of coolant,

this makes the bike appear to be using it when in reality the air had not been properly bleed from the cooling system.

05-06 models are prone to a worn water pump shaft. The Viton rubber seal would rub a groove in the shaft and when you notice the coolant bottle empty from the max to min marks within 1 tank of juice it's time to get it fixed. (Not to be confused with thermal expansion)

It's not an easy job, but at least the parts aren't very expensive. The 07 bikes use a teflon seal - which isn't prone to the same problem - so like the clutch slave cylinder, anyone with a problematic 05-06 model can fit the new parts and worry no longer....
