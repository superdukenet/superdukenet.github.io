TBird, the NGK KR8DI is a special for KTM. (I came across it in an NGK listing I got somewhere.) It's an Iridium plug, that's why the much higher price.

DCPR is M12 thread, 16mm hex, Projected insulator, Resistor.

K is M12 thread, 16mm hex, 19mm reach, Resistor, Iridium electrode.

KA is M12 thread, 14mm hex (you won't need a thin-wall socket), 26.5mm reach, A design series, Iridium electrode, -9 is 0.9mm gap instead of the standard 0.8
