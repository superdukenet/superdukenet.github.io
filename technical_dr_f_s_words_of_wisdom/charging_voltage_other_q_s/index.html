Post 4 by ktmguy, 22 Sep 2013, 03:57

#4
They mostly go under load. The way the original regulator works is to "bleed" the excess to the negative and from what I have heard it is that bit that fails.
An alternator like on a bike or car can easily push out over 65 Volts AC which the diodes convert in to DC and the regulator lowers it to 13-14 V (approx) mark depending on the charge needed for the battery.
The problem is that it is hard to measure even if you try to put a volt meter on AC over it the battery works like a giant cap and prevents you from seeing the AC on the meter, the popping of fuses and bulbs is an indication and high voltage (lets say above15v) will give it away.
