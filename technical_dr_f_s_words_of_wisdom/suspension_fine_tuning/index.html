Best to have a read of this and try to understand just the basics of suspension.

Start off with the standard settings and go from there, changing one thing at a time and going for test rides around the same route. You will get a good feel of what small changes will make.

Im the same weight as you with the same bike. I'm happy with the rear settings standard, and the front 13 clicks rebound and 14 clicks compression. This is for street.
