Ok I am curious as to those experiencing the 3500 - 4500rpm throttle hesitation whether ambient temperature could be a contributing factor.

The reason being I have noticed that the hesitation on hot humid days in the 30s almost disappears, I am wondering if this is just a coincidence or a contributing factor.

I am refering to the 2007/2008 model years so other no need to comment.

I'd be interested in the following information.

Bike year and location / do you experience this lack of power between 3500 and 4500 rpm / what is the average ambient temperature at the time - estimated is fine.

Post 2 by SDSmurf, 10 Jun 2008, 12:16

#2
Well, not so much a lack of power issue, but mine seems to surge/shunt at the crucial 3500-4500 zone less the colder it is.

I work night shift, so leaving work @ 07h00 it's 5deg cel and throttle is quite smooth. Then I fetch the Wife @ around 16h30 (15-20deg cel) and it's quite a bit worse...
I do this every day, so am pretty sure of it not being a once off or something like that.

I can def. remember in Summer (+25) it was bad, but that was still in the running in period, might not be so bad anymore.
