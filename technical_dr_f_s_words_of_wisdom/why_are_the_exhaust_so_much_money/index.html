I agree with you to a point and have felt the pain.............that said, you can't blame KTM too much as they are not the manufacture of Akro exhausts. Akrapovic, from what I know and feel, builds the nicest, highest quality exhaust systems in the world and not only for motorcycles, Some of their car exhausts are over the top and ten times more expensive than a motorcycle exhaust. Other manufactures also have some pricey systems and then their are the less expensive systems and the build quality reflects the pricing. Have you ever checked into the prices of OEM stock exhaust systems and not just from KTM. The Euro stock systems go for what I consider a ridicules price and almost force the buyer into an aftermarket system where at least you get some better performance and better sound along with the price.

(as a side note I think Akro is a major manufacture of titanium medical (hip, knee etc.) implants and you can bet they are pricey too )

Take a look at some of the welds and quality on an Akro system, it may help ease the pain..........................but probably NOT
