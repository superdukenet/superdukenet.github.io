The bike has just been in for service today (12 months, but only 2500 miles) and to have my persistent RHS casing oil leak fixed.

About half a mile away from home the oil pressure light comes on on the dash. Now, I am riding in torrential rain here so, after a brief stop to check there is actually some oil in the engine I slowly bimble off home.

At home I turn the ignition off and on, restart the bike to find the light still on. Spray a bit of WD40 around too to no avail.

I'm thinking there may be a bit of water ingress into the electrics here, so the radiator in the garage is on full blast, as is the dehumidifier to get things dried out a bit. I'll check again tomorrow.

I called the servicing dealer, who told me to use the KTM breakdown cover to get the bike back down to them!

Anyone had this problem after (really) wet riding?
