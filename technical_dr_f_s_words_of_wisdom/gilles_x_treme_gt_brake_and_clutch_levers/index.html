Installed the "Ergo" levers today, then a 40 mi test ride on moderate mountain twisties. Verdict vs. stockers: brake lever is a slight improvement (I normally brake with only my middle finger), and the clutch lever is a significant improvement in feel and precision. The front brake has been feeling a little mushy, so I will bleed and re-assess. Overall design and workmanship is much better than stock. Understated bling, nothing obnoxious.







No need to remove this chingadera from the stock lever (and/or break the thread and make a completely unnecessary trip to the hardware store like this guy... ), as there is a shiny new one in a tiny ziplock bag stapled to the inside of the packaging:



Picked mine up from Motorcycle Superstore on sale for $157.99 each. Plus the 10% coupon you get with your previous shipping invoice for ordering any-old-thing.

I have had the SD for less than a month, so the only other mods I have done are:
-KTM heated grips (dealer installed to sweeten the sale): get the job done, but not as good as the stock ones on my BMW. The switch feels a little flimsy, but that is to be expected for a $100 system.
-PIAA H4 headlight bulb: output is slightly better than OEM, but it does seem to do a much better job of getting cagers' attention.
-Direct wiring lead for my Tourmaster heated jacket liner and gloves. It is just barely long enough to reach all the way from the battery to the seat, so will have to get creative with that one.

On order:
-AKO Racing LED tail light. More to follow.
-R&G fork & rear axle sliders.
-Kriega US20 dry sack.
