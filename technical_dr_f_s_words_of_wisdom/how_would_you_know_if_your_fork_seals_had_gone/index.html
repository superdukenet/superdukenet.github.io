Dirty fork oil ? - you must be thinking of old Jap bikes.

I had a set of USD WP forks blow seals on a 2 year old CCM & the oil looked clean - very slightly green - well to begin with anyway. If you went for a ride it would end up all mucky, but wipe it way & leave it in the garage for a week or so & there would be a film of oil around the seals.

Try bleeding the air out of the forks, go for a ride, wipe the seals then leave the bike for a few days/week or so & see if it comes back.

Hi, I have had a leaking fork oil seal on my SD, the oil was clean and goldenish in colour. (Bike had done 7,000 miles)

If the seal has gone, best get the bike to someone who can work out whether debris was pulled up from the outside to the inside of the fork leg, (a typical USD fork problem), or if the fork internals were not totally clean at point of assembly (it does happen), or if there is damage to the fork slider. The damage needs only to be slight and the oil will seep.
