Is it really gas-charged? I would find that surprising ... Are we talking about that worthless POS that comes on SDRs, or the nicer of the PowerParts ones? I ask, as I would remove the OEM cheese, and put on a Scotts or GPR.

Out of oil, or piston (or other) seal failed/failing - ?

Never pulled apart a WP one, but have done Ohlins, Showas, and a French brand, the name of which I can't immediately recall. Most can usually be repaired and/or refilled. The internals aren't particularly complex. On some, you can alter damping (range, if adjustable) through re-valving and/or changing the oil viscosity. And when filling, that usually involves immersing the whole thing in oil while doing so (for the DIYer).

Usually, if you don't want to take it on, most suspension houses are well-suited (and willing) to deal with them.

Regardless, as it isn't functioning satisfactorily, no huge risk if you have the tools to take it apart to inspect.
