I asked this question without response in my project thread but thought I would post it as a separate thread for people who wouldn't see it there.

Turns out that the gas probes on my headers are not plugged into the wiring loom and so can be removed as part of a big refurbishment that I'm undertaking on the bike. Obviously I could just cut the wires off and reuse the existing probes but that's a bit untidy and I'm wondering if anybody knows / has found a neat looking solution to sealing the holes left by the probes in the headers. Ideally this would be non destructive as I may want to return the bike to standard at some point if I decide it's not a keeper and decide to sell it.

I cant be the only one to have faced the issue and hopefully somebody here will know of an easy / neat solution?
