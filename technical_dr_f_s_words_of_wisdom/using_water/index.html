Ok for you Stratkat, To try and ease your mind I've gone out in the shop and started the SD, temp guage has a total of 12 bars, after reaching normal operating temperature which imo is 6-7 bars, the cooling fan kicks on at 8-9 bars, I would say that you should have no worries at all as long as your not up to 10 bars, now in the racing world you may run at 10 bars cos of the rigors at hand, and I do understand your concern is based on "while not in motion". I am a temperature nut case and have been known to carry and sport a digital infrared gun in my tank bag along with my tyre gauge, then for the sake of faith and how that all works I carry no tool kit, but have just this past week hired a cell phone for the fist time in nearly 20 years, I claim to hear voices from it?

to the author AndersH, A slight variation in the reservoir level is normal, I personally run mine at the minimum when the engine is cold, when you fill the cooling system after any opening of it, always fill to min. level when cold. ride and top off accordingly. and for those of you in the know about the LC8, joe knows the coolant technically does not go to the tip of the V but only 1/3rd of the way up as far as the cooling jacket goes.
