That sound scary to me, the crunching noises would be freaking me out for sure, I would pull all the magnets and see what you see? if there is a big problem it should show itself on the magnet. I am not real sure how the oil passes from the engine to the trans but I would make it my business to find out and drain all the oil and take a 5 lb magnet and go through it for a few min's to see if you could pick up any metal. Inspect the oil filter and oil screen closely. If you find metal go to the dealer pronto, save your oil and other stuff for the ktm folks. HTH.
