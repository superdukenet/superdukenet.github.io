Post 2 by ktmguy, 02 Aug 2013, 03:17

#2
Hi, no the engine doesn't have to be cold. What it does is reset the basic parameters of some of the sensors, it takes about 15 min and you can actually hear it when the ECU resets.
Just don't touch the throttle, that is one of the conditions to keep the reset sequence running.
Have fun!
