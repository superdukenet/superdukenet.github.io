Fellas,

12.5 months ago I had my water pump shaft seal replaced because I was loosing a small amount of coolant. They fixed it under warranty, that warranty is now gone. It continued to loose some coolant over the last year and I saw some casting sand in the over flow tank. It seems that they never fully flushed out the casting sand when they did the water pump overhaul. So I decided to flush out the coolant system and possibly rebuild the water pump shaft seal.

After I bleed the system and I rise off the bike with water I heat the engine up and I see steam, normal.... and I see steam.... and steam

I believe that I have a leaking radiator
