Post 1 by DukeMonster, 05 Aug 2011, 03:02

#1
II found the solo seat on my SDR a bit to slippery on my last track day and found myself squeezing the tank/tech spec pads..with my legs with quite a bit of force to prevent me from sliding back. I never had to squeeze that hard before on other bikes. I went to a local upholstery shop to check out some materials to re-cover the seat with. He suggested the stuff for jet skis and MOTO X bike, but the material looked very "tacky" and felt like it might hinder side to side movement to much. Then I started checking out some leather cowhides with varying textures, but still felt it may not suit my needs. Then I noticed the backside of the cowhides and how it felt. I placed it against the smooth side of other leathers to test the abrasion resistance, and it felt like it would be just right. It was nice and grippy with pressure applied, and slid pretty well when pressure was lessened..just like one would want it when applying your weight on the seat on straights, and then shifting butt/weight on turns. I had my seat re-covered with it and will test on my next track day. I will do a stint in the mountains this weekend to see how it feels. The seat looks great..almost like a suede.
