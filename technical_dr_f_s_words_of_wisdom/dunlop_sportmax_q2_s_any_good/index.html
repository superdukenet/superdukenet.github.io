I have more than 6,500 miles on the D208s that came with my bike.
I commute 30 miles round trip on the bike when the weather's nice (50+ and dry), so I estimate I probably have about 4,500 commute miles on them, but even those miles are bagged about as aggressively as you get for my area. The remaining miles are attributable to fast, aggressive weekend riding on back roads and mountain roads.
