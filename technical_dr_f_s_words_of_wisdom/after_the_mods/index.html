Its the lack of gaps after a full stop that makes it exhausting to read

New pipes are going to smell a bit. I'd seriously look a Arka map or custom map rather than running the standard. You upped the air flow both before and after combustion and I would think you may be running the risk of being lean now. If you have or can get a TuneECU cable there is probably a custom map that matches your bike to download.
