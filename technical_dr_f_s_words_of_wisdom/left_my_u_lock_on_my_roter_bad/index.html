without one of these I'd have ruined several rear disks. Always put it on the rear disk as the front end is way to east to pick up with a piece of wood between forks and bike and wheel off somewhere quiet to remove the lock. Also always attach it next to the calliper so if you do forget you can't build up any momentum before it hits the calliper and so there is no damage to calliper or disk.
