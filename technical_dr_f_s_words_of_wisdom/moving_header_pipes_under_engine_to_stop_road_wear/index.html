#10
You need to adjust the front and rear spring load for your weight. If the preload is too soft, you'll get problems like yours.
Here's an instruction film clip where Keith Code explains how it's done.


On the superduke the sag should be between 35 and 50 mm at the front and between 30 and 40 mm at the rear.
After you've set the preload correctly, you can check whether the springs are hard/soft enough for you. The static sag (bike's sag without rider) has to be between 5 and 10 mm at the rear and between 25 and 30 mm at the front.
If you can't get both rider's sag and static sag between the above limits, the springs are either too soft or too hard for your weight and should be replaced.
