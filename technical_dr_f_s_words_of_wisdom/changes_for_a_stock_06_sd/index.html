I remember the "issues" that were said to be "fixed" on the 07+ SD were:

- Better fuelling/throttle repsonse at low revs
- Flighty handling (the 07+ has a less steep rake)
- Brakes were claimed to be better (although now I hear the oppposite!)
- Better tank range

Can't think of anything else.

Reality is the 05-06 model has a bit more power than the 07+ (unless you go for the 08+ SDR), and apart from the tank range, the above claims are not that significant. Also, the 07+ has different clocks, different head mask, slightly different panels, paint job (the styling is a bit more modern).

Some or all of the above may not be a problem to you at all, or the differences not enough to warrant the extra dosh for an 07+

Quite a lot of owners SD owners - regardless of model - invest in 1 or more of the following:

- a set of cans
- an aftermarket air filter (motobox is the best)
- remove the SAS
- apply some form of fuel map (pciii or tuneboy)
- a cuddly toy
- shorty levers
- 16t front spocket for 05-06 (standard on 07+)
- remove the 2nd/3rd gear limiter
- slow or quick turn throttle
- various bits of carbon bling
- steering damper (standard on SDR)
- rearsets
- tail tidy

Hope this helps.
