Carrying luggage is about the last thing the SD is made for but if you want to take it for a holiday trip you do need to take some stuff with you...

I wanted bags that don't look too awful, easy to fit and take off again and WITHOUT damaging the bike. Something most luggage stuff is very good at... After a lot of crawling around the web and at shops I bought these bags.

Both are from Bags Connection and the tank bag has the Quick Lock system from SW Motech. It click-connects to an adapter-ring mounted around the fuel cap using the bolts that are already there. No straps, no velcro, not tape, no zippers, nothing. Click on, click off. I've done 200 KM/h with it, solid as rock. And the best off all, it doesn't touch the tank unless you fill it solid with bricks. Nowhere! I had my doubts about this system at first but I can only say it's brilliant! Hard to imagine looking at the picture but you don't even touch the tank bag when riding. Great.

The rear bag matches the tank bag. It's fixed with 4 straps, two from the passenger-pegs and two rear with small rings mounted under the rear fender using existing screws too. Without touching the exhausts or bodywork. Only two pieces of tape to protect the side panels.

The tank bag has a variable capacity of 15 to 22 ltrs (bigger than the KTM bag). The rear bag from 25 to 38 ltrs. More than enough for a week or more.






Not cheap for soft luggage but GOOD STUFF!!
