#1
I've had the bike for a few weeks and could be a little better at riding it but this doesn't seems to be me. I have a very difficult time going from 5th to 6th and occasionally back down. Sometimes the bike is stuck in 6th until I can find the perfect rev match and it magically drops into 5th. I also get stuck "between" gears. Example: going from 3rd to 4th, the engine manages to end up in some neutral position.

I have noticed if I'm extremely quick with the clutch while up-shifting it's typically not a problem. There was one instance where the bike would not come out of 6th at all. I rolled over to the side of the road and played with the shifter and clutch until I was able to get to the lower gears and get rolling again. What's going on with this? I'm used to my vintage cafe that didn't care when you did a gear change at all.
