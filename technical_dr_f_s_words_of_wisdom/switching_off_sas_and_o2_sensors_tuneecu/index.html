For the SAS, switch off is fine but also block the hose or it'll still leak some air.
PCIII is fine, you can also import the PCIII map in tuneecu and load it in to the bike's ecu and remove the PCIII

As suggested the best advice is read up on it, you load the wrong stuff or do the wrong thing and it can fook your bike or ecu.
Don't mean to scare you, tuneecu is not scary, just read first before playing it is not an ikea table where you can start over.
