Jeez!

Yes it's all canbus!
It has also 5 ecu's in it.

Anyone not familiar with vehicle electrics should not mess with it as you probably found out.

The 5v is assumingly the test signal, when turned on the voltage goes to 12v. The headlight comes on when you turn the key but then goes off unless you start the engine to avoid draining the battery.
