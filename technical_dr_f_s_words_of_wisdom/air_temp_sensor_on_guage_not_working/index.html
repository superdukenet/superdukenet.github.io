There is a separate sensor for the dash, I believe it is in the headlight cowl. Start with checking the connector, it is unlikely the sensor itself is faulty but it is possible.

Just had a look at the electrical diagram, page with the dash on it. (download from the how to section)
It has a separate sensor in the headlight cowl. (see dash diagram third down on the left). Connector pin 6 and 23 from the dash grey and black/red wire.
Red/ black is the feed, grey is signal to the dash. Check the 2 wire connector and if that is fine put an ohm meter over the sensor, it should read a few ohm's (can't find in the manual what exactly) and it should change if the temp changes. If it is faulty it will probably read open (infinite).
