Boosterplug may as well be a scam. Tricking the bike into running richer across the board (by altering the air temp signal) will just use more fuel and may well lose power.

Rapid Bike seems to be similar, but based on changing lambda sensor values rather than temp sensor.


Go find a decent dyno. Any decent tuner will not offer products like this.
