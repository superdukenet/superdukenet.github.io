I plugged mine into the switched accesory spade terminal thingies in the headlight mask and stuck the controller down on the left hand side on the wee plastic scoop thing next to the radiator.











No real hassles with it unless I want to change the setting when I'm going around a left hander.

I made up a small aluminium bracket for mine which fits under the bar risers. It's "L" shaped and puts the controller just above the dash. It doesn't look great but it does the job nicely - easy to get at and not in the way at all.
