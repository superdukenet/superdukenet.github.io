Seeing as my bike is out of warranty now, I'm looking at replacing the stuffed slave cylinder with the Evoluzion Billet Slave Cylinder (http://www.ktmtwins.com/990superduke/99030.html) and the 'required for 05/06 model' updated pushrod (http://www.ktmtwins.com/950adventure/60032054400.html)
