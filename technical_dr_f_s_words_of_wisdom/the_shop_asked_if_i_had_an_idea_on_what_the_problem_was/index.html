Sorry to here the fuel pump filter did not fix it. But it still sounds like a fuel problem. Have you tried the obvious, changing the spark plugs, checking caps. not seated completely on the spark plugs. Blown head gasket. Secondary flys not opening all the way. Very dirty air filter. Tank vent partially clogged pinched. Valves out of adjustment or damaged exhaust valve. Bad black box.
