I am designing an adapter/access board that will allow me to plug an afermarket ECU directly into the stock harness. It will allow the elimination of the stock ECU, or will allow the stock ECU to remain in place and the aftermarket ECU to run in a "piggy back" mode, just controlling the fuel and spark and allowing the stock unit to still controll things like the idle motor and secondary butterflies etc.
It will also have a bunch of LED's for diagnosic use, 12v power, Vref, tach-in, tach-out, injector 1&2, coil 1&2, stepper motor's, possibly O2 rich lean?
I have the scematic 95% drawn up and the board is about 1/2 done.
The question I have is about ignition power to the stock ECU, I have looked over the schems a bunch and cant find a circuit that is labled " ignition" that goes to the stock ECU. Pin 20 of the 26 pin connector goes tho the dash (pin 9) and the speed sensor, I am thinking that this may be the ignition power source but I'm not sure. Pin 16 of the 34 pin connector goes to pin 8 of the dash ( FI light? ) .
Just wondering if anyone new for sure, Not a huge deal as all 60 pins of the stock harness will have jumper pads.
Biggest hold up so far is sourcing the ECU board connector, I found one place that sells it but it has a minimun order of 960 units, +$10,000!
I have asked the manufacturer for a couple of samples and am waiting to hear back from them.
