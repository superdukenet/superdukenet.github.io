My SD was delivered with the oil tank way overfilled. On the trip from the dealer it leaked oil everywhere. I think it was venting oil into the airbox or something, and it was then running down the engine and onto the ground. I removed the excess oil and the problem mostly went away. I continued to find small drops under the scoop for about the first 500 miles, but it now seems to be done dribbling.
