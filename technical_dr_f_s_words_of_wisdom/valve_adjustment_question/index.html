you want it to be a fairly snug fit and by that I mean the feeler gauge will feel a little tight but not so tight that it feels like your just pushing the bucket down
doctypo Forum member

Post 3 by doctypo, 27 Feb 2010, 06:20

#3
Checking valve clearance is really a learned feel and hard to describe, but you should be able to insert the feeler gauge with slight pressure ( and maybe little wiggle ) The true measure of the clearance is on the pull. The gauge should come smoothly with a light draw. Lacking the "feel" you can always use a Go/No Go gauge.
