First off, that 16t sprocket you ordered is a waste as they came as standard on 07+bikes.

O2 sensor can just be turned off in tuneECU.

SAS can just be turned off, but you will need to block the hose with either a marble or bolt if you are going to leave it in place. It does look hell of a lot tidier if removed though.
