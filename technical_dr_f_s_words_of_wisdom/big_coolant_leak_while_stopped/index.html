I had the strangest issue with my bike last Sunday. For about two weeks or so I could smell anti-freeze/coolant but could not see any leaks or signs of a leak. So Sunday while up in the mountains, when I stopped at a rest area and turned-off the engine, coolant would build-up in the expansion tank and over-flow which left a small puddle of coolant on the ground. It happened twice. A few minutes later when the bike cooled it would suck-up all the coolant that was left in the expansion tank until it was empty. A Japanese friend I was riding with called Marutomi KTM shop in Yokohama and they immediately knew what the problem was. I needed a “new radiator cap” of all things. Lucky for me they had one in stock so my day of riding was cut a little short. First time I have ever had a radiator cap go bad! Anyone else have a similar problem
