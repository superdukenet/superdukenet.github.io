Post 3 by Special K, 26 Jun 2007, 11:49

#3
Welshy, I have the R&G bobbins too and have found that the rear wheel spindle cannot be removed with them in place as the aluminium blocks (with the adjustment marks on them) catch on them. Have you had this problem?
It's ok for chain lubing but for rear wheel removal, I take the bobbins off and use long M10 bolts in the paddock stand instead of the hooks and screw the bolts into the threaded swing arm holes.
