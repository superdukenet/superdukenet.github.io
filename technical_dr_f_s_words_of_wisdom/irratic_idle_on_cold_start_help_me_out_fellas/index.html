think its disintegrated and or partially blocking the tank filter leaning it out on start up?

My experience in the past was very similar to this. The shop found a fault in the TPS and wanted to replace... I picked up a TuneECU cable and hooked up my 07 SD.

TPS was showing varying voltages, so I bought a new one.
When I went in to the throttle body to swap in the new part, I noticed that the old sensor had all kinds of dirt accumulated inside of it. I cleaned it out with a q-tip and some rubbing alcohol (then some water, so the plastic didn't degrade). Dried it off. Popped it back in. BINGO! Back in business!

I did the same for the rear TPS, but be careful! That sucker is sitting on a switch for the secondary butterflies. When you put it back on, make sure it's lined up, or you'll throw an FI light.
See my post on the secondary TPS alignment .

When you are reattaching the TPS (on both sensors) the way you adjust the voltage is to leave the screw a little loose, then rotate the sensor in place until you get the right voltage reading from your TuneECU software, then tighten the screw down. Takes a little patience, but in the end, it's worth it!

Hope this helps.
Good luck!
