Yeah, I've been sucking air into my exhaust so I put some fire putty on the end caps but I wish I'd used a silicone based variant instead, I'm not sure it made much difference, I don't think it held when running.

He got a silicone based blue stuff from there and much to our surprise it held up with no problems all day even while taking a track day beating.

It may of been this one http://www.halfords.com/webapp/wcs/stor ... yId_165691

Wasn't recommended for head gaskets but we were willing to try anything at that point for a temporary fix.
