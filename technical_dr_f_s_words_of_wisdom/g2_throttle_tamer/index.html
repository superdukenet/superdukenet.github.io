#11
Finally got the chance to fit and try out the "Throttle Tamer" today. Fitting was a piece of cake, the hardest part was removing the rubber grip from the existing throttle tube but even so it was done and dusted in 20 minutes or so. So does it work? well yes, it has made for a much less choppy ride and i don't have to be consciously micromanaging the slightest bit of throttle input. With the throttle tamers 400 profile cam it softens the first 1/4 turn so you lose that on/off jerkiness and makes riding with a neutral throttle much more relaxing. I don't know if it's available in the UK yet but i ordered mine direct from G2 in the US and it took only 5 days to get to me and it's a much cheaper option than a power commander. An essential mod.
