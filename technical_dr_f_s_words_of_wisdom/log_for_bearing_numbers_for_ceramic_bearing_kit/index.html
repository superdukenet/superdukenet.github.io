I've used the following >
Front Wheel - 2008 - SD
69062RS - 30X47X9mm x 2
35X47X7 - Oil Seal x 2

KTM part number for front wheel bearing >
0625069068 BEARING 6906 DDU2CG23S6NM x 2

I haven't changed rear bearings but the KTM part number is
0625062052 GR.BALL BEAR. 6205-2RSHC3HMTF7 x 2
0625060060 BALL BEARING 6006 DDU2CG23S6NM (cush drive bearings - looks like 2 required)
