#5
Oh, by the way. There is no need to pull the shock completely off the bike to swap the rear spring.

Remove wheel, hugger/chain guard, lower shock bolt. Undo preload ring all the way up the shock body, push bottomiing rubber up and out of way and pull lower spring bucket off shock. Spring will slide off at this point.

Have to get bike in air from swing arm pivot. I used steel rod (1/2" or 7/16" OD...can't remember) and jack stands.
