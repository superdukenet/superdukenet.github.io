Picked up my new SD yesterday.
Got it home (50km's trip) and found that it was leaking oil from the oil level tube (clear tube).
It had split at the top at the connector.

Has anyone had this problem before?

Not a good start to owning a new SD

Anyway I got some oversize clear tube and hose clamps from the hardware shop and patched it up.
Taking it back the dealer to get it replaced next weekend.

Did a 300km loop with the patch and only lost a small amount of oil so no big dramas I guess.
