#1
Oil sight tube cracked and I got oil all over my bike and rear tyre,resulting me to crash in turn #4 at brainerd international raceway Minnesota.Guys please be careful,this little piece of plastic can mess You up.
I will never put this shit on my bike again,I will plug holes with screws and washers and will be checking oil level thru upper hole on oil tank.Do not ignore this.I wish I did something else instead putting new tube when my first one broke.
Twisted Jester Forum member

Post 2 by Twisted Jester, 02 Jun 2010, 03:58

#2
Bad luck, not heard of this problem causing a crash up till now. Maybe you have some sort of case against KTM if you can prove that this was the cause of you coming off?

I check that pipe everytime I clean the bike. There seems to be some sort of defective material specification on some bikes, maybe it's to do with the temperature or age but to me it doesn't seem hard to fit a transparent tube that is capable of holding oil and not splitting. It's a serious design flaw, not to mention what a stupid bit of design it is in the first place. It's on the wrong side for a start for when you're topping up oil. Why they couldn't have just put a dipstick or sight glass somewhere is beyond me.
