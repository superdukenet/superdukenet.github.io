All the maps on the dynojet website are free to download...

Which one is the best? Well that depends on which KTM mapping you have in your ECU and your own bike setup. You'll have to load and test them yourself if you're not prepared to put your bike on a dyno.

If you have the standard mapping in you ECU, make sure you only use those pc3 maps that have been written using that mapping. And likewise for the 'akro' tune.

You'll lose Hp with the snorkel removed.
