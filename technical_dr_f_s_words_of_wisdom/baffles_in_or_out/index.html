on saturday had my leo alloys fitted and the baffles were taken out, did not seem too loud in the workshop but once out on the road i realised that these are the loudest cans i have ever had and over the last twnety years i have had some loud cans, complete racing systems, etc.

how many of you ride with the baffles out, i must say that after 3 hours riding yesterday i now like the noise or maybe i am going a wee bit deaf.

they must be ok as i had to ride past the guardia civil and they seemed to like them so much that after i went past my mate said they were writing my reg down so that they could send me a letter congratulating me on having the best sounding twin in spain!!!!!
