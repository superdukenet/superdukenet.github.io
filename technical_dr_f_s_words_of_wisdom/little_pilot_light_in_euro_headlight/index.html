Hey Guys,

I am moving steadily forward on the rebuild of my crashed superduke 990. I wired up the new headlight last night and i have a question. I got a better deal on the euro head light so i picked it up and had to splice it into the old headlight loom wire as it came with a different molex plug on the end. I did some internet research and I'm pretty sure i wired it correctly. light on top is high beam light on bottom is low beam but stays on when high beam is on. Little pilot light is on when low beam is on but goes out when high beam is on. My question is what is the purpose of that little pilot light? seams kind of useless!

I would guess that the little (5W?) light is used as a parking light and possibly as a fail-safe in case the low beam bulb blows, but I'd expect its primary function to be as the parking light - not sure if US-spec bikes have this, but turning the key anti-clockwise past the steering lock position will leave this front light and the tail light illuminated if parking the bike somewhere dark at night (sorry if you knew this already but I'm in the pub and feeling mildly oiled and quite verbose)
