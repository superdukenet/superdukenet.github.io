There's nothing unusual to look out for, it's a well made piece of kit and fits well, start from the front and work back, don't tighten anything up until it is all installed and looking straight, the usual stuff. Some say leave the bike ticking over for 15 mins, but this is only neccessary when a new map has been downloaded. You may need a mirror to check out your 'smug look' once installed
