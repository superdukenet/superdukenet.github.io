I have considered a v-twin. I looked at the vtr, tl but both of those are surprisingly heavy lumps and a bit uninspiring. A Ducati motor would be ok but would need to be at least the 999 engine. They are expensive for what you get stat wise. That left the Aprilia v twin from an RSV Mille. Good motor and you can get them cheap, I had settled on this before seeing a v4 aprilia engine on a bench, tiny thing and they sound nice. Unfortunately the lc8 engine seems to be a little lacking, many on here have said as much and they are also quite scarce which makes them an expensive option

Saying that I could build the whole bike using a v twin Mille engine for the cost of the v4 motor alone so I might end up using a twin yet. At the moment I like the idea of the v4
