Just check for yourself that the eliminators aren't fitted. Follow the wire from one of the O2 sensors to the first block connector, this is where the eliminator is fitted. If the sensors are still connected, then you need to get onto Dynojet or whoever did the instalation and ask for the eliminators. They come free with the PC, so don't take any BS about paying extra. The bad news is, if you had a custom map installed on a dyno, without eliminators, then you'll need to start again when you fit them.
