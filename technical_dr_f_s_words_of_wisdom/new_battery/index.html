Hey, I posed recently about buying a fancy new lithium battery. Turned out bad, to be fair I returned it and got an immediate refund and the chap says the battery is used fine in other Sdukes.

I bought a new YUASA YTZ14S - replacing the old one and it is the business. Full grunt starts at minus a degree or 2 vs no start with the fancy battery.

I didn't like the look of the banner, almost all mcycle dealers sell the yuasa around my patch of london as they all say it's the best aftermarket. I got mine from a yamaha dealer I think the sduke one is the msot powerful battery they make.
