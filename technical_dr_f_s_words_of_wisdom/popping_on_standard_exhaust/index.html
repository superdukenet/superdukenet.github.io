Post 2 by ktmguy, 17 Jul 2012, 22:13

#2
If it still has SAS system attached then it is completely normal. If that has been disabled then it isn't and you need to change the graphite gasket that is in the join 8 inches down from where the rear header bolts on to the cylinder head. This could need changing anyway as they are a service item every 9k miles. Mine never made it to 9 k miles

As above! The SAS bleeds air in the exhaust port to clean the cat, it is like having a air leak in the pipes somewhere. If the SAS is still connected the all pop.
