What year do you have?
Early LC8's had problems with the cylinder bolts not torked to the correct spec because of a "missing" washer under the cylinder nuts.
If you can't get the info on this site you might spend some time on the ADVrider.com site in the OrangeCrush section.
You might find the cases mating surface with the cylinder "eroded" and not a flat surface.
A bud has a 2004 ? KTM Adventure LC8 motor on his work bench that leaked oil from the base gasket.
Looks strange, like corrosion or electrolysis ate the surface away about .005-.010 inch deep.
Not sure what the cheep fix is other than some super type of epoxy, or machining the surfaces flat.
Good luck.
