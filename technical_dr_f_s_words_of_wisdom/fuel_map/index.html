The difference between the 'old' and 'advanced' ecu map is likely to be minimal and is probably more to do with the low rev range in an attempt to sort the snatchy throttle, so I wouldn't worry about using an existing PC3 map. Also, without a dyno setup, your fueling won't be spot on anyway, whichever ecu map you have, so just fit and enjoy then put getting a dyno custom map on your 'to do' list.
