My KTM dealer said that on "a lot of the LC8's" the passage way for the oil return line was not drilled all the way leaving a restricted passage and causing oil pressure to increase. This can result in oil leaking from various places. He said it would not cause excessive oil burning but when I took mine in last time it was very low. He said the fix was to drill out the passage which is at the oil tank. I'm taking mine in today for this fix.

Hope this helps some of you solve any oil leaking problems you may have.
