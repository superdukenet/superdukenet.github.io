Got a letter from KTM today stating

"KTM has received information from our supplier regarding possible problems with a limited production batch of front brake pads produced and subsequently fitted to some KTM Super Dukes that were manufactured within a known production period. Our concern is that these pads may become subject to corrosion affecting the adhesion of the friction material to the backing plate and therefore need to be exchanged at the earliest opportunity"

It then urges you to contact your dealer to arrange for them to be replaced
