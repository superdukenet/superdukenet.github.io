#2
to bleed it properly its not just a matter of filling up on an angle, it needs to be rock onto its sides while you 'massage' the pipes to try and pump the fluid in... then take it for a ride, top up and maybe even again.

after that if it keeps dropping then I would be looking into leaks in the hoses, etc

and if you havent done it already... buy a new radiator cap.... SD's are prone to issues from the rad caps
