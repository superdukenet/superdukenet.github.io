There IS a throttle cam for the SD, either from G2 or from KTM (G2 makes them both). I bought the KTM part in a KTM package (in ORANGE)- part # U6951154 from Munn Racing (USA). It's the same as the Adventure, or ALL KTM 4 stroke throttle cam (should come with a 100/200/400). It doesn't "cure" the issue, it tames it enough for you to technique it away. Supposedly, there's a new ECU map from KTM that also addresses the issue.
First, do the gear position sensor mod, then get the cams & remove all slack from the throttle cable. VOILA! Control restored.
