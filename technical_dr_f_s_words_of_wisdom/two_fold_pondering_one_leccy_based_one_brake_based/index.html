Hmm. Sounds simple to me (it always is when you're 300 miles away!!)

Brakes. If the bike is only 8 months old get a dealer to replace them. Don't f*** about as this might bugger your warranty (or worse if they jam at 80MPH). it may be you've caused the problem by over cleaning - they need a dab of grease to work and you may have washed it out! I rarely use brake cleaner and NEVER power wash brakes or bearings ever!

Heated grips. I have had the old style oxford grips for 2 1/2 years and no problems. Have you got them wired into an unregulated supply? They need the full 14+ volts the alternator chucks out to get properly hot.

Assuming they are wired right (they worked before so they must be) I suspect a dodgy connection is your problem. Maybe some corrosion in a joint. The grips draw about 4 amps (therefore offer a resistance of 3 ohms or so) so even a tiny bit of extra resistance will lower the voltage quite a bit - for example just ONE ohm resistance in a wire or connector will drop the voltage from 12 to 9 volts.

SO. Check the feed cables and joints (lots of WD40 in the connectors), make sure the ground wire is well connected and not corroded, get the bike to a dealer and get the brake calipers replaced under warranty.

Cheers
Rick
