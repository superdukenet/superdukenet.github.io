The last few weeks I've been going to the local NHRA drag strip for a mid-week "fun night" and really enjoy going up against those J-models with the Super Duke. For me it's fun being the tallest bike there by over a foot or two and having the shortest swingarm and overall length I made it all the way to the finals and ended up in the runner up spot out of about 40 bikes (most of them those darn Busa's ). I almost had the final win but didn't realise I was about to hit the rev limiter a few yards from the line as I had gone through the traps in 5th all night long...........that's why I want to run a shift light now. Even glancing at the analog tach is tough as you're trying to be quick on 5 or 6 up shifts, get into a low more aerodynamic tuck, keep a peripheral eye on the competition and all in around 10 seconds, seems a shift light would really be helpful.

Any suggestions on a good programmable shift light that will work with a 2006 990 Super Duke?

Thanks in advance.
