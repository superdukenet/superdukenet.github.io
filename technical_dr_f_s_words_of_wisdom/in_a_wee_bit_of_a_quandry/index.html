Looking for advice here folks.
That's me had the bike a year this weekend, clocked up 12078 miles.

Heading into work yesterday, noticed some shiny swarf on the bottom of the rear sprocket.
Checked it at work and got one of the mechanics to double check it for me.
Two of the sprocket carrier nuts had slackened and were rubbing on the swingarm, got a nice wee polished bit at the back just over the welded seam toward the back of the swingarm.
Christ on a chicken, they ain't easy to tighten, Allan bolt accessed through the feckin wheel.

I reckon it's fine, just a couple of mil polished off it.

Now, the quandry arises from the fact that my dealer fitted a new chain and sprocket at the 9k service at the end of June.
Had a couple of hiccups with them, waiting ages for my bike back n stuff like this.
Not entirely happy with the servicing, 3 or four mechanics in a year, although the sales side is pretty top notch.

I would be happy doing my own services, especially after this last wee niggle in a line of niggles.

However, by my reckoning, there is a year left on the warranty.

I have a muzzy recollection of reading that the LC8 engine had a reputation for lunching itself at just over 24k in some of the Adventures, so I will hit that milestone just before the warranty is out.

Is it worth keeping up the dealer servicing just in case my engine chews itself up, but have reservations everytime I eventually get my bike back, or, do it myself, negate the warranty but be happy that nothing silly will be wrong after it's serviced?


What do we reckon??
