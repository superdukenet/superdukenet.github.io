Shade, all you got to do to check your alternator is take a battery reading as normal with your multimeter and then take another one with the bike running, it should be higher voltage while the bike is running, I can't remember the exact amount but it might be something like 14.5 volts you're supposed to see, whatever it is, if you see an increase then your alternator is probably working ok.

Post 17 by ShadeTheChangingMan, 13 Mar 2010, 18:16

#17
final update for those who may check this later for info:

I checked the voltage from the battery when revving it at 5000rpm as per manual recommendations and I was getting 14 volts from a 12.5 volt battery so the alternator was working (phew!!)

I fitted the new battery and reconnected my CTEK charger cable, cleaned it out where it was filthy inside some connectors and the battery box and did it all back up. Voila, everything working perfectly. AND my clock was back working again...weird innit?!?!

Fired it up and did a ECU reset just for kicks...also had to warm it right up anyway to check oil n coolant. Topped it up, washed it lubed chain and it is ready to rock!!!
