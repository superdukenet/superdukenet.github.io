Post 1 by boney95, 05 May 2012, 20:04

#1
I've got a set of Brembo M4's on my SD. The calipers have two nipples each. One on the top of the banjo bolt (this one is not the problem), located on the inner half of the caliper, and a stand-alone nipple (the problem nipples) on the outer half of the caliper. Fluid weeps at the base of the nipples, so the fluid is coming up through the threads. I've torqued down both nipples pretty damn good, and I'm afraid I'll snap one off if I try to torque any further. I've spoken to the guy that I bought them from and he said that a crush washer is NOT used with these nipples, that I should try some thread sealant.

Thoughts on this? I'd hate to wreck these calipers...
DribbleDuke Forum member

Post 2 by DribbleDuke, 05 May 2012, 20:47

#2
Brake bleed nipples bottom out at closed position. They seal from compression at full closed. a replacement nipple is in order. Thread sealant is not a solution.
Google brake bleeder nipples and veiw images. All else is self explanatory.
