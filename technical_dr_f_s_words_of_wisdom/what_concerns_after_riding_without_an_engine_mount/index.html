I posted this question in another thread but I think it got lost in the posts.

I recently bought an 08 SD from a shop that had removed the frame sliders and forgot to replace the engine mount bolt used for the lower slider. I rode the bike twice in this condition, both very tame rides due to cold temps and road conditions. Is there anywhere in particular that I should be looking for damage, having had ridden the bike twice without this mount in place? As I look at the manual I can see that there are only three engine mounts; this one in question, the one at the swingarm pivot point and the support just a few inches forward of that.

I must be missing something because I can't see how the engine would stay in place while being ridden with only those low, rear mounts holding everything up!

I was out fiddling with bike yesterday and remembered your post about missing bolt so had a good look because I was not sure about how many mounting places there are, (and I thought I should know )

You're right, there are only three, the one you're missing, the rear swing arm pivot and a support bolt below the frame at the rear. So without the front on in the engine would want to pivot down at the front. So the real question is how much out of alignment was the hole through the engine to the front mounting bracket holes? If they are still in alignment then the engine has not mover and if they are nearly true then check for all connections as previously mentioned in your first post, (oil and coolant pipes) and I would also check the rubber connections from the airbox to the throttle bodies. But that answers your other question - there are so many other vital components attached to the engine that it is more or less held in place -together with the other two bolts there is very little chance of it moving too much in normal riding. Don't worry too much - just check everything - the best way to do this - and to get a feel for all the components that may be effected is while you are fitting a Motohooligan airbox - do this and you will be fine
