Keep the standard Brembo master cylinder.

Fit a dual brake line set up instead of the 1-2 T piece std design. Will give the same braking force with less pressure. ( At least HEL assures me this is the case)

Fill it with decent synthetic fluid like Motul 660 or Castrol SRF.
