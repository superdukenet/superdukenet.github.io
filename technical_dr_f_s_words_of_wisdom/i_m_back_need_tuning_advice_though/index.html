Hey all. Been a while since I was last on here. I've changed jobs and moved to a new place so that's kept me pretty busy lately. My bike will get here in a couple weeks and needed some advice on some issues I've had with it.

For those that remember, my bike's rear cylinder stopped firing for an unknown reason. Couldn't get the time to work on it/isolate it myself so I dropped it off at a shop. Long story short, it's fixed. Both cylinders fire now. However the very short test run I had with it before I had to leave it behind again didn't feel good. The bike now backfires...quite a lot. It starts and idles fine. But there's a good amount of popping that wasn't there before before on deceleration. Doesn't make me feel good, worried the bike might be running lean now. The shop that worked on it did a valve adjustment and claimed that fixed the rear cylinder. Don't really see how that would effect the tune that much. I have my O2 sensors blocked as well. Should I reinstall them for better feedback? Would O2 sensors and a new tune be my answer? Thanks as always.

#2
On the backfires.
This could be caused by an exhaust leak.
Look for the leak on the big "O" ring fiber seal before the "Y" going to the tail pipes.
You should be able to hear it, but if you have trouble, take a 2 foot length of tubing or lawn hose placed to the ear an where you think a leak might be.
