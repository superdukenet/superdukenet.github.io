From what I've read, the tanks are prone to distortion when removing and putting it back into place on the frame, just the tank. KTM Twins has brackets that should help. I just had my gas tank replaced because it leaked at fuel pump gasket too. KTM demanded, before replacing under warranty, to see the tank, it had warped at the fuel pump mounting area, so they replaced it. When they sent the replacement tank it had it's internal brace crushed in shipment and they had to send another. Just took it out yesterday, all seems well. It also pushed out this pinkish looking stuff when it went. Leaked all over the frame and stunk up the garage.
