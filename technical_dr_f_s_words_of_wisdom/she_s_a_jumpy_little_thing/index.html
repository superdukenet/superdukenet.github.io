Post 2 by ktmguy, 01 Apr 2010, 05:13

#2
It is a combination of things that are causing this which as a single term can be named emission regulations.
When you eliminate the O2 sensors with the plug in resistors it fixes the problem for the biggest part.
Throwing the original exhaust in the bin helps as do removing the secondary butterflies and balancing both cylinders.
Akra map and PCIII solve some more and it's smooth riding after that. Different throttle cams helps also, but masks the problem more than fixing it by changing the ratio of throttle movement and butterfly opening.

On my bike the biggest difference was the O2 plugs. (08 model)
A V-twin is always a bit more twitchy but my previous bike (aprilia RSV) was cream compared to the SD new. The above mods fixed it.
