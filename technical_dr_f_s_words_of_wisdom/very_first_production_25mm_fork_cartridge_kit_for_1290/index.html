Check it out, I got the very the first 25mm fork cartridge set produced by Barry Wressel of http://www.gpsuspension.com/ and http://www.kfgracing.com/ straight off the CNC machine today!!!

These cartridges have full preload adjustment and make changing out springs SUPER EASY! It only takes about 5 minutes to change a spring and you dont even need to pull the fork off the bike to do it.


They produce all their own parts in house and are the best race shop on the west coast hands down!!!


The final product will have black caps and orange adjusters on them but I was to impatient to wait for them to get back from the anodizing place so here is a sneek peek at them in raw form. The one on the left side of the bike with a black adjuster is the compression fork and the one on the right with red adjuster is the rebound fork. The caps will also have the GP logo, a part number, and either compression or rebound stamped in the cap as well. Something like this vvv
