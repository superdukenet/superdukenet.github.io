Ironlung,

I asked Meta the same question last year it took a while but I got the answer in the end:

Good afternoon,

Thank you for your e-mail. I have looked on the Thatcham Listing, (which your insurance company should have done) and the KTM is listed with the following product reference, E587TK (Thatcham No.: TMC1-196/1104). This product is not supplied by MetaSystem UK, presumably directly from Italy.

Please ask your insurance company to check these details, which are located on page 59 of the Thatcham listings.

Yes the self arming is immobilisor only, not the alarm. The loom connection has the immobilisor circuits 'broken' that are connected in order for the bike to run by either the disarmed alarm or the plug. Having the plug there made it really easy to find all the wiring required to fit a non-KTM alarm!!
