Nothing to it. Just take it slow for the first time and do it by the book. As far as capacity, remember 9nine0 from this site? Check his post at the end:



He worked for KTM and specifically with the LC8 engine. So, even if you don't have it topped off exact the first time around you have time with no threat to the engine to get it right.

Outside of that maybe some circa clip/snap ring pliers to remove the oil filter. I, very very gently, tapped a phillips head screw driver into the filter and then pulled it out. But that was a one time thing. I'm setup now for the next one. The arm portion of a binder clip (like used by desk jockeys in offices to hold reports together) might work if you get creative.

Newspaper and paper towels ready at hand. Have fun!
