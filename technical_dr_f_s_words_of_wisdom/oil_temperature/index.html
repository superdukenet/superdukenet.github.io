Hi mate. 120 C is fine for the oil.
Actually not that bad for race conditions, air cooled and turbocharged engines run much higher oil temps than that.
Up to 140c is fine with synthetic oil but it is best to avoid any higher or consider an oil cooler.

A better indication of trouble would be the oil pressure, if oil gets too hot the pressure drops which will result in all sorts of expensive repairs.
What too watch out for is sudden raise in temp, if you see that pull over as a bearing is about to give or so.
