One thing to be careful of with short levers on the clutch side is that some of them have a reduced lever throw compared with a long lever which means the clutch does not fully disengage causing clunky gear changes and wear/damage to the clutch and gearbox,
A short lever on the brake side also has disadvantages, you need more force on the lever due to the reduced leverage ratio and for trail braking you cant use your "pinky"........
yes with the clutch you can adjust them right out to get back the travel but that makes them awkward to use and reduces control if you have smaller hands
