As you some of you will be aware, I have had issues regarding the fueling of my bike, since May, KTM have inspected it this week, it's being returned in a much better state, and they have been very informative and helpfull about the issues, the jury is still out on the sub 4k fueling though, will do some miles this weekend.

What you need to be aware of:, The Akro Evo's are not a "KTM" recommended fitment (despite the web information), there is no map for that set up, our engines are different to 2007 models, the options for 2008 owners are quite simple:

1, Standard set up,
2, Race pipes, baffles out and race map, race only not for road use, (part 61005099000)

The race map would not suit the evo's, evo's are more restrictive, so technically, I am running the wrong map for my set up, until KTM release one
