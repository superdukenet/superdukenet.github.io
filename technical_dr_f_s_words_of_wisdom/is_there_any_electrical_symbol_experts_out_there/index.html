I haven't had a look at the diagram but I say they are the same. Picks up the metal from a screw or from a magnet, either way it has a square sinus signal. Only difference I know is 2 or 3 wire ones. 3 wire ones need power to work as they generally generate a magnetic field to pick up the screw head, 2 wire generally pick up a magnet so they don't need power. As I said generally as there are exceptions.
Surely KTM stuck with the same sensor.
