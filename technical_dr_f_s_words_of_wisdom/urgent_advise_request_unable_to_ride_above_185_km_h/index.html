#2
Possible loose or dimpled steering head bearings, worn wheel bearings, front tyre issues (balance, uneven wear, etc), suspension settings.

Steering damper will mask the symptoms, will not correct them. Fix underlying issues and then install steering damper if needed for pace/road conditions.

Good luck.

Hey Manuel,
I am a new Super Duke owner as well, my bike also did some weird stuff when I first got it. I set all the suspension settings to "basic" and it feels about perfect (I am 175 lbs.) If you don't have the manual, do a search and you will come up with the right number of clicks for the "basic" mode on rebound and compression. I suggest starting there, and make very small adjustments because the excellent suspension on these bikes is very responsive! Changing my settings by just a few clicks transformed the ride. There are a ton of threads on here about suspension set up, here's a good one: viewtopic.php?t=9160

Odelay!
