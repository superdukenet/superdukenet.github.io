Without going back over it......I had both mapped and on Dyno (Motorhooligan and then DNA mk3) DNA mk3 was/is better and the difference on my bike was 4hp at the top...and MUCH more grunt low down and mid range and on part throttle no comparison (Motorhooligan gave same results at 100% throttle as DNA mk3 did on 75% throttle Dyno pull...i have dyno sheets from Crescent to back this up )

Advise would be....if you had Motorhooligan keep it and buy spacer for the air box (if mrz32 sells them seperate)...if you were buying from new DNA mk3 or mk2 (as mrz32 results prove....DNA has the clearance required standard to cater for further tunning without limiting the motor) results from others say DNA mk1 is about the same as Motorhooligan and near the same money....

The only confussion in the thread was down to me...changing to 'R' headers at the same time as fitting DNA mk3 (BIG mistake on standard SD)

I bought my DNA3 direct.
As regards the DNA v the Motohooligan v, etc. I know some people did get quite excited in their defense / attack of one box or another.
All I can say with regards to the DNA3 i was personally satisfied that the DNA3 was worth the extra money.

I am hoping to realise the full benefits of the increased air flow capacity with the tuned motor.
So, in a word: recommended.
