For the 2nd time this winter i've pressed the button on the SD and been met by the 'click click click' of non-starting Superduke.

After the first time about 3 weeks ago we put it on charge for an hour and fired it up... ran up to temp for about 5-7 mins... then went to the bike today.... not a lot of life.... oh dear.

So, after the first one i had to get the bottom bellypan jobbie off, the side plate of the battery box.... but for the life of me i've not got a CLUE how i'd be able to get the battery out of it.... what's the trick ? I'm obviously missing something fairly simple... but due to it being on sidestand and a bit dark last time it wasn't easy to know the answer.

And the final question... what battery should i be replacing it with if i do swap? My usual battery of choice is a Yuasa... but i'm up for being persuaded on whatever is thought to be the 'best' out there for the SD.

Stick with a Yuasa on an optimate, Yuasa can be had for less than £50 on ebay as they are used in few bikes.

IIRC, belly pan off, take off the two screws move the wires to the side (cant recall if disconnecting one of the connectors helped) I believe the cover lifts up and out, messy job though.
