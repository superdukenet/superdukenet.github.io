With all the talk of problems owners are experiencing with their Dukes, I think there may be an impression building that this bike has some quality control problems. This may be a false impression since one of the reasons for these forums is to bring problems for group discussion and problem solving; a person with a problem is more likely to make a post than a person with a good running machine. To give a fair shake, lets find out what number of bikes have problems of manufacturing origin that have required more than one trip to the dealer for resolution OR have resulted in a failure of the machine on the road. The goal here is not to whip KTM, but maybe coalesce some group information on areas we need to pay attention to on our machines.
