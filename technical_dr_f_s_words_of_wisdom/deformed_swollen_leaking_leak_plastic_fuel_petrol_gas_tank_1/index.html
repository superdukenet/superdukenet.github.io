(Please excuse the title, I've tried to maximise the exposure of the thread with regards to Superduke.net & google/web indexing.)

I would appreciate as many individuals as possible to come forward and contact me regarding their superduke fuel tanks and any negative side effects being experienced. (From the UK only at the moment)

Please forgive me as I am not deliberately trying to exclude non UK owners – this will be made much clearer in the proceeding post that I will be submitting shortly.

Reports of any of the typical symptoms such as: leaking in general, expansion/swelling, captive insert failure, distortion, internal degradation, fuel pump leaks plus methods to reinstall tanks will be greatly received (pry bars, slacken rear mounts, cut down front mount rubber bushes etc).

Basically, any element that qualifies your dissatisfaction with the situation i.e., a fuel tank must remain dimensionally stable & must not be perishable in nature, please email me - I would very much like to hear from all SD & SDR owners. Please attach photos where applicable.

For those interested in knowing how many bikes in the UK could potentially be affected:



I would also like to know of any individuals who have challenged Bernie Tiller (KTM UK)/KTM Austria for an explanation as to why the tanks are proving to be defective in nature & the description of your account. You may have received a comment from KTM such as: “fuel tank may expand if removed when engine is hot” (I have received this twice already.) Or: “could be a blocked breather hose”.

Please contact me via jamiehastings@btinternet.com

Thanks in advance to those who take the time to respond.
