Hi and welcome to the forum

In the beginning I checked the level as instructed in the manual and constantly overfilled my SDR

The difference from the "min" to the "max" level is around 300ml.

The difference between a reading cold and hot is more than 300ml and the difference between hot just from idle and hot from a 1/2 hour ride is 1-200 ml. Thats the problem !

So if you fill up to max when its either cold or just hot from idling you will overfill !!

I therefore only fill it up to a little above the "min" mark after its hot from idling and then I check regulary after real riding.
