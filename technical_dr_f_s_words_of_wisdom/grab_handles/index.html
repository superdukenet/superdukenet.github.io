I am just testing the water to see if any one is interested in these .
I am ready to make a batch if there is enough interest.
They are made to suit Akros and i have not tried them on any other pipes ,I am willing to look into it.
My partner really rates them the only complaint is they are a little warm in summer.
They make pillioning a whole lot easier for me when the pace goes up and I overide the female speed limiting device .
They also give a little something extra to strap a seat bag onto.
Your feed back on pricing would be appreciated.
These are photos of the first made and is only spray painted ,they will be powder coated satin black when made.
They bolt into the sub-frame and take about 20 minutes to fit with no drilling required and all stainless fittings.
I am also about to look at making a mounting kit to take a Ventura rack and bag for us Akro loving touring wannabes.So rev me up enough and I wil get in the workshop and make a prototype.
