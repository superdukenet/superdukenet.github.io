MOD Done!!!

Yesterday I went to pick up my new SD-SDR at my bike wizard. Overall, the mod included:
-Fiting of complete 09 SDR front suspension with new radial Brembo calipers and both top and bottom yokes + Carbon Acra. front fender.
-Fiting of 09 SDR Rear shock.
-Painting of Swing Arm.

First Impressions (15 km to work..lol):
-Visual, not as important but absolutly amazing look.
-Breaking, very big diference, braking power increases significantly.
-Flicking of the bike much easier, the ground clearance has increased, bike sits much higher now and leans more on it´s stand.

Will post pics later on and opinions are off course very much welcome.
