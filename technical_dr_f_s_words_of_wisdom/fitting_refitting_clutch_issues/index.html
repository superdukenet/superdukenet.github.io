In your photo, there is waaaayyy too much of a gap between the last friction plate and the 2nd to last friction plate. I believe this is becasue the bolts have not been tightend up all the way or the metal between the last 2 friction plates is not installed correctly. The pressure plate bolts should be tightend up all the way until they bottom out pulling the pressure plate against the whole clutch pack (ie, no gaps between plates).
