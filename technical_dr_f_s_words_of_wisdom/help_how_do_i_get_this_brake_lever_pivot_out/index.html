Thanks guys. I did try that before posting with no luck, I dont have a vice and I was banging an 8mm socket into it while holding it down on the side of the bench. Not a winning method, in case anyone was wondering..
I just thought there might have been an easier way, seeing as the clutch was a 3 minute job.

Ended up putting a 14mm socket under it, and the 8mm socket on top and bashed it through on the floor.
Carefully is not my strongest suit. No real damage done apart from a few scuffs, and they weren't looking that flash anyway.

But no great loss, I just replaced them with some of those knock-off levers from ebay, they look and feel great. Easy fit, all the switches work as expected. I've ridden with a mates ASV levers before and theres not much in it quality and feel wise. Think i'll keep my stockies under the seat for a while, just in case..
