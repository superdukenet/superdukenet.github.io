I am adding a PCV with Autotune to my '07 SD and have also added the European head light and switch which means I can't use the rear light harness for power. What is the closest switched power source? I thought of using the fuel pump plug, but it seems to cycle on and off with the tank removed.
AGRO! Forum member

Post 2 by AGRO!, 09 Dec 2012, 00:00

#2
When you open the head light cover you will find on the right hand side this is facing bike from the front you will find 2 excessory leads.
One set has power on all the time and one set runs off the ignition so it's only on when the bike is turned on.
So connect to the leads that run off the ignition it's that easy.
By the way the Auto tune works great.
