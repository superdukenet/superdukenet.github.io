#4
In my opinion motorex is just overpriced and not better than any other good quality oil. On top of that it runs trough it like there is no tomorrow.
If you really want to be pampering the bike and money is no objection use Motul, I rate it better than Motorex.

Any good synthetic oil of the right grade and bike specific is fine.
So if the Hardley movingson dealers have that go for it.
I got good results with Elf, Total, Repsol, Silkolene etc.
You should be able to find Elf or Total in your region for a good price from an oil stockist as I find it always much more expensive from a bike shop.
I use Total full syntethic and in a 25 l container I paid $6 a litre last time straight from the importer in NZ. It is very good and nice green colour. At that price I rather change it an extra time than pay $60 for 4 L of motorex.
