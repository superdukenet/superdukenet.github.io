I always clean the chain before lubing it. I don't take into account the mileage since the last lube, the weather and dirt conditions are the main dictator, so the state of the chain tells you all you need to know.

Try Motorex chain cleaner, not cheap, does a great job and is good at cleaning other oily greasy things. (Smalls good too).
