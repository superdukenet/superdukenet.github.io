Re: installation of the Corbin seat for SD 990

No, not the replacement for the little hard pad on the SD990R, just the Corbin seat that replaces the regular dual seat on the the basic "real" Superduke...I am also not looking for opinions about style, opinions about the look of the Corbin, etc., etc...

I want just real technical info. from people who actually know something about the subject.

Changing seats seems to be a bit of a hobby for me..I have the original seat, the gel seat and the Corbin seat (I bought the last two in "as new" used condition for not much money)

Okay...so I get that you use the original lock from the factory seat, but I don't see how you are supposed to attach the cable to the latch...apparently you drill a hole in the latch? If so, where? And what position do you mount the key in?..and which position should the latch be in (locked and unlocked)? It has to pull the cable doesn't it?

None of this is explained on the Corbin website and their so-called "mounting instructions" skip over the installing of the original key/lock/latch. I wrote to them about this and they (Sergio) just replied to me with the same non-instructions from their website. When I phoned their tech support line the phone just kept ringing...no answer...of course it is the day after the Superbowl and everybody down there in the States probably has a hangover.
