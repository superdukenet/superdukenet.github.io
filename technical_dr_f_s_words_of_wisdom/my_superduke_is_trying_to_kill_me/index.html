That is the nature of the bike in stock form. All previous posters are right, do the up grades and the bike will run like a dream.

I have a smooth throttle hand from racing (not tooting my own horn, just making a point) and I HATED the on/off throttle response. Trying to crack open the throttle in a corner was nearly impossible without the herky jerky of the bike.

Here's a list of mods that helped my bike, and to what degree.


1 - PCIII w/ oxygen sensor removers and shelf map = slightly better throttle response with more power.

2- Yoshimura exhaust cans with custom map (map from another bike) = even better throttle response, but not perfect.

3. SAS block off caps with "can in the arse" removal. = This mod really shouldnt effect performance, but seemed to be better. It could be entirely physcological. Did stop the bike from back firing on decel.

4. Balancing of throttle bodies = better throttle response and the surging of the motor while at a constant RPM was eliminated.

5. MotoHooligan Box w/ secondary butterflies + custom map (again a map from another bike) = Nearly perfect throttle response, no herky jerky, and I can crack on the gas in a corner anywhere I feel fit.

Good news.

After reading more about the engine reset it appears I've been doing it wrong. The problem was that I was leaving it for over 16 minutes. I have since managed to reset the engine correctly on my bike and was staggered by the results.

I also adjusted the slack on my throttle to allow for a marginal increase in 'play'.

The result of both adjustments on top of the mods I’ve made (G2 throttle with Cam300 and O2 sensor eliminators) to the bike has completely changed the low end riding characteristics of the bike. I am very pleased with the outcome and would recommend the mod to anyone who is borderline about whether they should do it.

Only down side – I‘m still getting some pretty bad back-fires during heavy deceleration, but I am removing the SAS gumph asap to deal with that. Does anyone know how to do that?
