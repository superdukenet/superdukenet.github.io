Something I picked up yesterday is that - contrary to KTM spec - the clutch mineral fluid should be changed often, like with every oil change. So you get rid of dirt particles asap, to minimise the slave piston scoring and wear, and the o-ring wearing.
The OEM part is crap, obviously. - That pot metal alloy piston.
