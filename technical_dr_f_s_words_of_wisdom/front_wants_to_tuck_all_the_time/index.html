I've been struggling for a while on this one and haven't come to any great conclusions.
My SD really steers wierdly. Whenever leaning into a corner the front 'really' wants to fold itself into itself. If I'm leaning/turning left, for example, the force on the left hand handle-bar is quite strong. It takes a lot of counter-steering to get the thing round the corner. All of my other bikes, past and present, are a lot more neutral, and I'm starting to wonder if something is amiss.

I rode a mates SD a few months back and it steered and rode sooooo smoothly and neutrally I nearly didn't hand it back. Why isn't mine the same? I did chuck it down the road once but it was declared fit and ready (by TriCounty). But somehow it never felt quite the same. I'd only had the bike 11-days, and it was off the road for almost 3-months, so I put the handling down to 'I must have forgotten what it feels like'. Now, after riding another SD, I'm wondering if something is major league wrong with it, they are that far apart!?

It's due a service within a few hundred miles - should I check some obvious stuff first or throw it at a [different] dealer to get it checked over?
