I have an LSL damper with mounting kit to fit the "07 onwards bikes. It's an above the top yoke set up so it's visible - not hidden under the yokes like the SDR set up. I had it fitted for about a year but have recently swapped it for DRSwade's ex race Scotts unit, (which is better but probably three times the price when new).
