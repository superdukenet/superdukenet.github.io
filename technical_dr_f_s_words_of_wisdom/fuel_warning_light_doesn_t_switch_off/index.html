ok guys, I fixed it! The ballast from the HID was pushing against the connector in the headlight unit. A wire was crushed, so with some electric tape I solved the issue. The ballast is now at the other side of the unit, away from the connector

Unfortunately two wires are now damaged, but I hope they won't cause any further problems...
