I have an 05 model with Graves pipes and the Akra map on the factory ECU. Mine was popping and banging beyond belief and to be honest I soon got over the novelty of it. I removed the SAS system by taking off the factory plates with hoses, I fitted a pair of SAS block off plates from here.



I left the SAS valve in place on the rear of the airbox and still connected electrically, I removed al the hoses from it plugged the airbox outlet with black silicone. One of these days I may get around to disconnecting it to see if doing so gives me an Fi light but at this point in time i can't be bothered and I'm not Rossi so the extra weight won't hurt.

I also put in a BMC air filter filter while I had her all apart...and my bike has never run so good [/url]

#4
A friends who is a mechanic and whom I bought the SD off came to my rescue as I had the exact same problem. It was backfiring when changing up gears and everything.

He told me to replace the gasket/seal that fits in between the short rear header pipe and main pipe that joins directly at the swingarm. It is a circular 1 inch wide collar type gasket.

Once we replaced this I had ZERO popping and banging
