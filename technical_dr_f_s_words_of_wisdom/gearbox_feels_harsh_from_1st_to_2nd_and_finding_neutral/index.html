#5
I have an '07 990 and the gearbox is not the slickest in the world. As mentioned above the slave cylinder is prone to failure and most long term owners fit an after-market unit like an Oberon one.

Mine is often tricky to get into neutral but only if you are stationery and messing about at lights. Blipping the throttle as you shift the lever helps. I tend to snick into neutral just before I stop, (down from second) and there is never a problem. The gearbox will always clunk harshly when going from neutral into first, this is normal.

#10
After you bleed and/or flush/fill the MC (or RnR/replace the salve ...), adjust your lever, clean/replace/open-up the oil jet, do an oil/filter change, etc. - you are still likely to find it "clunky".

Just how the 990 LC8's transmissions are: If you were to search the site, you'd find no shortage of bitching and moaning about this.

FWIW - mine UPshifts much more smoothy when I don't use the clutch (roll off the throttle just long enough to unload the shift dogs and transmission) - in all gears. Of course, like anything, to do this well, it requires some practice ... At your own risk. Do this for enough years, and you no longer don't think about using the lever for upshifts.
