I'm going to rewire my indicators /turn signals + licence plate light this week. I had decided to use the Rizoma mainly because I hate the KTM licence plate light. Although the Rizoma thing is a collection of bits and pieces and nuts and bolts, and the KTM fender is good, the way it hides the wires and connectors. The problem is I ended up with a bunch of 4 spade connectors and the big square resistor thing for the Rizoma light, and I covered it with 25mm heatshrink tube, and it looked shit. So last night I decided ok, never mind trying to keep the KTM wiring so the original parts can go back, I'm going to extend the wires of the mini indicators so they run up into the underseat tray and have the connectors there, to connect to the KTM 4-wire connector.

oh, and this only because I got a smallerz - illegal - number plate. Our legal ones are exactly the same size as yours, I think. The one I had made is 170x150. I used the smallest of the Rizoma plates, it's about 160mm wide. The brackets for the indicators, in as far as they go and with the angle down - I think they're far enough from the exhausts.
