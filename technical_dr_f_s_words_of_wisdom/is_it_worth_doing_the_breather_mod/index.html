Little 1L Evian one works best.

I mounted mine just infront of airbox behing headstock/ecu. Tape lid on. Cut hole for breather pipe 3/4 up. Drill lots of holes round top. That way any vapour gets sucked towards filter and doesnt get underside of tank area all oily. And you can look up underneath every seassion and see if its filling up or not. Mine only went a bit mad when balance shaft seal went.

#11
Still running the check valve or PCV, it stops the gasses from returning to the crankcase, keeping negative pressure.
It should be moved as close to the crank as possible to limit up losses from the resistance of the surface area of the pipe as the air rushes past it.
tripoddave Forum member

Post 12 by tripoddave, 22 Apr 2013, 02:41

#12
For what it's worth:
Dynoing with and without the crank case breather back to back on the same dyno, same day (about an hour apart) - no appreciable difference.
Venting the cam covers - as above and, again, no appreciable difference.
We know that there are pumping losses due to the small size of the crank case but I now know the only way to fix this is to make the crank cases bigger. Adding breathers doesn't help in this instance because of stuff I don't understand around harmonics and volumes apparently.
