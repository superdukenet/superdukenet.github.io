Right from the start i thought the bike was unstable at high speeds due to its nature, naked and and all. I suspected the only way to counter this wiggle would be a steering damper. On this forum it seems not many have this problem. I am 180cm tall and weigh 80kg if that is of any relevance.

1. I can always slightly hear the brake pads scrub a bit (as they should) but the weird thing is that the sound is a bit louder at a point in each tyre revolution. Yeah i know you have to listen hard to hear it; i usually switch the bike off down a hill at night to hear it.

2. Changed my tyres and the front wheel needed approx 65 grams of weights on the one side and 25g on the other, also not being at the same spot. I had the guy do it twice but it came up the same both times. To the eye it looked straight but it cant be right?

Is there any way i can measure to see if the wheel isnt 100% straight? Ive never dropped the bike or anything but who knows what happened in the dealership...

Any advice is greatly appreciated!!! Yeah yeah i know, only use the back wheel seriously though...
