I really don't know why people keep buying PCIII or PCV for the SD?!???!

Tuneecu is free and allows you to adjust also the ignition tables and is a diagnostic tool. It can import PC tables so if you had a custom map done before you can just import that. It also avoid installing extra's on the bike that could go wrong or need need installing if your not competent.

Do yourself a favour, get the tuneecu cable and upload a suitable map for your bike that you can download from the how to section. Plenty of members here to point you in the right direction depending on year and country of your SD.

For your can and if it is an 08 model try guy's or james map, for 05-06 try the clincks map.

Did I mention the software is free.....
