I took my 2007 SD out for spin yesterday, about an hour ride, easy pace. Today I noticed a drip puddle under SD, and discovered that it is coming from where the hose from the clutch fluid reservoir enters the clutch lever bracket. The plastic fitting and the O-ring are loose and can be pulled away from the bracket. I never dropped the bike and hit anything with it. There is no damage visible, no signs of impact from an object. Can't imagine how this happened, nor how to fix it. (I'm an hour away from my KTM dealer.) Any suggestions on how to get that O-ring back in there? How does one bleed a clutch line (I'm guessing by the time I'm done, there will be air in the clutch line.

First picture below is the leaky clutch bracket. You can see how the O-ring is no longer inside the metal bracket.


Second, for comparison is the brake lever with a properly sealed plastic fitting and the O-ring inside its metal bracket.
