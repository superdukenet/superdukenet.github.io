How much oil did you put in and when do oyu check the oil? It takes 3l and a bit depending on filter and how much you drained, and the engine needs to be hot and the bike upright when checking the oil.
Did you check the valve on the oil reservoir tank outlet after you emptied the tank (ball and spring)? If that leaks the oil goes in the engine after you stop it.
Not bleeding the coolant circuit properly after draining it is not a good idea. It might make the engine overheat at the heads or so, head gasket?!
