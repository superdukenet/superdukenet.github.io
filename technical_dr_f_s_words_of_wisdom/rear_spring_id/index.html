Post 5 by SDNerd, 20 Mar 2012, 17:53

#5
I believe that KTM calls out 59 mm ID. Over here, the aftermarket rear springs are usually 2.25" ID (~57 mm), in 7" length. They fit the OEM SD perches - no problem. Note that 7" is slightly shorter than the OEM springs - but will not effect anything. Also there is plenty of thread length on the shock body for a 6" spring if you prefer that.

In Oz, should be pretty easy to find vendors of either Hyperco or Eibach. Hyperco's catalog list is better in this size. Don't limit yourself to moto shops.

220 Lbs., presumably w/o gear (and you should set sag with full gear BTW) - I'd go to at least an 1100 Lb./in. spring ...

I'm inclined to throw in that you're not going to be super pleased with that change without getting correct front springs also.
