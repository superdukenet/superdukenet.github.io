They are from Brake Tech

Actually, I contacted them in August, so it's only been 4 months of waiting. At first they said that they weren't available for the SD, but I found a cross application and then confirmed it with dimensions of the stock rotor. Until I get them on the bike, I guess I won't really know for sure, but they look right when I put them against the wheel. But then I had to wait until the factory made the right thickness material. At the beginning of October they said that the rotors were in but after I ordered them they told me the carriers were gold. I thought that would look kinda ghetto, so I waited another month for black carriers. I ordered them through my local accessory shop and the bill came out to about $2300 US, including new pads and tax (8.25%). Just rotors will be about $1000 US each. I talked to the Brake Tech guys at the motorcycle show and the rep said that any quality brake pad is good, so I guess I didn't need to get the ceramic pads too.
