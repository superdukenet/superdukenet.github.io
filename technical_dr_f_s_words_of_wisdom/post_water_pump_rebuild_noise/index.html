If the oil and filter hasn't been done then the filter will be catching the water that's in the oil. If it's caught oil then it won't flow oil = low flow and pressure. Does the oil light stay on longer when started from cold. Pull the oil filter out and see if it's is water damaged. If it is I would change the filter and not the oil. Run the same oil until your sure the water is out then replace.
