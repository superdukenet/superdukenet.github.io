Hi there, let me explain the issue
1. while connected to the TuneECU with engine off i notice that the TPS reading on some occasion will show 0.00v reading when you twist the throttle and release it back to 0% and it will show the P0122 error code. usually, i will turn the key off and on again or twist the throttle to 100% and release it again and with some luck the reading will go back to 0.64v on 0%.

2. i did the idle steeper motor test on the TuneECU and notice that the TPS voltage fluctuate wildly on 0% while the test is running. my theory is that any voltage fluctuation will affect the TPS reading. there have been cases where the idle go crazy if the radiator fan switch on but its not constant. could not replicate it all the time.

3. on some occasion when i press the starter button nothing happens and the FI light will flashes 6 short blip. P0122. i need to turn the key off and on again and the bike will start fine

So now what could be the cause?
1. Faulty battery? i did get the Soft 32 - Dat 101 last time when the idle issue is mild and rarely happen
2. Faulty Rectifier?
3. Faulty TPS?
4. Short somewhere?
