#2
It's a negative.
In a normal 2 wire coil it gets a positive all the time and a negative to charge the coil, the negative gets then "cut" abruptly which creates a spark on the outlet (plug).
This is the same with the exception that there is another negative which helps to develop a higher voltage spark.

Keep in mind that the ecu employs some tricks to make the spark more effective. A bit like what the cap and so used to do in the old point and coil systems.

In other words you can't just replace that wire with a electronic relay or something and expect it to work fine.
