#11
I secretly (well not anymore) entertain the idea of an RC8 engine in my SDR. Now think about that 180bhp 2009 model in a SuperDuke... wheelie mayhem!

Let's for a second assume that the engine can be slotted into the frame without too much modifications to the frame and/or engine. Let's say you have to machine some mounting points down, make some adapter plates etc. But no changing of the frame (chrome moly isn't easy to play with!). So you have the engine in.
This is where the annoying problems come into play. First you need to connect it all up. Dry sump, coolant, all the sensors for fuel injection etc. As mentioned you'll also need to make the airbox fit and naturally the exhausts.
Let's now say you've managed to connect it all up, now you might be faced with "silly stuff", like that the radiator isn't capable of cooling the engine, or that the SD carries less oil in the SD oil tank and therefore you might need a bigger oil tank and/or an oil-cooler. And so forth...

I would love to do it, but without a spare SD and a spare RC8, and/or tons of support from a dealer for those little parts you might need to get from one or the other to make it ift... it's a no-goer. Not to mention how much money would be sunk into it.

But just think of it! A one-to-one ratio of bhp to kg, SuperDuke!
