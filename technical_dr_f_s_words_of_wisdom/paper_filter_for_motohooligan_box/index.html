The only problem with K&N filters and sensors is if you over oil the filter and it spills on the hotwire airsensor (platinum wire) on fuel injected cars.
The SD doesn't have that but it will still not like too much oil.
Otherwise K&N filters are great, used dozens of them and never had a problem. Use the K&N cleaner and oil and it last for ages.
