Are they the standard units? If they are LEDs they will probably need a resistor in parallel each side to make them flash properly.

If they are the standard ones then it sounds like the regulator might be buggered. Does your headlamp get brighter when you rev the motor?
madagi Forum member Topic author

Post 3 by madagi, 29 Apr 2010, 08:55

#3
I had this and replaced both regulator and battery - not sure which was the problem as both checked out OK.

If your due a battery eg more than 3 years old I would go for that first and see what happens. Go to a Honda dealer for this as they are less than half the price KTM charge and its exactly the same battery.

If you still have trouble then it's the regulator.
