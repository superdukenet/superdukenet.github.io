Like a few others, I've noticed a low coolant level in the overflow. It seems to fluctuate at times. Right now, the overflow level is just over the min line...other times, it's at the level seen below. A couple of times after long-ish rides in warm weather I've noticed a bit of puddle on the right side. I don't see any indication of a leak from the water pump, the left side hose seems to be clamped tight enough, and the radiator cap is free of debris and fuzz. I had a 9k mi service done at about 10k miles, but the invoice doesn't indicate that the water pump seal was replaced (per some posters remark that there's a KTM bulletin regarding this interval replacement).
