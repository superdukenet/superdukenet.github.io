Joe, the trumpets are speaker breather ports I got from a company called Jaycar here in Oz and cost $5 each, but there was alot of rooting around to graft them to the plate the original snorkel fits into. I had the snorkel out altogether for a while but after reading a couple of posts on here it looked like you are losing about 2-3hp by not having it.
