Hi Guys,
So mid- ride yesterday i noticed a bunch of nasty looking "water" coming from the pump return hose. a bit of troubleshooting and it seems to be just a small split in the hose between the radiator and the engine (just above the crash bobbin in the photo below) & it only leaks when under a bit of pressure when the thermostat is closed.
however the murky brown colour and kinda greasy texture of the leak has me a bit worried.
My next check was the bikes oil, which thankfully isn't cloudy or showing any signs of contamination.
I've never had any cooling system issues with this particular bike, (my old 05 SD was another story) and as a result i've never flushed the cooling system or even needed to top-up the coolant on this one in the 12ish months i've owned it.
Next weekend i plan on flushing the system out & fixing the hose, but should i be worried about the amount of brown greasy gunk in this "water" - is this just a careless previous owner topping it up with plain old tap water and causing corrosion in the system?

Thoughts? Comments? Abuse?

To be honest this shows us nothing. Some coolants dry out different colours than others.
I have seen green, red, blue, silver, white...
The only way to determine is the coolant has a funny colour and or oil contamination is to look at the liquid itself.

On the other hand the spill looks suspiciously like water only which could explain the rust and gunk in the system.
What it does is rust the metal pipe where it is in contact with the aluminium of the engine (on the photo where the leak is and the hose is connected to the water pump housing) due to water having no anti rust properties.
Coolant is a rust inhibitor that stops thing corroding and running an aluminium engine on water only is asking for trouble.

My suggestion, drain and undo that pipe, inspect for damage and fix the leak.
Check air filter for oil contamination, if there is you might need a water pump repair kit, fit that if needed.
Fill with water to check for leaks and to give it a flush. you can start the bike but don't ride it as it will overheat and boil on water alone!
Flush properly and after that fill with the proper coolant mix.
