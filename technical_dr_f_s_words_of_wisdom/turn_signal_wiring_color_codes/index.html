I'm about to install some Rizoma indicators on the SD and wanted to check/verify the color codes for the stock wiring. The stock set up uses brown and purple wires, while the Rizoma uses traditional (at least here in the states) black and red. I did a bit of websearch and found this:

IEC DC: Pos - brown, Neg - grey
US DC: Pos - red, Neg - black

So does that look right? Just wire brown to red, and grey to black?

I believe you have it right.

On that note, are these Rizomas LED?

The SD flasher relay: anyone know if that's load dependent or digitally controlled - the schematic on P13-48 indicated a digital symbol, but I've learned not to trust all markings on moto/auto wiring diagrams and schematics.

I mention this only for the possible need for ballast resistors. Rizoma should note in their instructions whether these may be needed or not.

Easy way to tell if the SD flasher is digital - disconnect one of the turn stock signals, and see if the rate of flashing is different for the side with the disconnected one versus the side with both turn signals still connected.
