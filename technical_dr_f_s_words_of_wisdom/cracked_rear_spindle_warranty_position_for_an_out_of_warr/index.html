You are correct cat, however it was referred to as a spindle, as in the KTM Chassis Parts Catalogue, it is known as part no: 60010081044 - Wheel Spindle Rear Cpl.

I had this same problem about 9 months ago, and when I approached my KTM dealer, was told they'd never seen it before, so they took photos and referred to KTM Australia. I was able to get the new rear axle, and attached chain adjuster (all came as one part) for zero cost.

I thought my problem was isolated, and I had not overtightned the rear wheel nut myself (had been done at the dealer previously to this). This is quite a serious failure I believe and I'm quite curious to see how many people this has occurred to.
