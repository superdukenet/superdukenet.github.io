So, I went to install the HM Quickshifter I had just bought for my 2009 SuperDuke R. However, the version that's available on the market - the HM Quickshifter Plus KTM - as it turned out will NOT work with my R. The reason being, apparently, that any R from 2008 and forward has Beru ignition coils. These do not work with the HM QS. If you use a PC version, however, it shouldn't be a problem.

The guy I bought the QS from should be talking to HM as I write. I'll keep you updated.
