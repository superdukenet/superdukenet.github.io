#4
Mate, the unit comes with a replacement under tray and there are differences between the '07+ and earlier model kits. When I converted my '07 to a single seat I nearly bought a second hand early kit to fit but then decided that it might not be worth the hassle and held out until I found a later one. The main differences are the way the fuse box fits but there may be many other minor ones. I know people on the forum say it's possible and that with a bit of messing about you could get it to work.

When you get a new kit there are a LOT of bolts and nuts and fixings that come with it. They send you a multiple bag of everything so you end up with a lot left over. Try and make sure that the seller gives you all the bits. The instructions list all the bits together with their KTM part numbers. If you have any missing let me know because I still have all my left over fixings and could send you anything you're missing. I might have the fitting instructions too if you end up needing a copy.
