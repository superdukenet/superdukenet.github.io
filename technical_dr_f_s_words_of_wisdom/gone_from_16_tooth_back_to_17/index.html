After going away on my 05 SD last weekend and doing a fair bit of freeway riding to get to the good roads I found the 16 tooth front and standard rear sprocket to be a bit low geared, I put the stock 17 tooth back on yesterday and was amazed by the difference, still pulls like a train but is a much more relaxed ride at freeway speeds.

I was worried about it being a rather snatchy pig of a thing around town like it was when I fisrt bought the bike and it was stock but it seems to be fine, obviously I have to slip the clutch a little bit more around town but It seeam to run alot better than when I first got the bike, although I'm sure the Graves and Akra map have helped alot.

Anybody else gone back to a 17 tooth front?
