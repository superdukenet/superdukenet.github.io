Airbox for lc8 - jersen

Hi Everybody,

After months of design, manufacturing and development, I can suggest a full composite (carbon-free for the price) airbox for all LC8 engines !

Kit contents:
Airbox with K&N filter (true) mounted and oiled
Airbox sealing
Intake trumpets sealing
Special snorkel
Custom mapping

About screws, you just have to take the original ones and add 2 M6 rings just for the airbox, not for the snorkel.

plug & play installation: 20 minutes

advantages:
power gain
torque gain
conservation of the lower part of the original airbox to keep the fuel rail dry and dust free
easy cleaning of the K & N filter
intake sound........
price

** All materials fully comply with the specifications of an original airbox in terms of chemical resistance to oils and fuels and mechanical strength as cold and warm operating temperature **
