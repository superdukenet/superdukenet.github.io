Hi Mate,

There's no way that the forks will twist in the clamps by 3-4 cm but it's good idea to put the bike on a stand and loosen all the clamp bolts on as suggested. The forks will not be bent but they might realign accurately again. Be careful because as you loosen the bolts the forks can just slip out. It's sensible to mark the tops of the forks where they come out of the top triple clamp to ensure that they are in the same place when you tighten them up.

What you describe is far more likely to be the headlight unit being knocked out of it's mounting points or bending something. It's relatively easy to dissemble the whole unit. I would guess if you did that, when you put it back together again it will be straight and true.
