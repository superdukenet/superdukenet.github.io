ktmguy Forum member

Post 2 by ktmguy, 15 Jul 2012, 03:03

#2
If it does it only on the left hand side it's not the relay. The relay works both sides the same.
Check when it does it if both the front and rear are still flashing, I suspect a bad connection or corrosion at the socket or the bulbs itself.
If not, you have a switch or wiring problem.
The increased speed when flashing is when the resistance goes up, like when a bulb goes faulty or you remove one. Look for bad contacts like corrosion, bad negative connections to the frame and so on.
Hope this helps.

I've had something similiar on another bike... Regular bulbs.
Turned out the element in the bulb was broken but would touch and make a normal contact only sometimes - when the revs were at a certain speed etc.

I changed out both bulbs on the bad side and it worked fine.
