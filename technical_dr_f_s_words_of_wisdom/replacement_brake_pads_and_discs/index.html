Have tried a few non-OEM discs and none as good as or lasted as long as OEM - pads EBC HH or DP SPD, latter as good as EBC and don't appear to create as much dust or wear discs as EBC but don't last as long - just had one pop out today, though, albeit with only circa 1mm friction material left.
