That's a damn good question. The cheapie thing that comes on SDRs ain't great. The really expensive WP "race" version via PowerParts is decent - but as you point out, spen-dy.

My 2 cents: get a Scotts. On the shelf item; trivial bolt on; works great. Easier to adjust on the fly IMO. May cost a little more than some Ohlins models, and a little less than others. Easily/readily rebuildable, and with (probably) a different mount kit, good chance you can put it on your next bike.
