Hi

I have a 2011 KTM 990 Super Duke R with 8200kms on the clock

I filled up at BP on Wednesday as the fuel price went up, put fuel in at BP (unleaded) and went home and parked the bike for 2 hours, i went back to the bike to go down to the shop and as i started the bike it made a loud backfire and sounded as if it was running on one cylinder, there was no power and sounded like a very sick bike. To pull away i had to drag the clutch and give it alot of revs if not the bike would cut out and i would struggle to start the bike. The bike had no power and would struggle to ride. I drove it back home and called my mechanic and he replaced the Front and Rear plugs but that didn't solve the problem, there was a very strong smell of fuel and even before i had the problem i could always smell a strong aroma of fuel as if it was running rich. When you start the bike the Front Header by the cylinder is very hot but the rear is room temperature and not even warm, its running on one cylinder.

Has anyone had the same problem
Please help
