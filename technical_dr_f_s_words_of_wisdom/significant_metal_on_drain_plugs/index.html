Hi all,

i have a 07 SD which now has 16,000km. i bought it with 9500km on it and changed the oil at around 11,000km. no metals to be seen.
was changing the oil today and noticed alot of metal shavings on the oil tank drain bolt, and on the engine drain bolt. enough to make me worried anyway.
bike had done 5,000km since previous oil change, only street useage (but hard riding, went through 4 sets of rubber to 1 oil change ) and using motorex crosspower 10w/50 oil (as per the oil change previously). bike had used approx 400mL of oil over the 5,000km. i am worried about the oil usage.
both the screens were perfectly clean, i washed the oil filter out and it wasnt too bad and there was no significant metals in the oil to be seen. it was all obviously magnetic

Stupid me forgot to take photos of the drain plugs but the drain plugs in this photo look similar to what i had built up. similar size metal bits and maybe even a touch more on my plugs

i have now filled the bike back up with shell ax7 15w/50 and will run it for 1000km and change the oil again to look for metal. half will be hard riding in the hills and half will be highway cruising to motogp next weekend.

how much metal do these bikes move around? i thought they were normally fairly clean. bike seems to be running fine otherwise, no strange noises, making good HP. starts easy as.

not sure where to start, i think changing the oil after a good run will give me a good indication whats going on but what goes wrong with these things? i might check the valves in 1000km when i drop the oil again.

help??

If screens and filter are clean I would not worry. However you could change the oil a bit more often, let say - 1 set of tyres = 1 oil change. Or 2 set of tyres = 1 oil change. But not 4 set of tyres per change However you use it on the street its technically a "race use" and KTM suggest an oil change after each race weekend. About oil consumption some other member might add an opinion, but it varies from bike to bike and your consumption should be in factory tolerance.

I do not understand how it is possible to wear off 4 set of tyres in 5000km, what exactly your tyres and what you are doing with your bike ?
