OK dudes, help, advice or piss taking needed.

Guess if I had thought about would have known from the outset that my tank mounted WP damper would not fit the larger tank.

It turns ok if you steer to the right but to the left the damper and fitting bracket foul the tank. I cant take the steering bolts in any more TBH.

Also the fitment to the right where the damper hits the tank is now to high? Hmm was not expecting that one.

I have 4 options.
1. Dont bother with a damper (but I do need one so thats not feesable)
2. Adapt both mounting brackets
3. Mount it under the headstock like 07 bikes on
4. Buy a 07 specific one.

Here are some pics.



You can see how the the angked part fouls almost instantly and the mount will touch the tank on full lock, so will need a new piece desighning with a rose joint.
