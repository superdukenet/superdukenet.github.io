That bike is a superduke "battle bike" I think there are only 50 in the UK, basically KTM's way of getting rid of excess stock, its an 05-06 bike which came with single seat/akras and a carbon front mask as standard, the mask is ridiculously expensive. The 07> mask is smaller.

http://www.bikersjersey.com/product/pro ... x?id=36060
