Out on a nice ride out today, on way home my clutch was feeling a little strange and not returning fully on the way down the gears.

I noticed my reservoir fluid levels were dropping... Uh oh... We have a problem.

Stopped at a garage... i've got a fluid leak somewhere down by the slave cylinder... Crap and double crap.

Topped up the fluid and tried to bleed it to get some pressure in the lever but it wasn't playing at all..

Managed to get it engaged enough to fire it up and bimble home in 2nd for the last 12 miles or so.

Just waiting for a price from Premier bikes on a seal kit. Hopefully will just be the seal and not a new slave cylinder. If it is the slave, i'll be buying an uprated one for certain.
