Yesterday seemed like a normal start to any riding weekend. Checked the sight tube, oil is nice and amber and at the right level, chain slack is good, brakes are good, tyre pressures are good, plenty of water in the radiator.

I ride ~ 15 miles when I noticed that the engine is making a little more noise than usual. Okay, a lot more noise than usual. It sounded like a rattle coming from one of the cylinders. So we pull over, and I notice an oil leak down the left side of the bike. Not major, but there's residue all over the cover on that side. Went to a nearby hardware store to get a crescent wrench and a new hose clamp. I removed the OEM clamp, pulled a hose off, and this cream colored mayonnaise-looking shit started dripping out. I also noticed it on the sight tube.

The bike only has 8,100mi on it, all mine, and I'm the first owner. The bike has been meticulously maintained by the dealer since I bought it in July of 09. I'm assuming that this the result of the water pump seal going to hell on me. I won't be able to tear into the bike until Tuesday night or so, but I figured I would try to get some insight.



Anything I should be doing first? Anything I should NOT be doing? (Other than turning it on. I had to call and have the bike trailered back home after I discovered the mayo.)
