Today there was a tyre heat debate at the local moto watering hole (I hope this is the right place for this topic).

Using tyre warmers on a relatively short track 3km (2nd and 3rd most of the track), vs. not using them on a COLD day 7 degrees celsius (44 deg F).

I said that I couldn't heat up my Michelin PP One tyres beyond 50 deg C and I saw that the guys with the warmers were starting at 80 deg C.

Somebody said that their tyres were losing heat at the same rate at which mine were creating heat and that at the end of a few laps, we'd be pretty much the same.

I don't think I agree at all (considering I actually saw some hot tears in one of the tyres that had heaters on them).

Does anyone have experience comparing tyre heat on cold days w/warmers vs w/o warmers? Shouldn't the ones with warmers basically maintain their heat throughout the session?

Thanking you kindly for clearing up this technical question...

Unless your racing, or in the advanced group and feel the urge to push it in the first lap, I don't see the point of tyre warmers. I'll take it slow until they are up to temp. Especially on cold days they give you a false sense of confidence.

I'd rather save the money of tyre warmers, gas, and a generator. Just my .02 from an occasional intermediate rider.
