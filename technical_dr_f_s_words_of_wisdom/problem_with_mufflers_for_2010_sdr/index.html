Hi All,

I have a 2010 Super Duke R, which at the moment is stock with the Akrapovic headers and heavy CAT / muffler setup. I have searched the forums and online elsewhere, and have not been able to find a definitive answer to my question.

I am wanting to get a new muffler setup, and am looking at a Remus set secondhand, that came off a 2009 Super Duke. I rang the local Remus rep who was adamant that the 2009 mufflers and Y pipe will not fit on my 2010 bike, but could not tell me why! (I actually thing he was a bit pissed I was buying secondhand instead of new!)

The model year comparison sticky post doesn't really say there is any difference in the exhaust, so I am at a loss to know why the Remus has a diffent part for 2010 up bikes compared to 2009.

Anyone able to shed any light on this?

The ealier SDR had the same pipes as SD, the later (2007+) had significantly different shaped big bore down pipes.......but they all fit all SD/SDR

The only major difference is that the full AKRA system takes different silencers than the half systems (They join in a different place)
