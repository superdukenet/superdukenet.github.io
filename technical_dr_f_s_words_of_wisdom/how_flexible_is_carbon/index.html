Jermo Forum member

Post 2 by Jermo, 21 Apr 2012, 12:10

#2
Good quality CF can bend pretty far. Only the resin is the problem with cheap CF, that will crack especially when applied too thick.
In my experience Akra CF is from good quality with a good resin compared to other brands.

Also, good quality CF will make a cracking noise when bend, this is normal and doesn't mean it's broken so don't panic
