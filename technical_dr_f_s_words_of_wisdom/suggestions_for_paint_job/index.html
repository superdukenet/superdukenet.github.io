Why paint them? You can use vinyl very effectively to add very trick effects to your plastics.

I've sone this with loads of dirt bikes. It costs less than paint and you can take it off to put the pannels back to stock.

I put a white stripe (like the Ducati Monster) through my Black SD but removed it after a while cos I got fed up telling people how and why I did it. BTW. I don't have photos.
