Doesnt need to overheat for it to be a coolant leak. If a hose is cracked it will still do that. It may not show up when you stopped but with the system under pressure when the motors running it could start leaking. Remember coolant / water mix is still a bit greasy and its coming from the area where the coolant reservior is. The fluid looks too thin to be oil. Should be an easy fix, they usually look worse than they are.
