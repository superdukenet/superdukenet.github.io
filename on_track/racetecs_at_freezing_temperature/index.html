Post 2 by Stupid Luke, 21 Mar 2011, 22:42

#2
We did Snett a few weekends ago at about 3 - 5 degrees with no tyre warmers. I elected for BT016's as did Schnauzer on the thinking that a road tyre will be used to working from cold and cope with more heat cycles. They felt like shit every lap of every session and never got a decent grip right up until they spat me off.

Jamie (TJ) was riding on K2 Racetecs and had no real problems. There were a few marks on the tyre that suggested he had been spinning them up but nothing that gave him cause to worry on track.

If I had the day again I would be on Racetecs too.
