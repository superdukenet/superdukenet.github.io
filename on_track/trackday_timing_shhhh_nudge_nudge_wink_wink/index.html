Back in the day when all this were fields you could get an Alfano pro lap timer from a go cart on e bay for about 70 quid. Put the sensor thingy down in your belly pan (you needed an extended lead) and every time you crossed a magnetic strip you got a lap time, no turning on / off relying on bletooth witchcraft etc etc.I imagine this method still works even though Brands has been resurfaced.

I have no idea about the satelite bollocks or bluetooth jiggery pokery I may try it sometime in the future but until bluetooth can manage a reliable sigal between my phone and hands free kit in my van (never more than 3ft apart and travelling at 60mph max) I an unlikely to invest in that nonsense for my bike.
