#2
Lydden Hill. Near Dover Kent

Short straights, bumps on corner entries and exits, usually cheap. Had no facilities at all until recently but now you can plug in your tyre warmers in the new tarmac paddock

I go there a few times a year but have still never taken the SD there. Everytime I get there I get duffed up by a supermoto or similar and wish I had the SD.

Like Brands indy it is easy to learn but hard to get the best out of.
