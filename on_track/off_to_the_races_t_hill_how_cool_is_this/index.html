How cool is this? Three bad ass looking SDs lined up like the business!
It was great having us all in force under one canopy.





The races were great. We all did pretty damn well for a track that our bikes aren't really suited for. Despite not having the "legs" of the other bikes we were racing we managed to hold our own against the other guys in our respective skill/time levels. I personally dropped more than 3 seconds off of my best at T-Hill and with the GPS and telemetry data I can see where I need to improve in two weeks when I return for a school.
There was a pretty horrific crash at the start of F1 and I just barely made it through. I have it on video and will post in the future.
No fast yellow platers showed up for F40 so I picked up a Top Novice trophy! Ran out of gas in Clubman and had a decent finish in the other races.
