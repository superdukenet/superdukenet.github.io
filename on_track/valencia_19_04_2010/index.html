Post 5 by Ducati Pete, 03 Nov 2009, 23:18

#5
Jerez is my favourite.

It's fast, lots of fast flowing corners and has a great rythm to it. Although the track has 3 hairpins, it doesn't feel stop / start at all. The 2 straights aren't that long and the back straight is entered via a long-ish right hander that goes over a blind crest - so you can give yourself a run up by getting that right.

End of the lap features 2 VERY fast right handers, I find I make up a bit of time against the faster bikes though there, it's curb to curb and favours the brave.

Typing that has made me realise what a disaster last week was.

Oh, and the hotels are 5 minuts from the paddock,


Parc Algar is great in another way, it really does make Cadwell look flat! Despite having a massive crash there, I still rate it most highly - the crash was pure rider error in the wet. One thing about Parc Algar that is tough on a SD is the main straight - it's mahoosive! I call it the motorway section.
