Well after owning the SDR for 2 weeks I had a quick blast around Cadwell.

Only did 2 sessions, but felt very much at home after just 2 laps, seat height is somewhat higher than the TL I normally race and the power is much more top end orientated which left me watching the rev counter down the straights to keep it off the limiter...

Was only running wets as it was still damp under the trees so it was a little vague on the brakes.

Great fun, here's a few shots....
