Circuit highlights:
- 5.2km kilometre race circuit (well over 3 miles!) and 12m wide.
- “Laguna Seca” style corkscrew section.
- Anticlockwise direction
- 14 corners. 8 lefts and 6 rights giving a combination of fast sweeping corners through to hairpins.
- Big elevation changes with the highest point to lowest point on track being 50m (but not all in one go!)
