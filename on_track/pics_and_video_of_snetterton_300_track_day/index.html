Hi guys,

I did a track day a few weeks ago and using my HD GoPro camera got some decent onboard footage. I also paid some dosh for a dvd of professional photos, so I thought I'd share them.

It was my second visit to Snett 300 on the SDR and having fitted a Sigma slipper clutch this time was a lot quicker and smoother than before.

It was pretty busy in the inters and surprisingly I was only passed once the whole day (some old duffer on a Triumph Tiger nipped on the inside of me at Russells, or what ever its called now.) There were also a couple of SD's and a SM in the inters too!
