Klink give the Central Ticket Office a ring at the Met switchboard, (guess it might be a big one though ), just give them your details and see if they have processed your endorsable ticket or if they ever received it.

Your not then directly contacting the bobby who gave you the ticket, they will just say we have no knowledge of the ticket or yes we sent it to DVLA to be endorsed, either way you should be able to track it down if that helps.

If someone has lost your licence after you handed it to the cop shop they cant put the points on your paper licence bit (albeit will be on your cmputer record) and as such you can end up with an revoked licence. Not good.
