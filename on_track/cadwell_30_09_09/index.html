Booked on Monday as there were spaces and weather was good.

Rocked up and was in fast group, got my mojo on pretty much where I left off from last week and was soon catching an overtaking a few, been the fast group group there were some fast guys, one guy was an instructor) was amazing, leaving black lines every where, he was clearly a racer with his blade, wow, he had same leather on as me too

Not many crashes in fast group, but one in inters after lunch which delayed things for some time so I left the last session out as we would not have had long.

Not quite the same hen it's just you, must do more trackdays with superdukers, I was the only one there on an SD and think I held up the fort OK.

Took some footage but wont bore you with with that think we have seen enough Cadwell footage to last a lifetime
