Great day, marred only by being totally knackered from the off through lack of sleep and overwork the past two weeks. Nice to meet Big Ash and Twisted Jester, Jody and the others.

Maybe there won't be another chance because they had a LOT of issues with noise and one guy from MSV was telling me that this was their last chance to prove to the council that bike noise was something they could keep under control. (which they clearly couldn't as this relied on bikes never getting together in groups of more than TWO!!).
