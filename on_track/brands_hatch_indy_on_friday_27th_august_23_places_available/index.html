I thought I'd restart this post in the proper section...


There is a Focussed Events Trackday at Brands Hatch Indy circuit coming up on Friday 27th August - and it's the perfect track for a Superduke... ...and just a few miles out of London by the M25/A20 in Kent.

The facilities are superb, nice garages, edible food, history, heritage, and even new grippy tarmac in a few key places...

For all you London and South East Superduksters, there is no excuse:... It's summer, there's weeks to plan, save, book, tell work/missus/mum.

you can even book Tommy Hill for a one to one session for £30.

It is £159, but you can lie-in at home till 6.30am before you have to leave, and then be back home in time for tea at 7, with no need for a van, B&B or any other hidden expense.

For all you Northern Monkeys - c'mon. Get yer jabs, learn to drink shandy, and brave the Southlands at this awesome track.

I suggest a Superduke mass booking asap before places run out. ...For those who dare face Paddock Hill that is.

23 places left.

http://www.focusedevents.com/event_info.asp?ID=1742
