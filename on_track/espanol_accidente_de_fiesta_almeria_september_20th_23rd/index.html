We usually fly from Bristol to Murcia (Bourmouth very good price ....but a real pain for me.....Birmingham is quicker )

We hire a Van (3 bikes+ kit bags and tool box, wheel stands, tyres) its £50.00

You can take


1. Your Motorcycle.

2. Leathers, helmet, boots and gloves in a holdall weighing no more than 15kg.

3. One sealed plastic/metal storage box, no bigger than 1x1x2 ft, which can contain tools, tyre warmers etc weighing no more than 13kg.

4. One set of paddock stands.

5. One set of tyres or one set of wheels per bike. Any extras will be charged at £10 per wheel or tyre.


All the above are approx.....most people are above these...and nothing is said......(but within reason...as long as they can comfortably lift it...they will load it).....
