Well thanks chap (and chapess) for a good few days, shame some of you could not make it.

I promptly left work at 2100 on the Sunday all was dry in Sunny Scunny as you would expect but I saw big rain clouds looming over Cadwell. By time I have chucked everything in the car and en route its lashing it down, bollocks don’t fancy a sleeping in a tent with rain all night, should have just turned up on the Monday morning and had a good nights sleep.....anyway.

Arrive at 2230 and see Jules as no one else around where the hell is everyone? “all in Jody’s van” I look over to see his long wheel base sprinter thinking thought there were like 17 of us how do they all get in there? Anyway open the door to find it was actually not a “cab” but a giant bong , think I see some people I recognise through the smoke and introductions are made once again. Few beers later everyone asks where TJ was Dan and I go outside (in the rain) to find him trying to single handily put his awning up in 25 knots of wind and rain…… (was clearly a 3 man job too in this weather) his idea of using it was disbanded and the 7 man tent he had bought I was not going to erect by myself (and be the only one using it) so Dan offered the use of his van-Sweet. Was rather disappointed all the first timers had gone to bed when I arrived anyway………..

Wake up at 7am Monday morning, I think we all knew this would be hit and miss on the rain front, make my introductions to some new SD owners I had not met, everyone, jumps on the Tuono to check out there feminine side…….I was running Dunlop NTEC D211 racer 19psi cold rear and 30psi front, took them out on the first session and felt like I had a flat tyre, came back in put the warmers on for a bit longer and got some heat in them, provided shed loads of grip and an awesome tyre, recommended! Moments behind Shade when he takes a tumble at Barn, was gutted to see the SDR at the side of the track every time I did a lap .

One session is red flagged, come in to find out it was Ginge, the veteran trackdayer original king of Cadwell has been off, we all check on his welfare and his bike is stripped of its GOPRO as soon as it lands in the pits to check out his footage………… his bike is OK but Ginge is battered and bruised unable to partake in day 2, shame as Ginge had trouble with his bike in the AM only to find it was the tilt sensor was the reason it would not start/stay running.

Missed two of the sessions on the Monday due to rain, I think Novice/Inters got the short straw and got more rain, but dried for us fast boys when it was our turn. As usual was having fun with Jody out on track but could not keep out the corners, was not pushing myself on day 1 as really did not want to bin the bike as got another day tomorrow and knew that would be the fun one, plus I had someone coming to buy the bike on Wednesday when I came back from “my business trip“..

Nip to my folks in Louth for a quick shower and an ice run for the beer, ring the buyer for my bike only to find he has changed his mind……oh well, no drama., return for now my 2nd BBQ of the day, lots of beer and banter all night.

Everyone goes to bed at midnight thought I best do too, manage to sleep from 0030-0230 and then awake in the van for the rest of the night, not a good start. BBQ bacon breakfast (again) go out for two sighting laps and back in, give it half hour as still couple of damp patches, new today would be sun all day. Result.

During the course of the day struggle to get comfortable on the Tuono, its difficult as when I bought it came with a go faster butt plug which makes it uncomfortable round corners, then Ducati Pete comes past on his crash prepped SD, then as sessions go by TJ comes past, then I brake like fook to try and avoid the worlds slowest rider and Craig does us both on the outside (where I should have been) get stuck behind him and don’t see Craig again.

Pretty much get dusted on the Tuono by TJ, Jody, Ducati Pete and looking at Craig keeping up with TJ when I was watching I think if we had been on an open track I would have been properly overtaken by Craig too, (lets be fair yours did not count Craig!)

Got some footage from Jody’s GOPRO of him jumping the mountain but the tint of the screen does not make it very clear, had a quick look at the footage and im pretty much doing constant 1:53’s.

Call it a day at 1500hrs little bit knackered now on abouth my 6th BBQ and dont want to see another burger till next year!.

Nice to meet all the new faces, hope you see why we do this every year, hope every ones bikes repair well and cheaply.

Clear the SD has much more low down grunt, majority of Cadwell is 2nd gear, SD punches out the corners better I this gear as the Tuono has ram air it does not go mental till the latter of the rev range, I did have 45t sprocket for the rear which would have been ideal but could not get warmers on without extending the chain. The Tuono was no faster on the straights either, (my Tuono has no performance mods).

Miss my SD and wish was on one, BUT I still had fun.
