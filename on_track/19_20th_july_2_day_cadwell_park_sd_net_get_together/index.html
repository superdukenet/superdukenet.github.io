Good write ups guys.

Ben get well soon m8! Bike will be back with a bit of TLC and you'll be dragging your knee before you know it

Trip got off to a good start when Schnauzer decided at the last min that he could join us. Arrived to find Ginge (Sabre) had already setup his gazebo and had saved us all prime spots. Top man Ginge

Then proceed to fire up the gas fired beast that is the adopted SD.net BBQ. Sank a few burgers and brews as more of the lads (and lass) turn up and the banter and praying to the weather Gods begins.

Wake up at 6.45 to the sound of rain on the fly sheet WTF! this isn't what we ordered! Step out expecting black skies and wet ground but find almost clear skies and just a few drops of rain Things are looking good!

Once through the brief and out on track all is looking good. First session and everyone is getting along nicely, track is dry, grip is good and the real fast guys are playing fair with no silly undertakes ect (that will change)

Next thing we see is Superdan on the side of the track Have to say that was the last person I expected to see. Welcome to the crash club m8, its the only place to be

All is well with Dan thank God and the bike is hardly marked considering he jumped straight in to the club with a highside, talk about making an entrance!!

Anyway next session out I decide it's time to man the fook up and get my elbow down around Barn. And I only go and do it as well!! Unfortunatly I forgot to stay on the bike while doing it Not really sure what happened. Was cranked right over and lost the front just before the apex but I was off the brakes. It did turn out after then seeing the suspension guy that my fork has been bottoming out by the zip tie marker and needed another 2 turns of front preload. Now I'd truly love to blame it on that but I have to admit it may of been to much lean and not enough talent Slid for 30 feet then on to the grass, up on to my feet and helped the marshals dig my bike out of the plastic bales. She did flip as she hit the grass before embedding herself under the bales. I then stand there for the rest of the session chatting with the marshals as SD after SD rides past, with all of them I know chuckling away in their helmets and preparing the piss takes for when I arrive back in the pits. Ginge greets me each lap with a musical tune on his horn. Piss taking cnut

Anyway I get picked up and in the recovery van we set off. "That's 2 Super Duke owners I've picked up already" says the driver. "Yeah there are 14 or so of us here" I tell him. "hope I won't be seeing any more of you lot today" he laughs. "I'm sure there won't be" I tell him......
Turns out there were 2 other bikes down in that session. One is a Ducatti rider that is at the end of the pit straight. The other it seems is getting taken back another way. Don't be a SD rider I think.... Oh dear Ronin

A bit of superficial damage to my bike, most annoyingly a chip in the tank and the headlight unit is even more fooked than it was before. Straighten the forks in the yokes as the bars were facing one way and the wheel the other. Jody lent me a top slider to replace the R&G that snapped (cheers m8 ) as she flipped and she was good to go next session. I escaped with not a mark or bruise on me, though would happily of swapped a couple of bruises for that chipped tank Will cost a couple of hundred quid to get her back to her best but could always have been worse. Ronin's bike has got away with minimal damage and although he is feeling a tad bruised, he is also good to go next session. Will let him tell his own crash story though.

I'm taking it a bit easy next session but slowly the confidence starts to return but its a few sessions before I'm getting anything like the same pace around Barn again. Bike does feel a bit better on the front end with more preload but I'd be lying if I said I had full faith in her at lower speed corners. 90mph+ round Coppice ect she handles like a dream.

Then Superdan decides that joining with a highside wasn't enough and ups the ante with a second one. Unlucky m8, glad you are pretty much ok and although the bike has been through two highsides she is looking pretty good. Said it before but SDs do crash amazingly well.

Then Ben goes and joins the highside club. Damn unlucky to break his arm and such a shame with this bikes recent makeover that she is now carrying war scars... on the plus side we were able to scavenge quite a few track day spares off her, so at least that's something huh He also had to have 10x cameras in his face as his SD buddies hovered like Paparazzi at the rear of the Ambulance as it pulled up. As soon as the doors open, cameras thrust through the door and flashes firing and we are greeted by the one finger salute but a still smiling Ben. In a cast and with a shot of morphine but I'm still going with the thermometer sticking out of his arse as the real reason for the smile
Get well soon m8.

We manage to get through the rest of the day without any more crashing. And although the day had many events we all decided by a unanimous vote that the best moment of the day was Mrs Millar doing Freestyler on his new Aprila, while riding her KTM 450 EXC SM and even giving him a little wave as she went past Maria, we salute you! You legend!!

After a run to the shops, more BBQ is consumed, as is more booze. Much laughter is probably heard around Cadwell Park. Mrs Millar has the good sense to stay with us rather than nursing her hubby and has a well earned cider or 2. Fancy stressing her out like that Ben

A bit of rain in the night but pretty much dry by morning and weather is looking good again. Amazing that some forcasts has us down for 2 solid days of rain. The Weather Gods love a SD track day meet it seems.

Stew rocked up to join us for day 2. As others have mentioned we got a special mention at the briefing that morning. All the people who hadn't been there the day before were duly warned that if they found themselves next to a KTM at the staging area that it might be an idea to move to the back of the queue Was all meant in good humour and I thought it was very funny. Its not like any of crashes involved anyone else apart from Ronin's where he was the victim.

We manage to get through the rest of the day without any crashes in the fast group (SDs anyway) Razy had a off at I think the hairpin and was feeling a tad sore but himself and the bike were pretty much unharmed, apart from a damn chip in the tank

Jody def was, with the lack of a Superdan to challenge him, King of Cadwell. Well done m8 you were flying round there... Flying being no exaggeration, as we were told that he was doing his both wheels off the ground at the Mountain on a regular basis. Ronin and Sabre were doing the long wheelies over the mountain when it turned out that No Limits weren't black flagging for it as we were warned in the briefing. Sabre seemed a happier man when set free at last

I don't think for one minute that any of the crashes were down to peer pressure or bravado. Yes we were sometimes riding around a bit grouped up but this was because some of us were running similar pace to to others. Overtakes were made but I didn't see one by a fellow SD'er that was dangerous or even risky. That could not be said for others out there and I was undertaken and stood up on several occasions at some completely unnecessary and dangerous places. Where if the guy had waited a few more seconds he could have safely got past. Amazingly this didn't cause any crashes but I can assure you some use of the word cnut!

Everyone in fast group on SDs was putting in good times. It was such good fun to be out there riding with you guys. Was able to improve my times again I hope and learn a few things off my fellow riders. One thing is that I need to learn not to brake so early into Park. TJ did me more times than I could ever be happy with on the brakes there Nice job m8! and your pace is really picking up It's always a good thing to want to improve and go a bit faster I think. And what better way than on track with friends.

Thanks for a great couple of days guys. Pleasure to see all of you again. I had a fantastic time and a huge amount of laughter and piss taking.

Ben hope the arm is back to normal soon as is the bike.

Dan you made a fantasic pit hand. Can't help thinking that a swim suit and umbrella wouldn't have gone a miss though

Lets do this again soon guys. Return of the Fallen Pt2 has to be on the cards before the end of the season.

Oh and Luke you are a cheeky cnut
