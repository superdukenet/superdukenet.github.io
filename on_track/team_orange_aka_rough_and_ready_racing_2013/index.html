#46
With the forum being down we didn't get a chance to post up first race report. Here goes.

We qualified 4th out of 14 for first Endurance race at Snetterton, 2 spots behind Team Green. Not a track we expected to do well at being as it has 2 big straights were we get proper done over by he big Il4s, so very happy with that. I get to start this race. It's a Le Mans start so Stalker holds the bike and I sprint across the track and jump on. Make a good start and am 2nd into first corner. Hold 2nd into turn 3 but Keith of Team Green does me on a small straight but he tucks in behind the guy in front who tips in way to early into 4. I manage to go round the outside of both of them and hold the lead until the big back straight where they fook off past me with that extra 50bhp

Settle in and just try and put in constant laps and keep them in sight. Bike is playing up, missing a lot (found out a week later the TPS has gone again ) about 6 laps in I think I see a black flag waved at me after turn 2. Not learning my flags properly being a noob rookie I make a huge error and come off the track at turn 4. Fight my way back through car park and back to pits. They weren't expecting me and Stalker isn't ready. Panic to swap transponder over and get him out on track. I then head up to end of pit lane to see why I was flagged. Race control say I wasn't. I could swear I saw the marshal with a flag and pointing at me. Rookie mistake by me. Never again will I come off at first flag I think I see. We lost over 4 mins there and put us 2 laps down.

Stalker gets out there and puts in a great 30 mins. We start clawing some time back on leaders and more importantly on the enemy Team Green. James aka The GinReaper who is officially Team Orange manager now does an amazing job running the pits. Ronin, Superdan and Sabre are working the pit board and timing. While I was up at Race Control the scrutineers spot my weeping base gaskets. Looking for a min like they are going to pull me out of the race they wander down to our garage and want to see the belly pan off. I'm telling them its just a small weep when she's being spanked. 2 of them are shaking their heads and I fear the worst. Fortunately the chief used to do the Superduke cup and is aware of the problem. He says "clean it up and I want to see it when you come back in again." James is a legend and gets her ready to race again. I head out and try and just put in some consistent laps. Ronin is holding out the board and I'm doing between 2.12s and 2.15s. I've got all kinds of error codes flashing on the dash. She's misfiring and I'm sure she's going to die on me but that's the only way I'm stopping. Come in after my stint and we swap transponders. 3 of your pit stops have to be a minimum of 3 minutes, as I came off halfway round the track and not into pit lane our first stop doesn't count as one

Stalker is back out again and puts his foot down. Doing 2.10s - 2.12s. I have scrutineering breathing down my neck again. I beg for a break and he agrees to let me back out but we have to take belly pan off after every stint and clean the engine off with brake cleaner. We are slowly reeling some time back but it's hard to tell exactly where you are as people make pit stops at different times. It's just a matter of putting in good lap after good lap and see how it pans out. I head out and just try and do the same. When I come in again I head over to pit wall to see how it's panning out. We will have 50 mins left when Stalker comes in again so we decide I'll do a 20 min stint and leave Stalker to do the last 30. My bike is running like shit. Refuses to tick over and I have to keep her running by holding throttle open. Stalker is a good 3 secs a lap quicker so we might have a chance of over taking team Green. As it stands we are 5th and they are 4th. Stalker is on it and Team Green still need to make one more stop. It's starting to get exciting on pit wall.

With just under 15 mins left Monty of Team Green comes in and after swapping transponders Keith heads up pit lane. Just as he gets to end of speed restriction Stalker flies past now it's a mad last 7 laps. 116hp SD with a loon on board vs 180bhp ZX10 with a man who sooo doesn't want to lose. We are all on pit wall now waiting to see them come round the long right hander before they hit the straight. Every lap Stalker is keeping the gap. We just have GO!!! written in pit board ad lots of crazy hand gestures aimed at him. Starts last lap and he's still got a good 8ecs lead! That 2.10 mins seems to last forever! We finally see him round the right and he's still out in front! Crosses the line 8 secs ahead of the green bike what a result! Stalker doesn't come off the track after turn 3 like he's supposed to and into holding area and instead does a lap of honour

So the piss taking and gloating begins Monty wants to make an official complaint that Stalker didn't come off at correct point and get us a penalty no joy with that though. Adrenaline is sky high, was such amazing fun and for it to come down to 8 secs after a 3 hour race is pretty amazing. After about 15 mins Keith walks back into garage with official timing sheets. To our amazement and joy the team finishing 3rd have been given 2 time penalties for not doing 3x 3 min pit stops and so we move up to 3rd overall on the podium in our first race at a track we didn't expect to do anything at.

When we finally head up for prize giving it suddenly becomes clear that the 2 teams above us overall are not in the same class as us. It suddenly dawns that we have in fact come 1st in our class and are on the top step with the big trophy Team Green are 2nd in class and on the step below. There may or may not have been some piss taking at this point

So all in all a brilliant day. We've exceeded all expectations and not only made the podium but got first place.

A massive thank you to Ronin, Superdan and Sabre for coming along and helping out. The GinReaper is singled out for extra thanks. He went above and beyond in the days leading up to the race to get my bike running again. Without him I wouldn't have been racing. Cheers bud you're a legend

Next round is Oulton Park on the 18th May. Can't bloody wait!

Ok here goes. First off all what a fooking awesome track Oulton Park is First time there for me and although it only takes a couple of sessions to learn it, its such a fast circuit with a couple of blind corners so it takes a while before you can commit. Its super narrow and if you get it wrong its on to the grass at speed and so most likely off.

ACU day on Friday went really well. We had been promised rain for both days all week but it never arrived on Friday. I don't mind riding on wets but I'd rather learn the track in the dry. First time out on the SDR and the first session the rear shock as clearly not working properly. Was getting zero grip and was all over the place. Headed to see the suspension guy and he made a few changes to get it working properly. Spent the morning leaning it and building speed. Managed to put in some 1.57s in the afternoon which isn't bad. SDR was brilliant. So planted, loved to corner and the extra grunt is awesome

The promised rain arrives in the night. Its's stopped by the time we get up and although the wind is drying some of the concrete areas the track is wet. Stalker is doing the open sprint race as well as Endurance so his qualifying is just before Endurance. Some people are on wets but we are being told half the track is wet and the other half almost dry. Stalker heads out on Racetecs and his session is red flagged and he pops back to the garage and says its too wet for them. GinReaper and Ronin who have once again stepped up and driven for hours to help out get my bike swapped to 2CTs as its the closest to an inter we have and I don't want to ruin a rear rain tyre just to qualify in what is after all a 3 hour race.

FInally head out. Its a tad scary in places. Turn 1 Old Hall, which is a corner I struggled to go flat out at all weekend is dry all the way through until the exit when you need to get on the gas 2CTs are good in the damp but my head isn't allowing me to crack open the throttle. Its then dry all the way until Clay Hill (my fav part of the track) It's wet all the way through here and there is a fresh section of tarmac that is in the braking zone that looks super slick and is just that. I put in 20 mins and manage to make 1.58s which considering the conditions and tyres I'm happy with. Stalker heads out and gets down into the 1.56s and we qualify 5th.

Racetecs go back on and we kick off at 1pm. I start the race again and its the fab Le Mans start. I make a good start and am 2nd into turn 1. I manage to hold 2nd for 6 laps and am seeing 1.56s on the board. Then get passed by an RSV4. Become aware that right foot peg doesn't feel right at all. Glance down and the fooking thing is working its way lose It's had 1 and 1/2 days to do that and it decides to do it 7 laps into the race. I come into the pits and with the adrenaline flowing make a bad decision. We have to do mandatory 3x 3 min pit stops in the race. We should have done one then but I decide to go back out after a quick tighten losing us 1 min at least. I head back out and am now having to get past the back markers. Do another 7 laps or so and pit board say come in.

Stalker heads out and after 10 mins we know something is wrong when he doesn't appear. Race control tell us he's snapped his chain at Island. Thats a bloody long way from the pits and he has to leg it back in leathers with transponder so I can go back out. Poor fooker. Reaper heads off on mountain bike to meet him when we find out where he is coming from. By that stage he is only 300 yrds from us and looking fit to drop. We've lost 19 mins and when I head out so day is over. Maybe we should have packed up then but that's not what its about. I do 30 mins and come back in. Stalker is looking deflated and says its over because he was hoping they might let him use his ZX10. That of course would be an unfair advantage. I tell him to jump on mine and get out there. Again we should have thought about it and just made it to the finish and taken some points in the championship but adrenaline is funny thing and is screaming at you to just go go go. He puts in some fast laps and does a 1.54 which is pretty quick. I head out for my last session and am feeling pretty fooked half way through, really need to work on my fitness. Oulton is such a physical track. I come in and Stalker heads out again for final stint. About 10 mins in it starts to drizzle. That horrible stuff that isn't rain but is enough to start making the tarmac wet. Suddenly the red flag goes out, not something they like to do in Endurance unless its serious, normally its a safety car that goes out. They have stopped the riders between Druids and Lodge. Lodge into Deer leap is the last corner that is a big dip that goes off camber before you come over Deers Leap trying to keep the front down and onto the pit straight. A rider comes over who came through before they stopped the riders and says he thinks its a KTM that highsided and the rider is laying in the gravel not moving We leg it towards race control and they say 2 riders have crashed at different places and one is No6 which is our number They eventually let the other riders though and we watch them into the pit lane hoping Staker is amongst them but he isn't They tell us to head to medical centre and wait there. He was knocked out and they are bringing him back in an ambulance. I'd be lying if I said a small part of me wasn't wondering about my SDR but the main thing of course is Stalker. They come out after 10 mins telling us he is conscious again and they are observing him before making decision whether he needs to go to hospital. We decide to head to scruteneering and check out my bike. I want to see it and don't want to see it at the same time. They have it locked up as its been involved in a serious incident. They let us in though and it looks like its been in a proper crash but not as bad as I feared. Bars which were straight now look like clip on on right side. Race mask is hanging down, bendable leaver is bent is a way it was never designed to do. Carbon end cap on Akra is cracked. Rear set is half gone, crash bungs did their job big time and looking at the way they are pointing the bike entered landed backwards on the right side on a right bend which is kinda the opposite you'd expect from a highside. Word is he was a good 12ft in the air so he did it with style! Left hand side of the bike is untouched, phew. So all in all not too bad considering the size off the crash.

We head back and they let us in to see him. He's looking slightly dazed and can't remember a thing of what happened and is of course being super apologetic about my bike. Tell him not to worry about it, it aint too bad and main thing is he is ok. The piss taking starts and we have a laugh with the doctor. Ronin then breaks the bin by sitting on it They release him to our charge. Team Green are top lads and Monty drives his van and him back home before heading back to Weymouth with Keith. They managed to get 1st place this time in in our class so are leading the championship. We will need to finish above then in the final 3 rounds to stand a chance. With 2 of them being very well suited to the Superduke we have a good chance. So its repair time and then on to Donnington is a few weeks time.

My one and only high side still haunts me now. I was used to the back tyre moving about and as I did more and more track time those kind of feekings and the feedback I got, ( that is different from years of road riding), become more familiar. But I'd never directly experienced a high side effect, knew about it all in theory but that counts for nothing when it comes to knowing how to react when it happens.

All I remember was that it happened SO FAST! On Clearways at Brands - a corner I'd done countless times. But I was off the racing line and maybe a bit too complacent and suddenly there was a micro second of the feeling of loss of grip but then something new and it all went pear shaped. I still replay the memory in my head and it makes me feel ill! I guess you need to high side a lot to just get used to it. I still don't know what I should have done to try and recover the situation. I can't replay the details well enough to analyse anything. I ended up in hospital for a week with a dodgy back.

Because I'm not a really fast/competitive/frequent rider, it's set me back a lot that incident. I could lie and say a load of macho bollox but actually it's made me loose faith in really running on the edge of my tyres and pushing it and it will be a while I guess before that memory fades.

Post 116 by Superdan, 30 Jun 2013, 13:41

#116
Well we managed to bring a cup home again We finished 5th overall and 2nd in our class. TBH I didn't really get to grips with Donny. Struggled at Redgate and Melbourne Loop all day. Bike felt great at Oulton but just didn't inspire confidence round Donny. I don't know enough about suspension to start messing with it but I couldn't take as tight a line as I wanted at a lot of corners and the front end just didn't feel as good as at Oulton but did improve as the day went on. Not that it seemed to make a big difference to laptimes. I did some 1.47s but consistent 1.48s. For my first trip there I guess that's not bad but I'm sure with more confidence in the bike I could go faster.

Made a good start after Stalker put us in 2nd place with some good qualifying times. First into turn 1 and 2 but then a fast boy blasted past and I tried to stay with him. Tipped into the Old Hairpin and the front had a big slide just before the apex. Knee was on the deck and maybe that helped it grip again but it was enough for me to think back off a bit, binning on lap 1 of a 3 hour race wouldnt be the best thing ever. I'm nowhere near the pace of the top guys anyway so just put in my 40 min stint and let Stalker get some time back. My near highside happened at around 25 mins, coming out of the Melbourne Loop, a corner I have to say I hate the rear went as I gassed it.. Threw me up and over the front and i came down on my ribs straight on to the kill switch. Fell back into the saddle and then had to wait for the clocks to cycle and then find neutrol before she would fire up. 5 mins later rearset hanger starts to come lose so I stick leg out and pit early.

Stalker heads out and is putting in great times and managed to get some 1.42s. Did his 40 mins and I head out again. Just as I am halfway round the lap I notice the safety car flags are out so I have to close the gap to the rear of the pack. Pick them up 1 & 1/2 laps later after Coppice. Race gets underway as we pass the finish line again and I just try and put in consistent 1.47s and do my full 40 mins. As the stint goes on I'm starting to feel a bit better out there and could have gone a little longer but Stalker is faster than me and its all about the points. Team manager The Ginreaper and Stalker fuel the ZX10 for a full hour so he can go to the end of the race. An hour is a long time out there but it will give us a chance at a podium. We've no idea if the 10 will go an hour on a full tank so I have to be ready to do a small stint in case Stalker signals he's running out of fuel. He picks up some pace as the tank empties but then loses 2nd gear for the last 30 mins of the race. Turns out the left hand chain adjuster bolt has worked its way all the way in and the chain is slacker than a whores draws and is affecting 2nd gear drive. With a lap to go his fuel light comes on but he brings it home in 5th overall and 2nd in our class 20 valuable points in the championship. Poor Team Green had a disastrous couple of days. First Monty is knocked off track on the Thursday by some douche coming up the inside of him at Schwantz curve where there was never room to do it. Although he kept it upright over the grass he lost it as he approached the track again and landed on the otherside of the track unconscious. A trip the hospital and the all clear was given but he was groggy. The other guy trashed his bike but deserved it as it was a reckless and dangerous move and was only a trackday. As we had friday off we all chipped in to repair the 750 and he felt ok to race. He was doing ok in the race but then lost the rear going around Redgate and although he was ok the bike took heavy damage. This is the crash that bought out the safety car as the bike was in the track. Def a weekend to forget for him but at least he didn't hurt himself in this crash.

Next round is Cadwell in 5 weeks. A track both Stalker and I love and one the SDR should go well at for me. 2 days at Crashfest to get to grips with it again before the race.

Well here is the report. Main thing is we managed to win the whole thing we've finished first in our class before but not the whole race. Did it with a whole 1.30 to spare as well

We had some rain for qualifying but it cleared up and Stalker put us 2nd with a 1.40 flat so not hanging around
I'll be honest and say I struggled on Friday and race day with pace. A mix of being Cadwell rusty having not been there in over a year and physically after my op and being laid up for a month. Although I matched it I didn't manage a faster time than last years 1.44 which is very disappointing Fitted a gear indicator which helped with problems I was having knowing what gear I was in but also showed how many times I was going in to corners in completely the wrong gear. Sometimes my error but also the box sometimes just didn't want to go down 3 gears in fast succession at the end of Park.

Stalker started the race this time as my bike was still fitted with wets from qualifying. He was pretty much on the pace straight away and doing low 1.40s with a full tank. Just after halfway he put in 1.39 flat so he was chuffed to bits with that. Came in after 40 mins and I headed out. Within 3 laps its was raining at Park and Chris's Curve and its a bit off putting seeing slippery surface flags out every other lap and rain drops on visor. Still I was really struggling, couldn't find a rhythm and was making mistake after mistake. 1.46s just isn't good enough and I know I can go way faster than that. Was fooked after my 40 mins and fit to drop. It was still raining at Park as Stalker headed out and even a bit in pit lane. So plan was to fit wets to my bike and if Stalker needed to come in after a few laps he could just jump on my bike. It did start properly raining but fair play he stayed out for 25 mins and gave me a chance to recover. I then headed out in the rain but on wets. We were still in 2nd place at this point. As per normal for me I seem to find the 2nd session much easier than the first. Not sure why this is but always seems to happen. I enjoy the wet and managed to get into the low 1.50s within a couple of laps and then into the hi 1.40s. Seemed everyone else was struggling as I was overtaking people everywhere. Pit board confirmed we were back in first place and the track was starting to dry. I stayed out for my 40 mins until the track was dry so Stalker could bring it home in the last 35 min stint on a dry track. By the time I came in we had a 1 lap lead god bless that rain. Stalker did the business and we won it with 1.30 to spare. Great win and puts us back in the lead of championship. Bring on Brands in 4 weeks time
