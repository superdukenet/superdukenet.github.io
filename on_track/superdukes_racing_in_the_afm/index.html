AFM - Race Schedule
Written by AFM Editor

Schedule of Events in 2010
(tentative schedule, subject to change)

Month Date Track
March 20 & 21 Buttonwillow
April 24 & 25 Infineon
May 22 & 23 Infineon
June 12 & 13 Thunderhill
July 10 & 11 Thunderhill
July / Aug 31 & 1 Infineon
October 2 & 3 Thunderhill

** AFM 4-Hr Endurance Race (tentative); this event will likely have Open Practice Friday, Practice & 4-Hr Saturday, and Practice & Races Sunday.

Note:

1. All Saturday Practice days are open to licensed members. It includes 2/3 day of practice and races for Clubman, AFemme, Vintage, and Formula 40 Classes.
2. All Sundays are racedays and all are points events.
3. The tentative race day schedule for the coming event will be posted as it becomes available in the Announcements forum. This schedule is subject to change.
4. The Race Entry page has specific entry related information, including the dates entries are accepted.
5. All tracks have said they will allow camping on the facility grounds for our events. Call the tracks directly for info and fees. (IN (707) 938-8848; TH (530) 934-5588; BW (661) 764-5333.
6. See the New Racers > Race School for the AFM approved New Racer School providers
