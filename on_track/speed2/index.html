I've been hearing Scott Russell mention this "channel" all year. Finally figured out how to access it. First get a computer that is fast along with a good internet connection. Go to speed2.com and you will be asked to select your satellite/cable provider. Log in with your respective provider and walaa. Lots of cool racing from around the world.

Today I watched the Homestead AMA Supersport race, Goodwood Festival of Speed, World Super Enduro, Moto GP qualifying from San Marino, British MX, and British Roadracing (Isle of Man, NW200, etc.). Pretty stoked to have access to all this racing and a slow day at work!
