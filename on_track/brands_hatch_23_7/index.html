Yeah as TJ says, even sessions are 102dB at brands, daytimes are 105dB and the GP is 102dB.

My akras with baffles ran about 97dB under test, without was on the limit/just over so take em just in case the tester is a hard ass.

My Leos tested 105dB WITH baffles, but he tested them wrongly and stuffed the mic right inside the can (idiot) but he let me on anyway.

The have to test at about half a meter and at an angle from the pipe exit or the wind blast creates an artificially high reading. They ask for 5000rpm too for this test for a 1000cc V twin.
