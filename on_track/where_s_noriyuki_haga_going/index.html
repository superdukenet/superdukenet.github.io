Haga always looked good as a wildcard at Sugo, but you could never tell if he was the real deal - with local knowledge and the Jap dunlops.

When he joined the circus full time at P.I. at the start of 98 he was amazing, and on the YZF750, the oldest bike on the grid. He did the double the next time out at Donny - and to do that there, with no previous visits is quite something. The wheels came off in Italy when he smashed his ankles up, I really think he could have won that year had he been a bit more patient that weekend.

One of my favourite trivia questions is the last rider to podium in WSB and premier GP in a 8 day period. Well you now know the rider - can you remember the track and which GP legend he battled to get on the rostrum?
