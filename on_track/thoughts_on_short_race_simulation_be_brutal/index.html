Well I'm not particularly fast, and it is a bit hard to judge things from video but ...

To me it feels like I'd want to go in a bit deeper on the most of those turns and turn in faster when I did turn.
It looks like you have a tendency to hug that inside corner so you don't get the drive coming out.
A couple of times you went deeper, dropped in and had plenty of drive coming out on the inside of that guy, whose
lines also drove him wide, then seemed a too surprised to make the pass. Don't follow somebody that looks like they
could run wide.
