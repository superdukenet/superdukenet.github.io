#155
Typing this from my sleeping bag in the back seat of Chateaux Freelander in the resort of Trac Mon Circuit Anglesey.

Interesting first day. Bike and head to sort out since the crash and so long out the seat.

Track is brilliant. Really fast in places and lots of time with it on its ear. Had a few problems in the 1st 2 sessions with it wheelying everywhere. It was down to me running it in too slow, not carrying enough speed then getting to hard on it out the corner. Was lifting in 2nd and all the way through 3rd so was having to hold it on the back brake to keep it from coming right up.

Then next problem, it was real unstable on the brakes. Feel I could brake later but I was having to wait for it to settle at the rear before I could turn it. That was even with the slipper clutch and blipping on the downshift. Came together in the last session and I just dropped it down to 3rd for the hairpins as it was manageable going 4, 3. It was hooking 2nd that was making it try and back in.

Sorted most of those issues out by running it in a gear higher, carrying more corner speed, pushing harder on the front, trail braking into the turn a bit and getting to the apex on a closed throttle. Took a bit of mind trickery with my head still fresh from the crash telling me it was gonna tuck. Nipped away at it and it came good. Had to keep tweaking the suspension in between sessions mostly adding compression front and rear and a few preload tweaks. Managed to get it dialed in nicely.

Went out with an instructor for some lines up round the long fast rights into the tight left at Rocket. Boosted my confidence a bit as he was very complimentary of the bike and my humble efforts and said I should be up a group. Was good to hear after so long off the bike and 1st time at the track. (and only 2nd full trackday on the SD!)

All the SD's got lots of attention. Loads of folk saying how well they go out there. Few instructors were all over it in the holding grid saying it looks a riot to ride.

Was hard to string a good rhythm of laps together as there was a lot of traffic. Managed to get a 1min 47:5 in the last session with a few mistakes. I know where I can gain more time. Looking at the splits on my timer all the best splits adds up to a 1min 46. Sub 1.45's are on the cards with a bit of focus. Instructor was telling me sharp end of the Advanced group is about 1.41. The ole tractor ain't far away, and there's loads to come from the bike and rider.

Had a cracking battle with Skip in the afternoon session. Lapped together for almost the full session with pretty much nothing in it. Passed eachother a few times but all nice and clean. Was awesome, tucked in behind his back wheel though the long fast 120mph right before Rocket watching him leave big black lines on the track.

So, 1st time ever in Fast group tomorrow. See how it goes. Forecasts to totally piss down. Might get a few sessions in on wets and pack up early for the 6hr track home.
