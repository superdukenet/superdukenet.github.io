Two things--first: Would you ride a bike at 175 mph--then brake hard and turn? I am sure many here would or even have. Would you do it in sheets of driving rain? Perhaps. Would you do it in swirling, totally unpredictable "mini-hurricane" winds of 60 MPH? Well they did yesterday.

You had to be there to appreciate how vicious the weather got midway through the GP Sunday. I mean stuff was blowing everywhere--it blew a tent down and hospitalized two people.

Second--No pain, no gain: Nice riding by the Kentucky Kid--Haden in his "backyard"--Kentucky being a short drive away. Then you watch him get off the bike and they give him his cane so he can walk (broken foot).....These guys are something else.

True dat: T-shirt on the way out was the Nascar logo with the word "BORING" written in instead. Compared to these guys, it's a bubble bath.
