Cadwell is a safe place, never heard of a bike going walkies so you can leave it parked with the others

If you cant get a van and have to drive, worst case scenario I have a car and trailer that can be used to help you get home as dont live too far away.

Best off in a van though, as you will need a bit of stuff, deck chair most importantly.
