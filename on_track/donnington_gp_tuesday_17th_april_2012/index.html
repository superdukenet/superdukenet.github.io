Did a huge write up the good ol SD.net deleted it

2 wet sessions - great fun

4 dry sessions - reasonable fun, still not confident on the bike at all due to lack of riding

starting raining again so pack up and went home, rain has now continued all night and this morning (i only live 35mins from Donny)

FE did a good job of keeping the day moving, only 4 stopages total over the 3 groups. Amazing how chilled out the days are when Kevs now there

Mojo is FAR from returning, need ALOT more time on the bike, lap times were shite but was good to be back on the bike.

Lesson from the day, dont expect to do 6 trackdays total (the only time i ever ride) in the last 2 years then have 7 months off the bike and expect to happy with your riding abilities

Looking back at the GoPro footage i can see where all my speed has gone (ive actually lost 15 seconds a lap around Donny from my best times). Throttling off way too early, breaking far too early, not running enough speed on corner entry which is obviously slowing mid corner speed. The only thing im kinda happy with is corner exit and picking the bike up and getting on the throttle.

Oh well, onto Silverstone next Sunday and looks like it could be another wet day.
