done 3 days at anglesey this year and it is truly awesome
tarmac is grippy as , i prefer the coastal circuit which includes the cork screw but the gp is also fine.
You can stop in holyhead or even camp on the circuit. people do that lot.
its great fun and bloody hard work , probably my 2nd favourite circuit in the UK , theres always a bit of wind on the back straight but i've been lucky with the weather there this year and the scenery is very pituresque for north wales
Let us know when you fancy booking it
