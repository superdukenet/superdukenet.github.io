Hi all,

As the title advises Infinity Motorcycles has opened a new store in Norwich!!
This is their 8th store. Doors opened Friday 7th and there will be an grand opening day soon.........once I have received all the deliveries and arranged the store.
Product range is Rukka Gore Tex, Alpine Star, Dainese, Spada, Schuberth, scorpion exo helmets, I will be Shoei Assured and Five Star Arai dealers. Full range of Kreiga.

It would be lovely if you could spare a minute and pop onto Facebook and 'like' Infinity Motorcycles Norwich.

A product everyone needs that I now love is Vulcanet, it's unbelievable! It's a wipe that doesn't need water and you literally wipe your bike to get the dirt off and hey presto clean, just buff up with the micro fibre cloth provided. You need some in your life.......I'm convinced by it and will now let the gin reaper off cleaning duties....
