I got my Oggy Knobbs shortly after my 2008 SDR arrived and served me well when I caught my foot while rolling backwards down a slope out of my shed. Fast forward to late 2014 and I notice a crack appearing in a weld on the unscathed side. After telling myself that I should get that fixed for a few months, I returned from a ride out towards Dubbo without it.
Fast forward again to the weeks before the WSBK at the Island this year and I decide to drop the manufacturer and distributor a line to see if I can just buy the single side.
"No problem mate, that shouldn't happen. We'll put one in the mail for you. No Charge."

Back from the Supers and here it is. No questions or allegations about spills or rough use or incorrect fitting.
Gotta love Aussie manufacturers like this.
