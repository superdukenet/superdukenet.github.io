And i just got mine back today after having it all totally changed again to account for the big weight distribution changes we've made from moving the fork heights and wheelbase again.

Like I had to run about 2 or 3 full turns less of front preload than their data sheet suggested because my bike is a bit lighter than a standard bike. Plus the whole geo and wheelbase is different which changes the weight distribution. There is less than 10kg difference front to rear. So this is why its all went back in to be custom set to the bikes chassis setup.

You'll really like their work though, and good point for you is their data sheet will prob be ok for your bike. It seems a good enough base till you start playing with fork heights. MCT are great. Only slight issue i have with them is they seem to force ex battle settings on you which are irelevant now we have the wonderfull Nichols clamp, so it takes a bit of explaining to get them to do what i want with my kit. All sorted now though
