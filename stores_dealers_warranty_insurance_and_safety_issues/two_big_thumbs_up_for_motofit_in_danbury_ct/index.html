Two BIG thumbs up for MotoFit in Danbury, CT

Post 1 by Aphex, 09 Aug 2015, 04:29

#1
I just sold my S1000RR and went to searching to purchase a new KTM. My search led me to MotoFit in Danbury, CT. But after purchasing a new motorcycle yesterday (KTM Superduke R) and having such an amazing dealership experience. While they are not a BMW dealer, they do sell KTM/Ducati/Kawasaki/Aprilia/Husky.

I've been searching around for the right dealer to purchase my new KTM Super Duke R from for quite a while. Living approximaltey 1 hour from my three closest KTM dealers led me to reach out to them first.

I'm very glad I placed a call to Motofit in Danbury. I spoke to Dan over the phone and explained to him what I was looking for. He was quickly able to accommodate me and at a price that far exceeded my expectations. Unable to come see the bike right away, he forwarded me detailed photos of it and I quickly left a deposit over the phone.

When I came in to pick the bike up, Dan was on top of the game and had all of the paperwork ready for me. Dan and the rest of the Motofit crew made me feel like absolute family. I have never felt so relaxed in a dealership with absolutely no pressure. It was such a relaxing and enjoyable experience.

I rode home my brand new dream bike and got some miles on it smiling ear to ear. A few hours later my phone rang. There was Dan checking in on how my ride was, how I enjoyed it and made the experience that much better. I truly wish that customer service was this good everywhere.

From the reviews I had read in the past, maybe Motofit had some issues. However, I'm confident in saying Dan has changed that and has this place on the right track. While I don't see myself purchasing another motorcycle in the near future with how happy I am with this one, I will gladly and confidently recommend this place to everyone!

If you're in the Northeast and looking to purchase a new motorcycle any time soon, give these guys a call.
