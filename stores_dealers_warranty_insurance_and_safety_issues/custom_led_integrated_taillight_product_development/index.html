Who wants the best integrated turn signal brake light on the market?



I am in talks with Custom LED about creating an integrated turn signal taillight for the Super Duke 1290 R. When it comes to our motorcycles and integrated taillights, the choices are almost nonexistent. As far as I know, there is only one manufacturer out in France who's created one and they only ship to Europe. Not to mention their product looks completely different in design from the OEM tail light.

I have personally used Custom LED's taillights on two previous motorcycles, and when I say they are the best, they are truly leaps and bounds beyond any other manufacturer. Their LED's are the brightest, most reliable, and best looking. The taillight would be plug & play with the factory harnesses, utilizing the CANBUS system to avoid any error codes.

Creating the integrated taillight would take about 4-6 months from the minimum fill date (number of pre-orders). They would need roughly 30-35 pre-orders at about $135 each to justify creating the taillight. Let's get a list of members here willing to prepay for the integrated taillight and I will pass along the information to Custom LED. Once they give me the green light, all interested parties would prepay Custom LED directly to get the project started. Add your name to the list if interested.
