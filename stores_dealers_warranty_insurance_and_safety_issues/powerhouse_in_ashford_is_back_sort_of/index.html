Hi All,

the demise of Powerhouse was swift and quite predictable - Ashford is just not big enough for 3 bike shops.....well, that's what I thought.

I got a newsletter from Laguna- the Maidstone KTM(amongst other makes) dealer, who took over the old Signpost Corner/Ducati Ashford location and turned it into Triumph Ashford - saying that their new 'Performance Centre' was now open in Ashford, selling new KTMs & Kwaks(the Laguna website has no mention of it yet). It is at the same location as Powerhouse, so I guess they are kinda back. I have not yet had the chance to get over there for a look but I will do asap.

It will save me taking the trip through the Maidstone traffic for SD service stuff anyhow....
