I tried and failed to use Laguna to buy my SD

I went to Ashford first (formally Powerhouse) They had no '08 bikes in when I went in. I was interested in the 990 Supermoto at the time. They promised to ring me as soon as the '08 models appeared in either Maidstone or Ashford. Never heard a word...in fact I'm still waiting!

Went to the Maidstone branch Had a test ride on an SD and SM. Loved the SD and wanted to do a deal. They were very friendly and helpful, but wouldn't take my Ducati as a trade in. They took all the details and said they would arrange to trade it out and would get back to me with an offer. After a week I chased them up and they said they were still trying. Never heard from them since! Not really impressed with the customer service and I'd also hesitate to use them for servicing as a result.

In the end I bought my new bike from P&H at Gatwick, who were more than happy to trade-in my Ducati
