Hey,

just thought I'd post about this as many of you ask about HID conversions. I bought mine last year from . It was a kit comprising the balast, cabling and a bulb. No problems, with it, fitted easily and the info was good.

I've noticed the price has dropped now too btw.

Anyway, the bulb failed on my way to Bracken's today, I kow it was working when I left cos I always check it ignites before i pull off. But when I got there it was out. I called them up cos I had trouble selecting the bulb online to replace it and the helpful girl who answered the phone recalled my details on their system and pointed out that the whole kit was under 2 year warranty so they'd replace the bulb for free. Time is tight for me so I paid for a new one and will return my duff one for a refund instead of waiting to exchange them.

She said the symptoms of a failed balast are the same so if the balats wont strike the replacement they'd send out a balast immediately so I was all sorted before my trip next week.

Nice service, helpful and good value products in my opinion.

That's all. and no, they arent paying me to write this...they dont even know.
