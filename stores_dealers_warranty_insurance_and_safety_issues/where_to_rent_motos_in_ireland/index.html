He SDNerd,

The Motorbike rental market is very limited in Ireland - think it's down to insurance cost Plus the market is very small - maybe due to the weather? Anyway, don't let it put you off! Some of the roads and views are amazing. If you plan to come over I'd suggest you plan it during the Irish Road Racing season. (Check Youtube for some video's if you want to know why ... You will not see anything like it anywhere else in the world!!)

This years dates are below - they are usually around the same dates each year. Most are up in Northern Ireland.

May 7th - Tandragee 100

July 9th -10th - Walderstown

July 22nd - 24th - Athea
July 30th - Armoy
August 6th - Mid Antrim 150
August 10th - 13th - Ulster Grand Prix and Dundrod 150
August 20th - 21st - Munster 100
September 11th - Killalane
