Fitted a new rear tyre on Friday (29/08 ) ready for thr forthcoming KTM track day at Cadwell Park on the 4th Sept. Whilst checking my bike over i noticed the oil sight level tube had a small crack in it on the top brass spigot part of the banjo,with a very slow dribble of oil seeping down the tube it's self.
Saturday morning (today) i set off to my local dealer (Gear 4) taking the scenic route to scrub in my new rear,within 20mins i noticed oil on my lower crash bung i stopped and checked only to find the sight tube had another crack in it at the lower spigot joint Oil was everywhere,gear change lever,frame,rear hugger and (for f#cks sake) all over the left side of my new rear Dunlop,i was not a happy bunny
I turned up at Gear 4 in Market Deeping and spoke to Scott and explained the problem,he checked his stock but unfortunately they did'nt have one in i was gutted and the thought of missing the track day seemed to loom large. I asked Scott if it would be possible to borrow one off a new bike ? he checked with Jason his gaffer who gave the permission to canibalise a new bike, and i was on my way with the new tube fitted by the mechanic within 30 mins now thats what i call service so a big thanks to Gear 4's Scott and Jason for a top notch service,Cheers

I am already on here talking about an uneasy feeling about Lagunas maidstone.

I knew the answer before I phoned but called Laguna anyway (No other Suzuki dealer in Kent open on Sunday). Not in stock, £2 something. The teenage moron on parts could get me one for Tuesday.
