Post 1 by Bink, 25 Jul 2011, 19:39

#1
The recent temperature increase had some effect on the oil flow of my scotoiler. After exiting the motorway we had a short stop and I found an 1" of oil sprayed over the left hand shoulder of the rear tyre. Backed-Off the flow rate and went on. Thankfully no mishaps but did not feel confident going round LH bends as you can imagine.

When I came home I tried to wash off the lube but it did not get rid of all the oil. Then I tried some Babywipes and managed to get the tyre cleaned up.
I the proceeded to used the wipes on the manky, lube caked wheelrims .... and that worked a treat, then took the wipes to the fly-dashed leathers which now look almost new ... Great Stuff these wipes.
