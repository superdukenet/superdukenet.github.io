Just as a trickle charger they work fine.
One logical thing to keep in mind: when you need it most like cold winter nights there is no sun....
If you ride your bike daily to work and you want it to be ready at 7 am it's useless.
If you ride during the weekends it's enough to keep the battery in good nick if the panel sits in a bright spot during the days.

Edit: Tip buy a good one, some of the cheap shitty ones actually drain the battery a bit due to crap electronics if not enough sun. Maybe negotiate full return warranty if it doesn't work properly.
