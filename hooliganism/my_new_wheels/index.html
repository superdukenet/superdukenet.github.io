After one of the lads bought my 06 Superduke off me Saturday i was bloody depressed watching (and listening!) to him driving off. The plan was to head to England to find an "R" model. Then in a fit of pure laziness i went to Cork instead and bought this. Akras sound savage on it and the little fly screen is surprisingly affective. The '08 has a lot of differences from the '06, actually surprised me how many. Back shock needs setting up though the forks seem fine. The 06 seemed to have handled a bit better but thats just because I has the suspension set up for myself. Chuffed though
