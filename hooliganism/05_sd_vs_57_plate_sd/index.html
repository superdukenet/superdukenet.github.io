hi mate, with regards to standard SDs there are two models - 05-06, and 07-on.

They have a few differences, the later models have a revised front head mask, a larger tank, radial brakes, slightly lazier geometry, new clocks, and I think that's it. from late 2008 the new models also got a black swingarm.


My first question is this - is the 57 plate an actual 07 model, or leftover stock? I nearly bought a 58 plate bike before I realised how to tell the difference between the two models!

Simple to find out - just compare the two bikes you mention, easiest way to spot the difference is the headlight mask
