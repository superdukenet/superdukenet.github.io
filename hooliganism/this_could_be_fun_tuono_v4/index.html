With the roads getting ever more congested and the old bill going to ever increasing lengths to destroy biking as we know it. I have come to re evaluate my biking priorities.

If we agree that you have, or could buy an 05 Superduke for 4 grand sterling.

I would love a base model, used 1098 but for about 8 grand, would it be twice as much fun as the Suoerduke?

Would an RC8R, RSV4 or this Tuono V4 be four times the fun?

If my lottery numbers had come up tonight (they didnt ) And I bought a Desmosedici (which I probably would) would it be ten times the fun?

Aaah but what about trackdays. Well, I dont know about you, but I am happiest riding bikes below about 8 grand. Any more than that and I am too concerned about trashing them.

So anyone who buys one I hope they enjoy it.

I would spend the money on a cheap SD and a grands worth of track bling.

A dozen UK trackdays or 3 Euro ones.

Maybe a few weekends at Moto GP races.

A cheap flight to Thailand and a dozen Thai hookers.

I would chuck the remaining 7.5 grand in the bank.

Who do you think would have the better time?
