Those seats are bitchin'! IF YOU RIDE A 'BUSA!!!!!
I don't think anyone would order a Corbin seat if you saw their process. Take one apart some time.
There is a place called Sargent's seats or something like that in Northern California. The BMW guys swear by his seats. I think that there is another guy that the BMW guys love for custom seats but I can't think of the name. If you have a BMW buddy, ask him or search some of the BMW owner sites. Those guys put some serious miles on their bikes and know a good seat when they sit on one.
The best seat makers will have you make an appointment to come by and they will mod the seat while you take it out and try it. Expect to pay.
