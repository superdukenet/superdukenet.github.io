#7
Both Titanium and stainless steel can suffer stress fractures like this, the problem can occur if any part of the system is not aligned correctly before tightening everything up,
The best way to avoid this is to be very careful when fitting the system, loose fitting the full system first dont tighten anything down, make sure every joint is lined up with no tension, gradually tighten down the headers first but keep checking the other sections, take your time getting this right and the chances of stress cracking will be reduced to a minimum.
