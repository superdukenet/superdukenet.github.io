Alpinestars SMX plus are what I wear now. Puma V1000 are really nice too. I've owned them twice. I don't think you can go wrong with anyone of the boots anyone has mentioned. The key is to try them all on and get what feels best on your foot because all the different manufactures fit different. The problem here in the US is that you really can't go to one store and try all of them cause each store usually sells only one or two brands.
