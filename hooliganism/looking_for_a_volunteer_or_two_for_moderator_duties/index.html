The reason I wanna be a mod is because I want to give back to SD.net as I've gotten so much free information and help I've saved hundreds if not thousands of real world dollars by learning here. The fact that mods get free tyres, free helmet and free gas for life does not make a difference to me. At least that's what KTMGuy told me

See for the thread that saved me big money.

I've seen people trying to quote someone elses message and get the brackets wrong. Hopefully I can edit their message and fix that. I think that is petty enough not to ask a mod to do it just to keep the threads clean.
I also don't mind welcoming a newbie and try to steer them in the right direction when it comes time to answering basic questions or teach someone how to post a pic. Sometimes I need to bite my tongue and restrain myself from answering sarcastically and just be a nice person.

I'm not really sure whats involved in being a mod. I typically log in with my laptop in the morning, sometimes at lunch with the phone and also when I get back to my office. So yeah, I'm an SD.net junkie.

Being a mod here will not make a difference in my life in the real world. Not going to get a T-Shirt with SD.net Logo with the word Mod below it. If I'm given the task (Honor?) I will do my best to keep the blog nice, neat and trouble free.

Thank you for your time,

PS: If you want us candidates to start campaigning like the US presidential debates I'm all for it.

I know, don't need to be a mod to do that. I'm going to call the thread SuperDuke Dictionary. What do you think?
