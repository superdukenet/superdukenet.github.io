I run foam in my tank.
Its not about hard cornering its about surge during braking and accelerating.
Without the foam my SD sometimes cuts out under braking with more fuel in the tank than with.
Still need to keep half a tank in at all times just to be sure.
Of course this is just for track use. Never had a problem on the road.
