So finally, after all the snapped exhaust stud adventures, I get the system fitted. Starting to like the Evo silencer shape now as well but wish I could make them carbon. I think I will have a go at this, maybe make a mould and use Carbon weave sheet. That will be my challenge for the winter.

Also fitted the Motohooligan secondary butterflies. Old screws came out easily, maybe too easily but I locktighted them back in well and good.

Will book dyno at HMRacing for a new custom map to match the exhaust and butterflies but am going out for a test ride now. Sounds good though - deeper than the old steel system.
