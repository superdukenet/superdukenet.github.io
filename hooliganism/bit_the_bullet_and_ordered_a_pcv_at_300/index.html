I ordered these through ATVgalaxy in the US for $635 plus shipping - spoke to Tessa. Hopefully they'll arrive in time for when my wife and kid are away on a trip, while I have some "garage time" to tinker.

I haven't been impressed with the local dyno operators who have zero experience or interest in providing custom advanced (two cylinder) mapping. I would rather buy the tools to do it myself at double the cost, than to pay for a standard (not advanced) dyno tune.

I can hardly wait!
I've had some really good success tinkering with maps kindly provided by Doon and Speedmotorrad, but using the front buttons on a "zero mapped" PCIII to increase or decrease fuelling to the available "low mid hi" ranges shows me that there are still some improvements to be had.
