It's no problem to do a powerwheelie on third gear with 16/40 only using gas. Between 110 - 150 km/h it comes up easily if you accelerate , bend your arms and move forward at he same time you drop the trottle so the front fork goes down...and when it turns , you lean back til you have straight arms at the same time you pull the trottle. If you have the right technique it will come upp smooth and fine.....
