The safest bet is, no doubt, what the manufacturer recommends. I also know, however, that I've run regular in numerous bikes over a substantial period of time w/no ill effect. I'm quite sure they put anti-knock additives in the gas here in the U.S. I also know it's not about performance, as that's negligible between the pump gases, for the most part. However, saving money and gaining a little oomph is a bonus. I only report what I've experienced and tested.
