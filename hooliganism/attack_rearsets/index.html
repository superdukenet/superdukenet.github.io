ssxrnr,

These are the best rear sets I've seen in person so far. Attack makes great race products and since I mainly use my SDR on the track they were the team I contacted. Scummy was nice enough to let Attack use his bike for the fittings and I tested them around tracks in Northern California. They are very well machined and don't flex unlike the stock rear stets. They due very well in crashes as I low sided my bike last year. Simply put a new foot peg on and ran the rest of the day. Also the rear brake stays in the stock location behind the frame rails protecting it in a crash, many others, like Vortex, move the rear brake master cylinder outside of the frame rails. Good luck and let us know what you buy and how you like them.

Brian
