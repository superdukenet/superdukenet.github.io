Been reading this as it goes, and was kind of torn on whether to post "goddamn you're a fooking whiner" or not ... as in "Fix it, ride it like you stole it, and STFU already!". But I didn't.

I guess this thread started out of one owner's expectations. Can't really rail on someone about that.

I blame the Japanese Big 4 for these expectations. And I blame them in a good way. Many here haven't been riding since the stone age (:oops:) when nothing moto was all that reliable, we had to mix our own fuel, use an actual kickstarter, and WTF - turn signals?

Yes, we've come to expect our bikes all to be as stone reliable as our Honda and Toyota passenger cars and trucks. I have to confess a wee bit of schadenfreude every time someone starts a thread about a dead battery, some tiny oil seepage, or some other like insignificant issue. Their misery is so slight, and somewhat misplaced IMO. What always crosses my mind is "Welcome to the realities of moto ownership".

KTMs aren't the cheapest bikes, and it's reasonable to expect "better". Then again, it's NOT Japanese, and some may argue that this is justification alone for having purchased it. Its these other qualities we all purchased our SDs for. Qualities that the Big 4 don't offer in their products. One of those is its uniqueness and what some refer to as "character". SDs actually have character. As for uniqueness, this is part of the problem, isn't it? Low production volume anything will have issues. The manufacturer can only take the engineering and testing so far before it bleeds money. Its a great thing this site was created: Virtually all issues have and continue to be addressed. And the level of expertise of those who address them rarely fails to impress. Few moto forums offer this degree of support.

I have but one Japanese bike at the moment, and the euro bikes all have their little quirks and "issues". While the Japanese bike is very reliable, the one thing I don't like about it is how frail (as in lowest-possible-cost engineering) some of its bits are. And they're no cheaper to replace than the equivalent KTM, Triumph or Ducati parts.

All said, I'm not going to encourage you (or anyone) to sell or keep their SDs. But if its a leak-free, damn near perfectly reliable moto you want - look to the Land of the Rising Sun for your bikes. If its a unique, high-end, european bike with some distinctive "character" you seek - you're going to suffer some quirks that an engineer will always respond to first with a shoulder shrug followed up with "... we never tetsed for that ...".

I wish you well in whatever you decide to do.
