no point using higher octane than the bike is tuned to run on, i've seen that issue debated so many times in forums. I ran 99 RON in my old bike for a month to experiment and it made no difference I could find.

FWIW, i had a really old Mini many years ago, and I managed to get a view into the tank, I could see the bottom of the tank, it was spotless, there was NO crap in there at all. It's probably more to do with the vehicle being lighter and therefore feeling not as smooth accelerating etc.
