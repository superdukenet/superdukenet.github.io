CADScott Forum member

Post 2 by CADScott, 07 May 2013, 22:54

#2
I have them on my 2010 SDR, like them a lot, they definitely do seem lighter, bike is more twitchy and turns in a bit easier. You need to ensure you have the correct pressures, they seem to be less forgiving of low pressure and feel squirmy with even a few PSI low, which kind of makes sense if they are made thinner than standard tyres.
They seem to have good grip so far, and they are wearing well also, so far so good with them.

I have them on my RC8 and dont really like them that much. I used to really love 2CT's but these dont seem as good. I have had the back step away a fair few times on dry roads, havent tried them in the wet, no big dramas but then I wasnt really pushing on when it happened.
