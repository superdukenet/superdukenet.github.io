#2
I put on a Suter clutch about four years ago. Second hand from a guy on this site. I think It was about £400. I was very sceptical about slippers because I'd ridden for decades without one and like blipping the throttle and matching revs to road speed. It's what riding fun is all about. But the experience was amazing. With the SD big engine braking it was often hopping its back wheel into roundabouts and bends and the slipper absolutely eliminated this trait. I have it set up so that I don't really notice it unless really going for it on the road but its a real benefiting track. I wouldn't go back to not having one. Probably the best mod I've made to the bike after the Motobox and Akras. Definitely recommended.

I still blip the throttle and ride like I used to nearly all the time. Habit. But the slipper saves me on sloppy downshifts and in extreme situations.
