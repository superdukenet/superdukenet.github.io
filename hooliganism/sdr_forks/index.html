You may have to much air pressure building up in the fork. You may need some of these or similar ones: http://www.ktmtwins.com/ktm-990-super-d ... der-valves

We need more info on the water issue to give you some advice. My bike did however do something similar. After I turned-off the bike water came out of the tube under the bike towards the front. I haven't had the issue since.
