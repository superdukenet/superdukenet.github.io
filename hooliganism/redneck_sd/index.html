So I picked up my SD last Saturday and I've already jumped into a few of the things I've seen on this site.
I ordered my integrated tailight two days before I picked up the bike (should be here this afternoon) and I just pulled the bike in the house and have already hacked up the duckbill.

I also planned on making a custom exhaust and after seeing several people with de-catted stock pipes I figured I'd give that a try as well. So right now my entire tail is in pieces with my can on the kitchen floor (I'm waiting on my buddies angle-grinder to show up later).

I dont know if I'm the only redneck to ever own an SD, but I wasnt able to find any videos or sound clips of a Duke running with no exhaust.... so I made one. Its on my buddies I-Phone, and because the service is so crappy where we live I'm having trouble getting it onto my computer. When I figure it out I'll post it up on this thread.

Now I know its terrible to run an open exhaust as it burns the crap out of your valves, but what do you guys think the minimum amount of backpressure needed would be to keep the bike running safely (while still keeping the front wheel light ) . Any suggestions on how to achieve this in say a stainless pipe split into two tips?



Like I said I’m a redneck, and I think an open exhaust sounds awesome (my first bikes were Harleys).
Am I the only one here that feels like this?
