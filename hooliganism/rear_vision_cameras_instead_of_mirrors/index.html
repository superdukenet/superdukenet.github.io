#3
I tried one on my aprilia RSV as the mirrors on there are absolute useless.

I found the camera works either wide so you see all angles but can't distinguish what it is (normal car cop car etc.) or works distance but doesn't give you enough angle.

Most of these cameras don't like vibrations neither so mounting one on the arse end of an SD should be interesting.

The one I had was easy to install as it was wireless in between camera and screen. Just put power to the screen, wire the camera on the rear light feed and all was ok.
