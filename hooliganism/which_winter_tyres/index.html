For winter riding I use Michelin Pilot Road 2's they grip well wet or dry and last surprisingly well too. Everyone I speak to has a good opinion of them. So for the winter I think they are the ones to go for.

I even used them as wets on a drying trackday in September on my fireblade. They were fine whilst the track was wet but moved around a lot once it dried. Mind you, I had tyre warmers on them so not exactly what they were designed for.

On the road they would not get hot enough for this to be an issue. Without warmers they get up to road riding temperatures quickly enough anyway.
