Replaced my 06 for an 07 in April, I was fed up with filling up every 80 miles. Had 127 before reserve light lit on the 07.

I haven't noticed a difference in the power, fitted Evo's from 06 onto 07 with re-map. The thing has more mid range than the 06 & seems to wheelie better. Stability is improved with less head shaking on some fast high lean corners. Brakes are more powerful but with perhaps slightly less feel.

Fuelling is also improved at low speed but I think the Evo's are responsible for that.

Styling is a personal choice but I like either model (black obviously).

May put on an LSL damper.

My '07 is set up with- DNA, Full Akra system, PC III, and a custom map. I do much of my hard riding at higher altitudes, so I had the map leaned out just a bit. It still runs great below 1000 ft elevation, but it also still runs strong at 10,000 ft. At home (aprox. 1300 ft), I'm getting a solid 111 HP on a Dynojet dyno.
I can thrash it and the fuel light will not come on before 120 miles, if I'm doing easy miles I'll get more than 140 miles before the light comes on. I'm very pleased with this new map!
And, no , it does not run hot. I live in the desert, and I'm very aware of the engine temp..
