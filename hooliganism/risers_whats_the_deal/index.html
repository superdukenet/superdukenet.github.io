Sammy, just make sure that any handlebar you get is a 'fat' type or sometimes called 'tapered'. This means that it will fit into your OE clamps and also fit the controls and levers. There are a few threads on this - I remember measuring all the diameters and posting them up - I think the thread is called, "Handle bar and bends". Rizoma do some good flat style bars that fit right on. And they do them in black too!

There is another reason why I wanted flatter bars that is not to do with handling and weight distribution at all - and no one has mentioned this. The SD has no wind protection, (don't even come back and say it has a fly screen!! ) so even a short blast on a motorway at anything over 80/90 mph really gets your neck and shoulders. Tried the bigger screens but couldn't live with the ugly looks. I find that I just want to hunch forward more and lean into the hurricane. Those few inches more forward make a difference I find. Fitting risers and sitting more upright would be the opposite of what I wanted to do. The SD still has a LOT of grunt to accelerate at 90 through to 120+ and I want to lean into that when I overtake cages not feel my arms are being stretched out and I'm going to fall off the back Like a few have said it's all about posture on the bike where you feel happy.
