Hi everybody
Since i am a newby here (although few handles does ring the bell from monomaniacs.nl KTMAX for example) i thought i say a few word about myself. My name is Janos i live in Hungary and got the KTM disease about 5 years ago. Had and still have an 02 Duke II and an 06 SD. Just treaded (even trade) my 05R1 for the SD. The SD has 3000 km the R1 had 6000. I got home kind of late while it was getting dark so the pics are bit dark, but everyone knows how pretty orange SDs are arent we all . i mean how can you get home on time with this thing, it is so much fun so confidence inspiring smooth machine it is scary. Handling is beyond unreal- I love it, just so much happier with this bike. I rode close to 500 km and dont feel a thing. now the modding bug has bit me. My Duke II is modded to the gill, (Akrapovic, FCR41, bunch of carbon, powder coating, really unique) and i know this bike will get the same as well. Damn i am happy with this sucker, and i knew the minute i rode the bike that this bike is a keeper, i will keep this bike forever. Did I say i am haaaaaaaaaaaaappppppppppppy woooooooohhoooooooooooooooooo[/img]
