Hi all the weight is in the original silencer and fitting slipons saves a lot of weight, perhaps up to 9kg. You will also see from other posts on this forum that the full system makes very little extra power over the cans [on a stock motor]

My advise would be to buy Akro's and have you dealer fit them along with the correct map. Advantages being, it's a KTM accesory and as such does not void the waranty or invalidate your insurance.... and there well made

Mine makes just over 121rwhp with only the slipons and map [according to the Dynojet mobile dyno]
