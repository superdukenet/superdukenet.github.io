Power wont stretch a chain, only bad adjustment. The DID ERV3 is a superbike spec chain. A superduke is only tickling it. The GP1 guys with 200bph are using ERV's for over a season with barely any stretching.

RC8 rears fit. Or you can just get your sprocket supplier to machine a 525. Talon make rears pretty quicky and you can spec any colous you want.
