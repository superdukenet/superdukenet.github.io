I think, if I'm satisfied that the SDR would make a geniune 20 or so more hp than my SD (105rwhp), that's the way I'll be going.

I also think there are certain factors that are beyond the bike's control...
a) I'm just under 247lb (112kgs) in my birthday suit, so leathered up the bike must think I'm riding 2-up all the time ...
b) we're quite high up so have a 17% drop in power. So the (altitude-adjusted) 105hp actually equates to 87 genuine hp.

This combination doesn't give the bike much chance, to be honest. The 40t did help a bit so I'm going to get one (the one SDR is sold and they're taking the sprocket I borrowed ).

12months from now the bike'll be paid off and I'll trade it, I must just be strong for the time being . By then hopefully all the 'glitches' are gone from the SDR, and I'll be able to get a full ti system and pc3 with it.

If I'm not happy with that, then I'm on the slippery slope to a turbo hayabusa and damn all the sneers!
