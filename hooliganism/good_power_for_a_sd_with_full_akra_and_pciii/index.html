Just got back from motracs dyno in ellesmere port after having my bike set up
The dyno operator said my bike was a bit rich low down but very good through the mid range up to the top
the map i had installed was for the akra slip ons with dna air filter and o2 eliminators in and it was showing 120 rwhp when i got there
he found another couple of ponies up top and managed to lean the bottom end of a touch and the bike feels much smoother low down than it was
Final figures are 123 bhp and 75.5 foot lbs torque
