those threads you found, about "conversions" ??

why would you think they were unnecessary?? They're not. If you want to put a SD seat on your SDR, you have to convert the rear.

You need to change the following;

- seat
- plastic undertray
- light, indies, numberplate assembly
- rear plastics


it's not cheap, and it's not particularly quick to do either.
