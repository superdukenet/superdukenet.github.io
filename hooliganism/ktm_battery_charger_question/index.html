#2
AGM is a Absorbed glass mat battery. They are used in dirt bikes, jet skis and so on as the acid is in a glass mat which stops the plates from touching an the battery is also fully sealed.
If you have a battery like that it is marked on the battery
The original Yuasa is a normal battery so you can use the "big" battery setting.

In my opinion you are better of getting a proper trickle charger which you can leave on all the time like a c-tec or someting. But that's just my 2 cents.
