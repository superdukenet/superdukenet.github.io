I finally got my Mojo bellypan fitted to the SDR, and I have to say I'm impressed with the build quality, fit and looks.

The moulding wasn't perfect, but it's very well made with all the brackets required, no need for fabrication or dremelling, etc... I did have to add more heat reflective padding to the inside, as it was prefitted on the wrong side, but no big deal.

What I really like about it is that it looks like it was designed by KTM and you kinda don't really notice it... It simply tidies up the bottom of the bike - which always looked a little unfinished to me. Anyway, here are some early pics. Excuse the dirt. The rain today was unbelievable.

Cheers fellas. I'm really pleased with it. I'll take some proper pics on all sides after I've cleaned her up a bit and when there's better light - and with a proper camera instead of my iphone.

Cosmetically it finishes the bike of perfectly. The two big summer tests are: will it run hotter? (with less air around the headers) and will it touch down on track days? I suspect it'll be ok on both counts, as there's still plenty of space and cutaways for airflow, and the SDR is taller than the standard SD with stiffer suspenders, so grounding-out isn't usually a general issue like it can be on the standard bike, esp with heavy pilots.

I might also stick a decal or two on it, to please DucatiPete, as he likes that kinda thing.
