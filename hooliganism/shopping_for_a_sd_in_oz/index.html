Hi Folks,

I am sold on the idea of getting a SD. I am in Melbourne and originally wanted to get one in white trim. I am told none of these were imported in 08 and they will start to come in 09. Second to this i liked the 07 model in grey / titanium / anthracite colour. Some dealers still have 07 bikes in stock i have not seen a grey one.

The speculators are reporting the economic slowdown is going to hit retail spending. It is expected this will result in overstocked retailers dumping already committed import stock to raise cash. Once the surplus stock is gone the new stock will be cost valued at the current exchange rate and the prices will be higher.

Now might be the time to go in and negotiate a good price on a bike. If anyone is a aware of any new grey 07 SD at dealers around OZ. Let me know so i can try to get a good deal.
