Post 8 by RED ZX, 28 Feb 2011, 13:29

#8
The 690 Duke is a solid bike, but you will most likely grow out of it quite quickly.

As for the other bikes mentioned here, well, I think the 690 SMC is relative to what you're used to.

I come from a sports-/racebike background. With the exception of my SuperDuke R, I've only owned pure sportsbikes (Blades, Gixxers, RC8 etc). Last summer, I took the 690 SMC out for a test ride. I found it to be the most boring thing I've ever ridden. There was simply no punch in it at all. The throttle response was laughable.

On the other hand, I took out a race prepped by KTM 690 SMR on track and found it a lot better. So, if I were looking to go for a KTM single, I'd go for an SMR and then modify the hell out of it. As for both bikes out of the box, I'm sure they're fun for some, but not for me.

i guess what it comes down to is what sort of roads do you ride... as a 690smc will be faster than just about anything (except a husaberg fs570) along a road like the dragons tail (is that right?) and any tight twistie rd... where you cant use lots of hp. As for me I love the super tight technical stuff, i can do switchovers very quickly, put it sideways but stick me on a big sweeper and its all yawn (unless chasing somone)...
