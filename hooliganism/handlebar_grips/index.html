shadowman Forum member

Post 2 by shadowman, 29 Apr 2015, 04:15

#2
Go down to the local bike shop and get a appropriate sized inner tube.
Cut to length and your set. Used over my R&G heated grips.
Never wear out the heated grips, just replace the inner tube with a fresh one whenever, as well as getting all the grip you can use.
