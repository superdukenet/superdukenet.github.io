Just thought this might be an interesting exercise,
Very simple, a good set of bathroom scales will do the job and a paving slab roughly the same thickness as the scales (To keep the bike level, gives much more accurate readings)
paving slab under the back wheel scales under the front, balance the bike upright with the lightest touch until the scales settle, write down the reading,
repeat with the scales under the back wheel paving slab under the front, write down the reading, add the two together to get the total weight of the bike,
this will also shows you the front to rear weight distribution,
I weighed mine last month with oil but no fuel.... interesting
