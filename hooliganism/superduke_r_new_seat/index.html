They cut out part of the original seat and inserted layers of foam, then covered that in a thick cover, and then another grippy cover on top.

It's actually quite soft when you push down in the middle, but you still feel the edges of the actual seat against your thigh, due to the shape. I don't think anything can remove that.

I did 160 miles yesterday on the standard seat, and was actually ok - so with this upgrade I'll happily take this bike to the south of France now.

...and yes it's fully waterproof.
