Hmmm. The fact that it occurs after maximum fuel demand is interesting; I'm running the same setup (Akra map, Akras, but also a PCIII). Best guess: sick fuel pump, ECU glitch not turning fuel back on after closing throttle, bad TPS, loose ECU connectors.

To follow on a stream of consciousness; engine requires max fuel flow under WOT; then goes dead lean (20:1 or higher) on closed throttle. Plugs still firing on every cycle. Open the throttle back up, ECU kicks pump back in, takes a few moments for injectors to repressurize and send fuel to intake stream, intake to hit chamber, combustion to occur (snatch). Why the delay; on the fuel side, lack of fuel in the lines/cavitation at the pump=delayed delivery; on the electronics side, something affected by physical/electronic demands - weak plugs, weak coils (ahhhh, maybe not so much, I'd expect them to crap under WOT); engine still turning under own momentum, but then dies and locks up?

Whiskey Tango Foxtrot?

Motor is still turning even with throttle closed. So why does it lock up?
No stutter, just "takes a while to catch" when throttle is applied or stalls and locks up rear wheel .

Ahhh, I can't put a finger on it, 'specially twixt the electronics and fuel delivery side of it. Anybody else have another angle? Like a tipover kill switch? ZenBikers List Of Reasons A SuperDuke Would Stall:

1) Bad Clutch Switch (brilliantly deduced by MotoHooligan)
2) Restricted Intake - can't breath, can't run
3) Restricted Fuel Delivery/fuel starvation - can't eat, can't run, can take the form of electrical/electronic gremlin and/or stupid stuff like kinked fuel line, clogged fuel line (see: Lutefisk), clogged tank vent building up vacuum as fuel pump sucks out the fuel, and other things that I can't think of.
4) TPS sensors out of calibration sending ECU over the edge
5) Girlfriend is from Jamaica and practices Santaria and has placed blood curse on you for a perceived or actual infidelity.
6) Ignition component failing - seems to happen after heavy duty cycle
7) Loose electrical connector - why only after hard acceleration


now transitioning to stream of consciousness
Seems to have something to do with hard acceleration. What happens at WOT? Fuel pump demand spins up, plugs fire more frequently, engine rpm climbs, oil pressure rises (hopefully), electronics heat up from resistance/heat possibly leading to temporarily open condition, fuel tank temporarily pulls vacuum if demand for fuel volume exceeds vent capacity; let off throttle, bike goes into lean mode for pollution control, ECU cuts fuel to the injectors, bike is hot, tight parts sieze momentarily.

OK, question: you're on the throttle hard on a hot bike. You get off the throttle and the bike stalls. What effect does opening the throttle have? How do you have to restart the bike? Does it ever restart as in a bump start after the rear tyre locks up, or do you have to either by necessity or desire have to come to a stop and hit the starter button? Do you have to cycle the ignition?
