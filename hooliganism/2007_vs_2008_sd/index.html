#2
Assuming the 2007 is a 2007 not a belatedly registered 2006 then there's no difference at all between the 07 and 08.

things to look out for that are simple and obvious are the black swingar, different headlight mask and radial calipers.

However there are some bikes out there that are a mish mash. e.g larger tank but silver swingarm etc.

Ask lots of questions, if you find a bike, even post it on here and ask for opinions etc.
