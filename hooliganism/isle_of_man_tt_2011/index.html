Use google maps to search for a campsite - having said that, at the TT there will be farmers opening up fields with porta cabin toilets just for the event. Don't expect much as the good sites will have been fully booked. Most of the night life is centred in Douglas, but find time to look round the quieter parts of the island as well. Use this trip as a sighting lap and book up what you like the look of for next year - you will return.
