#3
As an alternative viewpoint to BUZZ, I just fitted Stompgrips in December and really like them - surprised at how much better it makes braking and stops all your weight going onto your arms. I've had to change my habit a bit about squeezing with my knees when braking hard but if you do then it makes a huge difference and lets your arms be more free and relaxed for steering and stuff.

Not been on track yet this year but I reckon they might well add something to the hanging off technique, give grip to the outside leg as you hang off the inside.

Why do so many track day bikes and pukka racers use them?

Personally I like the look of them as well but that's just my opinion. ( I grew up with British bikes like Triumphs and BSAs so rubber tank pads were the norm!!
