As i mentioned previously , noticed KTM uses coatings inside their engines. At least under valve covers and inside of head valley etc ....

I'm currently building up a 73 yamaha enduro and going to try this stuff out - thermal barrier for intake and exhaust ports - piston crown - combustion chamber - intake runners - valves ...

I'll be spraying the top of piston with it and combustion chamber and intake port inner. Will NOT coat the polished/massaged exhaust port so i can make a comparison of carbon build up or no build up between the two surfaces.

we'll see how this turns out.

The Yamaha's running a Webco head ( high compression ) so pre-detonation is possible , the coating is supposto help prevent the hot spots on a piston top reducing the pre-detonation possibility .......... same with the combustion chamber.
