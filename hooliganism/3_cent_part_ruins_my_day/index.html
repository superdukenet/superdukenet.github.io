Post 1 by Katoom, 02 May 2011, 03:36

#1
Was on an early morning run to the Rock Store and had just turned on to Las Flores. The ocean was smooth and calm and it was looking like a great day! Pulled over before the twisties to take out my jacket liner (that's right...it's warm in SoCal finally!) when all of a sudden I have hot radiator fluid all over the inside on my left leg! I decide to turn around and head home. There goes my perfect ride day. I nurse her home checking the fluid level several times. Turns out one of those damn 3 cent POS hose clamps KTM uses broke on the hose at the top of the thermostat. I can't belive my bad luck! A standard hose clamp never would have gone bad! I'm now on a mission to replace all the stock POS clamps with standard ones. I knew I didn't like the look of those things the minute I saw them. Damn 3 cent part ruins a perfect day. Oh well...at least it's a simple fix.
