hmmm ... I rate it a 9.9/10

also came from an off-road (& then supermoto) background, this is the funnest bike i've ridden. period.

been riding mine to work every day for the last year & a half, i always enter the office with a huuuuge grin my co-workers hate me on monday mornings.

have'nt had any reliabality issues with mine, but the usual gremlins are:

1. starter makes explosive sounds, almost every owner who has had this happened to them has got the starter replaced under warranty at no extra charge.

2. the clutch -mainly on 05 models- was also covered under warranty. I've had no problems with mine yet, so could anyone shed some more light on this?

3. low speed throttle twitchiness. problem solved with akrapovic cans & re-map. i'm not saying that it's a great fix (seeing as the akras go for 1000euros at best), but the weight savings are huge and the bike runs so much cooler (the stock cans run at about 600Â°C). Not to mention the power & sweet sound

4. best bang for buck mod once you get the SD: 16t front sprocket.... completely transforms the bike and helps ease the throttle twitchiness a bit. shorter gear ratios are a blast, as noone wants to ride at 250km/h on a naked bike like this

are you getting the R model or the normal 07 Superduke?
