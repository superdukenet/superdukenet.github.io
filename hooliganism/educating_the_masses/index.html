I've just returned from FE's 3 day event at Jerez.

It was very well run and we were lucky to get good weather and loads of open pit-lane track time.

I'm pleased to report that a great deal of riders were very impressed with the SD's performance.

It's no suprise to find that most of the GSXR/ZX10/ etc riders had no idea what the SD is all about. Most seemed to think it was a under-powered speed triple!
