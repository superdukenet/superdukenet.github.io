I still have a 625 SMC it is not that different than the new 690 as a matter of fact mine is faster than a stock 690. The main difference in the new one is it is much smoother and seems to have a little more top end.

I could not imagine trading a SD or an SDR for one of these. It would be like pulling the V8 out of your race car and slipping a 4 banger in it. For it being safer on your license, The SMC is too much fun to wheelie and slide around corners. As someone said they are much better on gravel roads and that is the only place that it will have an advantage over a SD.

Good luck and I'll see you at the dealer within a year to get another SD.
