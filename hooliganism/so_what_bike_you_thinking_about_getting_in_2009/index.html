Having ridden the RC8, I think the 1190 engine is too fast for a naked bike. I'd prefer that KTM continue to refine the 999FI engine in the current SD.

Don't get me wrong, I'd love an RC8, but only if I lived by Brands or the Nurburgring. ... I got into nakeds to slow down - well, ride quickly more smoothly, and at legal speeds - but the 1190 engine won't assist me there, and top speed would render the bikini fairing of the SD a joke - unless they make it with one less gear? LOL. ...The venom fairing solution is ugly too.

Give me the 125bhp engine from the SDR with less snatchy fuelling and I'll be happy.

The standard SD has an ugly rear end that now looks dated. KTM need to redesign the rear to look better with a dual seat with SDR lines.
