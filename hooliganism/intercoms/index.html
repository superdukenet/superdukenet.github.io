I can't praise Intaride kit enough bud.
Not bluetooth headsets, still connected by curly leads, although the intercom module can be connected to phones and stuff through Bluetooth.
They do do a wireless kit, I just can't justify the expense at the minute.
http://intaride.com/
Customer service is SUPERB!
I phoned up asking for a diagram to connect me headset to an Autocom kit as the training school I started working for use Autocom.
They phoned me back an hour and a half later to say the email address I had given them didn't work, could I give them an alternative.
Half an hour later, two emails and a further phone call to make sure I had recieved it.
