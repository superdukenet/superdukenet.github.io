As posted elsewhere, the KTM Ergo gel seat is an improvement, but not a transformation. It gets better with time though and it looks great.



An alternative is to recover your original seat and get a gel insert added. It turned my last seat into a properly comfy seat, and it was durable, grippy andd looked good IMO.



Whatever you do, DON'T get a Corbin seat. They "cup" your arse, and stop you hanging off. They also add 2 inches to the seat height of the bike, and IMO they look horrible. They are also stupidly expensive. I know, cos I had one.



Yuck.
