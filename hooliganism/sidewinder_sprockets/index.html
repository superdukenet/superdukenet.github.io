I've had bad experience with their customer service as well.
In fact still having it.
Ordered a full set (front/rear/chain) on the 3rd of may, received package on the 7th of July. It was an international shipment (US to Belgium) but still just over 2 months is a long time.
After unpacking everything I notice it was the wrong color chain and the rear sprocket was also for another bike. I had ordered in May to be sure to have everything before heading out to the alps for my vacation, so it was a major headache, seeing as my old ones were worn. So I had to go out and buy another new rear sprocket locally. The thing is, on their website they say that it is not advised to use the smartchain with anything other than their own Ti sprockets (they will most likely use this to rid themselves of any waranty claims as well).
I contacted the guy I had been dealing with for the order and shipping, explaining all this. Average response time has been about 2 weeks so far. They did promise they would send me a complete new set free of charge, but so far this has been but words. My last response from them dates from the 29th of July, nearly 6 weeks ago.

The quality of the product does seem to justif the price though. It's just not worth the pain of going through the order process.
