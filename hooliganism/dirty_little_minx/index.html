There are several places a 990 loves an oil leak from. Base gaskets both front and back. Oil pressure switch between the cylinders. Gear sensor below output shaft. Output shaft seal has gone on me before. Think the one on the SDR might be weeping. Best way is to coat the areas in talcum powder and go out and ride the arse off it. Come back and you should be able to see where new oil has leaked into powder.
