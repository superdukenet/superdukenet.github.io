#2
http://www.telferizer.com/

The bike is not listed on their website but just order one of the following bolt sizes depending on what you prefer.

Tank Bolt - M8 x 55

Handlebar clamp bolt - M8 x 40

I've just fitted a zumo 660 to mine and I think I actually prefer the one that comes with it mounted on the left bar, it positions it perfectly so I don't have to really lower my head away from the road to see it and doesn't get in the way of tank bags, speedo etc.

There are pictures of all the different mounts in the gallery under accessories or something like that, I think it was pingvin has his like mine on the left bar and Plipton has pictures of both tank and handlebar clamp mountings.
