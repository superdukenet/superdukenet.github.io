#4
If you know how to ride in the dirt you will be fine. If there is going to be a lot of loose surface a Scotts damper can help things a bit. Stock tyres are fine on hard pack. You would want something with a higher sea to land ratio for anything else. Not a whole lot of choice in 17s. Bald front tyres are shit for braking on gravel so favor your rear. Take it slow until your comfortable. She steers nice with the rear. Standing on the pegs when surface is rough helps a ton.
