Scotty Forum member

Post 10 by Scotty, 05 Sep 2018, 07:57

#10
Yes, those "silencers" (word used advisedly...) look exactly like the round Akrapovic ones on my old 990 road bike, carbon wrap aside. That, too, was deafeningly loud when I bought it and a pair of baffles (or dB killers if you prefer) from Pipewerx made it far easier to live with - an easy direct fit but unlike genuine Akrapovic baffles they don't have a female thread in them so I used regular M6 nuts with a dab of loctite on the threads - they haven't budged in 3 years. The baffles were a sniff under £20 each.
