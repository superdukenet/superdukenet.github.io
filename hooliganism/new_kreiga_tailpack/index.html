I got mine from VCUSTOMS http://www.vcustoms.co.uk/motorbike__kr ... 18630.html
After a lot of playing around I found a very good way of mounting it. Its going back on tomorrow ready for NW200 so ill take a load of pictures and put them up tomorrow. Instead of just using the seat to secure it I used web loops on the subframe and cut small slots into the rear side panels (hidden away under the seat) to feed the web loops through, they can stay under the seat permanently and just hook on the quick release hooks when needed
