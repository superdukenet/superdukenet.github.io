Just read in MCN an article from someone who mentions that on his supersports bike - he had fitted Akro and power commander and had it tuned and dyno'd and as well as improved response and power was getting much better MPG

I had mine set up a couple of months ago at my local dealers ( Powerhouse - sadly no more !! ) -
I am very pleased with the improvements but have experienced a reduction in fuel economy - to the tune of about 20miles less per tankfull - is this the norm ? Again is this the norm ?
