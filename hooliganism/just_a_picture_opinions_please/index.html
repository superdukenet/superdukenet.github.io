I agree with you spataro, I felt I needed some team colours too (and maybe give my viewing audience a notion that it is actually a KTM) I didnt really like the lack of orange on my black SD (and the orange one had too much IMO) so i fixed it by simply put orange vinyl stickers over the top. I did a trace of the decals onto paper, scanned it in, vectored the graphics and cut it with a vinyl plotter. I stuck the vinyl on so it was slightly off to one side of the original to give it a tiny drop shadow. I had to redo it a couple of times cos the vinyl I used wasnt quite thick enough, and the black background behind showed up a little bit, making it look browny. Fixed it with a nicer blockout vinyl! I think it really did the trick... Next thing to do is orange up the spring.
