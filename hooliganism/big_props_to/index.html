Rottweiler and Pro Line Race Prep in Lake City FL. Very long story short, I have a 2007 990SD that sat on the showroom for 10 years... Fast forward I bought it, did extensive opening up mods. Tons of fuel varnish in the intakes, I got it close but not right. realised that the injectors were partially clogged, tried to get new ones, but not available for the 990. Rottweiler has a beta-test injector kit that uses 1290 injectors. Installed those, was running super rich, but Chris at Rottweiler patiently pointed me in the way of a Powercommander with his map he's been using with his setup. Wow, so much closer, but as he's at altitude still not 100%

Took my bike to Pro Line, another great guy, Vinnie, put my bike on his dyno and got it amazing! Buttery smooth throttle now. Running about 115-117 RWHP at max. Without going nuts, I get big front wheel hops on every shift, still learning the bike so don't want to go too crazy yet... Anyway a happy ending to the story!
