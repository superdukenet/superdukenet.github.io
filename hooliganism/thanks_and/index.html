I want to thank all of my brothers and sisters here on the forum for many reasons. A big one is making the parting out of my beloved SD race bike pretty damn easy. I didn't have to resort to craigslist or ebay or any of the other places that a teeming with losers. I was spared the pain of those site because some of you lot purchased so much of my bike's parts.
I got to meet a few of you in person too!
Today a guy came by and bought what was just a hulking mess of a once bad ass motorcycle.
I do still have a few bits in the "parts for sale" column that I would like to see gone. Offers??


I also want to thank you all for the words of encouragement as I struggle with my injuries and try to have some semblance of recovery.

An American website which is very popular, which I love and who also happens to sponsor my team, they have started a new forum called "Riders on the mend".
Bay Area Riders Forum or BARF
I have been asked to share my story as it unfolds and thought some of you may wish to follow the story as well.

http://www.bayarearidersforum.com/forum ... p?t=371444

I am writing Chapter 3 at the moment and see the doctor Friday so I will have a Chapter 4 very soon, no doubt.

Thanks again to all you SD'ers!!
