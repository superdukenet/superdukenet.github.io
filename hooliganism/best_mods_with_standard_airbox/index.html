For those interested we ran my SDR on Kev's dyno on saturday. His advice was it you are not changing the airbox leave everythings as standard which is exaxctly what we did in the end.
Started of at 110bhp with Akra map and full Evo System. 40 mins later we see 115bhp, lovely flat torque trace and 79.6bhp at 6000rmp. Kev reckons its the strongest stock airbox output he's had on his dyno. And he's done a few if this forum is anything to go by.
Ran a treat and Anglesey, just wish I was as fast as the bike
