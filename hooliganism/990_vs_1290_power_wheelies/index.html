Yes.

A lot of people run a 41t rear sprocket (stock is 38t) so that makes the throttle a vertical lean indicator in the first 3 gears. Of course once it's had an aftermarket airbox, exhaust and custom tune, it's even better. It's also easier if you set the sag on your suspension, so your shock isn't eating a lot of that inertia.
