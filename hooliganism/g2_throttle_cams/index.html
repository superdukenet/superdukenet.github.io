Post 7 by Colonel_Klinck, 17 Nov 2011, 16:41

#7
Your throttle being snatchy can be solved by other means than a new cam. Your O2 sensors, stock fuel map, and stock airbox all contribute to the problem. Unless you're 100% set on keeping your engine stock I'd address those first. I replaced\disabled all of the above and I actually bought a bigger cam which increased the responsiveness because my bike was so smooth.
Colonel_Klinck Forum member

Post 8 by Colonel_Klinck, 17 Nov 2011, 16:44

#8
Don't think there is a supplier in the UK still There is either th US or Sweden I think. I bought mine from the US. TBH longer cams just disguise the problem. If you really want to get rid of it, get some O2 eliminators or tune them off in the ECU with TuneECU. Some after market cans, Motobox and custom map is the way to be rid of any hint of snatch and release the full power of the beast. I went from a 200 cam before mods to a .25 which is some 20/25% faster than standard. Love the short throttle and wouldn't have her any other way
