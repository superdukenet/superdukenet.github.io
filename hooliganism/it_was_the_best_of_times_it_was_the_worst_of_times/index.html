Shade, thanks for the PSA. I've had my share of rash, but, thankfully, its been some years since the last occasion. Your post is a reminder that it can happen at any time - even that putt around the block. ATGATT.

A good antibiotic creme like Neosporin or some Bactin (has Lidocaine) will take some of the sting out, speed healing and reduce scarring. As for getting the bandages off, rubbing alcohol should help dissolve the glue and a quick tug is usually less painful than a slow peel. You may also want to shave the areas where the tape will stick if you re-bandage. I used to be an EMT many moons ago. Heal up soon!
