I feel a bit pikey (but comfortable) having moved to East London. Here there are lots of Texaco petrol stations vs the usual BP etc.

I tried their ''high octane'' fuel and after 3 tanks to verify, it seems to have a remarkably good effect on my fuelling vs BP ultimate or the occasional fancy Shell stuff;
It is now almost impossible to get my bike to stutter, unless under 2k or something and mid range power seems a bit fatter.

Any other users have feedback?

I don't think the ron level is particularly high so do they have other nice additives or am I making it up? I did have a poor impression of Texaco fuel before trying this out.
