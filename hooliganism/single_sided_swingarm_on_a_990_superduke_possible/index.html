Hi guys,

I was wondering if it was possible to put a single sided swingarm on a 990 superduke (with adaptation work indeed) and if someone already tried it.

I search Google and the forum but didn't find much.

I remember a post with a guy mounting a Ducati swingarm with exhaust but I was not able to recover it.

Thing is that I found a guy who is selling his swingarm on "Leboncoin" (craiglist) for 480€ due to a slide but it was repaint... so I told myself that it was pretty cheap and that 990 will look pretty awsome with a Single swingarm and rear wheel of 1290 !!!

Things that comes to my mind in term of fitting will be :

- Depth beetween the 2 parts of the frame maybe thinner on 990 (how much ?)
- Alignement of front and rear spocket / chain
- Space on the swingarm for exhaust tube (maybe torse the tube a little
- Alignement of fixing point for rear suspension (lateral / horizontal)

That pretty much like it... parts needed will be the swingarm itself, the wheel, axes, rear breakdisc and caliper and sprocket.

If someone allready think about it
