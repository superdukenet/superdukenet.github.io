I have the WP damper. that came with my Race Pack.

If I had a choice I would have chosen the Scots. Its great to be able to adjust it on the fly, instead of having to pull over to make adjustments. The WP mounts under the tripple clamps.

It also appears that the Scots has a wider adjustmant range.

You need to research them as they function a bit different.

Scots / Ohlins are bullet proof, and hold up very well.

I've heard alot of talk about leaky seals on GPR's.

I'm not sure if you Dirt bike or not, but theres tons of info on the dirtbike sites about Steering Dampers.

I believe theres a thread hear about a guy who just fitted a scotts on his SD...
