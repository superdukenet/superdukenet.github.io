Took the SD to Devon this weekend. Was fab weather for it and rather than go down on the train with other friends I threw my rucksack on and headed south west.

Basically did 630 miles through Devon, Somerset, Wiltshire and Dorset. As always the SD ruled and took any road in her stride. Been a while since I've been round North Devon/Somerset coast and forgotten how beautiful it is. Also Exmoor National Park is stunning and offered up some fab roads. Really should get out and explore GB a bit more often.

Next time probably not get a bit pissed at night with m8s either. Riding with a slight hangover on high banked roads that some dozy tourist has decided to park mid corner to take a picture isn't the most sensible thing in the world. There was an abundance of Mojitos every evening though and I just can't resist a Mojito.
