#1
I just switched over to new gear this weekend. I was wearing a 38 on my rear and switched to 40. It made a marked differance. Where I was exceeding the speed limit at full loft, I am now able to lift the front to balance at at least ten miles an hour slower. I had a certain amount of trepidation when I first installed the extra teeth, but much fear was put to rest when I realised just how much smoother the loft had become. I do not clutch up as many do. I have found that the power is plenty and I myself think that plates wear faster if the are spinning at a differant RPM than the engine. There is much talk abount the advantages to clutch up and I am not one who cares to go that way. Sooooo, let the games begin.
