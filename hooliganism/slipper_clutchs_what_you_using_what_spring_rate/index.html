Post 7 by bic_bicknell, 04 Apr 2011, 19:00

#7
I've got a Suter as well. Got the middle spring plate fitted as I really didn't know what to start with. Love it....but I think that I will never be a good enough track rider to even come close to thinking about trying one of the other springs - this one seems fine and it's great for road and track. Maybe one day I'd just swap over to see the difference but I bet I couldn't. I was on it today in London traffic and down shifting quite heavily at the approach to lights so I didn't have to use the brakes. You can really drop it a few gears at a time and pretty much dump the clutch - you get the slight juddering feeling in the lever but that tells you the thing is working and it's a seemless, smooth deceleration without all that squirming about that I remember from the pre-slipper days. Really great bit of kit, you can still be a clutch/gearbox junkie and enjoy the riding experience but it just makes things better - not robs you of control.

PS. It's a shame that this adjustment is not easier/quicker to do or that it isn't infinate and remotely adjustable like a steering damper. But maybe one day?

Just fit one and enjoy it is what I say.
