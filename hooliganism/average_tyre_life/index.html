I have my factory Diablo Corsa III on and have noticed it is wearing quickly.

I inspected it before a 200km ride this weekend and thought I should get another thousand kms out of it.

Apparenty I was wrong. I was fueling up for a 500 km round trip today and found steel cords showing !!!!

Trip cancelled, but glad I caught it. The crazy thing is I only got 3900kms out of the tyre. Apparently the high speeds take their toll.

I found a somewhat local supplier with it in stock for $285 Canadian. I figure I'll run the same till the front needs replacing and then maybe switch to a longer range pair.
