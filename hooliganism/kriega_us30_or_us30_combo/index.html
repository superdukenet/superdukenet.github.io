Post 2 by hebgeb, 02 Feb 2013, 00:01

#2
I have US 20 & US 10. I am using US 20 as a courier bag, it is so right size and comfortable. Last time I flied with Ryanair and had no particular bag for it I used also US 20. It is the pretty universal bag for any life occasion if when you need it, besides motorcycle use US 10 I have used once, but with US 20 it gives nice addition to it; and their straps connect well. However I would not miss US 10 much in case I would not have it; US 20 has the best possible "real use" factor.

I've got the US 20 and 10. Love them. The 10 sits on my tank and fits perfect. Don't need to remove it to refill. The 20 is great for most day to day stuff. And with both together I have all I need for trips away. For me the combo is the way to go.
