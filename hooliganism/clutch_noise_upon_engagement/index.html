Hi there-
I have an '08 with 5.1k miles, Leos and Akra map - runs great - no real issues except I get a loud audible chatter / "chuka-ckuka" noise only during clutch engagement from a stop / rolling stop in first gear. There is no actual vibration felt through the bike or driveline and the clutch engages smootly with no chatter etc. I don't lug the motor and it still does it even if I try to slip the clutch more than usual. It is worst when leaving from a stop on an icline. Thoughts? I'm thinking maybe loose clutch hub bolts?

You may want to check the clutch plate bolts. (Know issue for them to be loose and back out) I dismissed the idea as a fluke for someone else, until I heard an a awful noise coming from the motor which I felt through the clutch. Pulled the cover and one of the bolt had backed out and was rubbing against the cover.
