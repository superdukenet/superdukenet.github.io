I know it's not our Superduke R. Believe me.. But at least it's a KTM.

We just got back from AFM round 3. Epic battles. Almost never at full grip. Always sliding, pushing, spinning and drifting. Amazing experiences. Our RC8R, still an 09 model, is greatly outgunned by the fastest of the fast in the AFM, but we are clawing our way closer to them. In one section of Thunderhill's back section yesterday, in a short climb up a hill out of a wicked-fast left handed sweeper that you go through nearly full throttle, the inline jockey ahead of us kept spinning his head back to see WTF was behind him. Lap after lap this bulldog kept chasing his heels. We'd lose on the straights and fight back on the brakes. What an epic set of battles.

Here's a few pics from the weekend. What an experience, riding a bike like that. Guess that's what comes when a bike is properly sorted. You can read about it more on our blog if you like.
