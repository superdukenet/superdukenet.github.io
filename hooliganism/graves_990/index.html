Well here is my review of the Graves exhaust.

The way it mounts to the bike is 100% better than Mivv's solution. The exhaust sits in a much better spot so there is a ton of clearance (tyre would constantly rub on Mivv's), they sound great when riding around but when you snap the throttle to make them bark there is a loud rattle and rushing sound. I assume that's due to how the "DB Killer" is done, it's basically just s smaller hollow tube at the end of a large hollow tube. So the exhaust gases are smashing into the end of the can then making their way out the smaller hole, they also sound very fluttery on decel.

I mostly hear the intake when I ride anyways so...
