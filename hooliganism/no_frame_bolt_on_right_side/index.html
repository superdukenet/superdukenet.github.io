The tab is most likely a hanging point used when they finish coated the frames. Dont ever attempt to tap the hole in the case and tighten the tab against the engine case, there is too much movement at that point and you will damage something.
I have made a reference to this in my "how to" tread - re building a bellypan for the 1290. I ended up removing this tab when fitting my pan.
