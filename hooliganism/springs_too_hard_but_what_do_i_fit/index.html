#7
I also have just installed .85 front and 150 rear on my 07 sdr with akros.
If you are using it for road use mainly you should be ok, I am 70kg with no gear on and have set the front at 40mm rider sag.
This is with the preload all the way out, on the rear I have set it at 35mm rider sag, running close to comfort settings with a few clicks here and their.
You are a wee bit heavier than me so a 160 rear might be better, but a 150 should work.
Our roads are very bumpy too and the bike is now compliant to ride.
