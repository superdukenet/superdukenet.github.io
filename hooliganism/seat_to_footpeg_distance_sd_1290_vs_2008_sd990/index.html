Post 8 by scamb66, 29 Sep 2015, 02:57

#8
I found the riding styles to be very similar, easy to hop straight from one to the other without any difficulty. If you are comfy on one, you should be the same on the other.
The 990 is a little taller than the 1290, the 1290 feels a little more planted and doesn't feel as light on the front end under hard acceleration.
The 990 is no slouch but the 1290 is in a totally different league when it comes to power delivery
Having said that the 1290 is also has better fuelling and is easier to tonk around through traffic. Although my 990 had a bit of a rawness to it that the civilized 1290 can't match.
Both are awesome bikes.
