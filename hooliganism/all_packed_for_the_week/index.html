I'm heading up to the out-law's cottage for a week with my wife and son. I wanted to bring the SuperDuke for the great Ottawa Valley roads. My son wanted his XR50R there... but he also wanted me to have my WR450F to ride with him up there. I wasn't sure I'd fit it all in my utility trailer and was thinking the 50 MIGHT fit sideways behind the two big bikes so I swapped my supermoto wheels on... but no go.

Angle the back of the big bikes out a bit and tie the rear wheels to the side of the trailer and voila, room for the 50!







Of course now I want to put the dirt wheels back on the WR seeing as I don't need the room!

I haven't packed a single item otherwise... the wife is not happy with me.
