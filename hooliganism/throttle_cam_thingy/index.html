You shouldn't need a throttle cam if you've done all those mods, should be running smooth if it was tuned right.

The G2 throttle cam just increases the throttle opening making the power come on less instantly than the standard cam. The G2 does however come with three cams so you can easily change cams without taking the whole throttle body apart.

I have one, it doesn't make a lot of difference.
