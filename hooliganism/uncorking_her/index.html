Just got a call for the shop and my baby is ready to be picked up tomorrow. After carefully breaking her in, I dropped her off for the first service.........and the full akro exhaust. The shop ( cycle improvements ) are experts with ktms and have a complete engine and tuning shop so I went the proper route. Pc111 and complete mapping. They called and said the full system has 4 hp over slip-ons but the real tweaking was mapping the cylinders individually! Apparently they were surprised at the difference form the front to the rear cylinder. I'll have the full review tomorrow!!

They also left out the Db killers because he claims it's not overly loud. They dyno'd it with them in to and it lost 2 hp!
