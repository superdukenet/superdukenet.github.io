I had the opportunity to demo an 08 MV Agusta BrutaleR from Pro Italia over the weekend and all I can say is WOW! The first thing I noticed is how compact the bike feels, not as roomy as the SD but at 5'11" it felt right. The steering is so tight and fast it is mental, even quicker than the SD. The inline 4 was just begging to be revved to access the 136 ponies. If you think your SD has snatch, try the Brutale, it lives up to its name! I wish I hadn't gone for the demo but I hear cost of ownership is ridiculous so I won’t be putting one in the garage anytime soon. Anyone else ride one of these things?
