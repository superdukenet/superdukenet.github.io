Up the road on the way to the good bits is a straight that must be best part of a mile long and as we all know straights are boring on a superduke so I stood up on the pegs and rode away up this road on the back wheel,I was on the back wheel for long time but only mention the length of the wheelie because I think it may be something to do with the outcome as when I put it down I had the mother of all lock 2 lock tankslappers first it went lock 2 lock for what seemed like forever then almost righted itself before somehow starting again for another half dozen slaps before finally coming back ,It was massive,I had given up on it and had lost a hand off a grip and was ready to faceplant ! the noise,the banging,the violence!! fooking heck

Anyhow if there are any tankslapper veterans or knowledgeable people out there I have a few questions in no perticular order.

Does the likelyhood of a tankslapper increase when you land a wheelie stood up ?
Does the likelyhood of a tankslapper increase when the front wheel isnt rotating anymore ?
Why has it never happened before ?
Do I really need to be having moments like this ?
Why dont the bloody gopro have a light on back to say its recording ?
Will it happen again
