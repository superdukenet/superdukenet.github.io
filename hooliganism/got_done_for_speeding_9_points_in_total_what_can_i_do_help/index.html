Thats a hard lesson learned there mate, but the Magistrates Court will always give a harsher sentance than the one you were offered through the post. The whole point of the fixed penalty offer is to free up court time and reduce overheads, hence making it tempting for you to just plead guilty by post. If you had a chance of getting less points by going to court and saying sorry (which for speeding is impossible as it's a minimum of 3 anyway!) then everyone would be doing it.

To be honest I'm gobsmacked you got the offer anyway - 47 in a 30 is banning speed really (and rightly so in my opinion). I'd say you got off lightly.
