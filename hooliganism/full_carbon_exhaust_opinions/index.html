Well I finally got these last week but since the bike is 60miles away in winter storage I didnt get a chance to fit till last night.

First of all they were well wrapped and seemed ok although one of the baffles hole in the caps was not drilled so I could access the retaining screw. Not too much of a issue as I was looking for something reasonably quite. Very very light. Not too sure on the link pipe either, should it not be more of a Y-piece.


Anyway got down to fitting and everything was ok however the cans were pretty damn tight to get on and didnt sit quite level. They did seem ok once everything was on. The cone's are just resting on the rear mudguard. Few crappy pics of it in the garage. Couldnt start it up as it was about 11pm.




Anyway I was busy all day but managed to get back early fo a quick blast. Started the bike up and bloody hell they were loud. These are sold as road legal but to me they werent. Waited for the bike to warm up and took it out for a quick blast. They werent quite so loud when gunning it but in and around town there no way road legal. Far too loud for me and thats with the baffles in place although they are only a couple of inches long.
I cannot ride it like this, its too loud for me. Now I do like the look but all the littl niggles have put me off. Im going to see what the policy they have on returns either that or better baffles. It needs something like the Arrow ones with a closed end pipe and perforations along its length.

Post 9 by Zenbiker, 31 Jan 2009, 20:29

#9
All carbon fiber is not created equal. Lesser quality pieces will crack and discolor with heat and exposure to sun; also, there should be a metal reinforcement or rim for rivet/screw attachement of the endcaps and baffle bodies, otherwise vibration will cause them to shred the holes and become loose. Having said that, I'm not familiar with this particular brand, so I can't say one way or another. I had SATO pipes and cans on my RC51, Dan Kyle swore they were less problematic than the full Ti cans, so your guess is as good as mine. The noise is a whole other issue, and the fact that one of the holes for the baffles screws wasn't drilled doesn't speak well for this company's QC.

Finally, big bore vtwins really challenge the limits of carbon cans by virtue of the high volume exhaust pulses. Unless you really trust this company, I'd be leery.
