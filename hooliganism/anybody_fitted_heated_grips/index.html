#21
I've the Symtec Heated Grips installed as well.
The rocker switch sits really nice and flush on the dash.
It's easy to install. Just follow the instruction.
I have it wired up to the Accessory connection under the seat. (it's controlled by the ignition switch so doesn't drain the battery).
A handy tip for putting the original grips back - use hair spray as it help to slide them back on and helps them stick when dry.

They work OK on the high setting - not too much help on the low setting.
