Hello fellas. I was wondering about the best choice for a 1290 SD. Most of the time it will be on roads and some-few trackdays. I used to own an SD 990 and with Rosso 2 I was not afraid so much in the rain. Being said that I was always avoiding rainy days because the torque of the SD was too dangerous in wet greek roads. Apart the wet grip, Rosso 2 was a great tyre for me in the road and also at the trackdays. So I would go another round with this but I know that Rosso Corsa has better grip.

So I was thinking that with the new SD and all of its electronics, driving to wet roads would be much safer than with the 990. So should I go to the Corsas or stay to the familiar to me Rosso 2. I am just thinking of Corsas because I think the new SD's electronics will help you in terms of mileage and wet grip, where Rosso 2 is better than Corsa.

Hope my words make sense, and looking forward for your replies.

Cheers everybody
