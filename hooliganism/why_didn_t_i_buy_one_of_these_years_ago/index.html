Had my first proper run on my 990 SD today and it is quite simply the best motorcycle that I have ever ridden! The suspension is controlled yet supple, roads that tied my old Speed Triple in knots barely faze this thing. The front end can be leaned on as hard as I dare (and I'm over 100kg!) and the Brembos are superb. I had also forgotten just how fantastic a big twin is on the road, such drive and traction and I love the way you can feel every power pulse .

Its so quick, agile and blatantly a wheelie machine. I cannot wheelie, but this bike can

I think I may have found my perfect bike. Time to stock up on parts, this one is a keeper!

Yeah, if you ride the right roads they are great fun bikes.

There are folk that like long straight a roads and doing silly shit on motorways, for them sports bikes are better.

There are loads of fellas that like to wobble off to the local bike meets and play top trumps with their latest and greatest HP4 / RSV4 / Panigirly. Whatever is featured in this month's Superbike magazine is best for them.

There are plenty of rich folk that spend their leisure time dressing as homosexuals and creeping around on Harleys, hanging is too good for them.
