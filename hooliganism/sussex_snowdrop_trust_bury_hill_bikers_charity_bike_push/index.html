The Bury Hill Bikers are a Sussex based motorcycle forum who support a charity every year. This year's events are in support of the Sussex Snowdrop Trust.

The Snowdrop Trust cares for children in their own homes, from birth to nineteen who have a life threatening or terminal illness. The Trust provides specialist paediatric nurses and the support team offers, practical, emotional and financial support for their families.

Our first charity event of the year is a sponsored 5 mile motorcycle push around a very hilly course in arundel park on 10th May. I'm one of the team pushing the bike and thought I'd take the opportunity to have a cheeky ask for some sponsorship - Whilst I'll no doubt love every second of pushing a heavy motorcycle for 5 miles in blazing sun, it'll be all the sweeter knowing that terminally ill children and their families will be getting some vital support as a result.

We have set up a Just Giving account at and I'd be really grateful if you could take the time to pledge a little support. Every penny counts and no amount is too small (or indeed too large!!!) If I raise more than £200 I'll even do it in full leathers! Every penny received from you will go directly to the Snowdrop Trust to help them with their invaluable work.

Many thanks
Toby
