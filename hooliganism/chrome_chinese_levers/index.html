There is more to this than you realise, the Chinese do not have these bikes here to copy parts from, they are not importing parts to copy either and they would not front the tooling costs themselves anyway
No the reason these parts exist and are being sold on the internet is that manufacturers or aftermarket parts suppliers are sourcing these things in China, yes OEM

Most of the Japanese bike manufacturers source parts in China. I know for a fact Suzuki source their titanium valves in China, BMW to, probably many other manufacturers are now doing the same thing, titanium valves are expensive to produce, China is one of the main titanium producing countries in the world.......

The problem with buying these parts from spurious non liable internet sources is that what they are selling will be coming out of the back door of these OEM Chinese factories,
Great you may think they are OEM standard quality at a bargain price, Noooooooooooo, they are most likely to be seconds, parts with slight faults rejected during factory QC or even worse from a bad production run rejected by the customer

This type of business is huge in China on the internet and even in high street shops, its normal practice for Chinese OEM factories, no one is liable here and there are no enforced safety standards,Nothing any factory manufactures goes to waste, nothing even if it is dangerous........ there are no morals, no waste, the only thing the Chinese care about is money

This is not supposition or opinion this is fact, I have spent most of the last 11 years working in China as a designer and product developer, I have seen these things with my own eyes hundreds of times....

Be careful out there in internet land
