I don't own a slipper so i can't comment but I do own a motobox. Its a awesome piece of kit. Lots of plus points and not a single negative that i can find.

Makes the SD into even more of an animal, especially feels like it in the high middle.

Makes it sound like an animal The induction roar is just plain rude. Over 7k I can't hear my akras over it. Truly mind blowing sound. Motoronin rode mine for 10 mins, was laughing like an fool when he got off at the noise and ordered one that night

Opens up the top of the engine, exposing the throttle bodies that just look fabulous.

Suppose the only point that could have a down side (financially) is that you should probably get a PCIII and custom map to go with it. Mine was burning quite rich when it first went on the dyno after motobox went on. Custom map delivered a load more torque and hp
