helllo folks ,just fitted the moto hooligan kit , ie the air box,sas plates, throttle flies and resister, which i purchased as a complete kit, followed the instructions listed on here via a laptop with it downloaded onto , sat in the garage to follow step by step which was invaluable and made it completely bearable . i also have fitted akros, with the remap from my dealer , 02 eliminator plugs, and 40 tooth rear sprocket with longer chain to fit, and in total all these mods, make it a super smooth power house , no i repeat no jekiness on throttle and smooth delivery of great torque, i havent fitted a power comm yet ! wondering if to do so yet , only concern is running too lean but with the ktm akro map it delivers gob fulls of fuel . anyway if anyone out there is thinking of doing these mods i can honestly say yes ,more power ,noise , and most important to me is the smoothness of delivery of power. as for fitting only annoying bit was the front sas plate fitting which no doubt you have read about, but i am a patient type and just perserverence got it done, everything else is done word for word off the instructions , bingo..next job clutch cylinder change to oberon upgrade ,,
