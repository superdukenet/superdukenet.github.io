Diablos mate. i had Pilot 2CT's on my Buell and was not a fan, wet grip was good but I didnt like the profile or looks.

Standard Diablo is a great all rounder sport/road/default tyre.

Corsa is stickier in the dry and not good for wet riding, will last less miles than standard Diablo.

Strada (just fitted) is a harder compound, supposedly more miles, best for wet grip. Personally i doubt I'll ever push it beyond it's dry/warm grip limit....so I'm looking forward to wet grip and savings! My first ride was in BAD rain, the tyre wasnt scrubbed in but it already had MORE wet grip than the Corsa I had before. Confidence inspiring!!

#12
Another option for good dry riding and even better wet riding is de BT014.

In the latest test of Sport tyres (Belgian motorcycle mag) the bt014 got the 1st place, 2nd pp 2ct and 3rd place was for the diabolo III.

They said that the bt014 wasn't that much better in the dry than the 2ct from Michelin, but that the difference in the wet was incredible.

quote: "it felt like the bt014 pushed the water away!" "the bt014 gives a lot of confidence in the rain and is as good as the PP 2ct in the dry"

From experience of a friend of mine who rode the bt014 on his xtra raptor: excelent tyre, but it was almost if the rubber disapeared after each ride. He only did 2500 km's with them. He rides a SD now and don't want them anymore because of the small mileage.

Here in Belgium a lot of sporty riders choose or the PP 2ct or the bt014, just because of the good handling in the rain + they behave very good in the corners and on trackdays as well.
