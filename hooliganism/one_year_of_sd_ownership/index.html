Well I’ve had my 09 Superduke for a year now, and I’ve done around 8000 miles on it and spent 6 days on track. I can honestly say it’s the best bike I’ve had, it makes me smile every time I ride it. Reliability has been good, it’s never actually failed to start although the clocks failed, and these were replaced without quibble under warranty.

Like most people on here I’ve modded the SD quite a bit, it was totally standard when I got it. I’ve stayed away from the bling mods and only added or changed things that make it better than it was!
I’ve only got one more thing to do to it, a custom map with Tune ECU next week for my Motobox and I’m done!

Here’s to another year of thrills!
