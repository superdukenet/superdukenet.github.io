Hello all. This is my first post on your forum. I've been reading many of the posts and after all the raves about the Superduke, I had to ride one. My local dealer had a demo and was willing to let me take it out. I have only ridden sportbikes up until now so at first, the upright riding position was strange. Once I got underway however, it was amazing how well I could see everything and over the traffic as well. As I got out of town and hit some twisties the bike came alive. The way it handles is amazing. As you're looking forward, the bike disappears beneath you. It is hard to describe the feeling you get when you are leaned over in a corner with this bike. It is just intuitive. No thinking am I leaned off enough, where is my turn in point, where is my apex... you just gas it and you're arcing the most amazig lines through the corner. I even tried it out droning on the highway for a few miles and found the windblast tolerable and the comfort excellent. I rode my current ride there and took some pics of both the bikes together. I am sure I am going to add this to te garage soon. Also wanted to ask the advice of the board. It is a demo 2007 with 3000 miles on it and I can probably purchase it for $10,000. What do you guys think?
