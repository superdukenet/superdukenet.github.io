#7
Now that everything is wire tied, I don't do much more than a quick go over. The tech inspectors make sure I didn't miss anything.
Before the safety wire I would check the brake parts pretty thoroughly. I put a dot of paint on each fastener that I thought might come loose. You have probably seen this from the factory too. I have about 20 paint pens for this, each a different color. When I remove a bolt, I use another color when it is tightened again. When it starts to look messy, I wipe off the paint and start again. Bear in mind that this may look pretty stupid on a street bike but I always put function before form.
