I noticed that the KTM factory touring windscreen bears an uncanny resemblance to an Orc shield …Like the ones carried by the big Orcs ( the Uruk Hai) that the heroes fight at the end of the first LOTR movie...the one the biggest Orc throws at Vigo/Strider and pins him to the tree with it. Maybe the film maker looked at Germano-Austrian design to come up with these semi-crystalline forms (But the metaphorical “horse-power” of the Mechano-Cubist sculpture Great Horse ( 1914) by French sculptor Duchamp-Villon, also comes to mind)…
