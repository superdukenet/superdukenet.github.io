They are meant to be the same animal.....only made in USA rather than UK (see site below)

http://dunlopracing.com/D211GP-A_PressKit.pdf

They are my weapon of choice for track days .....grip better and last longer...endurance rear seems to grip just as well as medium.........but last's alot longer (I use soft front...no issuses )

Front 30psi Rear 19-22psi COLD

Watch out for cold tear....where you need to drop pressure .....people confuse it with hot tear and increase the pressure and cannot understand why it gets worse

I found that the 190 rubbed my swinging arm at speed (ok static) ...but I was running standard chain and sprockets and 40-16 gearing (short wheel base....so wheel closer to engine / narrower part of swinging arm) they do grow
