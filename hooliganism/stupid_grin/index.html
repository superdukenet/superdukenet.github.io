It's really likely that this part is manufactured in ABS. Like most of these materials you could easily get the matt finish by using wet and dry with a lot of water to take off the gloss finish. Just be prepared to take a lot of time and use a really fine grade. If you use too coarse grade you will make scratches which will take forever to remove. If you treat it as a paint finish and go carefully you'll be able to get the matt look you want and if you want that semi-matt finish that KTM parts have you can start to T-Cut the surface back up to have a slight lustre. The skill is getting an even finish across the whole surface. And the really hard bits will be the three recessed areas where the fixing bolts go - I would do the insides of these first before you even touch the outside surface.

And one last thing.........test a little bit of the process on one of the undersurfaces before you take the wet and dry to the visible part - just in case I'm totally wrong about it working.
