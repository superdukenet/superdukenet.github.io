Post 5 by bic_bicknell, 14 Aug 2015, 07:07

#5
It's pretty hard to find matt finish parts as they have only recently become favoured. I have matted down all my parts by hand and they look great -much easier to keep looking good because they just need a wipe down with a WD40 soaked cloth and it also helps to make lots of different parts made by different manufacturers look uniform. It's a LOT of WORK though! Start by using different grades of wet and dry until all the shine is gone - you'd be surprised at how many imperfections there are in carbon gel coat so keep on going until everything is matt. It won't look that good when it's dry - sort of powdery white finish but you can see what it will be like by wetting it. Then I discovered that the best finish is with wire wool. You just go over every surface again using circular motions and it clears away any small linear scratches left by the coarser sandpaper. Finally I used a spray of ACF50 which brings a dull shine to the whole finish. You put it on then more or less take it all off again but it adds a protective coating and makes the dark colour come out more. I think I had a week off work and did all my bodywork, single seat unit, mudguards, belly pan, and all the extra little bits. But it took all week!
