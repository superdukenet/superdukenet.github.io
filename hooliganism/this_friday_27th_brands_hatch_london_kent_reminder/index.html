There'll be at least 3 of us riding Superdukes at Brands this Friday (me, Shade and Hawkman) so if anyone else is around and wants to book-in, or even just turn up and be our brolly dollies, please do... Clinky has threatened to stop by, and perhaps Weeksy too...(?)

Weather forecast can't make up it's mind, but it is supposed to be getting warmer and drier after this Thursday... so fingers crossed.
