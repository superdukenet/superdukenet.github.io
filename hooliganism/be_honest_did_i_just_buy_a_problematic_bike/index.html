I don't think so. This despite the issues that some, including myself, have had.

The major parts are very good, now that the rear hub issue has been fixed.
All new models, including BMW, have problems the first year. My main gripe is the way KTM addresses these problems in some areas.
After a slow start KTM Australia addressed the hub issue correctly by replacing them all. That some places in the US still say a few mm of play is in within tolerance is unacceptable.

What KTM needs to work on is improve the quality control and response time when there is an issue.

Otherwise this is a great bike, what else comes even close??
