I never use a torque wrench on those bits.

Axle bolt - non metal to non metal - tight but don't stand on it.
Pinch bolts - don't do much - many a fork bottom has been the victim of over tightening - nipped up tight with a short wrench.

Caliper bolts - nice and tight!
