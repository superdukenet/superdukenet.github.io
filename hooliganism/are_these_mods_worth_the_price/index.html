Experience has taught me that, unless you are going to get the heads gas flowed the only mod that will help you is to free your airbox up a bit.
No need to spend loads of money on a DNA3 or Motohooligan: just buy a K&N panel filter and screw it to the hole you cut in the top of your airbox using self - tapping screws. We know from testing that it is every bit as good as a motohooligan but for $30.
