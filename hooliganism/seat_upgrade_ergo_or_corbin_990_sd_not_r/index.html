#3
I have the standard and the comfort seat.

I like the standard seat for everyday riding and commuting. It's wider, flatter and doesn't kick you into the gas tank.

The comfort seat is much better for aggressive riding imo, the face of the seat (the top most flat part) is skinnier than the stock seat. Although it has more padding and provides less feel/feedback it just works better for me when leaned over. The comfort seat is also shit for 2 up riding as it kicks you into the tank and angles the passenger down towards the driver. I noticed on longer rides the comfort seat created some pressure points on my legs where my thighs contact the edge of the seat.

The ergo seats can be had pretty cheap now though, I've seen them go for $80 on ebay in good condition. So it's worth a try.
