Lesson learn today. Dont crash your bike. It's a feckin nightmare. It's not just all the obvious damage, it's all the little bits that go along with it. So here is the bike as it sat at about lunchtime. Subframe is still a tiny bit out, but this exaggerates to about 3-4mm at the tail end. Will have to spend a fair bit of time getting cans/panels and seat lined up.


Had to pretty much remake the dash as it was twisted and ripped. Filled in a blank to cover hole for ign. Replaced ignition barrel with master switch, then covered with carbon effect vinyl. Should look pretty good once mask and instrument arrive.

1st time working with vinyl wrapping. Almost starting to ge the hang of it now but it's really frustrating. Looks ok from a distance bit it shows every tiny ripple or imperfection in the panel underneath. A few little creases but generally it good enough for what the bike is being built as.

It's a cracking little bit of kit. Loads of screen colours. Shifts lights are adjustable etc.

It is wired-
Red to ignition live
Brown to good earth (you'll have to extend this wire down to the battery, yoke isn't a good enough earth.)
Blue goes to the blue/white wire from ecu to tacho (3pin plug on right)
Green goes to speedo pulse signal. Just put the new mask on and forgot already. It'd either the black/orange or the brown/black in the 3pin plug coming from speed sensor. Just poke an led test light up the back of the plug with it all plugged on and ignition on and turn the wheel. You'll see a faint on/off of the led lamp as the bolts pass the sensor when your on the right one.
The white is for set up. You touch it to earth to cycle through the options and set everything up. I still have to set it up for gears but not got a clutch master cyl yet so can't ride it. Dunno how accurate it will be.

Dunno if anyones still reading this thread............

......but if you are........ clutch master cylider arrived today. So finally got a run on the bike.

Feels good. Everythings tracking straight, no strange handling or anything untoward.

Set up the gear display. Just to note to the guys who are doing this.When you set it into gear inticator set up mode you have to go within a few seconds of activating it or it shows a fault. Touch white wire to earth to go into set up, touch it again when it shows gear display selection, then go immediately. If you go a bit higher up the rpm the more accurate it is. Dont have to go nuts, just to about 4000rpm in 1st, then it'll flash to second, speed up a bit, and repeat through the box. Just need along quiet stretch of road to do it right.

Very very impressed with this little bit of kit. It's super accurate. Even trying to trip it up it instantly shows the gear. He only way to confuse it is to roll along at 2mph with the clutch in and blip the throttle and it will jump up to3rd, then instantly back down to 1st as it works out wat your doing. But out on the road riding it changes instantly at any rpm.


Bike feels good with the improved version of the homemade quick throttle i made. The thinner Reanthal grips on Bash's bars feel nice too. New longer levers instead of the stubbys i ran previously are alot easier to ride with especially on braking. Spent a lot of time properly bleeding out the clutch and brakes to get the levers feeling good,

Removed the secondary flies and shafts. Another very good mod, bike picks up quicker off the throttle and has a noticable amount of low-mid rpm power

Might just be in my head but it feels lighter. Had a few mid hight wheelies and just kept snicking it up the gears with the quickshifter an it felt effortless where as before it sometimes felt like it needed a bit of a handfull to keep it up there.

Very happy with it.
