Costs more than a superduke?! Not where I live..
The nuda is a couple thousand cheaper than the SD here. I had a ride of the Nuda R a couple weeks ago. Its up there with one of the best handling bikes Ive ever ridden. Better than my SDR. The brakes were over the top (so I loved them) and the suspension was great too. The bike has dirtbike foot rests and desperately needs rear sets as trying to change gears was incredibly awkward when combined with the seating position.. Otherwise your boots are going to get burnt up by the pipe. Speaking of the pipe, the stock Nuda R pipe sounds awesome! Couldnt believe it was a stock pipe. The motor however.. I found it a bit boring .. It didnt excite me at all. Felt a little doughy down low and didnt have enough midrange punch for my liking.
Still a cool bike!
