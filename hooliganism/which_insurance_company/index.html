Post 15 by ShadeTheChangingMan, 20 Jul 2007, 13:08

#15
If bike advanced training is anything like cars, you would be better off with ROSPA. They teach to Police standards and the test has grades, which grade you get depends on how long you go without a retest. IAM, whilst I'm sure do fine work, only test you once with a pass/fail system, also the instructors are 'volunteers' who have passed the test and now pass on their knowledge. I'm sure a lot of them have qualifications, but it's not a pre requisit. At the end of the day it's down to: Do you want the best training possible, or do you want something that lowers your insurance premium. ROSPA will do both, IAM only the latter.
