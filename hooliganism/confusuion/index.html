I dont know for sure if this is the case, but i think people are confusing a "snatchy" throttle with a "sensitive" throttle. To me, a snatchy throttle means the bike will surge, not be able to hold a steady speed and RPM, and be very jumpy. But this is not the definition of a sensitive throttle. Case in point, my bike: I can hold a steady RPM no problem, the bike does not change the speed, it does not surge, it runs just fine at low or high RPM's. This is not a snatchy throttle, but it is sensitive. Just going over a bump, with the movement that causes in my wrist, will cause the throttle to open that tiny bit more, and the bike responds to it. Then the throttle closes an instant later, and the bike responds as well. It is annoying, my jap bikes never did this. But that doesn't mean anything is wrong woth the fuelling, it just means the throttle is sensitive. There is a big difference between the two.
