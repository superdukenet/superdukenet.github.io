Got some duc porn from evil bay, stumbled on this looking for injectors.
I am amazed by the development Ducati put in to this, well made and according to them the oval throttle bodies matched up with the oval inlet port shape was good for a fair increase of HP and torque.
This lot is supposed to be for a 848-1098 model. From the markings on the butterflies they aare the equivalent of 56mm and the injectors are the above the inlet trumpets conical spray pattern with 12 holes pintel model. The injectors supposedly (again) should be 550cc and good up to 170hp.

All in all nicely made and I'll have a go at mounting the injectors on the RC8 stacks in the near future.
