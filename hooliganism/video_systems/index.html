i use a VHoldR, but im about to sell that to a friend, and upgrade to the VHoldR Contour, which records 720 HD. Its easy to use, and best of all, no wires...

Its got a double sided tape mount that u can put on your helmet or somewhere on the bike. I bought an extra mount and put it on the bottom cowl where the '990' sticker is. Made for some cool videos.

Unit is all self contained, and pretty durable. You will need to buy a larger memory card tho, the factory one is too small.

http://vholdr.com/

Hope that helps
