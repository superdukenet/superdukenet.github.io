You guys may have seen my mate on here posting 'Stumpy'

He is an avid Gixer lover and adores his K3 1000. However is getting a little disillusioned with riding on the road on a 150bhp machine that's only happy when screaming along.

So he did 20 miles on the KTM today, before hand he wasn't a lover of them and said "i really wanted to hate it"....

But he loved it...

I'll let him post the rest, but i rekon after our Spa trip in Sept, we may well find him shopping for a Superduke in black !! (he hates orange LOL )

Considering how little he liked the KTM before he rode it, it's a hell of a shock that he now wants one....

Although, everything he said about it, is the same as we all say... THe turn in, the handling, the 'toy' like perception of riding it, the wide bars....

Come on Stumpy fella... give the boys a report on it
