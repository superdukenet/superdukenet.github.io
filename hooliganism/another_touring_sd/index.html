I bought some new, small, saddlebags recently and decided the best thing, as I have akra slip-on's, was to make some heatshields so the saddlebags don't melt and fall off!

The heat shields are attached to the bolt that holds the akra's on(the one that goes through the carbon fibre "strap") and also to the three small screw holes in the exhaust, these hole are small and are for the small curved carbon fibre "bling" shield that KTM make.

Worked a treat , took less than 3 hours to fit. The heat shields were made by a mate as a favour (I bunged him a wedge though)

The heat shields stay cold when going along, air gets between the exhaust and shield

They're only fitted when I'm going touring, and need the saddlebags.

I know in the foto's they look wide but they're not much wider than my waist

The only time they get warm is when I stop, after a while the heat from the exhaust warms them as there's no air going past, but they don't get quite as hot as the akra's and I generally take the bags off when I'm booking into a Hotel
