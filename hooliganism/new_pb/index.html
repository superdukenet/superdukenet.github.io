I (absolutely did not officer) hit a new PB last night on the way home from work. Now I'm no demon .....I commute the same route a LOT....but last night there was this chap on a Bandit 600 who I see on the road in the city a lot, he races around making loads of noise and giving car drivers hell. He was particarly aggressive towards one chap in a Vauxhall at one point; hitting the rev limiter (clutch out, rolling along at 3mph) and spewing black smoke over me (I was behind him). So, anyway....I'm kinda following him heading out of London and I just got fed up with his on/off throttle control so I backed off a bit, hit my natural rhythm and was then up to me usual speed. Caught up with him and that was it....clearer roads ahead....hit the gas and he coudnt keep up. I could tell he was trying to too. I was having a blast with a nice tail wind so it felt like i was going slower than i was...look down and I'm tickling 136 on the speedo. nice. eased off. Funny how 96mph feels slow after that eh?

in summary: i fucken LOVE this bike, more and more every day. I swear it's the best thing I ever bought....
