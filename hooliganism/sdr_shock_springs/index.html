160nm rear and 90nm front spring on 05-06 SD
170nm rear and 95nm front spring on 07-present SD
180nm rear and 95nm front spring for SDR (all years)

These guys usually have a rear 170 spring on ebay give them a call they might have what your after:

http://www.ftrsuspension.co.uk/

If you weigh less than 12 stone maybe the 170 rear will be better for you, id leave the front forks alone.
