Some on here have/have had the wheels and say good things about them, but unless you are very skilled (I'm not) the wheels may make no practical difference to you. One of the regular complaints is range given tank size, but I only see people bitterly complain when they are going on long trips with others and are worried their buddies will be cross. So, unless you are skilled or regularly ride long distances with grouches, I would get the cheaper bike - especially if you think fatty may have abused the more expensive one. Then as weeksy says, you'll have $2,500 for beer.

I'd try to get them to bid against one another to see if I could get the cheaper bike for even less. (And, I love mine and have spent hours getting my parking lines just perfect).
