One of the most frequent causes of oil seepage or leakage is due to cleaning of the screens during an oil change - they must be replace perfectly - in my case one was not sealed perfectly and it continue to leak until I could get it fixed properly- unfortunately it's difficult to locate the precise source of the leak -

So, if you have recently changed oil and cleaned the screens you might check on that being the culprit - Mine was a royal pain to fix -
