I have been following this thread when it started as its always good to price shop when purchasing any high price item. I read it again today and didnt think much about it until riding home tonight. I have had several bikes in my 20 years of riding. This is buy far the best bike I have ever ridden. I know everyone says the same thing but I love this bike so much I searched out and joined a Superduke forum just so I can learn about the bike and meet other people that I can talk Superduke with. Once I started riding and fell in love with my SD, resale value was of no concern of mine. Im sure there are people that paid less than I did. Im sure I got a better deal that some. What matters is I feel I got a fair price for a quality item. I ride my SD every day now. I ride to work, go to the store and do a couple 2,3, and 4 hundred mile rides on the weekends. After 3 months of ownership I have over 5000 miles on her. At this rate I will have over 20,000 miles in my first year. I usually keep my bikes for 2 or 3 years with less than 10,000 miles on them. I will have that in the next 3 months. What kind of resale value will I get for a high milage bike if I were to sell it in 2 or 3 years with 40,000 or 60,000 miles? Dont care, I know I will have had a blast with each and every mile. Wheeliman, you cant go wrong with this bike. I never thought I would own a bike like this. My R1 got stolen 2 years ago and I had been holding out for the 1098 Ducati. I flew to Colorado to meet with my boss and do some riding on his bikes one of which was an 07 SD. 4 days after I got home I had an 08 SD of my own.
