I havent done much touring but I've been logging my fuel consumption to see how it performs compared to how I ride it.

On average I get 10-11 l/100kms on the weekly commute.

But when I do longer rides I've gotten between 6.6 - 7.7 l/100kms and thats with about half of the trip being twisities the rest highway/freeway.
On the highway, long straight sections I'd whack it in 5th or 6th gear depending on the speed limit and let her chug along at low RPMs.
Not sure it helps but maybe you'll get more of an answer from someone else?
