Post 11 by loony888, 22 Jan 2011, 04:39

#11
Brakes are one of the few parts of a bike that makes me nervous to fiddle with. I frequently refer to the manual and torque every bolt to the proper setting when I have them off or apart. I don't think I'd stray from the manufacturer's choice of material and hardness. However, I do use anti seize on the button bolts on floating discs. I apply a small amount and torque to the proper tightness. In 10 years I have never had one loosen, and it makes taking them off a breeze when necessary. Anyone who has had to heat those bastards with a torch and hammer at them with an impact wrench knows what I am talking about.
