Cannot believe how piss poor the quality of the plating is on the fasteners and banjo fittings on this bike. KTM should be ashamed. And I don't even live in an area where corrosion should be a concern.

Since I've started my SD beautification project (wheels, rotors, rearsets, etc) I've noticed a lot of corrosion and started replaceing the offending fasteners (axel pinch bolts, rotor bolts, etc.). I've noticed my brake and clutch line banjo fittings and bolts are rusted at the master cylinders. Looks like shit!

Brake lines will be no problem as I can source them from several different manufacturers. The clutch line could be another issue as it appears to be a smaller diameter than the brake lines. I obviously want them all to match for asthetic reasons and am thinking black lines (SS with black sleeving) with red fittings and blue banjo bolts. Anyone know of a source where I can purchase both? One stop shopping for clutch and brakes?

Any help would be appreciated.
