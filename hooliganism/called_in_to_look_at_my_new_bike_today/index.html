We go to Northern Spain a couple of times a year, empty roads, fantastic surfaces, fantastic scenery, nice people. This time it's Santander to Aviles, A Coruna, Vigo, Ponferrada, Gijon and then back to Santander.

We used to have a luggage vehicle as well which was great, this time though we are carrying our own luggage. Last Trip in September I had a major off went off a mountain, lost the front end Broken collar bone, fractured shoulder, broken rib, crushed and fractured vertabrae in my back T12, 9 hours on the operating table and loads of titanium in my back and I'm as good as new. The Spanish emergency services and the Spanish hospital were fantastic.

I have to get back on the bike and go back it's in my blood, My wife thinks I'm barmy and shes probabley right.
