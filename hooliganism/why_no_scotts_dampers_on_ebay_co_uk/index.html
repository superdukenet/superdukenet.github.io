That's interesting Freestyler!

Because I was really hooked on the SD concept in '06 but just couldn't come to terms with the front headlight cowl design thing. Just seemed to me to be an afterthought, like the designers had run out of ideas. I imagine that they were all sat round having lunch, a bit of Austrian beer, a few gurkin things, bit of sourkraut cabbage etc. and a nice wedge of germanic, orange cheese. Suddenly one of them holds up a nice hefty 30 degree slice of cheese and says, "This is it! A simple wedge of orange will suffice for that bit on the front we can't be arsed to design!".

And so the first generation of SD came to be.

But when the SD became a commercial success, (despite the ugly nose), those lazy fookers got fired and some better designers got draughted in to put on a bigger tank, get the steering geometry fixed, add the radial brakes, and finally sort out a design for the headlight that matched the awesome "edge/stealth/unique to KTM" styling of the overall bike.

Oh, how I will get attacked for this comment. But only by pre '07 owners who have inferiority complexes.
