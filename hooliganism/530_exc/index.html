Valve chexk intervals are not that close on an EXC mate, thats air filter cleaning and oil changes. Unless its an SX in disguise....in which case AVOID.

I really wouldn't recommend a 690 as your first off road dude. A friend of mine just bought one, he is a pretty good off roader and a strong lad with little fear. He had it on maximum setting for a while, like 2 hours, that got turned to normal after it tried to kill him. Even that was too insane and it's now on the softest map setting. That is still pretty insane off road and he says when its not trying to kill him its trying to rip his arms out of their sockets. It's got uber torque and it can kick in when you really don't need it and get you in a lot of trouble. It's also a big heavy bike.
On the road with SM wheels it would be maximum setting all the way of course and would be a fooking great laugh. Off road I really think you would have you hands full and would get far more enjoyment out of a much smaller bike. It makes my 450 seem like a pussy cat and its anything but! Even a 450 I think is too much for a first bike. I say that from the experience of doing exactly that. With hindsight a 200 stoker or 400 would have been plenty and I'd prob be a faster rider if I'd bought one.
