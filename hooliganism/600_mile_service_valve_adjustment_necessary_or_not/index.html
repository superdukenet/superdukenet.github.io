I had it done, the valve check that is. The valves were within tolerance, but the hardening had delaminated from the cam lobes, and the edges of the valve buckets had crystalline fractures with pieces missing. So, I got a new set of cams and buckets as part of the first service. Not sure what it might have looked like, or if it would have made it 5000 mi.

Here is one of the cams with less than 600 miles on the clock:



A close up of one of the buckets.



Sorry for the crappy pics. They were taken on my i-phone with poor lighting an no flash. The buckets looked much worse in person. The white areas on the edges of the buckets were sharp and crystallized. I'd guess the buckets failed first and abraded the hardening from the cams.
