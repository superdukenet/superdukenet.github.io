Battery life seems to be all over the map. The OE Douglas battery in my '95 BMW M3 lasted 14 years. The OE Yuasa in my '97 Bandit 1200 went 12 years. Most of my bike batteries go 8 to 10 years. The OE GS battery in my '02 FZ-1 is still doin' it's job. It never really gets too hot up here in the mountains but the overnight temps can be frigid. It was -27F here on 4 February earlier this year. I hook my car up to a Battery Tender+ every night, and my bike batteries get charged one hour per week on Battery Tender Jrs.
