Dealer: All American KTM
Score: 11

Purchased the motorcycle sight unseen (phone/email), my first communication was with Trevor in sales. What I appreciated most was his professionalism around the transaction; he was knowledgeable and straight forward, and very pleasant. I also had the opportunity to speak with R.J. (proprietor) whose experience with the brand, model, and industry was felt, it was obvious that both of these gents had been with KTM for some while. They were beyond courteous/mannerful, and handled all of my inquiries without a fuss, the purchase transaction couldn't have been better. R.J. even went as far as delivering the bike to me (7:45am as promised, in the shop KTM truck, he was even wearing KTM orange) along with going over the features and the like, his helper Kenton (sp?) seemed like a very nice young man as well. The team at All American KTM gave me much confidence and assurance in the entirety of it all, so far so great with the ownership experience, I'd highly recommend them to any prospective local and non-local customer. It should come of no surprise that they're one of North America's best KTM dealers out there.

Thanks, All American!
