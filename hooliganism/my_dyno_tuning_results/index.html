#1
My local tuner(Craig "Huey" Stewart at Marietta Motorsports) tossed in a PCIII on the Super Duke and then spent a couple hours tuning a custom twin cylinder advanced map for my KTM.

Bike now puts down 115HP at the wheel, up from 103HP in stock form. Below is a chart of 4 dyno runs:



Blue is bone stock bike
Green is Leo Vince slipons and a map loaded into the ECU from the KTM dealer
Purple is the same as green, but with the secondary butterflies removed
Red is the final run with the Power Commander, Leo Pipes, flies removed and custom map

So for about $1200(pipe, PCIII and Dyno time) we have a 12HP increase(roughly 8% increase over stock) in power, more torque, and the throttle response is explosive. Just hit the gas and the thing just goes ape. Nothing smooth and subtle about it, it finally lives up to the image that KTM markets it as, a brute of a streetfighter, raw, etc...

Nice work Huey!
