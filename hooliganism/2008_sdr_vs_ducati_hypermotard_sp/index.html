I currently ride 2008 SDR but have been looking at the updated 2013+ Ducati Hypermotard SP. Used those could be find in $10K range and I have been really thinking to say goodbye to the SDR and get HM SP.
Hypermotard SP comes with ABS, TC and few different riding and ABS modes. Also, starting 2013 those are liquid cooled with service intervals of 18K miles, which is much better than before. I love the SDR but the new HM SP is so amazing reading the spec. A little over 110HP and 66TQ. Which puts it very close to the SDR numbers.

What do you think?

I know this probably wont help but in 2007 I demo'd a brand new 1100 Hypemotard, a used 950 Supermoto and a brand new Superduke back to back and despite owning KTM's for over 30 years I ordered the Hypermotard, it had considerably less power than the KTM's but I preffered the overall feel of the bike.
Unfortunately mine turned out to be a "Friday Afternoon" build and it suffered multiple warranty and recall glitches so after 1800 miles I bailed
I now have the 1290 Superduke and I can honestly say I will struggle to find something better (for me).
It is of course 100% personal preference when it comes to bike choice so I would advise a few test rides if you can.
