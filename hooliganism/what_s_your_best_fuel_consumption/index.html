I've gotten up to 200 miles from 4.4 gallons of fuel, but usually average around 170 and need about 4.2 gallons at fill up. My low fuel light usually comes on around 160. I do lots of full throttle blasts and keep rpm above 4k in twisty areas, but I don't go much above 4k rpm in regular around town commuting. I have a Motobox, Akra Evos, Akra map, O2 eliminators and SAS removed. Before those mods I had to keep rpm up higher because the throttle was so jerky. It seems really efficient to me considering how strong it runs. I've documented every fill up from new till now (7600 miles) and average around 47mpg. I live at 4,000 feet elevation and I'm not sure if maybe that helps my mileage.
