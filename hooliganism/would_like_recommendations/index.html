Hi MrBlond, I ride my (06) SD every day through Brisbane traffic and find it is perfectly suited - high bars and narrow bike mean easy to squeeze through gaps, adequate steering lock good for slow maneuvering. Clutch and gears are nice and smooth, and since I changed to a 16T front sprocket, added Leo Vince exhausts with the Akra map it is smooth enough low down. For me engine breaking is fine, nothing too harsh - but as a few others have pointed out, big bore V Twins have alot more than inline fours.

As for motorway riding, I find 100mph+ speeds sustainable no problem but then in 15 years of biking I have only ever owned one faired bike so maybe I'm just used to the wind.

Now the bad news - SD fuel economy is pretty dire in town, coupled with the 06 small tank size I get 60 miles before reserve light comes on. Occasionally stalls while riding but this something I have always experienced with big Vs, and owning an SD will seriously affect your ability to keep your licence (I have just 3 points left till I have to catch the train!).

All in all it is without a doubt the finest machine I have ever owned and so much fun I can't believe everyone doesn't have one.
