#6
The dealers are full of shit.

I will tell you exactly what has happened. It happened to mine and my mates also.

Take off your brake fluid reservoir top and throw it as far as you can.

Then go and buy a complete new reservior from KTM (about 16 quid)

You can change the whole thing if you like or just use the inside seal and new top as I did = no more corrosion on brake lever.

What is happening is that the seal has failed and brake fluid is blowing onto your brake lever causing the corrosion. That is why it is only the brake and not the clutch lever.

Ktm dealers wont acknowledge this to avoid warranty claims but I bet they sell a lot of new reservoirs

Note: changing the top for the nice adonised orange one wont solve it as it doesnt come with a new seal, not silly are they
