#2
Aside from getting a vernier caliper to measure the diameter of the headers (I presume the Akrapovic ones would be bigger), the obvious giveaway is to look under the chin spoiler to see if the front header is straight or looped - the full SDR header has a loop to get the optimum length. If you can't see the bike in person, ask the seller if they can take a photo for you. The SDR wasn't limited to 2008 production only, but someone more knowledgeable than me would have to give you the full breakdown of the details.
