In terms of competition for the Superduke's ethos and target market, I've come across:

Bikes that actually work:

Aprilia Tuono (old or new versions) - Nice and powerful, but handling isn't as good. Old Tuono is too long. New one is too tiny.
Ducati Streetfighter - Nice bike, and one I would consider. Not quite as pretty as the Superduke.
Triumph Speed Triple - Lovely engine, Great brand, not quite got the chassis parts, but a real contender.
...and maybe the Ducati Monster 1100S (full Ohlins, yum) - Better than you'd think from the originator of this class.

I'll ignore the Honda CB1000R (yawn) & Z1000 (yawn wider)

These are a nice idea, assuming they don't break down:

Moto Morini Corsaro 1200
Benelli Tornado (as you mentioned)

Not sure I'd include the Aprilia Dorsoduro, as it's more like a Supermoto or Big Trailie, competing with KTM's 990SM and SMR...

They always say the Devil has all the best tunes. I think the motorcycle media long ago sold its soul.

Triumph must be handing out some big incentives as their bikes always get good reviews. I have ridden most of them and wouldnt swap my Superduke for any of them. I have been on all but the very latest, (slitty eyed) speed triple (I used to be in the IAM so I know a lot of old bikers) it is a good bike but nothing to get the heart racing.

Funny that loads of journalists seem to have Triumphs as long term test bikes and later seem to buy them at favorable rates. Hmmmmmmmmmmmm.

Funny that they are all running around on Dunlops and Dunlops are winning all the tyre tests everywhere. Hmmmmmmmmmmmmmmmm.

Used to be the media got the red carpet treatment at a bike launch and a corporate reach around for all I know That doesnt seem to be enough for them anymore. Journalists were telling us the new ZX10 would be a fire breathing Moto GP bike on the road able to piss past S1000RR's with ease. Now in MCN they reveal it is just another Jap il4 1000cc with no midrange and all its power up at ridiculous rpm.

I wont be trading my SD for anything just yet. I wont be trading it for anything I dont get a decent test ride on, ever. Then if I like the bike, I wont give a deep fried shit what some motorbike journalist is paid to think about it.

I dont believe a word written in these new bike or new vs old bike reviews, in truth I often skip them in the magazines I read.

Anyway, batten down the hatches because the V4 Tuono is coming and every other naked bike will be crap. Except for the Speed Triple; that will still be best for pensioners and journalists
