All the post '07 models have the same screen. Skidmark screen is wider and taller. There's also a KTM touring screen which is taller. I've had them all over the years but always returned to the standard small screen because it looks so much better. The KTM screen didn't seem to offer much better wind protection and looks ugly and the Skidmarks screen was not that well made in my opinion. Bit rough round the edges.
