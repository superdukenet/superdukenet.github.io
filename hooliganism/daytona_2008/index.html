Well, I know most of you will cringe, but I painted them. I have done this in the past with great success. The key is preparation, and I use appliance paint, a very hard and durable enamel. I did have a spot near the pegs that chipped, but the beauty of black is that a sharpie touched it right up. I was worried about the heat on the clutch and ignition covers so I used high heat paint for grills and stoves. seems to be holding up well. I had planned to remove the swing arm and have powder coated { I would never paint anything like that] but I rather like the look now with the middle parts blacked out. the swingarm is balanced by the bottom of front forks. I do want to black out the engine mounts, but now it's time to ride. One of the great things about bikes is making it your own.
