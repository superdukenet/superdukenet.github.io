#11
My first position I tried was on the closest holes one hole up from where std would have roughly been but I couldn't get the linkages on one side to fit in the position I wanted as by the time you got the connector and nuts on it wouldn't go short enough, I think it was the brake linkage.

I then went one hole back and another hole up.

In my own mind I always go diagonally as my thinking says that if I'm adding compression to my knee by going up one then I'm lengthening the leg again by going back one to counter the compression to some extent.

This has ended up being my preferred position, I did try one more hole up and back which would be the last hole you can use (highest and furthest) but I didn't like it, I never actually tried it on track but it was so uncomfortable on the road for my leg length at least that I knew I wouldn't be able to live with it.

So I'm basically one hole forward and down from the furthest, highest hole.

They work fine on the track, if I have my boot too far over on the peg then my toes touch but that's my issue not the adapters, I'm also using different pegs which I think are shorter than stock anyway.

I did still get the gear lever down a couple of times at Cadwell recently but that was on a high compression positive camber bend.
