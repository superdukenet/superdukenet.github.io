Problem solved guys

Thought I'd go out and start eliminating problems. Big grey connector with some dielectric grease. While there I thought I'd disconnect the PCIII. Problem solved Took her for a spin and runs a treat. Went to take out PCIII to send back to the US and noticed the earth connection had worked itself a bit loose and so wasn't making a good contact all the time Tightened that up, reconnected the PCIII and hey presto all is well
