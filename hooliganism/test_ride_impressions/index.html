#1
BAD
Mirrors - poor
Vibrations around 6000 revs - will take a bit of getting used to
Tank - not metal so will need a new tank bag
Exhaust under seat hot - advice please - does this mean I cant put a reasonably large soft bag on the pillion?
Bungees - only anchor points appear to be the piilion pegs

GOOD
Felt really easy to ride
Felt incredibly light, even tho its the same weight as my CBR600F
Riding position - wonderful
Cornering - excellent
Windblast - was pleasantly surprised how little up to 90

BRILLIANT
Test ride was on winding country road with quite a bit of traffic. Had to be patient to overtake but when the chance came - WOOOOSH - it felt like being on top of a Boeing 747!

CONCLUSION
Agreed P/X price on my honda, paid a Â£500 deposit and pick it up a week tomorrow.

I am excited!
