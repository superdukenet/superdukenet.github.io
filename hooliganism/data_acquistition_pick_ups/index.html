For the gear sensor, like TJ said.
RPM you can get from the injector pulse (in big grey plug on the right side behind the tank panel) or the feed to the coil packs.
The easiest for the speed might be to get a pulse from the front wheel sensor. When you turn the key on the bike the speedo tells you the size of the wheel/tyre so you can work out how to set your XT GPS up from that.

There is a link on this forum for the wiring diagram of the 08 model, if you can't find it pm me with your e-mail and I mail it to you.
