Yeah, it's so much fun that I've fitted a camera to the handle-bars to review the overtakes when I get off again; I've changed my route for best overtaking, which actually means 'most rewarding' - which actually means a winding lane with alternating no-overtaking spots so that I have to time things well to overtake, then get between gaps of oncoming cars as well as leaving time to decide whether to pull in or take another car as well. If I leave too early and there isn't much traffic, its not as much fun as having to use the bike to it's full.

When I get home I edit all the overtakes and save them as a little movie. That's a bit sad, really! But the kids love watching them - well, that's my excuse!
