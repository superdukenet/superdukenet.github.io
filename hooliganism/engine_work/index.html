I can not comment on before and after but I've had my SDR heads done...
Danny Boyd did mine at Tri-Valley Moto.
Flowing heads may also move your power out of your usable rev range, Harleys have this problem. You can flow them really well but they will more often than not lose power or get no gains as they don't rev enough to use the extra flow.
SDNerd will have a good explanation for you, Flowing is good but velocity is also needed.
Apparently just a good valve job will gwt you noticable results.
