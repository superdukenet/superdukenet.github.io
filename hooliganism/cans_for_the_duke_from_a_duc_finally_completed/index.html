well after another set back (came off of my hypermotard), a lengthy repair of both me and an almost finished motard... i finally got the pipes fitted to the bike... it looks great, sounds even better.... now wish i had only done it sooner.

The pipes are almost as loud as the FMF pipes but have a much deeper and bassier note to it. The pipes weigh 3.5kg all up and have changed the handling of the bike heaps, tips in like nothing else and ofcourse i can feel a decent increase in the grunt

How do i load a video? And anyone in bris is welcome to come see it (bottom of glorious.. just pm me)

Oh and pipemasters exhaust did the y piece (they didnt like making a custom ball socket joint) but they got there in the end... looks good, quality work. And they now have a jig so that they can make new custom Ypieces or full exhausts for the mighty SD.
They did me a good price so i promised them a plug (is that ok?).

The guy to talk to is Peter Gray (07) 3252 3939 or
