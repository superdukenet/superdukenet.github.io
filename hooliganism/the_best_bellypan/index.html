I can only tell you of my experience, i hav the Skidmarx 1!

Its comes in gloss black with full fitting kit and heat tape! (those are the good points)

With Akra headers you have to very slightly modify it at the front, its scrapes on track at anything past a DECENT lean angle at the offside rear mounting point (altho now i have stiffer springs for my weight it is less of a problem). Mine also has stress cracks where fitted altho in fairness these have never got any worse since day 1 (and most of mine are covered with decals anyway).

I dont like the look of it in plain black but with the decals on it looks fine! You dont really get alot for £100 nowadays tho do ya
