Ergonomics is great and up to 130kph is no problem, above 150kph is hell. Tank range is not impressive as the SD is thirsty, but you already know that. But people go touring with 916's so we can't really complain, and except in France, there are normally gas stations that are open and/or accept major credit cards

Bought the PacSafe bag, but had to use several extra straps to keep it in place. I guess the best is the Ventura system and use the stock exhaust for touring. I guess we can all live with 3hp less on touring.
