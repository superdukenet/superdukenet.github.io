Every new bike has problems. SD has so few that even Honda would envy it. The point is that all SDs that are around still have waranty so whatever the prob you'll fix it for free. I have changed the starter and the clutch pump all under waranty and now the bike runs smoothly and problem free. I think of them as minor problems. We here know of 2 or 3 major problems that have been solved with either change of engine or the hole bike. Even so the problem bikes are still few. Even jap bikes have problems. I know of few CBRs RR that have had problems. So I'd say take a ride and see. Its more possible not to like one because of how sharp the bike is rather than of problems regarding technical stuff.
