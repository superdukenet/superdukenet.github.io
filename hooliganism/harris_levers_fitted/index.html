Just fitted my Harris levers

£138 Delivered, including VAT (after blagged discount)



They appear to be identical to the KTM ones made for the SD/SDR and RC8, but those are £109 each lever.

It would have been nice to have some instructions, as it took me a while to realise that I needed to cannibalise my OE clutch lever to re-use the pin-locating barrel, but then I guess i did work it out for myself in the end.

Quality materials and finish (as you'd expect from harris) and plenty of adjustments, with a great price (for folding levers) so - Recommended kit.
