You can flip the axel spacer blocks around to bring the rear wheel in closer to the swingarm pivot and make room for a larger rear sprocket on the OE chain. I run 16:40 now and reckon I could easily run 16:42 on the standard chain length.

It's no bad thing to slightly shorten the wheelbase too.
