What do you ride in when it's cold?

I have a leather jacket and pants with good armor, but they flow air so riding when it is freezing out hurts - even with a warm base layer. I've been looking at oversuits to wear over my leathers (both rainsuits and lined textile suits). But it's hard to find a good fit because of the "hump" on my jacket. And, the oversuits do not seem as protective as my current gear so I don't want to ride with just a textile suit. I'd love to find an oversuit designed to wear over track day quality leathers.

So, what do you wear to keep warm?

Also, any recommendations on warm glove liners?

I've used coldkillers type stuff for years, it's the single most valuable piece of kit I ever bought, especialy under vented leathers. There are a few different makes about and one in particular should be avoided, I think it's Frank Thomas, the windstopper material only covers the chest leaving your stomach 'kin freezing. If you buy one, and you should, always check the windstopper material covers the whole front.
