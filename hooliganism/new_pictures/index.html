Almost ready for the summer, just an oil and filter change to do!!

Main change is having the swing arm powder coated satin black. Note that I've left the chain adjuster block area original to avoid scratching in that area. Also masked off were the inner faces where the rear wheel contacts and the inner area where the shock absorber joins. I forgot about the rear caliper slider but this fits over the powder anyway













Last weekend I had the exhaust off to give it a clean and to add some silicon sealant to the rear join. Today I've balanced the throttle bodies and bunged up the SAS tube off the back of the air box with a marble!
