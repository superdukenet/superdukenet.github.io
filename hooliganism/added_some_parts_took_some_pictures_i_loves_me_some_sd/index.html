#11
I think you'll find that your chain is not too tight. If you look at the angle of the swing arm, you'll see that it is very shallow (front sprocket, swing arm pivot point and axel close to being in line).

If you were to pull your shock and move swingarm through it range of travel the chain should have just a little slack when all three are lined up. Bikes with more swing arm angle require momre chain slack to achieve the same thing.

If you were to run much more slack I think you'd find that the chain would start making contact with the swing arm. Don't ask me how I know this.

John
