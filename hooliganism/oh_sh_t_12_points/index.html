I would seek some free legal advice if I were u, yes the Police officer is right even holding a phone is illegal, as is turning the knob on your radio, and eating an apple at the wheel, as your not "in proper control" of your vehicle, you would need a statutory defence for the phone i.e. Police exemption etc were you stationary of moving?

I think your best option is to seek free legal advice get a solicitor to put your point across about the hands free kit and your extenuating circumstances and cross your fingers, you would be very unlucky getting a lengthy ban of six months.
