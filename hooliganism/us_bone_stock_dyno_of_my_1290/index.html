With 450 miles on the bike, I took it to the dyno. From looking at the Aussie charts, I expected it to be running rich and have a torque dip around 6-7K. Mine was 148.5 hp- close to what the mags get, but torque was pretty flat at 80 and peaked at 90, and air was a little lean. Air mixture went from a high of 15 to an average of 14.5. It was around 65F, and mechanic said it would be richer when the temp was hotter, which it usually is in Florida. I have searched, but can't see how to add pictures like on other forums.
