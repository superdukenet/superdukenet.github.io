Twisted Jester Forum member

Post 25 by Twisted Jester, 18 May 2011, 00:05

#25
All the cool kids run LSLs these days

Love the LSLs, as soon as I sat on the bike after fitting them it just felt right, I smiled to myself there and then, it just felt more purposeful and the first spirited ride out they all just made sense in the twisties and then even more so on the track.

Don't really do long commutes on the SD anymore so can't really say how much sacrifice they are for comfort over distance etc it does put more weight through your wrists which was kind of the intention of going for them in the first place but I used to do all my mileage on sportsbikes anyway and it's not as bad as that.

I've fitted the CNC top yoke now which has the extra mounting holes and put them as far forward as they go which I think is slightly more forward than the stock SD location but haven't tried them out yet, feels good when not moving though

Loads of scope to cut em down as short as you like as well, as because there's no rise the taper and bend starts further in to the centre
