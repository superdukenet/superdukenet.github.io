Yeah, definitely get your suspension looked at, shouldn't be getting the clutch cover down.

Pegs can still touch down though, if you find that's still an issue after getting your suspension sorted the either get rearsets or get some of the cycle control rearset plates to mount your existing ones further up and back.

Here's a pic from their website, I'm using these and they do the trick for me, a nice budget alternative to rearsets



http://www.cyclecontrol.com/index.php?page=products/rsa

To start with if you can't get to a suspension professional then you could just reset the suspension as per the settings under the seat and in the manual for sports or std and see how you get on.
