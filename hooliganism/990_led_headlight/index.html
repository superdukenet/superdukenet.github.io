Well I did it. And I only needed the help of 3 friends, one professional mechanic, and 2 Light Emitting Diode specialists. Nah, it took me 10 minutes.

This is what comes in the box for $45 + shipping, less than $50 to my door.



Both bulbs. For size comparison purpose.


The top LED is short beam, low LED is high beam. The LED's point up once installed.


Low beam hallogen


Low beam LED


High beam hallogen


High beam LED


I did have to slightly shave the white plastic collar that secures the bulb to the SD lens. I used my Dremel tool. I also had to cut the back of the rubber plug that covers the back of the bulb to allow the heat sink to breathe.

The light is pure white. I personally dislike the blue hue of some bulbs and this bulb has very pure light. I think they claim 6000K.

So far, very satisfied.

I'll go for a nite ride soon and find out.

Sitting on the bike, low beam


Sitting on the bike, high beam
