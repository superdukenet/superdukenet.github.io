Hi guys,
No pics at the moment sorry, but MCN Australia (who are usually very well informed) has confirmed KTM will show its 09 line-up at the Milan International Motorcycling Show in November. It will include a RC8R (for the Super Bike assault), the Venom using the RC8 engine, the 690 Stunt (!) in road legal guise, an new version of the Super Duke (no details yet) and an updated Adventure using the 1190 engine in two guises: a hardcore Dakar model and a softer touring machine... So interesting times ahead..
