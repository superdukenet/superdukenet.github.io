Not keen on those, I don't really get what they're trying to do, the whole point of rear sets (that's proper rearsets not oem footrests which everybody seems to call rearsets) is that it sets the pegs to the rear and up so your in a more prone racing position and so your legs don't just get squashed upwards to obtain the clearance, the sato ones don't go back at all, only forward and up which would force your knees up around the tank. Their RC8 ones are all set to the rear from standard so it's as if they think the Superduke should have a more MX type position with your feet further forward which is just completely wrong.

These PP Tuning rearsets are my favourite for the Superduke so far and cheaper too, plus I prefer that the levers move with the pegs and not seperately so the action is always the same wherever you move them too.



At the moment though I'm sticking with the cycle control adapter plates and original footrests as I like the Moto hooligan heel plates too much to give them up and they do the job just as well anyway
