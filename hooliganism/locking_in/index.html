Shade, what I think is happening is this: I doubt the pressure your knee can exert on the crash bung is stabilising you in the corner.

But by reaching for it with your knee you are getting more of your weight forward and to the inside. This is putting more weight onto the front wheel - hence extra stability.

Taken to extreme you could push your foot forward towards the front wheel, supermoto stylee As you often see Rossi and Stoner doing these days.

Sports bikes with clip on handlebars automatically put a lot of rider weight over the front. However, the price is sore wrists and lower vision. The Superduke bars and riding position give good leverage and more opportunity to move our weight around on the bike. For track angles of lean you need to get as much weight as possible to the front wheel without holding too tightly to the handlebars - which is what you are doing.
