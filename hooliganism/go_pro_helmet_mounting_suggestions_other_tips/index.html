Post 4 by 41KS, 23 Oct 2012, 16:45

#4
Been in that question, best solution is measure it 9 times and then stick it to the helmet. Use center position, so you can reverse camera if required. When positioning camera angle, it should be more upwards than you logically think (because of leaned forward body position). Adjust it before important footage, or most likely you will get picture of your speedo and road surface only. Personally I do not feel weight of Hero2 on the helmet at all.

Helmet mounting let you check in retrospective how much (and how effectively) you look at important things in traffic For cool driving footage other mounts on the bike might be more effective

Its a big time waste however, to learn and spend the time of video editing etc. Plus I never had so much mis-shifts when wearing GoPro at first times All will go in place again with the next drives.
