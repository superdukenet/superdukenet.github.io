well after pipes, intake (motobox etc), DVS (oviously after proof), tuneECU and dyno tuning, what are the next performance mods that can be done before you start changing cams, increasing compression etc (obviously blue printing and gas flow of heads but i consider that pulling the engine apart) can be done next?

After talking with INDY84 one of the things that could be done is change over to an electric water pump... in cars this can re-gain up to 25hp obviously on a bike it will be a lot less but 5hp is not out of the question... all done without further strain on the engine (essentially reducing work for motor). Similar concept to going for a 520 chain-sprocket set (which is also happening soon as i have there is a company that puts a lifetime warranty on their sprockets).

my only concern with an electric water pump is that it could fail and you wouldnt know until it was too late AND the extra current drawn may be too much for the alternator to deal with and would use up valuable hp anyway while trying... so you would end up losing power to the battery (not getting a chance to charge, especially sitting in traffic)

Has any body else got any ideas? To me its (electric water pump) more a theoretical thing but heck if i get bored it may get done in the future.

Sorry to bust your bubble lads but electric water pumps only save HP when you run a total loss battery system.
The power to run the pump must come from somewhere and as soon as it runs it uses electricity which needs to be generated by the alternator to top up the battery so you use the same amount of power as you try to save.
On a high performance engine the pump needs to be always ON so no gain to have.

I like fiddling with the bike but sometimes it is just fine to ride it for a while as it is James.
