You will open up a can of worms here!

Pre-'07 owners will swear the earlier bikes are best. Sharper steering geometry, better engine-(with slightly different cams than later), The raw original SD concept.

After '07 you get a slightly revised steering geometry that makes it a bit more stable and less quick to turn in. You also get a bigger fuel tank that gives 100 mile range over the previous 75 or so and a restyled front headlight and clocks. Any you also get radial front brakes. Owners of these think these are the best.

They are both great bikes. Either model, take your pick of the ones that are looked after the best and have been owned by someone who cares. A combination of any of the performance mods that might have been done would make the difference between which bike performed better but much, much more than that - the standard of the rider.

If it's your first bike you wont be able to tell the difference. But the colour does make a difference. Think hard about whether orange or black - it will place you on a side for ever.

Keep in mind . the earlier SD's with steeper steering angles have their nuances . Head shake / less stability ! My 07 would sometimes shake like a sob on me till i learned how to be lighter on the bars. Point is when pushing my 07 pretty damn hard into the turns ( at speed ) i could tell the front wanted to wash out ( Too short of trail ). This is with the factory setup ( 1/8th" fork tube sticking above the triple clamp )... i have sense lowered the tubes about 1/4" into the triple clamp making a world of difference and stability improved / less head shake / and that washout feeling much much reduced ( basically gone ).... Point is if you get pre 07 you may need/want to go with that Nichols triple clamp so you can give yourself some rake back and some stability . 2 cents
