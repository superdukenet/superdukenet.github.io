I wish either Shoei, Aria or AGV mad a lid with a flip down Sun Visor, inside or outside the main one.

Wearing sunglasses is a pain in the arse - you can't 'flip' them up when you hit the shade, they're uncomfortable, you forget you're wearing them and take off your lid with them still on and I expect they're not an ideal item to have inside your lid in a crash...
...and carrying a spare visor - and having to change it - is also a pain. I like to ride without any kinda bag whenever I can, and sometimes I end up only having a pouch or bag cos I need to carry a spare visor.

I think Shoei might have a new graduated tint visor on the market...
