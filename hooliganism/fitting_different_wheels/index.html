They are hard to come by, unless you have the Dymag Carbon wheels plus the original set like... err... em.. me!

The 08/9 RC8 shares the same front but the rear is 6 inch wide. The 950 / 990 Supermoto or SMT will fit / are same wheels.

So when checking on e bay always have a look at Brembo, Marchesini, KTM wheels etc not just Superduke wheels.

Brembo wheels were on early Ducati's and some Aprilias but whether they would fit or could be made to fit I wouldnt know.
