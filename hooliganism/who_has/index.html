My motivation for this post comes from the amount of time spent on wheelie talk. I thought that if people could see just how unlikely the possibility, time from freakout and over the back, suddenly moves at a differant pace than the time it takes to get the wheel up to its happy spot. Is simply not the case. There is loads of time to correct such behavior. Wheelies, warp time. The "oh shit" pause in throttle pull has already caused the front end to an oppisite path. You are just as likely to be able to say "oh shit" three times while still yanking back on the twist grip before you need to quiet the yanking and set it into nuetral throttle. Try it, three "oh shits" prior to doing anything. We will call the fourth "oh shit" the big toby.
