Tyre manufacturers understand their tyres best. You should run what they recommend. Most top shelf sports tyres are recommended to run 35/36 psi both f/r on the street.

I regularly run my rear up to 40 psi with good grip and good wear results.

I live in a warm part of the world and summer time riding will see road surfaces around 60 degrees cent. and high pressures are not a problem.

Of course a bit of melted sticky tar on the tyre does'nt hurt your grip levels either.

30F and 31R from cold. These pressures work excellent on the street. Warm up quickly, offer good compliance and don't squirm around.

Haven't had them on the track but would suspect I'd be looking at 28/29 or there abouts.
