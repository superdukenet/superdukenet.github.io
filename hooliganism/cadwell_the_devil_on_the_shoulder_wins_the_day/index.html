oh yeah... what a fooking great day up in the wolds of lincolnshire. Spent the day having a right scream around cadwell park , what a track..! Six sessions ,2 on dampish track and 4 ear to ear grinning dry sessions. Was at the front of the group ( novice ) all day . Had great fun carving the big inline fores up around the bends ... WICKED!!!!

But alas readers the devil on me shoulder was pushing me tooooo hard on the last session.....FOOOOOOK low slide coming out of mansfield. Oh well.

The rg crash bungs and rizoma engine casing have safed the day. very limited damage , and the rider walked away. If you are going to track your bike ,, fit these items..Sticker rubber is diffo needed up front.Will be back on the road v soon

If you are thing about a track day , cadwell rocks for the sd . will be going back shortly.

ps there was another sd there an orange one. THey are diffo slower but they do stay rubber side up
