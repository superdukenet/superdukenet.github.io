Looks like I'm going full circle after quite a few years away.

Not that I've been away from riding. Far from it.
Since trading the 990 in 2010, I've had a string of bikes and a Wife who justifiably has wanted to kill me more than once.

I'll run through what I can remember.
990 smt, v4 touno, dorsoduro 1200 and 750, 3 dr650's, 2 1190 adventures, 2 vstroms, superduke gt, xt660, 690 duke, 250 excf and Lord knows what else.
The 1190's and the gt had electrical problems.

I'm riding a Caponord Rally and I really love the comfort. That's the only thing it has over the Adv. Apart from not breaking down. We do a few trips a year and it's great for all day comfort, so I'm going to keep it. Oh, I also bought a trials bike. It doesn't take up much space, so I'll probably keep it forever.

The problem I now face is that I don't have a bike that gives me the fizz (or an erection) like the KTM's do, so I've decided to buy a bike just for fanging once and awhile.
I'm going old school. No electronics to fail or interfere when you don't want them to. A fair weather bit of fun with sticky tyres.

I thought of the old v twin tuono but age, reliability and repair costs are steering me towards either a 990 or 990r Superduke. I have my eye on an R which has a KTM comfort seat at a good price.

My question to you is.... Which one? Standard or the R? The R looks so good.

Appreciate your thoughts.

Hi Rich.

I bought the very first of the 1190 adv's in 2013. The S with electronic everything.
It had constant traction control failures, suspension and dash failures. It also had a fuel tank problem the spewed fuel everywhere.
The bike was so flawed that KTM replaced it with a 2014 model. I rode this one for about 1000km and the traction failed on this one.
I soon traded this one in. 15 models on seem to be ok.
The GT only had a flapper valve motor failure. It was coming up for the warranty to expire. I've just sold it. I'm on my 4th Aprilia and they've all been faultless.
