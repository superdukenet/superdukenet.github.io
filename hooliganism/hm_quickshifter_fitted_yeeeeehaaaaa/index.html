To be honest I'm not sure as it was fitted in the HMRacing workshop and I was chatting at the sales counter when the actual wiring was done. But I did end up rewiring the whole thing once I got home to hide some of the visible wiring and generally tidy it all up - also so hide the control box under the seat rather than have it exposed.

I remember that that there was one wire that comes down to the quickshifter, (obviously), and one earth lead that came down to mount onto the engine low down for a good earth connection - I'm pretty sure that they recommend not to use the upper part, valve covers etc. But I don't remember any wire coming from the battery. Can't be sure though. It seemed to me that the whole unit was plugged directly into the wiring loom at the top of the engine and went directly to the spark plugs.

There are two versions of the HMQuickshifter though - one that plugs into the PC111 and the one like mine that goes direct to the plugs and cuts the spark.

Sorry that I can't be more helpful.
