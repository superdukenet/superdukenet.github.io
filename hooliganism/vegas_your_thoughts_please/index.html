Ok as some of you may know I am joining a trip from Toronto to Vegas with 2 other blokes. My concern is, obviously like everyone else I like to go on bike trips that challenge me a lot and present some beautiful riding roads. My concern is that this is close to 4000 km one way and I know a lot of it is going to be boring but for those that know Vegas and surrounding areas is it worth it for me? Will a be on some breathtaking, exciting roads or is it pretty much straight line brain numbing stuff? Obviously it may depend on the route but I assume there are only a certain amount of ways to get there from Toronto. I am looking for constructive opinions and thoughts as I value all your views. If it really isn't worth it in relation to a riding experience then I wont bother. Time is precious for all of us so I really need to pick my trips carefully, it sounds exciting but I have no insight. Cheers for your help. The plan is to leave Sept 11th so I don't have a lot of time to make up my mind.

For me it is the journey and not the final destination

Not a lot of great roads unless you hit Wyoming/Colorado/Northern New Mexico/Northern Arizona/Utah.

Vegas is flat & silly, but going across Utah/ N. Az will be nice. Avoid "I" (interstate) roads at all costs.

It'll be snoozeville until you get to Colorado. Get a cruise control thing for your right hand. And satellite radio.
