Apart from getting it set up and working in the first place, when it comes to just loading a predefined map like you would do initially on the PCIII it's not much different.

On the PCIII you still have to open the appropriate map file with the PCIII software on a laptop and you still have to send it to the PCIII.

You do the same with TuneECU and open the appropriate map file in the software but you send it straight to the ECU instead.

The more of us that get this up and running, the more chance there'll be somebody near you who will be able to sort it for you free of charge if you don't fancy doing the PC side or sorting the cable out, stick a post on the official topic and see if anybody is near you.

Failing that, used PCIIIs don't fetch as much as they used to now TuneECU is around so personally I wouldn't bother buying new as you can get 2-3 used ones for the price of a new one and I've never met anybody that's ever had a fault with one.
