Having previously owned an SD for two years I 'upgraded' to the SDR and have now spent a year riding it on road and track.

For what its worth, I'd say the SDR is.....

Fast roads......better
Twisties......same
Track.......much better
Town......worse
Bumpy roads.......worse
Looks..........much better
Reliability.......same
Long distance...much worse

still not sure which I prefer....I guess I really need both!

I'd be interested to hear other people's opinions.

Cheers
