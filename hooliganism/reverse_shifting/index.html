There is a short brain adjustment period. My track bike has reverse shift. The First day I stuffed up several times. The second track day only a couple times.

But gotta say, they should be reverse shift standard. So much easier stomping down to change up when you're used to it.

Now I can go between track and street bike with ease, but if an opportunity arises to change the road bike to reverse cheaply, I'll do it!
