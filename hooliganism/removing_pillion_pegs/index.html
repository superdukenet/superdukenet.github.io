If it's a new bike then you could get some of those transparent self-adhesive patches they sell in car shops to protect against stone chips. There are a couple of places where the rear panels do vibrate against the rear subframe and wear away the paint. I've got CF panels and removed the whole pillion footrest hangars and they've worn through and left marks.
