So , my reply was this.

go test ride one first there awesome .
A powerful v twin , that has instant power , soon as it has a whiff of gas its off you go !
Just dab the brakes there on .
Handles unreal , with quality suspension front and rear , no fekking cheap budget parts...
Power as , jap restrictors in my bike ( Akra re map and cans) from new.
to allow you to ride it like it was designed .


Not the prettiest looking machine , granted , but quirky and different , gets a chat everwhere i ride , how about your's ?? i said.

Happily for me it ain't another R6 - R1 , GSX 6 750 etc etc , FZ , FZ1 , CBR etc etc...
plastic trip the light fantastic mould ....
plus the seat is better than that Fz1 fazer i bought and that Honda 954 (feccing uncomfortable) Fire blade , oooh my piles.

I love twisties and this bike eats them and comes back for a second breakfast .

189mph it dont do , 154 flat out , which i aint done yet ...... but sunday power rangers it does do , easily , very easily .

Whatever floats your boat , but always try to have a informed view , go test ride one , dont even buy one , but you got to ride one

Knowledge is power .
