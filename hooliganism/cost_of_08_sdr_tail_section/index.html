#2
The complete kit in the UK for an SRD single seat conversion is about £450. You get the official KTM kit with the plastic seat unit, rubber seat, undertray, back light, and all the bolts and fixings. People have done a parts list but it's way more expensive to buy bits separately.

One thing to remember is that in converting back your bike to a single seat, (or vice-versa), you have to reuse many of the parts so the dual seat unit is not a complete kit to sell on.
