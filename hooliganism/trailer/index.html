My father and I just purchased a 2008 Car Mate Sportster 6 X 12 enclosed trailer. Although we haven't picked it up yet, we aren't new to the trailering world. We have used several open trailers in the past but have turned to the more secure and cleaner enclosed trailer. It will fit our two bikes SUperduke/hyper motard in a staggered formation with plenty of room for storage. The trailer has all alluminum roof and sides with a 3000lbs axle. It also comes with all LED lights as standard for $2999.99 Canadian!
