One67 Forum member

Post 2 by One67, 30 Jan 2012, 10:01

#2
It's there to put oxygen into the cat so that it gets very hot and cleans the emissions.

When you put after market pipes on with no cat, you don't need it.

So you can just remove it or stick ball bearings in the tubes, it just looks neater with less hoses.
ktmguy Forum member

Post 3 by ktmguy, 30 Jan 2012, 10:30

#3
It also stops the backfiring in the exhaust when removed. I'm all for noise but I didn't like the popping of this.
If you don't want to make any other modifications it helps to reduce the throttle surge a little bit when removed. Doesn't solve it but helps a bit.
