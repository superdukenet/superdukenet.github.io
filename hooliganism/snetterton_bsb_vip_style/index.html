Had a great couple of days out this weekend at snetterton with motoronin courtesy of Red Torpedo and Hein Gericke.....thankyou to the Duchess!! ........ We were guests of the Swan Honda BSB team, the first day - saturday was qualifying and first race - pretty standard fair. Second day however was the two feature races etc. So i rocked up at ronins and kicked his lazy ass out of bed for the second time in two days, and off we tootled down to snetterton. Arrived at the gate and produced our passes..... continued on to the bike park where we had agreed the day before that we would try and park as close to the VIP enclosure as possible..... well some nugget had left the gate open and it was just too tempting! We burbled straight into the VIP area parked the SD's outside the Swan Honda truck and wandered on in. For the entire day the SD's were the only bikes parked in the VIP area...... Nice!
