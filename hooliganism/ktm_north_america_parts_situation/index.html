For the record I submitted a complaint with the Better Business Bureau. I dont know if it will do any good, but here is what I sent in:

<cut>
I purchased a new 2007 KTM 990 Superduke on 05/10/2007. When I picked up the motorcycle both side panels were damaged. The dealer I purchased the bike from ordered new side panels prior to the 05/07/2007 purchase date. As of today, 07/10/2007, the dealer has not received these panels. I have contacted my dealer several times and they have informed me that KTM has a very large problem delivering parts to North America. I thought perhaps if the BBB contacted KTM it might let them know consumers in the US demand better service.

I would also like to point out I am not the only owner of a KTM experiencing this problem. Please see the following link to a KTM forum expressing consumer frustration.

http://www.superduke.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=2334

The dealer I purchased the bike from is: Wilwert's Dubuque, IA, 52003 Please note that I have no objection to the service from Wilwert's. My complaint is with KTM North America and their ability to deliver much needed parts.
<cut>
