I have never seen mine loosing coolant, but very occasionally, I can smell a little coolant, and it's always been when the bike has ran really hot (either city traffic or a spirited long ride), I'm not too worried about it as it doesn't drop a lot.
However, from new, I had to keep topping up the coolant and didn't know where it went. They changed the waterpump seals under warranty, but screwed up first time (they seal failed after only 50 or so miles and the bike ran out of coolant, ending up with mayonaise oil). After they fixed the waterpump second time, the coolant has nearly stayed were it was (maybe dropped 2-5mm in a couple of thousand miles).
