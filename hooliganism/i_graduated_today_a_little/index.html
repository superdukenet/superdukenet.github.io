So i took my lil SuperDuke out today for a bit , went to a local car/bike show , hung out enjoyed the music and then a burger. Exciting huh ?

So done with burger , on the way home down the coast hwy i take the turn to my house , house is about 1/3 mile up twisty road - you know the kind , the kind ya get used to like the back of your hand and you just look forward to that stretch because it's all put together just right . so i make the first turn at street 100yrds - take second turn 200yrds come to the stop sign. Make the left turn for the 1/3rd mile fun section and pullin away from the stop into the turn i bang the throttle and for the first time i lit the rear wheel up ! Wahoooo babay !! I been wantin to spin the rear wheel ever since i got this bike a couple months ago. Smooth as glass too while spinnin and just full of control. One of those things ya know that we all own these SD's for "TheGrinFactor" ... I'm gettin better !!! I've graduated , come up to another level ROFLMAO !!

Can you tell how stoked i am !!! Oh Man ! allright i better stop. Just had to report , brag , crow like a rooster , whatever
