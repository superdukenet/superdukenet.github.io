I'm using these:
http://www.ktmtwins.com/950supermoto/60112064044.html

I also wear summer weight gloves year round (Held Steve). The 'Start' setting is what I find myself using all the time. Start is to heat them up quick then the 'On' is to keep them warm...but I find 'On' does nothing for me. So I put them on Start for a while then off...Start then off. Kind of a pain but warmth is very good. I can attest to warming from the inside out. Here in SoCal, couple mornings this winter were in the 40's-50's, and while not terribly cold, an hour commute at 80mph would turn my digits to ice.

Easy to install and use. Well worth it in my opinion.
