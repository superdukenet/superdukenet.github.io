Both, really. I have both an AGV TiTech (light, quiet) and an Arai XD Supermoto/DS helmet. The AGV is sleek & quiet, good for long days & hi speeds. The XD is really great for around town stuff, lots of air & way better visibility. MX helmets w/goggles seem like a lot of junk to deal with, and VERY noisy above 60MPH. The XD (even with the peak) is OK until around 80, then you better be careful when you turn your head.
