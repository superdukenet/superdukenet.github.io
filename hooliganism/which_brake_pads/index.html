#7
I put Carbonne Lorraine XBK 5 pads on my SD and couldn't be happier. True two finger stopping power (actually sometimes I only use one finger on my ASV shorty lever) good initial bite and progressive feel. I haven't done any stoppies with them. I'm sure they are very capable of doing stoppies, but I usually wrack my balls when I try doing stoppies, so no stoppies for me. As a plus, they don't seem to generate as much dust as the OEM pads and unlike the OEM pads rarely squeal.

Oh, and if you want a usable rear brake, go for the CL RX3 pads. I find myself actually using the rear brake to settle the bike now. Before it was useless.
