Huh, I run kerosene and liquid oxygen in my bike. I used to use hydrazine, but its harder and harder to get (thanks sooooo much "war on terror").

Without boring everyone on the differences in octane ratings in different regions of the planet, big octane numbers are NOT an advantage. The lower value you can get away with (without engine knock), the better. I know it seems counterintuitive, because you're paying more for the additional iso-octane (and less of the good volatile stuff per unit volume). I'm confident there's more on this subject elsewhere on the site (and yeah - I'm too fooking lazy to look it up for you - try search). Race gas is a waste of money unless you are running additional timing advance (beyond the stock maps) and/or higher compression ratios.

Anyway, the only fancy-schmancy fuel I could see being advantageous in the SD, are those that are ethanol-free (for the sake of the dreaded tank 'mumps').
