#14
Rallsy. General consensus is that 16/40 gives the best balance of acceleration for the SD gearbox combined with top speed. For general road /track riding. You just need to try things to see what suits your own riding. I got a set of different rear sprockets from a Sd racer off EBay and tried them all out. Got loads of different options. Going up a tooth or two on the back makes the bike rev out quicker in every gear.

Having messed about with wheelies, I reckon Sabre has a point though. If you're into wheelies the 16/40 runs out of revs a bit quick in first so going to his favourite combo would let the bike keep the revs for longer so you have a bit more time lofted before having to change up.
