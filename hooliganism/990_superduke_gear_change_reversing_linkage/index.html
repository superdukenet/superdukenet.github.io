Colonel_Klinck Forum member

Post 2 by Colonel_Klinck, 30 Apr 2015, 22:11

#2
Are you planning to ride on track? It's there it really makes most sense. It allows you to gear change up with the bike on it's side, without unsettling the bike with the big foot movement it requires in normal shift pattern. It does take a little getting used to and you'll fook it up a few times till the muscle memory kicks in. Personally I much prefer it. Just seems to make more sense to tap down on the lever to go faster.
