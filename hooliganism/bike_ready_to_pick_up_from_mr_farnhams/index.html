Bob rang me today to tell me its all done and ready to pick up.
ill go get it on friday and go to Brands Hatch practice with him.
I will hopefully post some dyno printouts later when i get them.
He told me on the phone he was well pleased with the gains and has ended up with 130 at the back wheel.He originally dynoed it when i took it in at 121.He's gas flowed it removed my shafts and fit the RC8 stacks. he told me he was suprized with the stacks as at first and with previous experience on other bikes all they have done is move the power to the topend and lose out lower down but he said its gained at the top and lost nothing lower down.All in all with the work he's done ive gained power everywhere and said i will notice it all through the rev range.
Apparantly ive gained 9 bhp but at the top its jumped 15bhp at certain revs.
Cant wait to pick up and ride,notice how the weathers changed though LOL.
