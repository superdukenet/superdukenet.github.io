#6
Hey mate, did a few routes for you. Thought i'd post them in the thread rather tham PM so other guys can use them if they come up

1st one is a good borders run. The A701 when you leave Moffat is superb.



Worked a out a bit of a loop round some of the best roads i ride. The little loop at "B" next to Queen Elisabeth Forrest is called the Dukes Pass. It's like a rollercoaster, all 2nd and 3rd gear, loads of humps and jumps. 2nd bit of the road open out and there is a long straight with 4-5 humps you can get monster roll on wheelies off.

The section from "D" to "E" is called the Sma' Glen. Another great road. Not the best surface, and can be a bit tight and blind in bits but it takes you through a huge glen with cracking scenery.

I've not really been way up north for ages so had the end it at "K". If you look N/W for there you'll see Ullapool. A few of my mates go up there for a seafood festival and say the roads are awesome and deserted, so might be worth a look up that way too.

You wont be dissapointed with those routes.

Vikings routes are top notch! might I suggest that once you get to Fort William you try the A830 to Mallaig. AFAIR. Smooth tarmaced roads with whiteline borders.
You'll like it so much you'll want to go back.(Erm.... you have to.)

Btw there are also a few East coast borders routes that are so tempting.Turn of at Moffat and go the A708 and you will eventually come to St Marys Loch. Probably one of the most popular Scottish Sunday rides/meets.

Selkirk/Galasheils and A7 to Edinburgh takes you to the Capital. The best Scottish road riders and Rally drivers Earned their craft in the Borders Jim Clark/Jimmy and Colin Macrae/Steve Hislop/Louse Aitken Walker/David Coultard/Johnny Dumfries.

Checkout the network of roads in that area. Proper SD country

Seriously you'd be too knackered to go much further

Real Playground.
