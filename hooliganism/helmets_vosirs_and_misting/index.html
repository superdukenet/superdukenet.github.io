I know some of you lucky buggers live in nice warm places so you never get this !

I have been an advocate for years of my Shuberth S1 Helmet, had 2 over the last few years but now I'm looking to replace again.

One of the things that does bug me is the misting up over the winter, I know other helmets have the same problem but has anyone got any other helmets/visors that don't have this problem.

I know its a common theme, there are things like Fog City, etc, and Shuberth have just brought out a new S1 with a pinlock but before I splash the cash I just wondered what others have found to be good, off the shelf.

As i have specs as well its doesn't help but last week duing the fog and rain and dark it was almost impossible to ride just becasue of the visor.

I really rate Shuberths, anyone got any thoughts ??
