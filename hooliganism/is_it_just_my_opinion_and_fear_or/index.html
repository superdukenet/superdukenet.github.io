Probably a little (or lot) of both omky756!!!

I'm one of those new members that you are referring to and can honestly say that I'm not out to rip anyone off. I have been a member on KTMtalk since 2003 while I was riding a 300exc - just tooling around and looking for way to pass the time in central BC when the winters were -30C. I found that I spent a little too much time on that site and really only had minimal info to share - the 300exc was bomb proof. Since then I have moved on to home ownership and family life and I really don't have the time to be on sites anymore - heck, I rarely have time for riding! During my ownership of two superdukes I have been a regular visitor to this site, I just didn't have any problems to post about that required asking so I guess I am in the naughty group and guilty as charged

I am only trying to offload some parts that I had purchased with the intent of trying to help someone out. I have no issues, I may put the parts on ebay (have never done that before ) and may just throw them in the trash if there is no interest?

As indy84 states "trust or faith is needed or you'll go through life being a bitter person". As a for instance, I just sold my paddock stands and a barely used rear 2CT to a guy that didn't bring enough cash at time of sale. I asked him to drop the remainder back when he had chance because I had a good feeling about the guy, loaded up the stuff in his truck and away he drove. Before he left he said that I gave him a really good deal on the stuff, we shook hands and he said that he would drop back later to settle up. He stopped back in an hour and we ended up talking tyres and common riding areas and he was fascinated with my new FS570 in the garage.

TBH, I don't really care if my stuff ever sells, but if it does someone is going to get great service and a great deal - that I can promise!
