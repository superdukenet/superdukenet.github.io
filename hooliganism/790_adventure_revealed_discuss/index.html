real world wet weight will be the deal breaker for me.

I have a 450lb dual sport right now. Close to 100hp at the rear wheel, full protection, 12" of travel front and rear, solidly built wheels, and ready to go. With my usually day ride stuff on the bike (spare tube, basic tools, tank/rear bag, etc) it's right at 450lbs. I'd love to have something that was closer to 380-400lbs with a similar setup, but with a twin cylinder motor (not interested in singles).

long live the 950 Super Enduro!!!

The other issue in the US is AT BEST it'll come in as a late 2019 model from what I've heard. the bike shown at EICMA was a prototype vs. the 790 Duke that was in full production version and will be in late 2018 as an early 2019 model. So that puts the Adventure even later. Wish they would make another Super Enduro...
