i got michellin pilot 2ct's on mine and have not done me wrong yet, road riding and weekend blasts are a treat with the dual compund, not felt any sliping as yet, but you will go through the tyres quickly if you are hard on the streets with them.

doing a track day on these in a few weeks, I would only get 1 - 2 track days out of 2ct's as phillip island circuit is where I go and is hard on the left side of the tyre and when the track temp is above 20deg it chews through them...!!

I would not buy these just for track, but for road they are perfect in my eyes.!

I got the 180 on the back and not a problem at all with it..!

Post 22 by Menlo-SD, 18 Apr 2010, 03:59

#22
I just put a 190 Michelin on while doing a 520 gear and chain swap. The 190 has a steeper profile then the 180 and is close to the chain but there's no evidence of the chain rubbing the tyre. I use my SDR mainly for the track where a 190 offers some advantages over the 180. If I were to use this bike daily on the street I would stay with the 180.
