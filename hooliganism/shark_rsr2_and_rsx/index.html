#8
All my helmets are L and always have been, from Simpsons to arai Gigas and RX7`s to Dainese and now to my fave of all time my RSR2.
Used to love the designs on Shoei but just never fit my head proply!!!
With regard to wind noise and the naked bike, with correctly fitted ear plugs the noise level is more than exceptable and better than any of my Arais have been. It never leaks in the rain and it never mists during winter/colder days either.

The only time i have a problem is when i have the KTM tank bag fitted when i am out working, at motorway speeds (75-90mph) the wind tends to deflect of the front and straight up under the helmet and causes lift, although this happens with all my helmels!!!

The build quality and finish of the shark i cannot fault and they are the only manufacturer i no of that package the helmet with protective stickers on the areas of the helmet that will be rubbing on the inside of the box (inside the helmet bag). these stickers dont just peel off either, you have to use abit of heat on them like proper decals.

My only gripes with shark are 1. the tinted/dark visors are bloody expensive and, 2. im having trouble finding replacement cheek pads (ive lost weight recently and my face always thins out around my cheeks). Apart from those 2 this is my favourite ever helmet!
