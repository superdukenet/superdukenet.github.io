If Im riding just a medium pace I won't bother hanging off... but if Im really getting into it I hang off.

I once made the mistake of staying upright while going (too) fast and it cost me a $12k ZX10R and a week's stay at the hospital. Had I hung off I would've been fine.

Always, always hang off when going fast. The extra few degrees of lean angle you save could mean the difference between eating dinner and eating the curb.
