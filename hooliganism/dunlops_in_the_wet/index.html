I'd like to give the Dunlops a bit of a rave too. Was not expecting anything great but they are fantastic in the dry and I have found them pretty good in the wet [its the middle of winter here]. As said above I don't push it on wet roads.

I have been running Dunlops for years and love them.

Started with D207 then D208 then Qualifier then (with the Duke) D208RR.

After my stock tyres wore out I put the Qualifiers on.

Sounds like you need to back off the throttle in the wet before you get hurt. This has nothing to do with the tyres.
