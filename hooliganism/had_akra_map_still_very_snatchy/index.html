As this is the cheapest alternative, costing nothing but a little fuel. I would do the idle for at least fifteen minutes routine before throwing any other solution at it. It is important that the idle time at the installation of the new mapping is adhered to. I have noticed that not all days are the same as far as snatchiness is concerned. I would not like to attribute to just rider input nor to a finnicky bike as I think that both situations can exsaserbate simultaniously. In my experiance, the more I ride it under these conditions the more it settles down. This is what happens when You purchase a bike that has a soul. You need to meld your psyche with the Cyclee.
