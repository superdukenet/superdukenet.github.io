I wintered my bike every day last year and used ACF50, then Scottoiler 365 when that ran out, and even WD40 on the headers, and the bike is corrosion free and cleans up as-new.

You do need to keep applying the stuff, and it does collect dirt and grime fast. If you apply and then rub it off with a cloth is stays in all teh nooks and crannies, which is what you want, but it turns a weekly 2 minute job into a tough 30 minute chore. I opted to spray liberally, and then hose it all off (carefull) once every two weeks or so.
