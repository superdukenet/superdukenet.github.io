#3
The more your 'Track v Road' goes towards 'Track' ...the more you would want an SDR (08>)

The difference is about 7-8hp extra with the SDR at the very top end over a SD with similar loss below 7,000rpm and this seems to be repeated with each mod..so with similar modifications the SDR keeps the advantage

Much better rear shock (I have SD with SDR rear Shock.....so much kinder to rear tyre now) and more ground clearance (which is more important than you might think)

The pre 07 SD (small tank and non-radial brakes) fit between the SDR and the later SD regarding handling and power, when they brought out the SDR they softened the standard SD 08> slightly (steering geometry and softer cams…but gave it a bigger tank and radial brakes)
