A few of you where wanting ready to run weights on these
The wheel weights are as follows....

Bare weight Front 6.0lbs Rear 8.5lbs

Front weight with Carrozzeria wave rotors 12.5 lbs (rotors are 1.8 lbs light per set then Galfer wave rotors)

Rear Weight complete w/rotor, sprocket carrier and 525 sprocket 14.3 lbs
Stock rear comes in at 19.4lbs
