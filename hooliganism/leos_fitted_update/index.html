You can't have the akra remap with standard cans. According to a KTM bulliten, there is a risk the bike 'could catch fire', I guess from the extra fuel and cat combination. The KTM akra map is for 'baffles in', they don't do one for 'baffles out', however, as you are not running akras, the chances are the akra map won't be a perfect solution, so in or out the mapping is unlikely to be right anyway. The good thing is, the akra map seems to err on the rich side, so it's got to be better than no remap at all.
