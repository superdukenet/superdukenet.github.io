I've been thinking about getting a track bike and found a great deal on a Daytona 675. I enjoy the SD on track, and love it on the street, but the on/off throttle makes cracking open the throttle in low speed corners more stressful than it needs to be.

So, I've got a dilemma: get something with smoother fueling, or put a MH box and clip-ons on the SD. (Or, maybe quit being a pussy and learn how to ride with it.)

Please share your wisdom with me, and especially if you know whether the MH box tames the on/off throttle.

Do you have any other mods on the bike? I know when I put FMF pipes and had the acra map installed on my old SD it smoothed the throttle out a lot. Although it wasn't that bad to me to begin with. When I got my SDR it already had the Moto box installed and the throttle seems pretty smooth but it also has lots of other stuff done to it. Throttle cam, tune ecu, 41 tooth sprocket.
On another note the 675 would be fun at the track. you could do a bunch of stuff without worrying about it being street legal.
