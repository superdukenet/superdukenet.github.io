TJ, that was a very thorough reply.
Thank you for your time and trouble.

Regarding using the same PC3 as the standard superduke, even if the connectors are the same, the engine isn't, right? I mean, it makes more power, it revs higher, etc.
The problem is I live on a small island and there is no one to do custom maps here. There are no tuners here, and no dynos.
I can play around with a map, I have done it before, but I would need a base map. The base map from the standard superduke will not be suitable, right?

I guess I will try the cans and the Akro map first, like you said.
I read here that someone mounted FMF cans and the Akro map and the problem was solved.

I wanted to know for sure if the akro map helps anything, since the dealer (only ktm dealer on the island) is new and sells basically dirt bikes and quads, so he does not have the necessary equipment yet.
Owner is a friend and he said he will buy the equipment as soon as it becomes necessary, but I don't want to ask him to buy it just to install the akra maps if they don't help in the throttle issue.

The throttle tamer is out of the question, since, like you said, the throttle turn is already too long.

I don't know about 08 onward SDR being worse with the fueling or not, but mine is surely bad. I have previously owned several other bikes, and excluding the MV f4312R, they were all far, far better.
Even the 954, which at the time had the reputation for abrupt throttle. I had no problem with it. Sure, you had to respect it and use proper throttle inputs, but it was never actually an issue for me.

The SDR's chassis is incredibly agile. After my second ride I told the dealer that the bike, once moving, felt like it weighs just 100kg!
It feels like you have nothing bellow you! It is incredibly agile and changes direction with just a thought, you hardly have to move a muscle...
The problem is that that very same virtue only exacerbates the throttle issue.
Once leaned over on a slow corner, opening the throttle again in 2nd or even 3rd gear can really upset the chassis.

My bike seems to have a lot of throttle play, I will try to get it reduced.
The throttle itself is also not very smooth (the actual throttle, not the fueling), so that doens't help either.
Perhaps it needs lubricating.
I will ask the dealer to address these two issues.

Jester, again, thank you very much.
Your help is greatly apreciated.
