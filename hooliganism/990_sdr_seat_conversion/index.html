This subject comes up every so often.

It's far cheaper to buy the whole set up from KTM for the single seat conversion than to buy all the bits separately. It is fairly straight forward but will take a good few hours following the instructions.

The best place to get a kit in the UK (in my experience) is from Jim Aim. They will deliver within a few days. Very reliable. Just ring them and ask if the kit is still available.
