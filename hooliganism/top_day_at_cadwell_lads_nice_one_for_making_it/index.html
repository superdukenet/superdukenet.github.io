A big thank-you to Weeksy for getting the thing to happen.

I've been meaning to visit Cadders for the last 8 years, I'm glad I did it with the superduke massive.

Thanks to CC for leading me 'round on the scouser's 950 so I could remember where I should be going.

Picked up speed gradually in the morning, well aware that if you make a mistake on such a narrow track it can all go wrong. Managed to ground out my left peg on the left flick of the goose-neck (bit lazy hanging off on lefts.)

Daisy duke overtook a RC8, half way down the start / finish straight! It's all about the exit folks.

PM gave me a chance to try my new (second hand) wheels. Not as much fun as the dry, Harry Monk led me round to get my head around the wets, and then again, picked up the pace in small increments.

The last 15 minutes was me, Harry and Steve!

I WILL visit cadwell again, I'd like to get to know the place better.

Righto then - superduke.net 09 trackday. Who fancies a few hot laps of Brands Hatch Indie. I reckon we could fill 3 groups because it will appeal to more supermoto riders.
