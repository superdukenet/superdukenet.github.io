The R is extreme, the suspension stiff, the seat uncompromising and the engine is nuts, crap below 4000 but with a strong midrange and a top end kick at 10k revs like no twin I've owned. After riding more normal bikes it feels like an mx bike the way it wants gears and the way it wheelies. It's closer to my old 525SM than any other bike I've owned but can be used normally and tour if required.
It's def a love/hate thing tho !
