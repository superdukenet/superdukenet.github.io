I had a chance to look at a Ducati Streetfighter S yesterday, and arranged a test ride for tomorrow. In reading some Ducati forums online, I found that MANY SF owners are very upset about surging and rough, lean running at low RPM. Similar problems to the fueling issues with the SD, probably due to poor closed loop ECU control using oxygen sensors. The new SF owners are considering solutions that will sound familiar to SD owners. They are trying FatDuc O2 manipulators to alter closed-loop fueling, Termignoni slip-ons exhaust without catalytic converter and factory ECU remap to run open-loop all the time, and hoping for help from the PowerCommander V. Sound familiar?
