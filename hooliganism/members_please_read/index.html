I may not share the interests of the majority, but I don't come here as often b/c of the low signal to noise ratio.

I love learning more about the SD, but don't have time for all the irrelevant banter that I have to wade through to get to the good stuff.

My suggestions:
- add regional forums for GTG's and for sale stuff. I'm not going to buy something posted FS in Europe or Australia, and won't attend a GTG there unless I happen to be traveling in the area. I think it would be great to have a Texas forum to bring a greater sense of community to the local crowd (we'd prob. even let Joe visit it).

- Have a link to go to the first unread post near the top of every thread.
