I have the full GB racing kit on mine plus the KTM axle sliders. I also removed the KTM bar end weights in favour of some flashy little orange ones.

Tested the kit out at Cadwell on Monday, approx 70 MPH lowside at gooseneck.

Lower crashbung worn and bent (bent the engine mount) fixed with a swift dap or two with a rubber mallet, have ordered a replacement bung for £25 ish from GB
Axle sliders a bit scuffed
Gear lever spun round to face vertical. Undid bolt, realigned lever, did bolt up! (my linkage bar had also come loose which I lost on the next session but thankfully colinel Clink lent me one !
r/h rad panel scuffed but a mechanical polish removed most the damage
akro can has very light scratches which I'm sure will polish when I figure out what polish to use !
Very light scratches on R seat unit.
Overall very impressed as 20 mins of repairs had me out for the next session !
