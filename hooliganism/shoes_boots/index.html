I've been riding with my Salomon hiking boots for the past few years. I've had two offs and they've been great. They've go up around the ancle and you can tuck the laces in. They have good grip are very comfortable and I can take my bike up a mountain and go hiking...

I've recently purchased full leathers and Sidi Corsa boots which are great for protection (and looks) but I've found they give almost too much feel, i.e. a lot of vibration causing my foot to go a little numb.. and you can't really walk too far before it gets uncomfortable..
