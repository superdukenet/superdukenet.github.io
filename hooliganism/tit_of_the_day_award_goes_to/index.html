Me

On our ride out I managed to hit Jocke up the arse on a roundabout We were coming off the A41 at a fair speed, approached roundabout, I looked and saw space for me to go, thought I saw Jocke go, looked again and there was still a gap before the car and so I went but unfortunalty I hadn't seen Jocke go at all and it was all too late Locked the front, hit him on the rear right of his bike. Story goes that I flipped over the handlebars (can't remember that) and landed on my right hip (sore) calling myself every cnut under the sun for doing such a stupid thing.

Guys were on it like a flash, had my bike and myself up. Amazingly Jocke kept his bike upright. More amazingly he was totally cool about the whole thing where he could have easily gone nuts as hitting someone like that is a schoolboy error

Damage to my bike is minimal, bent key, scraped tank shroud on left, smashed mirror, scraped clutch lever (already scraped from Bedford off) and scratches on headlight mask where it decided to fook Jocke's right handside can

Damage to Jocke's is a fooked Akra Evo can (see pic) and his home made billet numberplate hanger (so sorry dude) and an indicator. Fingers crossed there is no damage to the subframe.


Thanks to Shade and all for not taking the piss too much And to Jocke for being so cool about it all.

If anyone knows a good place to get a Evo can I'd be most appreciative.
