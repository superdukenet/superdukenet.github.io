Mate, you're lucky you didn't strip the internal thread in the housing - that's more common than stripping the thread on the mirror.

Any bike mirror will fit as a replacement, it's down to your taste and budget. There's loads of threads on here showing what people have done. There's no right answer, it's all subjective.
