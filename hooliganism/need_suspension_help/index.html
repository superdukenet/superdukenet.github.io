Definitely rebound for the "kicking you out of the seat" issue.

Are your tyre pressures set correctly? as this may explain the front/back end step outs. Also ensure you dont have too much preload at either end.

Best bet is to get the suspension set up correctly by someone who knows what they are doing with regards static sag & rider sag and then you can play with compression and rebound settings safe in the knowledge that you have a good base point.
