I have worked with a lot of cnc machines, robotics and automation controls. After watching that race, the camera appears to be rotated by a servo motor. I dont think the gyro is in the camera. Rather, the gyro is located somewhere else on the bike, it's actuating a position encoder, and that is driving the servo.
A gyro stabalized cam has a more analog motion.
