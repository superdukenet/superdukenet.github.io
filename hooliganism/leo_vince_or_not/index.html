#4
Hang on to your dosh for a full system or at least replacement headers.
A slip on will save on weight and give you a little gain, but the headers are a bit shit IMO.
Akra Evo, Remus headers (will fit any slip on made for the SD/R), Fresco headers (not as well researched by forum members.
Try and save the major mods to do at once if you are looking to save money as each one requires a dyno tune
eg. Motobox/Rottibox/DNA, Rc8 stacks/DVS, Exhaust Slip on/Full system.
