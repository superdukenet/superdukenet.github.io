Post 2 by ShadeTheChangingMan, 07 Feb 2008, 22:58

#2
a few factors that could be coming into play:

road conditions: is your council gritting or worse, using that sugary crap on the roads at night? this stuff is really slippery!

your braking, do you grab it hard and fast? progressive braking is key.

weight distribution, where is your weight on the bike? Do you lean back when you brake? you need to get your weight over the front end gradually, let the front tyre bear the weight and compress slightly then apply more braking force.

Tyre pressure, what pressure are you running cold on the front? if it's too high or low you can experience poor handling/braking. I keep mine around 34-36psi and it's always good.

Sorry if any of that was obvious...but it sounds like you've got quite a problem there so just want to throw out all suggestions for you.

Finally, yeah, it could just be that those tyres dont like this cold and dont warm up enough to get the usual grip they are designed to give. I've never used them myself so cannot say what they should feel like.

Hope that helped, but I kinda expect it to all sound familiar.

yeah, what smoky says is true but also check that the pressure gauge is accurate, i had a tyre fitted and inflated to presure by the garage...rode it..felt llike crap, we double checked it....inflated to 40 psi instead of 36 and it was better. I resigned it to being a shite tyre but when i used my own pressure gauge it was way low....i inflated to correct pressure and bang, it was perfect. Called the garage, told him his gauge was off and he said "yeah, we tested it next day and binned it, sorry".

Moral: have a trusted and tested gauge
