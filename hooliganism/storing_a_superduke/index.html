Post 2 by wrebitup, 01 Sep 2009, 18:44

#2
depends how long she'll be sitting, and the climate... fuel stabiliser might be a good idea. If stored till spring and it gets pretty cold over winter, i'd take the battery out, leave it indoors on t.charger. I also like to clean and lube the chain before putting her away. If you have untreated concreate floor in the garage, and not planning on getting the front stand, i'd park the front tyre on something - maybe a piece of plywood, anything really - just to get the rubber of the cement.
if you went to track and running water/waterwetter, do not forget to replace it with coolant before parking her for winter!!! (only if stored below freezing pt)

cheers!
