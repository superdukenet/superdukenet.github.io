At this years NW200 Rutter, Jessop and other BMW riders fitted a small piece of perspex to their fairings to take the wind pressure of the brake lever.
The riders found after travelling at 200mph the brake lever came back to the bars and needed a couple of pumps before it worked but the perspex cured this problem.
The stewards didn't grant them dispensation to use the perspex, even on safety grounds so they had to remove them.

Just read this on an old MCN blog.

Still recon it's a waste of money on a superduke though.
