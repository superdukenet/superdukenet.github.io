I had to go to the shop to replace the clutch slave cylinder for one from Sigutech. While talking to the boss there is spotted a nail in de rear tyre, lucky bastard that i am. So i had to decide, put another Pirelli on the rear or change the whole set for MPP 2CT wich are the ones i like better.



Three days later i returned to the shop, because the tyres i ordered cam in.
Rear tyre was no problem. Then it was time for the fron end to get new rubber, but when we tried to lift the wheel we heard a strange sound en with no force at all brake pad number one came falling down. I mean really falling out of the caliper.
I read simular here on the forum (can find it anymore, BERT HELP PLEASE) and i was really schocked by the state the brake pads were in.
Don't want to think about what could have happened on the road or even on the track.
