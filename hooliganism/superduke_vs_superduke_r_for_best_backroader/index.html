I have an SDR, and I love it in every situation I've had it in. My prior bike is a Monster S2R800, and while it's sprung softer than the SDR and has a softer seat, it's nowhere near as comfortable and is sluggish handling in comparison. When I bought the R, I was torn between it and the standard SD. I think I'd have been happy with either, but bought the R because of the engine differences, the steering damper, and the way the tail looks. I don't think you'd go wrong with either.

I am actually surprised how well the SDR suspension handles on bumpy roads...to a point but then we are talking very bumpy stuff. Then you just have to slow down (while GS BMWs just continue ).
