Took the bike out for a quick blast down some fav roads yesterday afternoon. Was my first road ride since replacing the .50 G2 cam with the .25. All I can say is wow!!! Not a mod I would recommend if your bike doesn't fuel properly but if it does, this turns it into even more of an animal I suddenly find the front wheel lifting coming out of corners and roundabouts off the throttle all the time. I am in no way a wheelie man and it's an area I need to man the fook up in but this mod just makes it happen without really trying. It actually might make controlling a wheelie more difficult I guess but that's not something I can answer. With 6 points now on my license this could be a self defeating mod Still too much fun to take off though

Sorry TJ, I know I said I'd bell you if I went out but I only went 20 miles the other side of Chipping Ongar and back. Wouldn't have been worth the ride up to Essex for that.
