Post 8 by bic_bicknell, 08 Apr 2010, 05:39

#8
Most Dyno centers, even those experienced with setting up the race Superdukes, recommend doing a single map for both cylinders. But there are a few on the forum, (ktmguy and I think Joker), who have run dual maps.

In principle this is better as the rear cylinder has different temperature etc. but you may need to modify the threads where the sensors attach to the two header pipes so the dyno can put in the wide band sensors - you need to check with dyno shop first. Most people don't bother and get good results with a single map.
