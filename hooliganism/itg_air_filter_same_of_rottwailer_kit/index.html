Truth to be told - Rottweiler owns their base plate design, and orders upper foam part from ITG (non branded OEM order). Rottweiler has claimed the foam density is different on their filter - really I do not know if it is truth or not. If you order from ITG you have to do your own base plate, as they supply you aluminium base plate without the holes. So if you want to screw filter in I suggest to buy Rottweiler or MH kit (last one is especially beautiful piece).

In case if you are prepared for some work, JC 30-65 is the one you need for SD. Good luck with DIY

PS. The link in previous post shows ITG upper sock attached above JC 30-65 filter (like a hat). No need for doing this, I just tested it and bike runs nice.
