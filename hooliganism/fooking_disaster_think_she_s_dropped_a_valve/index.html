Went out for a quick ride this morn. Got 10 mins from home, opened her up and she died No major noises that I heard but it was sudden. I hope it's not something I did last week, I didn't touch the valves at all. Fook knows how much damage its done on the inside. Going to have to strip the rear cylinder and have a look. Not what I need less than a week before the next race and am feeling very broke without this. Maybe I should have changed the valves when I had the heads ported and polished 8k miles ago. Too late now though. Will update when I've found out what's caused this.
