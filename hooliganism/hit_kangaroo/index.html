I hit a roo tonight while riding home from work (I live in the Adelaide rural area). We
have a bad drought at the moment, so the risk of Roos running into you is
much greater. I was going down a hill. I pasted two cars (one with his high beam
on). Coming out of a left hand sweeper, after pasting the 2nd car, I saw a flash
on my right. A roo was going full stick into my side. It was a small/medium size
roo (Walaby size). Bang. Crush. Shudder. Bike shook violently. Sharp pain in
my leg/knee. The thing had clipped me. I think I knee'd it in the head. I
stopped in the next town to assess the damage.



So, I'm up for R/H fairing. If the oncoming muppet in the sedan didn't
have his high beams and spotty's on, I might have seen the roo
on the side of the road.

Does anyone know how much these fairings cost ?. If price is silly,
I probably won't worry about it.
