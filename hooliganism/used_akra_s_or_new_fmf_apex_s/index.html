I was like you looking for a set of cans where someone gave good feed back with before & after dyno runs to see the exact gains of all the different makes & model pipes. I was wonder could a budget exhaust produce the goods.

I did search for a long time but did not find what I was looking for & the Akra's were to expensive in my eyes even though I have used them in the past on other bikes & could not justify double the price compared to these Leovice exhausts.

My intentions were to buy a set of Leovince, FMF & Wings cans to do a shoot out between them & sell the ones that I did not want, but after testing the Leos first I am happy with the results & am sticking with them, I do not think there is much more Hp & Torque gains to be had from any of the other cans so have not gone any further, the Akra's & some of the others have a 1 to 1.5kg weight advantage over the Leo's.

Before anyone trys to shoot me down, I am not here trying to promote the Leovince cans, I am just providing the facts on my finds with them.
