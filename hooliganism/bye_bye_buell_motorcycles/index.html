I feel for the guy. I had a business that had a serious contract for equipment used by an industry leader, but could not raise money for it in the hayday of the internet startups that sucked up (and lost) all of the investor money out there. I had much passion, blood, sweat, tears into this product over several years, and it was accepted as a great product. But got caught in a cash flow deal. It hurts. The company I have now I started 25 years ago and it too is in an industry of passion. It would hurt big time if I lost it now.

I hope he is not tied up too many years with his contract. Now not even having his own brand, and having to work on heavy machinery that could never think of true competitive performance cannot inspire a guy such as him.

One thing the company never truly had was a sense of style. It was said above that we don't want to buy ugly bikes and that's true. Look at the originals.....function but no inspiring styling cues. Now look at the latest.....same problem. But he does not need my analysis, I just hope things look up for him. Watch the video and you can see what he's feeling. With his name he could do a boutique busines building a few bikes at a time and probably do very well.
