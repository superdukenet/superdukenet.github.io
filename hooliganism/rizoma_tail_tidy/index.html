I've been looking into them a lot today, it seems to me that the R&G one will not allow the indicators to be fitted or seen if you have the longer round Akra race cans.

And the Rizoma one is just way too complicated....it seems to stick out more at the rear too due to its larger plate that helps it go beyond the cans.

Neither are a great solution if you have round Akras IMHO. Evotech do a tail tidy for the Duke which shares the same rear light as the SDR but again looks like it's location for the indicators is too high for the cans.

Basically the indicators need to go under the cans at the bottom of the number plate section I think.

I really want a tail tidy for my R but still cannot find a good one. Any more ideas anyone?
