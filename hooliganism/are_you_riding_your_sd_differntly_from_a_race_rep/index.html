I traded my 05 Blade for the SD... The blade I had to climb off a bit to help yank it over, mainly through changes of direction. I just got into the habit of climbing off all the time (when riding quickly).
SD on the roads, I ride almost like a motocross bike, elbows up, against the tank. Didn't think to do it, just realised what I was doing after riding it. Started learning how to back it in, and sitting up on the tank feels much better and more in control...

At the track I actually climb off more on the SD, because ground clearance isn't that great (compared to other nakeds it's brilliant though).
