Ok, so I received the new Yoshi's in the mail yesterday. I have to say I am really impressed with the quality of the cans. The welding is beautiful and the cans fit and finish is like a piece of jewelry. I cant wait to mount them this weekend...not that I will be able to ride with the 2.5 feet of snow we have on the ground.

As a technical aside, I have always been of the opinion that any fuel injected bike does not need to be remapped when fitted with a slip on exhaust as long as no changes are made on the intake. The ecu should be able to correct foe the increased flow as long as the airbox is not gulping any additional air.

Anyone else have thoughts on this?
