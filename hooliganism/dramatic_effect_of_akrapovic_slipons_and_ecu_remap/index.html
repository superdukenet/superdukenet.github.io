I know the regulars on this board are fully aware of the effect of this mod, but I just have to exult about the effect of this change on my '08 SD. I just had the 1000 mile (1600 km) service done, including valve adjustment, and also fitted the Akrapovic titanium Evo slipons. The ECU received the factory remap for the Akra slipons. The weight of the stock mufflers was stunning when I took them back to keep at home. I am thrilled to get that heavy thing off my bike.

The effect on the motor's performance was dramatic as soon as I left the dealership. The motor is much smoother at all RPM (this might have something to do with the valve adjustment as well, of course). Previously, the engine was hard to hold at a fixed RPM below about 5000 RPM. Now, it is stable at any RPM, all the way from idle up. There is no more afterfiring on a closed throttle during engine braking.

I assume much of this improvement is due to the completely open loop operation of the ECU with the remap, with the O2 sensors out of the loop, as I have learned from members of this board. The overall effect is to make the bike much more enjoyable to ride. It still has great punch from low RPM and the top end is as strong or better. I don't know how fuel consumption will be affected, but it really doesn't matter because the drivability of the engine is so much improved.

My advice to any SD owner is to run, don't walk, to get an aftermarket exhaust (I assume it's not necessarily just Akra's) and the Akra ECU remap from KTM. It changes the bike dramatically for the better. You won't regret it.
