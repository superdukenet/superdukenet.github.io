So a strange thought occurred to me today and it was this.

Does anybody want to buy a pretty SDR 08/09 with an 05 engine?

Now I know I could watch breakers and fleabay for an SDR engine but the idea just popped into my head that some folk might love the styling of the SDR but not the twitchy power, whereas I prefer the opposite.
