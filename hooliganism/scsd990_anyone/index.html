Good day from way too cold Finland atm.

Ive had SD for a while. The same bike twice actually. Made my second big mistake 4 years ago and sold her. Found and bought back last winter. Drove last summer a bit and felt lack of power. Started googling if anyone have put some positive pressure on intake manifold... I couldnt find any, and that where the idea came from. Gotta have it supercharged.
Started making some preparations, benched injectors and replaced with bigger ones. Got rid of evap shit. Moved battery etc. cables from the hugger to underneath seat. Made new brackets and so on. I'll be adding some photos if anyones interested and I find out how to do it...
