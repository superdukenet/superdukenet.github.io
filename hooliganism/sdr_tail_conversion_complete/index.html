boney95 Forum member Topic author

Post 13 by boney95, 23 Jul 2011, 22:47

#13
It sits great for me. I like the harder seat. You can move your ass around a bit easier. I'm using the 17mm seat raisers, but my feet still flat foot. With the SD seat I would be on my toes just a tiny bit. But the biggest change is the width. The SDR seat is a lot more narrow (which you want). Thus why I can flat foot it w even the raisers. I recommend the SDR seat.
