I dipped a butterknife blade in dish soap, then slid the knife in between the handlebar and grip, roating it all away around the handlebar. It separated where the grip had been held in place from the factory with adhesive, made the grips slide off easily, and I was able to reuse the factory grips after I installed grip warmers.
