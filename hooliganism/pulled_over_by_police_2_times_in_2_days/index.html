Post 3 by Grimace, 11 Apr 2007, 01:13

#3
I went and bought a piece of stainless steel, about the size/thickness of a standard ruler.

I measured it up, cut it, and bent it carefully with a vice and a pair of vice grips and the edge of the work bench.

Measured it up again and drilled four holes (two that mount to the plate, two more that mount to the fender).

Fits perfectly and looks good.

Looks like this.. (cheap rendering is close except the two lines at the top extend upwards too far, but I don't own a digital camera so use your imageination....) The outer tabs is what the plate bolts onto, the lower cross section is where it bolts to the fender.
