Thought i would have a go at doing some graphics for me black shark helmet.






Debadged it first then sat and looked at it for a while. then it was time for some A4 paper and a pencil.
Basically drew some shapes and cut them out to see what they looked like....finished product was a bit different to how i first imagined it....and when you have the desired shapes you use them as a template for the vinyl.












When applying the vinyl spray loads of water on the surface and vinyl so you can move it about till it's in the right place.....then use a credit card wrapped in a cloth to expel out all the water.

Gonna do the bike next....change the side panels for orange graphics and do 999 on the bottom and maybe front headlight mask.
