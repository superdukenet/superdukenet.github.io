I removed the "can in arse" last weekend based on the various threads on this site and elsewhere. I ran the hose that comes from the fuel tank (you can tell by the gasoline smell) down to some of its "brothers and sisters" (secure and tie off with zip ties - preferably orange). The other hose I plugged with a .10 cent vacuum cap and stowed under the seat with the wiring harness and tool kit. Would like to trash the "Can in the arse" but holding on to it in case Barry mandates national motorcycle emissions, etc. testing.
