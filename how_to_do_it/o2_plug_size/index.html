completed the O2 removal yesterday. The plugs worked great not too long washers were not needed. When there was a about 2 or 3 mm left I had to get on it I guess it the flange at the botom of the bung but a little WD40 and a longe allen key and it was a done deal.I removed it once it was done clenaed up the WD and add Anti Seize to the threads and reinstalled the plug.

Note I did discover that inorder to do the rear correctly I had to remove the header. Additional work but it got done right.
