You can fit the EvoTech tidy without cutting the bike (SD not R). Fitting instructions show cutting off the duckbill right under the rear light, but I preferred to mod the tidy rather than cutting the bike so you can return back to standard if you need to. Modding the tidy means original number plate light wont fit, so I fitted two threaded bolt LED's straight into the tidy.
