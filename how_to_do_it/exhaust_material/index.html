#16
when i polished mine up i just used autosol . started with a medium wire wool and lots of autosol and hard work - tho not putting to much pressure on - the wire wool gets rid of the tarnishing . i just got progressively finer on the wire wool then to a fine scotchbrite pad with autosol and finally finished off with normal rags . took some bl##dy time but came up well . only took a minimum of "maintenance" polishing to keep on top of it . looked spot on with some heat through the pipe to give it a nice bronze finish .
hope this helps
