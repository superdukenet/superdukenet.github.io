I have installed a gear indicator to my SD.
Here's the how to:

The Acumen DG8 gear indicator has 5 wires that are connected as follows.

ACUMEN DG8 --> 2005-2006 Superduke

TACHO Brown --> BLUE and WHITE (can be found in the tachometers wiring loom)

SPEEDO Orange --> BLACK and ORANGE (can be found in the speed sensor wiring)

NEUTRAL Green --> GREEN and BLACK (can be found in the multi-func.-digital-speedometer wiring loom)

IGNITION SWITCH Red --> YELLOW and BLUE (can be found in the tachometers wiring loom)

GROUND Black --> BROWN (can be found in the tachometers wiring loom)

Soldering all connections is highly recommended! Also make sure that the wires are well insulated and don't connect to each other.

After this I set up the DG8 as instructed in the installation er... instructions.
You have to to do this by riding the bike, but it's no problem. Takes about 3-5km and a couple of minutes.

All connections can be made with the battery connected and the ignition OFF.

Before cutting wires, double check that the colour are correct. There are many wires and many colours in there...
