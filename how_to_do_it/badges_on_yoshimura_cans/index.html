Post 9 by Linga, 04 Oct 2012, 05:06

#9
I usually de-brand everything. It's the business I'm in but I don't see why I should go round advertising people's businesses for them when they don't pay me to do so.

All labels and badges will come off somehow. I'd drill out the rivets and remove the Yoshi plates. Any glue will clean off somehow with some sort of solvent. Depending on the size of the holes I would either fill them, put in some more pop rivets or best still source some small, black rubber grommets and plug them up, they will be very discreet even if you have to enlarge the holes to fit them.
