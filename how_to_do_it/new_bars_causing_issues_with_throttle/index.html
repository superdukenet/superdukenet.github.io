Superdan Forum member

Post 2 by Superdan, 03 Aug 2011, 18:57

#2
Follow the two cables from the grip throttle, about 10 cm away is a rubber cover, under which is some adjustment for the throttle to tighten it up, you need a 10 or 8mm spanner if I recall.

Make sure your grips are free to spin back without fouling the bar end too.
