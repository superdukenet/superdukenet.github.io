Dear Facujak72.

Forgive me, I am drunk. Listening to mad music and intent on inebriation. But...

You are a good boy indeed to be considering these two improvements to your poor standard Superduke's aspirational abilities. As it was born, the poor fooker was restricted beyond belief - as though it had a strangulation about it's orange throat. Your ideas for a Motobox and free breathing exhausts will make it a happy bunny.

But the Akra map, as supplied by KTM, will not really be good enough for your lovely baby, or you. It doesn't matter that you ride on track or road. You need to get a custom map done so that the fuelling can be optimised for the free air flow goodies that you, as a caring parent, are gifting your baby. If you are planning to spend hundreds of pounds on a Motobox and upwards of £500 - £1000 on an exhaust then why not spend a few extra pennies on a map to make them do what they do best? The fuelling and ride and power and experience will be very, very noticable.

You have two options. Get a PC111 and get a custom map at a dyno center. (£150 quid) Or do what a lot of the guys are doing on here and get a tune ECU thingy and do it yourself. There is a lot of help on here. It will follow by other more reassuring people in the morning I'm sure after this drunken fool has collapsed into a coma.

Good luck.
