#2
Ethanol in US gas pumps causes excessive expansion and contraction of the plastic gas tanks mounted on Ducatis. This may lead to cracking or leaking in extreme cases, but for most Duc owners it has meant bubbling paint only. When drained of gas, the tanks will generally return to their original size. It has been a well documented problem since Duc began shipping the tanks to the US. For the most part Ducati has been very good about replacing the tanks under warranty. The maker of the Duc tank is the same as the SD tank...Ascus or Ascari, or something like that. So far it has not been much of an issue in Europe due to the availability of regular gas. It has not yet been an issue for my SD. Nasty stuff that ethanol...bad for CO2 emission also. Should be banned.
