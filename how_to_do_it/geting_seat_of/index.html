Preey once you get the seat off you need to put fine amounts of grease or vasoline on the rubber things on the bottom of the seat and that seat will come off and on like you wont believe.
The rubbers keep the seat from rubbing on the sub frame also to get the seat off you need to unlock first and the key wont come out of lock when its unlocked so just incase you might be locking it back down and taking the key out OK then lift back of seat up and while you do you need to push down on the middle of seat and pull back with a wiggle and it will slowly start to slide back. GOOD LUCK.
