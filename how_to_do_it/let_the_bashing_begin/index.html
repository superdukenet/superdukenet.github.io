The diamond plate is a 12" by 24" aluiminem I got from Lowes. Bent it down by hand on the edge of the work bench. It just sits on the seat and the bag straps hold it in place. The rear strap needed to be criscrossed to keep it out of the exaust. Works real well, tested to 100 mph. The bit of fairing under the net I pick up at the Ohio Vintage Days. I used it as a mold for the fiberglass sheild.
