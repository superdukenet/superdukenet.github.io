I have heads in the hands, I will be surprise if there's no gains to get there!! Match valve seat with combustion chamber/intake-exhaust port, remove sharp edge lengthen the radius, polish exhaust port, replace the rough casting finish at the intake by a sand blast surface. I got good result by giving more air to this engine, remove 2nd files/shaft and ITG air filter make a great difference so I guest that use a larger throttle bodies like SDR (but its use 3mm larger valve) and head port gonna help too

Ok so:
I gas flowed the heads,
Fitted race cams,
Put breathers into the crank cases and cam covers
Fitted cp carillo high compression pistons
Modified stock throttle bodies, with different velocity stacks
Rc8r throttle bodies
Fitted Akra evo and SDR evo full systems
Tried no airbox, modified stock air boxes and DNA stage 3

I tried pretty much every combination I could think of and the best result I got from a non- SDR motor was 125.5 at the rear wheel with no airbox, Akra evo system and stock throttle bodies with flies removed.
Almost as good with my modded airbox or the DNA.

Of course, if you want to give it a go yourself I have a set of refurbed barrels with £500 worth of cp carillo high comp pistons looking for a good home and I'd even throw in the heads and cams but they're damaged so may not be salvageable. All yours for £250 plus carrage but don't say I didn't warn you!
