Techmount Radar Detector Mount

Installed the Techmount Radar Detector Mount on my 2017 KTM SDGT on the Right Control Panel area ( http://www.techmounts.com/products/inde ... uct_id=289 ). Added a few extension pieces to get the weight correct. Power supplied by a HardTap Radar Detector wire kit ( https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B0062 ... UTF8&psc=1 ) connected to a PDM 60 ( https://www.revzilla.com/motorcycle/row ... ckQAvD_BwE ). Power could easly be drawn from the OEM accessory cock or there are power lines below the instrument panel. Best,
