Post 5 by Bert, 28 Jan 2012, 13:01

#5
Get yourself a tuneECU cable and disable O2 sensors in the ECU. No need for eliminators then.
The sas is there to lower the emissions of the bike.
Just get a couple of SAS plates () and also disable SAS in Tune ECU (it's called SAE in the program). How to remove the SAS is also explained in the Motobox installation manual.
