Post 2 by QuietRider, 27 Apr 2014, 01:53

#2
While there may be an optimum sequence of steps, I really think the only key thing is to let things line up "naturally" by working the fork up and down a few times with the fork pinch bolts loosened on both sides of the axle. You probably will need to snug the right side pinch bolts to tighten the axle nut, but after you have everything together loosen the pinch bolts on both sides, straddle the bike and use your weight to work the forks up and down a few times. Ideally you should put the front wheel against something rather than use the brake, but I think either will work. I do it with the bike on the rear stand and don't engage the brake. Then tighten the pinch bolts and all will be well.

You did remember to tighten those pinch bolts, right?
