OK,

Tired of Photobucket's fee based membership, I opened an account with .

The process of attaching pictures is actually easier than Photobucket. I'll try to explain.

Create account. Create Album. Upload pictures to album. All those steps are pretty straight forward.

Once you are looking at the picture you want, you would let your mouse hover over the down arrow on the top right corner, and click on get share links. There, you will choose COPY on the BBCode (Forums)

Once done, you come back to writing the post on SuperDuke.net and you'll paste. There is no need to use the img button [ img ] [ /img ] as it's already inserted from imgur.

Now the royal pain in the ass is going to be moving all the pictures from Photobucket into imgur and link all old threads/posts to the new location. That's going to take a while.





If I could figure it out, so should you. Good luck.
