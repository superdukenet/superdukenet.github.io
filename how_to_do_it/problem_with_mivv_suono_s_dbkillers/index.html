Okay. I was getting p@sswd off everytime I took em out for a Trackday the little grub screw holding em in managed to drop down into the muffler casing instead of sticking to the allen key and coming out nice and easy through that horrible little opening. Anyway, I opened the hole up to allow an M5 machine screw thru and welded an M5 nut in on the inside of the annulus. Now I put two nuts onto the screw to lock them into position at the right stand off. Now I can take the killers out with ease and coz the screws are nice and long, they transit all the way through so can't drop inside.

Rubbish pics might help, but anyone needing more info just pm me..;
