#6
the main reason that thing costs so much is it is built around a custom prism. You cant really tell from that pic but it's about 200mm high and weighs over 2Kg due to the glass. and yes, it comes in antistatic wrap and I use a wrist band to earth myself when handling it. It also has a special bolt on handle for us to use when inserting and removing it from the light path. Special air and liquid cooling with heat sinks and contact paste, plus sensors, power cables and ribbon cables all jammed into a projector head.

I love doing jobs like that....as long as I have enough time to do it of course! You cant rush that one.
