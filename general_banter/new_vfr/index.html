#27
I like the new VFR. It's heavier than I prefer, but that doesn't degrade the appearance. It won't be something you mistake for another bike, and it's got some interesting engineering concepts, even though I'm curious to see how the dual clutch transmission and narrow rear/wide front cylinder banks work out with respect to longevity. Depending on how coolant passages are routed, I'm interested to know if there will be hot spots created on the rear cylinder bank because it has to do the same amount of work with less material to disperse heat and possibly less coolant volume.

Regardless, I've always liked VFRs, and I admire the fact that Honda made something with a different approach to styling and engineering. I bet the looks are going to grow on people.
