Nobody discussing this here? guess I'll start...





460lbs, 160hp.. 50mm Marzocchi forks, Brembo monoblocs, Marchesini forged wheels, etc etc. Sounds stacked with quality parts as usual from Ducati, and that power and weight is unheard of in the cruiser market. Closest competition I assume would be the VROD with 120hp and 600lbs and the VMAX with 200hp and 630lbs, neither particularly agile bikes. Also the word is that Ducati worked with Pirelli to design a tyre specifically for this bike, they wound up with a 240mm section and apparently a nice round profile so it can still handle with a steam-roller mounted on the back.

I think it looks good and I can't wait to try one out. Its also super fun listening to die hard Ducati guys bitch and whine about it as if its the end of the company.
