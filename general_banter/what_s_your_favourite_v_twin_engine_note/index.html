#21
Have to be the 90 degree V-twins for me (and even the TRX850 with it's 270 crank sounds pretty good for a "pretend" V-twin).
The old 888s and 748s with open Termis are favourites, sound a bit creamier than the 916/996. Less bark although not as deep a note.
As for the Jap V-Twins, I prefer the TL1000s to the VTR1000s (too much whine from the gear driven cams).
Other configurations... The Aprilia RSVs always sound a bit buzzy/sewing machine like to me, same as the Buell 1125R/CRs (72 degree). Although not much difference from the 75 degree 990 Super Dukes, I think the Super Duke sounds a lot better (at least my mate's with open Staintune cans does).
