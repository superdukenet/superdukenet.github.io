Whilst stopped at a BK/Little Chef car park one summer this happened. My chicadee and her friend, both on GSX-Rs had parked up very close to each other, and for some reason I was parked some 5 or so places across the car park. These two SuperHero Transformers roll up on their plastic superbikes (you know, the Motrax catalogue, loud exhausts, cheap leathers, expensive race-rep-helmet, etc). They spot the Nürburgring stickers on the two GSX-Rs, and started making friendly with the ladies.
Now, these guys knew the 'Ring all right!! They go there "all the time" (and in 7 years I've never seen them ). They said they had "season tickets" (should be year ticket or more correctly Jahresausweis)... but what was the most gobsmacking fact they told the ladies was that.
"You better get there at the end of this season. There's going to be a big party there because they're changing the Northloop. They're going to cut half of it off and make a track in between."

There you have it guys! They're going to cut the 'Ring in half and we're going to party because they do it... needless to say we didn't rush to the party and seveal years later the 'Ring is still very close to it's original 1927 incarnation.
