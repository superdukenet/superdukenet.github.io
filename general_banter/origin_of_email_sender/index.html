If the card is stolen its your problem, not the bank - read your merchant T&C's, they don't indemnify you against fraud. The bank or merchant services provider will do what called a charge back, they have 6 months to do it from the purchase date and it will hurt as they still take their transaction processing fee (Standard rate around 2.75%), charge you a "Charge Back Fee" and also deduct the total sum.

Your out of pocket and the bank still get their percentage, so much for credit card fraud being a victimless crime. There's some justification to the argument that banks/merchant service providers are benefiting from fraud because of them keeping the transaction percentage and charging the charge back fee.
