I've had a look at those, but I opted for the Archos path.



This is the smallest solid state previous generation Archos recorder with the bullet camera. Sadly I've not been able to test it out properly, nor have I had time to fit it.

I need to fit it stealthy, so it's not visible at all. The most annoying part is that the cable has got all the controls on it. And the microphone is in there too. My initial plan is to mount it near the battery so I get a view of the front wheel. Then the recorder somewhere where I can get to it fairly easily. Suggestions please! Then I was going to butcher the controls and put in secondary buttons/lights on a separate cable. But I'm very afraid to damage something or to screw it up (for example electrical disturbances).

So whilst the ATK2 has got it's shortcomings, it's sure got some good points too!
