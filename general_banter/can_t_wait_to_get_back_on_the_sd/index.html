Latest update.

Seven weeks having a stupid cast on, even if it is a modern plastic one that comes off at night, it's a pain in the arse. For the last three days I have not worn it and have self diagnosed myself cured. Op scars are healing well over the top of the last ones from ten years ago. Hopefully I won't be having more in another ten. I've been walking about OK and everything feels fine, a bit stiff and weird feeling but I can now get my leathers and left bike boot on. Been riding the Yamaha into work but might try the SD tomorrow to give the gears a go. I think it will be OK. Have a hospital appointment on Thursday when I hope they will officially tell me to lose the cast. I will look a bit of a fool if they say, "Well, now you have to wear the cast for another month before doing anything strenuous", but that's life I guess. I will have to put it on for the appointment and act like it's not been off before. Can't wait to get on the SD again, even in this shitty weather.

As you no Bic, the cast only came off my wrist around 6 weeks ago and it felt very stiff and very weird, almost like it wasnt my wrist The specialist told me not to do anything strenuous with it and let it heal and expect to be back in the gym in around 3-6 months

Anyway, due to other health concerns ive been out of the gym for the last 18months and was determined to start back at the begining of 2011 no matter what . So after only 2 weeks out of the cast i hit the gym, ive spent most days working on flexability and stretching it out everyday and its paying off! After only 3 weeks in the gym im already deadlifting over 200lbs again and benching over 150lbs again (OK so nowhere near what im used to but i havnt done any exercise for 18months plus the broken wrist) and all pain free with the wrist

What im saying is, only you no your body and your pain threshold, dont let the specialists dictate to you and take it day to day! Do your physio everyday and small excersises to get the strength back in that ankle and calf/leg as soon as possible (id recommend high repitition lunges and the stationary bike) - and wel see you out on track come april mate
