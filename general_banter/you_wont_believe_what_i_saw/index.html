Saw a very interesting TV program about this a few years ago. There was this oddball bloke that drove round the UK in his VW camper investigating big cat sightings, he had loads of pics of animals that had been attacked by these cats and pics of paw prints.

2 expert big cat hunters came over from the states and looked at his evidence, there replies were a little like this;-

That's a dog paw print
That's a dog animal attack

repeat repeat repeat and the best part of the program was the little weirdo in his VW all but breaking out in tears as the experts told him he was wrong and that nothing they had seen gave any indication of big cats being present in the wild in this country.!

So Danny unless you bring back some pretty good evidence I'm gonna struggle to believe you saw what you think you saw.
