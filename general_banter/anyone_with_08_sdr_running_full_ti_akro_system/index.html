freestyler i have the stainless race headers and they are identical looking to the Ti system, same shiny welds in the same place and same bends, its a very nice looking system. they come apart the same way and are coupled with springs just like the Ti system. and for the record i must concur with the magnet test. i held a strong magnet up to the race headers and it didnt stick so i tried it on the stock headers and muffler and it didnt stick to them either so there you have it first hand hand for all you sceptics out there.
