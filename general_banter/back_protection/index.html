Nice ! After going to the m/c races at Laguna Seca multi times and spending time drooling in the pits and looking at thier light weight stands they use, I fashioned my own stand from bits and pieces I had access to. I cut down a heavy dudy work bench top and fashioned some welded folding legs. I do not have a lift to it, but use my loading ramp. When it is not up on the legs, it is only 2 inches tall. I had a local shop bend a small ramp and place it in front of the folded up ramp and can roll my bike up on it with ease. They also bent a piece that I bolted to the front as a bump stop so the bike has something to rest against as I roll the bike up onto the stand one manned. It took a bit to get use to, starting off with my heavy Ducati ST4S. EZ PZ with the SuperDuke. The KTM 950 also gets up there. So nice not to work on the floor. I have an old office chair on wheels and can sit on my ass and scoot around the stand and do a lot of maintenance from the chair.
