I don't live there, but have been several times. Barber Motorsports Park has a tremendous track, and while there you get to go see the museum which is incredible. 4 stories of great motorcycles/history. They have a complete restoration shop on the basement floor where they can even fabricate parts as needed. They also have a library of motorcycle books that is gigantic. Apparently you can see the library with permission but I've not. http://barbermuseum.org/

I have been there also. It is a great place to put on a must see while in the area, but a little removed for a standard overseas tour. Your best bet is to do a track day and visit during a sanctiond race.
