Well, joe hates to announce it but I had just had another off! It hurts like hell and I have to work hard today.

It just happened no more the 1 hour ago, I was taking the faster way to work, stopped at the stop sign 30 yards from

my house to take a left and this crazy young bitch was going right the opposite way, she approached the stop sign doing 55 in a 25,,,, so

I was in a hurry to get in front of her and lost the front-end, WHAM!!! DOWN ON THE LEFT SIDE AGAIN AT 5 MPH!!!!

freeking sucks!! the whole crash scene took 5 min's, my ribs hurt like hell, the first thing i did upon standing and picking up the bike,,,,

she slowed down and waved as if to say sorry, I am in a big fookin hurry and nearly tried to run yer ass over,

well, i rode the busted up bike right back to her hole and parked her, went in and told the missus,,,

"sorry" to say i have just been run off the road by some bitch and I have gone down again!

well, I blame her now... but ultimately it was my own fault for letting her actions influence mine.

joe thinks the pirelli's are junk in the 34 degree weather, slick as snot!!

it only damaged the previously damaged parts and sliders and killed my pride and ego.

joe has to find the blessing in the curse somehow, It is my desire, my destiny, my lesson.
