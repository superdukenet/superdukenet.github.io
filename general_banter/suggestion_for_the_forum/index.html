First off I'd like to say thanks for all the great info and rainy day entertainment I've gotten here since I caught the orange fever. I had an idea earlier that I think might make it easier for us newbs, and maybe even a few seasoned hooligans to get a good KTM education here. A sticky glossary of all the SD jargon you guys use. Just a list of the words, acronyms and phrases you guys use and a short definition of what it does, what it means, etc. Example:

Lambda probe
SAS
AFR
TSP
RB (still haven't figured this one out)
100 cam

and so on...

Hope this will help, and if not, no worries.
