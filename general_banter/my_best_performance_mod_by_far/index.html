I've had issues with my suspension since I bought the bike. Although I had it dialed in by KTM suspension guru, Josh Barnik, the bike was just never quite perfect. It worked OK on the street, but got squirrelly when I started pushing it. It was absolutely terrifying on the track. Wallowing around like a pig and taking every opportunity it could get to try and chuck me off. I decided I had to do something about it.

So I did. I lost 25 lbs. Now, that wallowing suspension is taut. So taut it nearly bounced me off when I crested a whoop dee yesterday. I freaking love it.
