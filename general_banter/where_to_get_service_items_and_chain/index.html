Post 2 by Colonel_Klinck, 18 Mar 2011, 16:38

#2
You might find oil filters slightly cheaper elsewhere but Gear4 is pretty competitive and they do next day delivery.
You will need.
1x oil filter, don't bother with screens as in my 40k miles they have yet to have any damage to them, just clean them.
Crush washers for sump plug and oil tank drain plug. O-ring for oil filter and gaskets for both screens.
Oil is maybe £3-4 cheaper elsewhere but I still go to my dealer.
Fuel filter does not need changing.
Clutch fluid and brake fluid again from KTM dealer.
