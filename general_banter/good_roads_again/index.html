I relocated to Bristol yesterday. Rented out the big house I've spent five years renovating and moved into a small terraced house with no garage or driveway. Had to sort out a storage container type place to keep all my tools and workshop stuff and bikes of course. It will be very different life for a while.

But being back here makes me realise how shit the roads were in Surrey for bikes. Out here the roads around Somerset are awesome. Did a good eighty miles today and ended up down in Dorset. Even with bank holiday traffic it was great riding. Frome, Glastonbury, Shepton Mallet, Dorchester.

Very hot in black leathers here though. Only bearable at speed, traffic lights a nightmare!
