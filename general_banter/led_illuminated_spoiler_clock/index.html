Had a small accident and ended up with an extra spoiler of an SD. Wondered for a while what to do with it and then decided to go for a LED-illuminated clock to hang on the wall. All I need now is a proper wall for it.
It has 12 3mm LED's on it, a 9V battery as a power source and a LDR-resistor (Light Dependent Resistor) to automatically adjust the LED on/off/brightness when the amount of light reduces. Think it works out just fine and should be a KTM factory manufactured fan item. Will sell the rights for the patent for gents in Austria, pls PM me
