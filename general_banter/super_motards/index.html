Excellent forum here for SM's.

I've not ridden any, but have been close to buying most of the above and read lots on them.

Expect 1 rebuild a year on them. Full rebuild that is £1000GBP is usual on a yearly basis.

Tank ranges of 50 miles not uncommon.

However they seem to be amazing fun and the best pleasure you can have on 2 wheels.

I was tempted by the Husky 510smr or sm510r if being picky but in all honesty the 610SM made a lot more sense.

In the end, common sense prevailled and i got the SD again.
