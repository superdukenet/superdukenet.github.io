the GS`s, well, not my cup of tea but in fairness a very good bike if you are looking for that sort of thing, still, i believe they are very very overpriced.

so my next bike, a naked for sure, tho i admit i am between the factory V4 Tuono and the SD1290.

Aphex, i went with a 15 ZX10R, it was a bike i knew i would not get the most out of, i do enjoy it but not in the same way i had the 1290.

not done as much track this year but it has way too much power for me to use, i lapped quicker on the RC8 i had and that was 40hp down on the zx10r.

my hate love with KTM started in 07 with a SD990 which i had for two years, then i went with the RC8 with clubman kit on, i had that for two years and then i went crazy waiting for the new SD to come out and bought a new SMR990 which i enjoyed but got fed up looking for fuel, still waiting for the new SD i went and bought a VFR1200 untill the SD1290 came out.

saw the SD1290 at the bike show in Valencia in November and by December 2013 i had the SD in the garage............. then i had oil leaks, coolant leaks, binding brakes, wobbly wheel, fork seals going bang,............. everytime i was come in from the track something would be leaking from somewhere......,..,.. at the end my bike got shipped off to Austria and they lent me a new SD1290 to use whilst mine was being sorted out.

when my bike arrived from Austria it had passed throught KTM Spain and then to the dealer, i picked her up all happy and when i was at home looking at her i knew something was not right, someone along the line had forgot to put the clamp on the exhaust and it was just slinding off the cat and only held on with the rear peg holder...........................

ah, i also had faulty steering damper which too be honest was masked by the poor quality of the original one
