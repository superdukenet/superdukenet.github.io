Hi everybody.

I was wondering what camera do you use when cruising your bike around the town? Or do you use any camera at all?

I have used a Contour+ action camera when i cruise my beast around the city. Couple days a go i bought new camera Sony FDR-X1000V, camera can shoot 4K quality videos. I really like that i can adjust my new camera via smartphone and i can use my phone as remote control.

Well, the Contour+ had same kind of feature but it didn't work as smooth and always something wrong with it. Sometimes it started to record but after couple minutes camera went down and i had to remove battery and reinstall it to get it work again. I think i'll just but my Contour+ to the retirement and focus on my new Sony.

I got a Drift Stealth 2. Big + is its small size and HD quality.
Comes with helmet mounts included in the box, waterproof to about 1m without the need for a case.
Operate with a remote or via your mobile.
Its pretty cheap too.
Negatives, no replaceable battery is probably its biggest. Get about 2 hours recording time.
Best bit is you dont look a dick with a Go-Pro mounted to your helmet. Just ask Schumacher.
