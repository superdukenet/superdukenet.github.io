It was a great trip a the weather was PERFECT! We did three states and Canada. We took our time and enjoyed every bit of it. Cant' wait till next time!

The mighty Columbia River Gorge . Two weeks after I shot this we were standing at the headwaters...


We stayed at Rustic Cabins and Camped....




I did a lot of fishing and that was dinner many nights...


Great fishing lakes everywhere...

Real Good fishing at Kokanee Glacier...


This was the best road of the trip, Rattlesnake Pass Idaho...

We camped here and I pulled out the best fish of the trip...

This guy lasted for days in my cold pak...Fish for breakie, lunch and dinner....


The scenery was off the hook...


These guys were always on our minds, although we didn't see any. There was plenty of evidence that they were our neighbors...


My little wee strom was loaded down but she performed admirably...


It was a great trip and I can't wait till next year... Bella Coola?

As great and beautiful as it was it's always nice to be back home!
