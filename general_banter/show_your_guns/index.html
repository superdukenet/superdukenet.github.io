I love things mechanical, including guns, but limit my interest to air guns. Canada has some pretty stiff gun laws and even air rifles and pistols are restricted and require licensing if they shoot over 500 fps. The parts to upgrade certain airguns to shoot from 1000 to 1500 fps are available and not restricted, but the resulting modified gun requires a firearms license. These single shot guns aren't going to do me much good during the zombie apocalypse, but it is fun and cheep to shoot these with my son, plinking away in the back yard. Less than $10 for 500 rounds!

My father had the job of shooting the main guns on a battle ship and later spent quite a while roving round the sea exploding old mines with anti aircraft guns after the second world war so perhaps I got the love of it from him.

As a child it was somehow discovered that I had a talent for it so I ended up shooting everything from skeet through running boar to hand guns and long range target shooting doing OK along the way. Then a serious phase which involved as many of the government funded toys as I could get hold of and playing with things that explode. That was followed by some time with shotguns again bagging things that fly for the pot and butcher.

Haven’t fired a shot now for close on a decade and strangely I never missed it at all. Occasionally when I hear a shoot in the distance it makes me smile but mostly I’m done with killing things and breaking things. I’m more in a saving things and making things mode these days.
