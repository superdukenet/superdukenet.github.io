I can be a grumpy bastard at times but I’m not short, dour or monotone so I do not make this defence of the midget (as many seem to think of him) out of any sense of solidarity.

I have no idea if he will ever win the championship but he has won a shit load of GP’s against the very best of the rest, wet and dry and on all sorts of tracks. Weather he eventually bags a championship or not the man is a wizard on a bike and graces the top flight of the two wheel racing world. The fact that Stoner before and possibly the Mark bloke now can produce the extra fraction of a % more consistently doesn’t in my view detract from what the midget offers.

To address the question, no I don’t think he should be penalised for the crash. Bit of a freak outcome from a very minor contact which could not reasonably be anticipated. If he had directly punted him off then I would be arguing yes for the penalty as being punted off from behind due to somebody else’s mistake ruins the entertainment for everybody.
