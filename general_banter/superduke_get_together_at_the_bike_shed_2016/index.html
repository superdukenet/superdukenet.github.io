Who's up for a Superduke forum get together at the Bike Shed sometime this year?

Met up with Millar and Jules last week and also been there a few other times since it opened up, it's a great place with a good vibe and great food and Dutch and his lovely wife are usually there to make sure you get treated well.

No problem with parking in London because threre's plenty of secure off-road places on Bike Shed property so we could do a bike meet or if everyone wants to drink it's got great transport connections to the tubes and main line stations.
