Bit of a baptism of fire for the old girl today. About 200 miles around S. East bouncing off the rev limiter, both pegs down, both wheels off the ground a couple of times. You do notice the damper when the bike is airborne (or the lack of one on the 05). Very happy shopper, thanks Cavham
