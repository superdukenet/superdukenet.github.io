Just got home from what seemed to be the longest train ride ever. Although how glad am I that the SD was left under her blanket this weekend! Why was I even entertaining the idea of riding up there?

Was a fantastic night and had the added surprise of Shade turning up unexpectedly. Revellers were Superdan, Twisted Jester, Freestyler, Razy and myself. Dan (Motoronin) was truly the host with the most and put on a fab spread of snacks and booze. Maybe even a bit too much booze as I found to my cost Drinking 1/2 a bottle of Rum (turned into 3/4 of a bottle when I was making excuses for my early exit) wasn't the best move for longevity and I found myself smashed by 1-ish I think and making my way home to Dan's for a conversation with the big white telephone

My shame wasn't compounded by the guys when they got back as they decided not to go with Dan's idea of gang raping me Cheers guys

There were some very sore heads and queasy stomachs this morning. Superdan had to shoot off as the poor bugger had a 12 hour shift starting at 4pm. Photos were viewed of the nights activities and much laughter and shaking of heads as the blurred memories were bought back into sharp focus. I'm sure Ronin will have them upon here for all to see soon. Personally I'm once again never going to drink again. My poor head and body feel used and abused but at least my anus doesn't.

Great night guys and shame some of other South East regulars couldn't attend. Always next year!
