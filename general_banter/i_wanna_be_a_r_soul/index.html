Hello all, first post newbie here and my KTM is not fluent yet so please be gentle.

Last Friday I had a test ride on a '07 SD and after 28 years of devotion to big jap fours I have decided to make the switch to the KTM.
Only trouble is I want the R and nothing else will do, this is based on nothing more than pure emotion as I still haven't seen one in the flesh.
I have tried to kid myself that I could save a barrow full of money and get a low mileage '06 and buy a few trick bits but it's no use, it's got to be the R.
So I am busy selling anything that isn't screwed down to get that bike as I don't want to get finance. I've seen a few used ones but people want 8.5k (UK) is that a realistic figure? when they were 8.9 new.
