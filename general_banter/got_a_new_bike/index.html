Well I could not resist any longer so I went out and got a new bike. But first let me say, I'M KEEPING THE MY SUPERDUKE-R TOO! I wanted an RC8R but just could not spend the cash so I went a got a 2011 Suzuki GSX-R750. This is the first sport bike I've had since I owning the original Honda Hurricane 600 back in 1987. Jumping off the Duke to a sportbike took about a week to get use too. Still takes a few miles to adjust. Both bike are great. For street riding the Duke cannot be beat, for the fast stuff up in the mountains and trackdays the Gixxer is rock solid. I wanted a 600 but the 750 was only a little more money.
