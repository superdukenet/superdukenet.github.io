Sheesh, despite all the "hilarious" cynicism, London is a great city! Avoid the tourist trail and find it's arts scene. (I work there btw)






Or, book a trackday at Brands Hatch...not too far if you have transport.

Drink in the Castle Pub in Camden and get the opportunity to punch Amy Winehouse in the face.

Check out venues for gigs at Brixton Academy/Shepherds Bush Empire/Bar Academy Islington.

Shopping and drinking in Covent Garden.

Chinatown.

Harrods/Knightsbridge (if you like that sort of thing).


Tell you what, if you tell me what you like to do I'll make some more specific recommendations.
