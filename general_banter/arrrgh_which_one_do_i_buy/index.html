All other things being equal and if the smaller tank range is not an issue*. I would buy the 55 plate. If it has done 9000 miles or more this may be a benefit as the 9000 mile service is a biggy. The early bikes had a couple more BHP as standard too. Try to get it for 4 grand though, then it will be a bargain.

*Early bikes had 15 litre tank and run out of fuel after about 100 miles. 07 model onwards had a 17 litre tank.

I have an 07 SDR cos I am a tart for the orange frame. It is not a better road bike than a standard SD. Ok if you do mostly fast rides and trackdays though.

If you are mainly a road rider want the extra fuel capacity, nicer graphics, more bling brakes and clocks you can keep a goldfish in get the 58 reg. Especially if it is white / black.
