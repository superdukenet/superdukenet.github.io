Hi All,
Some of you know I founded a U.S. 501c3 Charity seven years ago to help with the emotional side of living with Rheumatoid Arthritis and related conditions in young and middle aged adulthood. This summer I will be riding my Superduke across the U.S. for my org I figured I would do a shameless plug today in case any number of the great folks in this forum are in a position to help the organization. Over the years our local and national programs have helped make thousands of new understanding friendships for what could be a very isolating illness.
