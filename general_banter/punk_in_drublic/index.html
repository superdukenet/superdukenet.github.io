Fifth gear a motoring show over here actually did a test with a guy driving a car before and after smoking a joint and he actually drove better after the joint than before.

I think the RAC also did a test and had similar results.

This doesn't mean get caned and go driving but does go to show that having a moderate joint does not necessarily affect your driving for the worse.

These days I only smoke one joint a night in the late evening and have never felt the need to smoke one during an activity such as track riding or other sports but for some people it helps them to relax if they're feeling a bit nervous or otherwise, it doesn't mean they're caned and if they're mindset is better because of it then they're more likely to be safer on track, I think it's unlikely that a single moderate joint will make somebodys reaction times slower if they're a regular smoker.
