Went to Dutch's latest bike show at London't Docklands today, the third one he's put on. Each time bigger and better than the last. He's done an amazing job of pulling together some really great bikes and suppliers into a really fitting venue and filling it with the cutting edge of cool London people. I could be a cynic about this latest trend of custom brat style cafe racer scene and how it's attracting the swarms of idiot, wannabe trendies willing to spend a lot of dosh for a bit of the action but I can't because I had such a good time and it really is a chilled and seductive vibe. Big, mash, mixture of bikes, steam-punk engineering, arthouse and old school cool.
