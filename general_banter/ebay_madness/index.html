Might be a sign of the times but looking through ebay at various things here and you can scroll down a full page of some that have zero bids.

There are also some things that people go nuts over and I'm sure pay more than retail.

I just picked up a pair of dainese leathers for $150. About 62 UK pounds.

Get this. I bought some Hein Gerieki (spelling) leathers a couple of years back for $70 and just sold them on ebay for $227.
Yet I got zero bids on a ladies jacket started at $50

I sold a model car as well that usually would sell for around $400.
It only fetched $235.

The best was an old surfboard that was beaten to a pulp by my Son.
I was going to dump it but decided to stick it on and see what happened.
It sold for $68.

It seems almost everything has a value. So before you take a trip to the dump??
