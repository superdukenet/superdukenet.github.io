Here is an article about a J.D. Power and Associates study examining the reasons people give for not buying a bike:

http://www.motorcycledaily.com/10december08_jdpower.htm

Of course price is a big issue. No test rides, the model not being available at a dealership, and crappy dealer service all are big barriers. No surprises, but I'm glad to see I'm not alone. I like my dealer (well, the service side anyway), but the sales side doesn't allow test rides of most bikes I am interested in.
