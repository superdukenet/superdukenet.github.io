Another night popping out to my out-buildings for a sneaky fag and a gin and tonic. Superduke relegated to less than honourable guest in a garage converted, temporarily, to a woodworking shop to make my kitchen work-surfaces. Sort of hate having cleanliness and metal tools, the smell of oil, petrol, etc. overtaken by sawdust, dust and the heady smell of teak oil. Prefer torque wrenches and allen keys to routers and sandpaper.

But it's all good and I like sitting there, even though it's cold and pissing down outside. Having a fag, contemplating life and sitting on my bike like a 15 year old, just good to be the owner of this great bike and loving every minute I've had it.
