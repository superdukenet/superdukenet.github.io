To put the British pothole thing in perspective I will state the following.

I commute to work mostly on a Yamaha twist and go 125 scooter type abomination. (but it's a great commuter machine) and very cheap to run The potholes in this country are so bad because of the lack of investment and poor repairs. In London the roads after this winter's frost are really appalling. I'm now on my third set of fork seals for the Yamaha in five years and a lot of this is because of hitting holes that are at times six inches sheer deep in the road surface. They could throw you over the handlebars if you hit them unaware. (the other reason is speed humps but that's another story).

I'm on a mission to sue a London council for the cost of repairing my forks and replacing my rear shock - just for the hell of it. So I carry around a little plastic ruler and If a ever hit a pothole really bad I stop and take a photo of the hole with the ruler in it and a picture of the road and conditions. I'm waiting for a real beauty - the mother of all potholes. I don't really need to have hit it bad - just see it and I'm going to get the picture, lay the bike down and get a picture of it on the floor, get a witness to sign up to the fact that there is a great big fooking HOLE in the road and then start the process of CAUSING THEM AS MUCH SHIT AS POSSIBLE!

Something needs to be done about this. Mt ex wife is Californian so I have spent a lot of time out in the States. Man....your roads are like RACETRACKS! Our roads are like MOTOCROSS tracks!!! That's why I spent five years on a KTM Supermoto - to be honest the best machine for London road conditions.
