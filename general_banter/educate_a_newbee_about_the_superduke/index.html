Hey,

Yep design wise the SD is considerably better than the X1.
The O2 sensor does compensate enough - that I have seen.
You will find that the buell will have a 'morepowerful' bottom end... but the SD will be able to beat that by spooling up quicker and kill it with power.
Weight is mch better and a muffler set will save you another 20 pounds!

Suspension is worlds away from what I have read about the buells. The SD is razor sharp!

I am sure you will not be disappointed, we are all testaments here. Everyone is impressed over Tuono's Speed Triples, R1's, Gixxers, 600's, 1L, sorry I cant name everyone's old bike!

Sorry - no idea about cylinder walls, but I expect nickasil coated Alloy for weight. It is the lightest and smallest engine in it's class.
