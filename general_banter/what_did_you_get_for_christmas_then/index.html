It's as simple as it sounds but I'm not sure the Witch of your household deserves Sonos.

You plug the main unit ( about 8 inches square, small enough the Witch won't notice) into your router, assuming you are allowed such an appliance, then wirelessly connect it to as many rooms as you want or can afford S5 players.You dont need speakers in each room if the Sonos units are to your liking.
