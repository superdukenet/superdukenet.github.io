A few weeks ago I noticed some parts for sale here. I contacted the member and we worked out a deal with shipping. He lives in Canada and suggested waiting till he came to the US to ship the parts to save on the related cost......good idea! After he'd shipped them I get this email:

"Hey man - here's the tracking number. I noticed one of the end caps for the pegs was missing when I was packing things up, so I took the liberty of ordering some from the uk directly to you. Hopefully there in a couple weeks ....
Cheers man!"

In this day and age it's so easy for people to just brush things off to the next guy. Not that the caps were a big deal and I'd probably not even given it a thought if one were missing. The simple fact that he stepped up to make things right no matter how trivial speaks volumes to me. It would have been simple to just say "ah well, it's just a plastic cap"

I felt that his actions deserved a mention.

So here's to you GeneralLee! If you're ever over my way hit me up and I'll buy you a beer!

Thanks again!

Darren
