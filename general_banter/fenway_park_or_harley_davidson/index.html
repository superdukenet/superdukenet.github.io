I get bags of rags from a thrift store. They are ussually all cotton t-shirts and work good for all types of cleanups and pollishing. I save the ones that feel like they have value for what they represent on the shirt and as the bundle shrinks the ones that seemed too cool gets used. I was at the bottum of the selection and had to make a choice between two shirts for the final wipe down of the SD. On the one hand Harley represents American motorcycling and a long heritage of Orange and Black and this being the pre Holloween clean the colors would have enhanced the wipe down. On the other side we have Hallowed ground represented by the Best Baseball team ever in a State that has Plymouth Rock and Boston Cream Pie. If I did not already use my Jimi Hendrix shirt I would not be put in this position. I chose and will not divulge for I feel that it would pain others too much if they knew.
