A Quick Hello to everyone out there.

I want to let anyone out there who is thinking of getting a SD know, do it do it now.

I Have just taken delivery of my brand new 06 model (at a stupidly low price!!) finally taking the plunge after countless years on Jap sports bikes.
I sold my K6 GSXR 1000 after realising i may be a bit big and getting to old for them.

The Gixer is a damn fine bike and i was sad to see it go but i did tend to ache after a ride out. On my first ride on the SD my saddness was gone witin 200 yards its so comfortable and stable i was tackling my favorite B roads as quick if not Quicker than the Gixer while running the SD in!!!!!!!!.
I did just over 150 miles and was pain and ache free!!! what a top bike.

I did have to fill it up twice though but, as my other hobby is photographing petrol stations in the south of England its not a problem!!!!!!!!!

So DO IT DO IT NOW!!!!!!!
