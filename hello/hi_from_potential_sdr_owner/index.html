Hi there!

I currently ride a Buell XB12, which believe it or not is a fun bike - better than you may initially think anyway!
The Buell has to go though, as just shakes itself to death - the number of parts that have fatigued and bust from the vibs is just unreal. Plus the 08 XB12 models have had really crappy fuelling (... and I then go for a snatchy 08 SDR?!... )

And of course I am just sick of getting passed 15m out of every corner just as soon as 600 supersports get on the gas. Would be nice to fiend them off to 120mph instead!

The only hitch may be seeing what comes out from KTM 09 - especially if they fix some of the SDR more obvious faults? Not sure about the venom... Could be fun if it comes out next year.

Live in London, commute 1-2 days a week 90 mile round trip (fun on a SDR...). Use bikes all year round.

Cheers
