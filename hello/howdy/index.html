So Tuesday saw me separate Gin Reapers good lady wife from her SD, and me cover my first 80 odd miles on it taking it home, last bike was a '03 Tuono - first impressions are good, but needed to get home so pretty unadventurous road choices, definitely need to get out and do some proper riding on the backroads, fortunately someone has arranged a bank holiday this weekend!
