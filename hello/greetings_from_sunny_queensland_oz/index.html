Hi, been reading the site for a while and trying to decide on a new ride. It was down to a choice between an MV Agusta Brutale 910 and the Superduke. You can probably guess how I chose. Picking up a mostly stock black 06 model this afternoon.
The Brutale is a great bike no doubt, but for the money and the feeling you get from riding the SD is the winner for me. Also the fact that the future of MV is kind of up in the air a the moment wasn't very confidence inspiring.
I'm trading up from a '95 GSXF750 i've had for close to 6 years, not because I particularly liked the bike, more family circumstances. In saying that, its been very reliable and I don't have many bad things to say about it, apart from how ugly it is.
This will be bike number 8 for me, been through a big group of two stroke boy racers and a cbr600Fi before the suzuki.

Really enjoying reading all the info on here and thanks to all for putting the time in to making the site so useful.
