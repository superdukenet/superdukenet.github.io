Hi all, names Allan.
Spent first half of my life in Adelaide (no known crimes against my name) and most of the rest in Hong Kong.

I noticed a few members are around my age, I'm 53 today, and have a similar never too old attitude (some people say immature but they would be wrong). A common cosmic connection perhaps? or to be more scientific we were obviouslythe best swimmers of the lot, hitting the apexs of the fallopian tube at the right angles and unbeaten on the straights. Or maybe our Mum's never bought us a BMX bike.

Anyway, seems like a lot of nice people and good info on Superduke.net and am looking forward to reading the posts.
Cheers!
