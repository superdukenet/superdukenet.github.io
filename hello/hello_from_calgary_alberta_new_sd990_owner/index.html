Hey hey new forum!

Proud new owner of a low KM 2008 Super Duke 990! This is my SIXTH bike in two years....

2004 Honda XR650R Supermoto - great fun, but flakey lighting package, kick start only, and needed a valve job!
2010 Husqvarna 510 SMR - Also great fun, but a little small for me (6'5" 250).
2009 KTM Duke 690 - well built, lots of fun - but a little underpowered.
2009 Aprilia Dorsoduro 750 - gobs more power than the Duke, but a heavy bike and not the best range on a tank!
2009 Aprilia Mana 850 - bought it for wife to learn to ride. She dropped it. Sold for $1000 profit.

and NOW my ultimate bike. It was between this and an Aprilia Dorsoduro 1200, but this has a lot more style.


2008 Super Duke 990 - just over 8000km (5000 miles).
- Airbox delete
- PC5 with autotune
- Canisterectomy complete
- GPR stabilizer
- Billet bits and bling
- Leo Vince exhaust

Picked it up for $8000 CDN, which is around $6K USD. Not a bad deal I guess. Looking forward to learning more about this bike from all the PROS on here!
