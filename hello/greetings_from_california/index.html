First time KTM'er and owner of a beautiful '07 Super Duke. 150 miles on the clock and so far so good. Thinking about the 2nd-3rd gear sensor mod as my first mod. Just got the suspension sag set as the suspension was very stiff from the dealership. Look forward to learning more about this bike.

The kids are without a doubt, the best thing ever. Before that I was a truck driver for about 17 years.
