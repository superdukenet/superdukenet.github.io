Hi all, my name is Nigel. I reside in winchester, UK. Have been riding since 1995 came to bikes late at the age of 30. I love Twins, since purchasing a TRX850 in 2003 I have never looked back, mostly had Ducati's, owned an RC8R for a while and always fancied a SDR when the right one came along...... and it did just picked myself up a MINT 2008 SDR with a mere 3300 on the clocks. AKrapovics, carbon and in awesome condition. Last bike was a Panigale 1299S, which was simply too much for our Southern English Roads. This little beauty is superb fun. Have a couple of track days booked for next weekend in France and fully intend to get acquainted with my new purchase.

Welcome to the forum mate. You have a nice selection of bikes to compare the SD to. Actually after a 90's 900 Superlight Ducati which was great for fast, smooth A roads and crap around town it made a huge change to have a bike that seemed to enjoy hooning about at any speed, even on backroads where the SS was never comfortable.
