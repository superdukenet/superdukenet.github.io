White ones are fastest. Tell me about it My first ride on my white SD was at 29*F. It was at least 14 years from my last ride on whatever bike. I did'nt know that 29*F is -1,6*C, I presumed it must be much warmer. lImagine this: I had my visor foggy at first breath, still continued the ride. Outside the town some cars was in front of me at 90km/h speed. It was getting dark. Shitty bumpy roads. Visor in full fog, visibility 10% or 20% or so - and it was my first ever time to roll the SD throttle up - JESUS that passing speed felt extreme. I was instinctively on the bars to avoid wheelies. Having nearly zero bike sensation and doing this again and again was addictive (=mixed fear and exitement). I repeated these passings that evening more and more, until I was back after an hour in total dark and did not feel my fingers anymore because of cold. Yeah IT WAS fast for me at that evening... I confess, did not wanted to see or think about bike at least three days after that

I have never felt it so fast thereafter however Have to find some technical roads to ride, actually considering to move me with the bike to Spain therefore...

Hi everyone,

Finally after creating a new mailbox, several emails to the admin I got myself approved.
This must have been the most difficult board to become a member on

Anyhow I am new to motorcycling, from a country called Estonia, located in Eastern Europe.
The reason which brought me to this board is that I bought myself my first bike . A new KTM superduke 990R ! 2013 year model, has not been registered yet as we have winter here at the moment.

I was choosing between the Honda CB1000R and the Superduke 990R. As I am quite tall (1.93m), I felt more comfortable on the duke with its higher seating position, I did like the softer suspension and the ABS on the Honda which should benefit beginner drivers but in the end opted for the Duke. Haven't driven it yet, so we will see if I did the right decision once summer comes my current knowledge is based on reviews and feedback from other owners. My friend has a CB1000R, he likes it a lot, but I just couldn't have the same bike as he has

The Superduke 990R is completely new and sat at the local KTM dealer, so just had to take it as there are no new ones available anymore. It came with 2 years warranty.
Allready ordered the crash protection bundle from GBracing, waiting for it to arrive.
