Hi All,

Traded in a 2006 Blackbird for a 2010 SD 4 weeks ago. A couple of friends have an SD and I wanted another Vtwin. Did a lot of research including some great info from this site and decided on an SD.
Looked around locally and after a couple of duds, found what appears to be a well maintained one with 21K on the ODO.
How do I like it ?.. glad you asked. Bloody brilliant Suspension and engine are fantastic and loving the rider positioning, especially after having to lean forward for the last few bikes I've had over the years.
Anyway, glad to be onboard and looking forward to enjoying the SD.

Received my own OBD2 cable / KTM Adapter from Lonelec yesterday (took 3 weeks to the day). As soon as I hooked it all up it worked.
Mates Lonelec cable was obviously kaputsky (deceptive as it was new still in the packaging). I suspect it was the KTM adapter cable (not the actual ODB2 cable).

Anyway confirmed standard map is loaded (KM611EU0704501Map) but is different when I do a compare to the downloaded standard map (KM611EU0704501Map).
SAI/02 Sensor/EPC are unchecked so I need to determine if unchecking SAI/02 Sensor/EPC changes the mappings or not ?. I'll search / ask in the tech section.
There's a significant difference when F6ing between them both.

Other than that I've done 1100kms and its run great - especially in the colder morning
