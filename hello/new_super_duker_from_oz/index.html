Finally had time to take the 1290 out for a ride on Sunday with the new Bodis de-cat / link pipe too, Made for a much more enjoyable ride, I did the ECU reset after fitting the Bodis, It certainly made a difference as expected down low in the RPM, The 3-4k RPM range where it normally chugged along being a pig it purred along nicely & was able to gently roll on the throttle without having to knock it back a gear, Also reduced the decel pop considerably

Running the stock muffler for now, Going in for the Akro map ASAP / next few days hopefully then I might cut out the baffle tube in the stock muffler

Loving this bike so much, Cant even put it into words, I cant believe how in tune with the bike I am already, Feels like I have been riding it for a lifetime & like it was just meant to be my bike, Best upgrade I have ever done!

#28
Very interested to hear how your audio is with the new Olympus mic mate. When i have my Drift on the helmet mount i use a Sony knock off with a short extension lead and have it tucked up inside the front right side of the helmet. Absolutely no wind noise no matter what speed and the audio is quite good like that. And when i have it on the handlebar mount i use a mic that i have in the little compartment under my seat plugged into a lead that i have run permanently from the bars to the seat, awesome sound. I Just keep the mic level on the camera set to minimum otherwise it overdrives easily. I tried to get rid of the wind noise (onboard mic) with a few different ideas and plans but all failed to some degree, external mic hidden away is the way to go Good luck and enjoy that awesome bike.
