Welcome! You don't say where you're going to be doing your shopping.
I got very frustrated when I was shopping for my SD in UK because of people advertising their bikes as newer than they were. Basically people in UK seem to think it's OK to advertise their bike by the year the bike was registered versus the year the bike was built. This mean there was several bikes out there that were advertised as 2007 and 2008 bikes but they were in fact 2006 bikes or so. Anyway, beware of this, and happy shopping!
