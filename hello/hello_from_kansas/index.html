Welcome to the madness comrade. Nice bike - one of the fast black ones

On this forum you'll find the repair manual that details how to drain and fill the coolant. To find it you use the search button and as a hint try searching for "EPIC" as in EPIC thread.

On page 228 of the manual you should take note that the bikes need to be burped to remove any trapped air in the system during refill. This is done by raising the front wheel 50cms.

Enjoy that ride.

Hello everyone. I picked up my new 2015 duke last Thursday in black and orange. And I have to say I'm impressed. I've owned a lot of bikes in my near 40 years of riding and this bike is damn near perfect. Currently, I have a BMW K1200GT, Roadstar Warrior, and Honda CBR 1000 in the garage.
