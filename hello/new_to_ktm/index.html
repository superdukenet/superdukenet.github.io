Hey, I wouldn't worry too much about it until you get one and see what you can or can't live with. Funny I just sold my 07 GSXR750 to get my latest SuperDuke. Yes the throttle is super responsive and jerky at some speeds, you certainly can't drop down to 40km/h in top gear then still be able to smoothly accelerate away like on the GSXR. Some riders are annoyed by that and some not.

I had an 07 SDR which was totally standard apart from Akro pipes and I never felt the need to change it, now have an 06 and am intending to change the airbox etc but more for the look and ease of maintenence than taming the response.
