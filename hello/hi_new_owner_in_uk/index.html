Hello and welcome

I bought a used SD with 48,000 miles on it, it had been well looked after and was fine (granted I didn't keep it long but only because a bargain came up !)

I've heard of adventure bus's with 90,000 on them and it's the same engine!

Mods well you have aftermarket exhausts which would be the first thing on my list, followed by the gb racing crash kit £300 well spent IMO, after that it's levers, rearset plates, smaller front sprocket (if you feel you need it, I never have) and whatever you fancy really, take a look at the mods thread to give you some ideas on how to spend money

Hello and welcome.

I too had an RSV and moved to the Superduke, not been disappointed in 6 years of owning the Superduke.

Also like you, I had a spine injury, not as severe as yours sounds. In order to make life a little more comfortable I had the suspension at both ends modified, which included a softer spring at the rear. Most will say the softer spring is a route to poor handling. Well I guess http://www.reactivesuspension.com/ did a cracking good job, because I have no complaints.
