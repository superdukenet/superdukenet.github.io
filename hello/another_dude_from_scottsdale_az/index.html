Gents,
Looking forward to letting this forum consume any free time for a while. Recently picked up an '08 sd, and couldn't be happier with its awesomeness. It has most of the common go fast parts, minus removing the secondaries, and has the basic akro tune, and farts through yoshi slip ons. I bought it with 10k miles, and have since then serviced it with new fluids and plugs. I was planning on a new waterpump, and a valve adjustment, but everything seems great at this juncture, other than some tuning issues with the colder weather. exhaust gaskets, graphite ring, are next on the list, and the left slip on is loose at the y pipe, which is getting re fitted as soon as i send it back to Yoshimura. Other plans are a fabricated belly scoop, some fabbed trumpets and more carbon fiber. - Ryan
