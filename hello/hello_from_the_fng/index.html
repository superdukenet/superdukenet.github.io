Hello all, f'ing new guy from the Washington, DC area.
I think I may have the only Super Duke in town! I never see anyone on one in or around town. I can't believe more people don't have this bike! It's super fun to ride, and it's definitely a head turner.
I joined the forum to seek out other SD riders, track day event buddies, advice on maintenance, performance upgrades, aftermarket goodies, etc...
My previous bikes (2001 Suzuki SV 650, 2003 Yamaha R6) had ample dealer and aftermarket support, but as we all know, the KTM has less support spread more thin. The nearest dealers are an hour away from my house even though I live in a major city, so it looks like I'll be ordering lots of stuff online and doing lots of work myself (I like to do it myself anyway).


2007 Super Duke. Orange, of course.
Current Mileage - 260 (still running it in, so I'm kind of puttering around but still having fun).
