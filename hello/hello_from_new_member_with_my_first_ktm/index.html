Welcome on board dude.

SAS is there just to help emissions. Allows air into the headers on the overrun to help burn off any unburnt fuel. If you leave it on with an aftermarket can you'll just get lots of pops, bangs and flames on the overrun. It's not a difficult job to install block off plates and I'm guessing there is a plug from KTM to stop the ECU giving a FI light if you disconnect the SAS system.
