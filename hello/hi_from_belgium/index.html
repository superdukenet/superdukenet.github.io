Hello, I'm Remy from Belgium, 35 years old, married with 2 children. On a SD since july 2010.
It's a black 05 with Akrapovic exhausts, Akra map (that I'd like to change), tankbag and Diablo supercorsas.
Previously I rode japanese sportbikes like gsxr 750, 2 zx6r's and a 06 ZX10R on road and trackdays, and a little bit amateur endurance races with the ZX10R in 2009.
While my 10R was for track only, I bought a 99 SVS650 for the street and discovered the twins with it.

I sold both svs and 10R and got my SD last year because I didn't like 4 cylinders anymore.

Now I'm happy on this great bike
