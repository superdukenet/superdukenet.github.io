In London there was Bracken who have since shut down but I think Paul - who was their mechanic is still operating from the same place in South London. Not sure of his number but a forum member (Think No. 47) here always lets us know his number. It will be posted up soon I'm sure.
I was in London 'till last year,

Best place for what you need is HMRacing who are now in Edenbridge, Kent
http://www.hmracing.co.uk/

They used to do a lot of tuning on the Superdukes battle series bikes, (all the Bracken race bikes as well). They've done all my power commander/dyno work as well as fitting their quickshifter and can also do suspension set up. Not too far from London really, only about an hour, mind you depends on where you are in London.
