My name is Kyle, and I drank the orange kool-aid ten years ago. I race local motocross and national enduros/GNCCs. I am 20 years old. 4 months ago I was riding my 08 Duke 690 taking my girlfriend home when a drunk driver ran through an red light and hit us. Breaking 18 of my bones, including my back and legs. And even more in my girlfriends body. We both, are upright and walking now. God is good. So with my beloved Duke gone I needed a replacement. Last week I purchased an 08 SDR. She's everything the old Duke was plus some. Plenty of useable power from idle to redline. *Spoiler alert* I do work at a local stealer. But I'm not in it to make money, if I was, I'd get a real job. Lol I just love riding. I'm always open to riding with new people and finding new roads so if you're close, let me know! Keep her rubber side down!
