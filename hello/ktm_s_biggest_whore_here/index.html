Hello folks, my name is Andrea Pessino (not female, just Italian), I just joined this great board... Italian by birth, I have lived in Southern California for almost 19 years now.

I was already "The World's Biggest KTM Whore" when two weeks ago I was attending the motorcycle show in Long Beach, CA and I had the opportunity of test ride a Superduke. Big mistake... I should know better than riding a KTM bike I don't already own! The test ride was amazing... it really is as if KTM makes their bikes with me in mind. I could not stop thinking about it, so sure enough, I picked up a brand new Superduke R two days ago. A truly spectacular bike...
