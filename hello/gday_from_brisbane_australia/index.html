Thought its probly time to introduce myself, I have been surfing around the forum for about 3months. Lots of good info that has helped me out alot!
Im Nathan (25), From Brissy Australia. I have an Orange 08 Superduke 990 which i have had for about 3months, really happy with it. I commute to work and ride most weekends on her.
