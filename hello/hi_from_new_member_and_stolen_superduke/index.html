well evening all, this is an introduction but also a post to watch out for a stolen Superduke

Been using this forum since owning my SD a year and a half ago and its been great, never joined before but wanted to give you all a heads up on my bike which has just been stolen (hope this is in the right section, apologies if not)

Description :

2006 black Superduke
Registration : BX06 VRU
Carbon front fender (KTM)
Solo seat conversion (KTM)
several ktm orange bling bits
akros

Totally gutted as not only was this my main transport but also my hobby, cleaned and polished every weekend and was so looking forward to summer on it. It seems several guys in a white transit came down and lifted it along with all chains and such. so annoyed as i think i heard them and saw a van drive off but they must have come back for it later on. Was stolen from Bury, Manchester, UK in the early hours of Thursday morning (31st May).

If you guys can obviously keep an eye out, i know its probably in bits now but the solo seat conversion (£540 from ktm) is quite rare for that year so not many about and the ktm carbon fender might pop up on ebay etc. Just got brand new corsa 3 tyres and new mot..not a good month for me.

anyway, waiting on insurance but not sure if i can bring myself to get another Superduke, i love the bikes but the amount of time and money i put into it i'm just not sure. Might go back to my sportsbikes

ok i'll fill you in a little bit..

woke up thursday morning at 7.00am to find it gone. Reported to police. Got a call and went through the motions then an email at 2.00pm that day saying case is closed. One neighbour said she reported a white transit with 3 guys changing the number plate outside her house, this was reported at 1.00am thursday morning. did the police investigate...no. So, we ring police and ask about this...their reply was 'do you think they're connected'....honestly i almost laughed.

Yesterday i find out from the neighbour that the van has been found. The police have not been in touch with me and i only know this talking to my neighbour. I cant believe how incompetent they have been and i am not happy at all.

I just want to get it all sorted with insurance and then i might actually put a complaint in with the police as it just feels like they couldnt be arsed with it at all. And this isnt just a little toy, this is my main transport and hobby, something i have spent a lot of time and money on.

Dont think i'll see again, we all think it was stolen to order and i must have been followed. i suspect its in bits now or out of the country.
