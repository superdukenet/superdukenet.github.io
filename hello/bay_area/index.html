I traded a Hypermotard last Saturday for a black SD-R. My local dealer refused the trade so I had to go about 50 miles back to the dealer I bought the Hyper from. The new Ducati's have such terrible lean surge issues I think I'm done with them unless they do can something about it.

The test ride impressed the hell out of me, and I have seen nothing in 200 miles to change my initial impression. This thing feels as solid as my BMW GS seemed, but with a lot more character and style. The seat is MORe comfortable to me than the GS, or at least equal. How is that even possible? I love the ergos and the engine, and I have yet to even dip a toe into the available power. I have an enthusiasm for riding I haven't had in years. My girlfriend is totally stoked that I got a bike she can ride on too.

Go orange!!
