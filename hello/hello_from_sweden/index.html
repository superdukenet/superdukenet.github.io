Greetings all,

I'm back riding a SD again after my crash in 2008 and a year on an RC8. The RC8 is great, but it's not for me. The RC8 was terrible to ride in town, but it was awesome on the track (apart from I almost broke my neck trying to look straight ahead while riding it...)
I've just got a near new orange 08 that I'll start playing with shortly. The RC8 will be for sale in a week or so. No more sports bikes for Nickeluring...
