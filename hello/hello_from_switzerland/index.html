Hey Guys

Been reading along this Forum for a while now and thought it might be appropriate to introduce myself!

28year old living in Switzerland (ZG) spending as much as possible of his free time on the SDR. I have bought my SDR1290 in May 2015 and have ridden it for around 6500km this season. This bike is just the best thing out there, period!! I actually almost signed for a Diavel this spring, after having ridden a Speed Triple 1050 for a while. But the test ride (out of curiosity, because of all the positive reactions and press) blew my mind so i had to buy it instantly .

So now I am still breaking the law a lot (plan was to settle down a bit with the diavel) and the SDR-Virus has infected me. My bike is black (#TeamBlack Hello Folks! ) and i have modified so far:

-KTM Power Parts Seat and rear Seat (im 1.87m, so i do really need it)
-KTM Power Parts Tank-Grips
-KTM Power Parts Tail-Tidy (to be replaced by R&G as soon as shipped)
-Small round mirror left
-Akrapovic Slip-On (W/O DB-Killer)
-Akrapovic Carbon Fiber rear Mudguard
-Racefoxx Fuelcap in Black
-Racefoxx reverse shifter (just tried to mount with original rearset, somehow won't work... )
-Rizoma Indicators all around (Rizoma ZERO, fits the bike perfectly with its "edgyness")

I am riding mostly in Switzerland and Schwarzwald (D) - happy to meet some other riders nearby, always up for a spontaneous ride!

I will post some pictures when im done with my pending work and mods. For those who have it, my instagram, which is basically all about Bikes and Cars : https://instagram.com/time_runs/
