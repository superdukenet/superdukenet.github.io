Hi!.. thougt ill intruduce myself!.
Bought an Superduke 2007 Anthracite last weekend.
former bike was an cbr600rr and i do not regret one sec that i changed from the cbr to the superduke!
the last five former bike's was
'05 a Honda CBR600RR 2005
'04 a Ducati Monster 900 1997 the only bike that had brakes that compared to the SD
'04 Honda XR400, still going strong
'03 a Honda Supermotard XR650R 2003
'02 a Honda VFR VTEC 2002
And always some enduro/offroad bikes mainly Honda's that are standing in the garage beside the roadbike for som fun riding offroad.

Currently im driving in the SD and having fun like hell!
waiting for the 1000km service, 350km still to go before that.

The only thing thats bothering me about the SD is the noisy gearbox in the first 1-2-3 gears, but maybe the running in period solves that,sometimes when i put in the second gear the gear box lever spatter against my foot when engaging the second gear.

Otherwise i love the bike! it really behaves like a supermotard on steroids


//Marcus -Sweden
