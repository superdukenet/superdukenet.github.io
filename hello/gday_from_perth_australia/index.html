Welcome from sunny Melbourne, I did a similar thing , but made a costly error,
I went to trade my Honda blackbird XX in on a Honda CB1000R, when I got to the dealer they also had a 05 990 superduke, so I test rode the Duke as well, having never ridden a KTM road bike previously, I was soooo impressed with the KTM.
I test rode the Honda and The KTM back to back 4 times!, in the end I bought the Honda as it was younger and better maintained than the Duke on offer

Unfortunately it was a costly error, I owned the Honda for five weeks, I enjoyed it, but didn't love it, it was just too
Honda, did everything well, but gave no feedback, no "wanting to go for a blast", it just gave a basic transport feel,and
I just couldn't get the Duke out of my head, So five weeks later I traded the Honda and my BMW track bike for a
SD, it appears to me that regardless of the specific model, super Dukes are something special

Enjoy!!!!
