Long way away from most of you here, just picked up a used 990 ('09, about 7k on the clock) and joined this community almost immediately afterwards. Used to ride another - but much heavier - naked Vtwin before this, the Yamaha MT01. Thank you all for the collective wisdom here - its been great reading through most of the posts.
