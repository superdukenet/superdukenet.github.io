We've done a bit of the dukes pass (mind u, u have to keep an eye out for gravel in the road up there... ) we normally start in Balloch for breakfast then head to Aberfoyle up over the dukes pass to Callander and onto the Green welly to refuel and a cuppa and then onto Inverary thats only a short run for an afternoon it's only 93 miles or we head to Oban for a run as the road down from the welly to Oban is also a good road.....

There was one run we did and it was a monster of a run (up and back all in was 325miles... ) we started at my m8's place in Dumbarton and headed up to Onich just outside of fort William and caught the ferry across to Ardgour (only take 5 mins & £2) and then did the scenic route (beautiful country views) u can't really go that fast on these roads as their only tracks enough for one car but have plenty of pull ins to pass on couple nice pubs and cafes to stop at and before u know it ur on the Mallaig road yahheeeee it's only 40miles long but it gives a whole new meaning to twisties....

U can go to the most westley point in Scotland this way once u have caught the ferry across to Ardgour follow the road all the way to Salen it might help if u have a sat nav so u can plan the route (the only thing I'd say with this run is stop as much as possible and start as early as possible
