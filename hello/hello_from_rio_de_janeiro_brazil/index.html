Hi,

I just bought me a new 2010/10 black&orange Superduke 2 months ago and I am enjoying it very much!
Because of the dumm paper burocracy here in Rio to get the license plates for the bike took me 1 month to get licensed and I coudnt ride it far away. It is almost reaching the first 1000 km's now.
I am having a few small issues with the bike since I took it from the dealer. The chain was too loose, the throttle has also a gap and the bike dies sometimes when still cold. I hope all this gets solved by the 1000km's service soon.
Before having the SD I owned a 2004 Honda 250cc Tornado, a 2008 Honda CB600 Hornet then a 2007 Husqvarna SM610.
My bike has no mods yet, but I'm already planing them.

Cheers,
