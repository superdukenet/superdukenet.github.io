Thanks for the welcome.

I don't miss the zx10 at all. In any power mode it was just way too much for public roads and I'd find myself riding everywhere at 4k revs or less which isn't much fun let alone the insurance cost.

The duke on the other hand has a much better riding position and a noise to die for, the insurance is a third of the zx10 cost and the black and orange combination looks awesome. I'm really pleased with my decision to change.

I bought the bike second hand and have only ridden it twice so no the chicken strips are not down to me.
