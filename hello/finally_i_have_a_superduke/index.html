After wanting one for so many years, at last I have a Superduke.
£3000, 2007, 30k miles. Bit tatty in places but that's all gonna change anyway - I am a serial fiddler and usually don't stop until there are no more things to do. Picked it up last Sunday and had a nice long ride back to Sheffield from Cardiff, awesome engine, ridiculously comfy, sooo light and quick steering, love it! Shame it's not stopped raining all week, hasn't stopped me though
My main bike's an MT-01, high comp, full titanium, brembos, v stacks, airbrushed..... Theres no more mods left to do it (except a turbo and bst wheels ) now I have a two of my dream bikes!
I'll be spending quite a but of time on here, 100+ technical/mod posts to catch up on!
