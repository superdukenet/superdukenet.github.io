Hello all,

I live in Beamsville, Ontario and just recently bought an 07 Black sd with a full Akro exhaust and a few other goodies. I don't have the bike quite yet. Going to pick it up in just over a week depending on cooperative winter weather.

My first bike was a 750 Honda Nighthawk. Next came a ZX1100. I thought that was an awesome bike. It was sold during school and later came a 'filler' gpz 900. That's the bike I've been riding for the past three years. All the while I've been saving up for a ZX14. I was so sure that I was going to buy the zed. As it turns out I had a chance to ride a smaller bike and I had a blast. I began to think more about insurance costs, potential tickets, and what truly makes a ride fun and I turned towards the naked bikes.

I was planning to get a Tuono but I didn't see any at the Toronto Motorcycle show. I did however get a chance to sit on a Superduke and I loved it. I'm 6'5" and I was quite comfortable on it. I just happended to see one for what I thought it was a good deal and just went for it,

Anyway, I'm very ecited to pick up the bike and see what I can learn from thie forum over the the long winter months.
