Weather broke this morning [middle of winter here] so we got in a feww hundred k's...

YES my arse is sore! I gotta say I wanted to say harden up to all the moaners out there about the SDR seat but I wisely kept my mouth shut until I had tried it...

I didn't think it was possible to make foam that hard???? I mean its sposed to have air in it somewhere isn't it?

Fortunately I am a mountain biker so I am used to 'ergonomic' saddles wrecking your walking style... I am hoping the KTM customer response commitee are busy making a 'comfort' option for the hard parts cataloge [thats ironic] right now - failing that i am off to the local upholsterer...

Gotta get some stone guard though - I was getting nailed by our crappy metaled roads.
