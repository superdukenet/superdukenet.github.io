Afternoon Everyone

I will start by saying Happy New Year to you all

I have joined up here as I am looking to buy a 990 Superduke this year. I have been reading reviews on them and they sound like a great all round bike.

I was originally looking at getting a Yamaha R6 but after just sitting on one in the showroom it was blindingly obvious that I wouldnt be able to ride it for more than about 20 mins without getting serious back ache! I broke my back after falling down some stairs a few years ago and the lay down position becomes pretty uncomfortable pretty quickly!

So I popped into a local bike shop who happened to have a SuperDuke which I had a long sit on and didnt seem to get any twinges etc

I am going to have a good look round the forum to try and pick up on anything to look out for
