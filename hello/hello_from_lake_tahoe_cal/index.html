Well, its been 3 months since I got the 2008 demo with 600 miles on the clock it only has 2800 now, but toughs have been some of the funnest miles ever. Been riding mostly in Marin Co.- US 1- ect. because I've been working down there. Holy Shit the roads in the bay area are Fantastic. The Super Duke Loves them too. My girlfriend got a 09 1100 monster all sorted -pipes-olins-ect and i have had the chance to ride it a few times. Its crazy how different the bikes are and after a few miles all i can think is, Give Me Back My Super Duke. It's all good cuz she loves her Monster especially after the Olins. I had the Acras put on before I had ridden it but I still find it a little touchy especially compared to the 2010 Adventure R which has stock pipes. It is also amazing how tame and easy the adventure is compared to the Super Duke. Before every ride on the SD I slap my face and say "Wake Up Dumb Ass your about to saddle a BEAST. Can't wait to get her all sorted and I'm so glad to have this place to help along the way. Any map guys in the bay? Thanks
