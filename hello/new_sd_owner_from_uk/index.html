Hey fellas,

A week ago I became the proud owner of a 2010 SuperDuke 990 in black. Previously I owned a Kawasaki Z1000 but wrote it off. Always loved the SD's so took the opportunity to finally own one.

I'm big into modding my bikes, my zed had thousands into it and I plan to do the same with my SD. So far I've upgraded the cans from standard to yoshi carbons. I've upgraded the levers and fitted some Rizoma bar end mirrors. Next will be upgrading the indies with Rizoma ones. At the moment I think the bikes mental so I won't be looking at any sort of tuning yet.
