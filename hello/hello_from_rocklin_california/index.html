I too was a graduate from the Duke II. I had them in the garage at the same time. It took me close to a year to sell the little one for the right price. When it finally happened it was a very happy day for the more sensible half of the household. They are two completly different rides and I will get another II when I either win the lottery or rob another liquer store.
I talked to a former Superduke owner today. A gentleman from Austin. He and I agree that it is a very sharpenned intrument for just a tool around bike. It asks much out of its rider. It seems to be a hard bike to tool around on.
What other bikes do you have in the stalls? I take my hat off to anyone who can ride this bike day in and day out in the style and expectations this bike was designed for. Or I could be a huge pussy and need to shut the fuckup.
We have such crap backroads in these parts that the Duke II was actually much more suited for this neck. Santa Barbara county has much wealth and it does not seem to trickle down to road improvements.
Up the road a piece is Duke4Dirk. He'll pop his head in real soon I am sure. Anyway welcome home.
