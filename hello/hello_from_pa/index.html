Hello all, I'm Mike and I just picked up an 08 Superduke in black and am loving it. I was shopping for a used Ducati hypermotard before I noticed a Superduke on craigslist. I managed to score a great deal on a used 08 that only had 1300 miles on it and still has another year of warranty. I'm very happy with the bike so far. My last bike was a 06 Speed Triple and the SD trounces it in the handling and comfort departments. My observations so far:
1. The final drive ratio is just plain stupid-high. Need that 16T front sprocket as soon as possible. After a quick look online it seems I have to get this from the dealer??
2. This is my first experience with Brembos, and they live up to their hype. Love them.
3. Stock exhaust needs to go. I'd like to make my bike to sound as aggressive as it looks.

That's it. If anyone is in the Reading, PA area, shoot me a PM and we'll go for a ride.

Mike
