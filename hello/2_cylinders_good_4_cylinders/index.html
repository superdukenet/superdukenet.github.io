Prior to purchasing bike checked out OK.

Orginally purchased from Inmoto (Croydon) offical KTM dealer who confirmed the sale, that it was UK machine, fitted akras and remapped. They were somewhat shocked and suprised to learn that the bike was being sold after only 2 months.

Inmoto delivered unit by van, the 23 miles were accured during one trip up and down the road prior to it being garaged. There was not even a mark on the footrest heel protectors.

The person selling it was also selling a GSXR 1000 (05 plate) and simply had a change of direction with regards to biking coupled with some personal problems.

Having racked up a number of miles now, I am super impressed with the machine, its eveything and more, and it looks A1: sex on wheels (although wife is not super impressed with ths comparsion)
