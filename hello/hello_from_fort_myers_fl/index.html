Hi all,
I recently traded my '08 Kawisaki Versys for an '07 SD in black. I made the trade in Clearwater, FL, and rode the SD back home to Ft Myers. Man, what a machine! I love this bike! It's everything I've ever wanted from a motorcycle. So much more bike than before. I should also mention that I test rode a Ducati Hypermotard for a couple days before I bought the duke, and I was a bit let down with it. The SD is better in every possible way.

I love this bike so much. Every chance I get, take it for a ride. I even commute to work in Naples whenever the weather is nice.

One tiny gripe though, I seem to have some sort of electrical issue. The turn signals blink really fast at times. This seems to be RPM/Load dependent, which leads me to think that maybe it is a voltage regulator on it's way out? Any suggestions on this are welcomed.

Thanks,

-Greg
