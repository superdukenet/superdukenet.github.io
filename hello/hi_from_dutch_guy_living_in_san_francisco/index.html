Hi all,

Coming from The Netherlands, I live and work in San Francisco (for a Dutch company). The Bay Area is literally (yes, literally) the wet dream of every motorcycle enthousiast. There is beautiful scenery, hills, twisty roads, mountains, sunshine and people just love their motorcycles here.

For everyone who is not familiar with The Netherlands: It is completely flat, it rains a lot and the only twisty roads are to be found next to our rivers and canals. Those twisty roads however are infected by bicyclists on every singe sunny day of the year.

In Holland I had fun on a KTM 640 SMC and commuted on a BMW C1 200 (Google search that one guys, in the US you missed out on this beauty)

I recently treated myself with a 2007 Super Duke 990, with some nice Akra's, a power commander and only 11k miles. This is going to be a good summer! It has the erratic idle issue though... so I need to get my hands dirty first.

Best,

Martin
