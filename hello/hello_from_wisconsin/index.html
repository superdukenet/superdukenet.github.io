Welcome

For Quick Turn throttle you want this

http://g2ergo.com/shop/catalog/G2-Quick ... TM-43.html

This uses their 050 cam which will give you a 10% quicker turn, or then you could fit the 025 Cam which I think is 20% quicker turn.

A few of us are running these with the 050 cam and are happy with the results.

As far as Mods go there are various things you can do to strip your wallet of cash, the best and most popular ones for engine performance should in my opinion probably be done in the following order as and when you can do them.

Cans
Akra Map
PCIII/Tuneboy/Rapid tune Depending on preference
Motobox and SAS removal

Proper Suspension set up of course should always be high on anybodies list to make the most of the handling.

And then there's plenty of bling available but I'll leave you to discover this for yourself.

Motobox is made by MotoHooligan a member on here and his parts can be found in the Parts and Accessories section.
