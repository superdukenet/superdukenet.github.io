Got a hall pass from the wife and did ACH today. Open all the way up for the first time this year! Still some slurry in sections and a one lane signaled section but not too bad. Was really foggy first thing this morning until we got up high then it warmed and sunnied up. Not too busy at Newcomb's...but way more than last Saturday. Nioce to meet you Carl who posted up last week...just got his new 07 SD from Bert's...looks great! 3 of us at Newcombs...almost enough for a Superduke club! After breakfast we tooled up the the tunnels...some dirt and new pavement but fun.

But...fricking cops all over the place. A few guys got hit today for sure. Really takes some of the fun out of the ride. Again, it's the first open day so maybe it will calm down but they just aren't cool. One of our buddies got a ticket for a track tyre on his bike at the Shell station after the ride getting gas! WTF. That's BS.
