Hi there! New SD owner here! Juho, 24yrs and the SD is my second bigger bike (had a 636 -04 kawasaki zx-6r before). SD is -06, titanium grey, it has Leo Vince Slip-ons, the noise is quite something! Bought it as a "small" project: water pump had failed very badly and the engine might need a larger overhaul. The shop said that the water pump had been changed and new oil and coolant changed, also the engine has been washed because of the waterpump failure. And some golden bits were found in the screen, supposedly from bearings. It still has the oil pressure warning light on so i haven't gotten the chance to drive it yet, but still madly in love with it! (Call me crazy if you want!) i figured i have the whole winter to fix it, but would be great if i could ride her even a bit before winter so i would know what to wait for when winter ends! The paper filter looked a bit odd (i drained the oil out and removed filter and screen and used new oil to flush the system just to be sure). Any tips for the new guy? the dealer said that the bike had been test driven after the water pump replacement and we started the bike also at the dealer and it sounded great (no weird sidenoises, atleast didn't hear anything.
