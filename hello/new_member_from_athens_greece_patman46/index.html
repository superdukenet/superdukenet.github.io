hello to all of you... Happy to be member of SD forum.
please let me introduce my self:
i am patman46 (panos is my name) and just few days before i became happy owner of a SD (my07.5).
after 2 years with a fantastic Tuono Factory (which was fully modified with a lot of racing parts such as Akras Evo6, pc3 + electronic shifter, soutter slipper clutch, brembo monoblock calipers, brembo rcs19 brake master cylinder, brembo rcs 16 clutch master cylinder, ermax windscreen etc) I just bought a SD (just with 3.000km) but lot of stuff on it (since the previous owner was a fanatic but he decided to race this year with a R1).

This is not my KTM, since i also owned 2 Adventures (950 & 990) , a KTM Duke 640 Last edition (1998) with which i used to race supermoto in Greece, and a KTM EXC200.

bikes i owned in my motorcycle life:
1988-1990: aprilia AF1 125

and this because all the above bikes where excellent (fantastic in fact)

a bike which reached 143,8hp on dyno, (294 max speed on speedometer... actual 285 on gps), one bike which made me and my gf to travel from athens to budapest in one day (1984km only with one overnight) fast and mostly very confy...
