Hi All

Thought it was about time I joined the club, I have had my 2007 SD for about one and a half years now, and love it, I bought my SD just before I left the out skirts of London,UK to live in Prague, Czech Republic.

I have located a expats biker club (35 members) from all around the world, and we have pub meets in the winter and bike rides in the summer, the roads aren't great in Czech, especially after the bad winters, and the car drivers are even worse.......... but we have found some great routes and can easliy get to Germany, Poland, Austria and Slovakia in a couple of hours from Prague.

Im going to make you all jelous now and say that our best route is a short blast through the Czech forests to the biker caves, were you ride into the cave and up to the bar and order your beer (Non Alcoholic of course).

I have attached a picture of my SD, since the picture I have added orange shorty levers and fork bobbins.

Thanks for reading and looking forward to getting great advise and tips and hopefully be able to contribute and help fellow SuperDuke owners.

Cheers

Carl
